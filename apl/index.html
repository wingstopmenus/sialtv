2018 season

The APL has only ever finished one season: Sharjah, 5 to 21 October 2018. Balkh Legends won the final, chasing down Kabul Zwanan’s 132/9 with 138/6 to win by 4 wickets. Mohammad Shahzad finished as the season’s top scorer with 344 runs, and Isuru Udana took the most wickets with 17. The five clubs playing in 2026-27 are new — none of them inherits a 2018 title.
