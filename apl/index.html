2018 season

The APL has only ever finished one season: Sharjah, 5 to 21 October 2018, played for the Koh-E-Zafar trophy. Balkh Legends won the final, chasing down Kabul Zwanan’s 132/9 with 138/6 to win by 4 wickets. Mohammad Shahzad finished as the season’s top scorer with 344 runs, and Isuru Udana took the most wickets with 17. The five clubs playing in 2026-27 are new — none of them inherits a 2018 title.

Frequently asked questions

When does APL 2026-27 start?

The first ball is on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM Dubai time), in the UAE. The season runs into January 2027, with the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027.

How many teams play in the APL?

Five teams: Akcel United Kabul, Balkh Zwanan, Kandahar Warriors, Nangarhar Cosmic and Paktia. Each team plays a double round-robin, and the top four reach the playoffs.

Why is there no full fixture list yet?

The Afghanistan Cricket Board has not published the full fixture list. Only the first ball is confirmed. Predicted schedules circulating online are not official and are not shown on this page.

Who are the captains?

Azmatullah Omarzai (Kabul), Ibrahim Zadran (Balkh Zwanan), Rashid Khan (Kandahar Warriors), Rahmat Shah (Nangarhar Cosmic) and Rahmanullah Gurbaz (Paktia). The five captains are also the Afghan Icon players.

Where can I see the full APL schedule and every squad?

Here — the APL Schedule carries every confirmed fixture, and the APL Teams page has all five squads. The full fixture list goes on the schedule page as soon as the ACB publishes it.

Who won the last APL season?

Balkh Legends, in 2018 — the only season the league has finished. They beat Kabul Zwanan by 4 wickets in the Sharjah final, chasing 133. The 2026-27 clubs are new and don’t inherit that title.
