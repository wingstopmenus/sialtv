Fazalhaq Farooqi — Bowler

Fazalhaq Farooqi is the new-ball spearhead of Akcel United Kabul for APL 2026-27 — the joint-highest wicket-taker at the 2024 T20 World Cup, a left-arm fast-medium bowler, and a Diamond pick at the Dubai draft.

Farooqi’s name first appeared on an APL teamsheet in September 2018, when Nangarhar Leopards included the then 17-year-old in their squad for the first season. He did not play a match, and his Twenty20 debut came a year later, in October 2019. The wait looks stranger with every year that passes: by 2024 he was sharing the top of the wicket-taking chart at the T20 World Cup, with 17 wickets.

His best T20I figures tell the story of what he does with the new ball: 5 for 9 against Uganda at that same World Cup, swing and seam doing the damage before batters have settled. Born in Baghlan on 22 September 2000, he is a left-arm fast-medium bowler who bats right-handed — and the left-arm angle is the point. In a league where most quicks bowl right-arm, Farooqi’s swing across right-handers is a different problem.

Kabul took him in the Diamond category at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026. Alongside Dushmantha Chameera’s pace and Mujeeb Ur Rahman’s spin, he gives Shane McDermott an attack with three distinct threats — and Farooqi’s job is the simplest to describe: take wickets in the powerplay, and the rest of the innings gets easier.
