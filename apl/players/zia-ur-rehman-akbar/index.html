Eight years on, a Test cricketer returns to the league

Zia ur Rehman Akbar is back in the APL eight years after his first taste of it — a 28-year-old left-arm orthodox spinner who played two matches for Paktia Panthers in 2018. Balkh Zwanan took him in the Silver round for a reported $20,000, and he arrives as a Test cricketer now.

Balkh Zwanan picked Zia-ur-Rehman Akbar in the Silver category at the APL player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported $20,000. He joins a 19-player squad captained by Ibrahim Zadran, with Mickey Arthur as head coach and Tillakaratne Dilshan as mentor. The league restarts on 27 December 2026 in the UAE; the fixture list had not been published when this page was written.

Akbar is not new to this league. He was in the Paktia Panthers squad for the first APL season in 2018, when the side was captained by Mohammad Shahzad and carried Shahid Afridi as its Icon player. He played two matches, including 1 for 41 against Kabul Zwanan on 5 October 2018 — and that is the sum of his first APL story, two games as a young spinner in a star-studded squad.

What has changed since is everything else. His ODI debut came against Bangladesh at Chattogram on 11 July 2023, and his Test debut against Sri Lanka at Colombo on 2 February 2024. He returns to the APL as an international cricketer, a left-arm orthodox bowler who bats right-handed, and a bowling all-rounder in a squad that already has three other spinners.

Where he fits at Balkh Zwanan

Akbar’s role at Balkh Zwanan is depth with a difference. Ghazanfar’s mystery spin, Qais Ahmad’s leg-breaks and Salamkheil’s wrist spin are all right-arm or wrist — Akbar is the left-arm orthodox option, the one who turns it the other way and ties down an end while the others attack. In a double round-robin of eight league matches, that control is what lets Arthur rotate his strike bowlers.

He is also one of the few players in this squad who has played the APL before, which counts in a dressing room full of debutants. The top four reach the playoffs, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. APL Schedule will carry Balkh’s dates once the Afghanistan Cricket Board publishes the fixture list, and the season in full is on APL T20.
