Nineteen, from Baghlan, and bowling with the stars

Salaam Khan Ahmadzai is 19 years old, an Emerging pick from Baghlan, and one of the youngest players in the APL. Balkh Zwanan took him at the Dubai draft for a reported $10,000, and APL 2026-27 — starting 27 December 2026 in the UAE — is his first season of senior franchise cricket.

Salaam Khan Ahmadzai is in the Balkh Zwanan squad for APL 2026-27, picked in the Emerging category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026. He is one of three Emerging picks for Balkh, alongside Azizullah Miakhil and Abdul Aziz Khan, and one of fifteen Emerging players picked across the five franchises.

The draft took 90 players from a pool of 462 over eighteen rounds, and the Emerging category — a reported $10,000 salary — is the league’s way of putting young Afghan domestic talent on the same stage as the overseas stars. His record so far is at Under-19 level: 19 U19 ODI wickets, with a best of 4 for 11 — the figures that got him noticed.

The season starts on 27 December 2026 in the UAE, and for Salaam Khan it will be an APL debut: he did not play in 2018, when he was eleven years old, and he has no senior franchise record anywhere yet. The squad he has joined is not a development side. Balkh Zwanan is captained by Ibrahim Zadran, the national opener, coached by Mickey Arthur and mentored by Tillakaratne Dilshan — and the Emerging picks are there to learn from them, and to push for a game.

Where he fits at Balkh Zwanan

Salaam Khan’s season is about the chance, not the guarantee. Balkh Zwanan have three frontline seamers ahead of him — Luke Wood, Christopher Wood and Abdullah Ahmadzai — and an Emerging pick earns his games in training, in the nets, and in the moments when a senior bowler needs a rest. Eight league matches in under a month means those moments will come.

What he offers Arthur is a right-arm medium option who can bowl in the powerplay without leaking runs, and a young bowler who has already taken 19 wickets at Under-19 ODI level. The final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. APL Schedule will carry Balkh’s dates once the Afghanistan Cricket Board publishes the fixture list, and the season in full is on APL.
