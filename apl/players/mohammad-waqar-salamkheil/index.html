Left-arm wrist-spin — the rarest kind in the squad

Mohammad Waqar Salamkheil is the left-arm wrist-spinner in Balkh Zwanan’s attack — a 24-year-old from Kabul who played in the very first APL season, for Kandahar Knights in 2018. He was a Silver pick at the Dubai draft, and he returns to the league with a Test cap behind him.

Salamkheil’s name was called in the Silver round of the APL player draft on 3 November 2026, when Balkh Zwanan picked the left-arm wrist-spinner at the Le Méridien Dubai Hotel & Conference Centre in Dubai. The draft completed all five squads for the revived league’s first season: 90 players were selected from a pool of 462 registered cricketers, across six categories and 18 rounds.

Salamkheil was one of Balkh’s five Silver picks, alongside Thomas Moores, Zia ur Rehman Akbar, Abdullah Tarakhail and Colin Ackermann, and his selection put him in a nineteen-player squad led by Ibrahim Zadran for a season starting on 27 December 2026 in the United Arab Emirates. He is a right-hand batter and a left-arm unorthodox spinner — the wrist-spin option in an attack that already has Ghazanfar’s off-breaks, Qais Ahmad’s leg-breaks and Zia’s left-arm orthodox.

He is not new to this league. His APL debut came in 2018 with Kandahar Knights, and he has a Test cap for Afghanistan since — one Test, with no ODI or T20I caps — which makes him the kind of cricketer Arthur values: a specialist whose one skill is rare enough to change a match on its own.

Where he fits at Balkh Zwanan

Salamkheil’s value is scarcity. Left-arm wrist-spin is the rarest bowling type in T20 cricket, and Balkh Zwanan have the only one in their squad — a bowler who turns it away from right-handers and into left-handers, which is the opposite of what every other spinner in the attack does. Arthur can use him as a strike option in the middle overs, or as the surprise that breaks a partnership nobody else can reach.

With Ghazanfar, Qais Ahmad and Zia around him, Balkh can bowl spin for most of an innings and still ask a different question every over. The top four reach the playoffs from five teams, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. APL Schedule will carry Balkh’s dates once the Afghanistan Cricket Board publishes the fixture list, and the season in full is on APL T20.
