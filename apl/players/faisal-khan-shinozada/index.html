Eighteen years old, and already a World Cup star

Faisal Khan Shinozada was the first name called in the Emerging round. At the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, Nangarhar Cosmic took the 18-year-old batter as the first pick of the Emerging category — one of 15 young Afghan players selected across three rounds, and one of 90 players picked from a pool of 462. The price was a reported $10,000, and Cosmic had won the Emerging game at the APL Huddle, so they held the first pick.

The World Cup numbers explain why. Born in Kabul on 29 February 2008, Shinozada is a right-hand top-order batter who does not bowl, and at the Under-19 World Cup in 2026 he scored 435 runs — the third-most in the tournament. His highest Youth ODI score is 163 against Ireland Under-19, and he followed it with 110 against India Under-19 in the World Cup semi-final. Two hundreds in one World Cup, at eighteen, is the kind of record that gets a franchise’s attention.

His senior start came quickly: a T20I debut on 25 September 2026 against Nepal at Nisshin. For Cosmic, he is the future of the batting — a top-order batter who has already shown he can score hundreds under tournament pressure, and a player the league’s playing conditions let a side include in its XI as its emerging player.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic’s Emerging group is three strong — Shinozada, Bahar Ali Shinwari and Islam Khan Zazai — and Shinozada is the headline of it. Around them sit the overseas stars and a captain, Rahmat Shah, who has been Afghanistan’s Test and ODI captain since July 2026.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, with four playoff places on offer. The APL T20 season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. At eighteen, Shinozada is the youngest player in the squad.
