From an Under-19 semi-final to the APL

Azizullah Miakhil is a 20-year-old batter from Nangarhar who went from an Under-19 World Cup semi-final with Afghanistan to Balkh Zwanan’s APL 2026-27 squad. He was an Emerging pick at the Dubai draft, and the season starting 27 December 2026 in the UAE is his APL debut.

Azizullah Miakhil is in the Balkh Zwanan squad for APL 2026-27, picked in the Emerging category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, at a reported $10,000. He is one of three Emerging picks at Balkh, alongside Salaam Khan Ahmadzai and his Afghanistan Under-19 team-mate Abdul Aziz Khan.

The 2026 season, which starts on 27 December 2026, is his APL debut: he was 12 years old when the first APL season was played in 2018, and he has no record in that tournament. Balkh go into 2026 led by captain Ibrahim Zadran, with Mickey Arthur as head coach and Tillakaratne Dilshan as mentor.

Miakhil’s route into the squad is the Emerging category, the draft’s pathway for Afghanistan’s brightest young players, and his record in 2026 explains why Balkh took him: a World Cup semi-final with the Under-19s, batting right-handed at the top of an order that reached the last four. He has no senior international caps — this is the step up, and the APL is where it happens.
