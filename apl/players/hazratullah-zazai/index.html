Hazratullah Zazai — Batter

Hazratullah Zazai is in the Akcel United Kabul squad for APL 2026-27 — the most destructive opener of the league’s first season, returning to the APL eight years later in a new club’s colours.

Zazai’s 2018 season is the reason this page exists in the form it does. Playing for Kabul Zwanan — the city’s first APL side, captained by Rashid Khan — he scored 322 runs, more than any other batter in that club’s run to the final, where they finished runners-up to Balkh Legends in Sharjah. He was the opener who could change a game in six balls, and the league has not had a season since to see whether anyone could stop him.

Born in Paktia on 23 March 1998, he is a left-hand opening batter who played for Afghanistan from 2016 to 2024. The new Kabul club is not the old one — Akcel United Kabul are a new franchise, and Kabul Zwanan’s results stay with Kabul Zwanan — but the city’s name on the shirt and the opener at the top of the order will feel familiar to anyone who watched 2018.

Kabul took him in the Silver category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026. He will open the batting for a side captained by Azmatullah Omarzai, coached by Shane McDermott and mentored by Nikhil Chopra, when the season starts on 27 December 2026. What Akcel bought is not just a batter: it is the one player in the squad who has already done, in this league and this city, exactly what Kabul need him to do again.
