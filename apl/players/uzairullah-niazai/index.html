Uzairullah Niazai — Batter

Uzairullah Niazai, the 19-year-old who hit an unbeaten 101 against India in the Under-19 World Cup semi-final, is Akcel United Kabul’s Emerging pick for APL 2026-27.

Niazai was one of the young Afghan names every franchise had circled before the draft, and the reason is a single innings: 101 not out against India in the Under-19 World Cup semi-final in Harare. It is the hardest stage in age-group cricket — a semi-final, against India, with a final on the line — and a 19-year-old won it unbeaten. He finished that World Cup with 193 runs at an average of 64.33.

The semi-final was not a one-off. His highest Youth ODI score is 140 not out against Bangladesh Under-19 in Bogra, and he is a batter who scores in the games that matter rather than padding numbers in the ones that don’t. Born in Laghman on 17 April 2007, he bats right-handed and bowls right-arm offbreak, and the squad table lists him as a batter — a batting all-rounder in the making, rather than a bowler who bats.

Kabul took him in the Emerging category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$10,000. He has no senior international cricket yet — no Test, ODI or T20I — and APL 2026-27 is his debut season in the league, and his first time batting against senior international bowlers. It is the biggest jump of his career, and the most watched.

Where he fits at Akcel United Kabul

Uzairullah Niazai plays for Akcel United Kabul in APL 2026-27, captained by Azmatullah Omarzai and coached by Shane McDermott, with Nikhil Chopra as mentor. The squad of nineteen was set at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026 — 90 players picked from 462 across 18 rounds.

Niazai is the youngest batter in the squad, and the one Kabul’s fans will watch closest. He will bat in the middle order, where the game is fastest and the bowlers are senior internationals — and if the World Cup semi-final was any guide, the occasion will not frighten him. The season starts on Sunday 27 December 2026 in the UAE; Afghanistan Premier League will follow every Kabul match, with fixtures on the APL Schedule and the standings on the APL Points Table. The full squad is on the APL Players page.

Five teams chase four playoff places in a double round-robin, so every team plays eight league matches — each opponent twice — and one side goes home after the league stage. The final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Kabul are a new club with no APL record yet, and that is the point: everything this squad does in 2026-27 will be a first.
