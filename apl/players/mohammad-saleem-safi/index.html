Mohammad Saleem Safi — Bowler

Mohammad Saleem Safi is in the Akcel United Kabul squad for APL 2026-27, months after taking 6 for 140 against India in a Test match at Mullanpur — a right-arm fast bowler, and a Silver pick at the Dubai draft.

Safi’s name travelled further than most in June 2026, when he took 6 for 140 against India in a Test match at Mullanpur. It is the kind of figure that does not need decorating: six wickets against the best batting line-up in the world, in the longest format, from a 23-year-old quick. His Test debut had come on 2 February 2024, his ODI debut on 5 July 2023, and his T20I debut on 17 January 2024 — all three formats inside seven months.

Born on 9 September 2002 in Baghlan, he is a right-arm fast bowler who bats right-handed. His domestic cricket has been played for Kunduz Province, Amo Region, the Boost Defenders, the Band-e-Amir Dragons and the Amo Sharks — a tour of Afghanistan’s domestic structure that explains why he looks at home against international batters: he has been bowling at the country’s best for years.

Kabul took him in the Silver category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$20,000. APL 2026-27 is his debut season in the league, and he arrives as the quickest Afghan seamer in the squad after Farooqi — the one most likely to bowl the hard overs, at the death, when the game is on the line.
