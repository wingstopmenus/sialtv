The UAE seamer leading Cosmic’s attack

The first APL season passed Junaid Siddique by entirely. It was played in Sharjah in October 2018, when he was 25 and two years into his new life in the United Arab Emirates — working, playing tape-ball cricket in his spare time, and still a year away from his international debut. His name appears in none of the five squads that season. Eight years on, he arrives as Nangarhar Cosmic’s fifth Gold pick.

The record since is that of a genuine international seamer. Born in Multan, Pakistan, on 6 December 1992, Siddique is a right-arm medium-fast bowler and a right-hand batter who plays for the UAE. He has 100 ODI wickets in 71 matches, with best figures of 6 for 49, and 126 T20I wickets in 91 matches, with best figures of 5 for 35 — the first five-for by a UAE bowler at a T20 World Cup, against Canada in 2026. His T20I debut came against Oman in October 2019.

For Cosmic, he is the pace spearhead. In a squad where the spin options — Shakib, Zahir Khan, Shafiqullah Ghafari — are plentiful, Siddique gives Toby Radford the new ball and the death overs, and he knows the UAE grounds better than any bowler in the side. That local knowledge matters in a league played entirely in the Emirates.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic’s bowling has three different ways through an innings: Siddique’s pace, Zahir Khan’s wrist spin and Aftab Alam’s seam, with Shakib’s four overs of left-arm spin tying it together. Captain Rahmat Shah leads the side, Toby Radford coaches it, and Mark Boucher mentors it.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, with four playoff places on offer. The Afghanistan Premier League season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Siddique will bowl in the country he now calls home.
