A Test cap, in the last round of the draft

Khalil Ahmad Gurbaz was born in Khost on 28 February 2001. He bats right-handed, bowls right-arm leg-break, and Paktia picked him in the Supplementary category at the Dubai draft on 3 November 2026. No salary was published for the Supplementary category.

He was one of Paktia’s two Supplementary picks, alongside Sediqullah Pacha, and his selection put him in a nineteen-player squad led by Rahmanullah Gurbaz for a season starting on 27 December 2026 in the United Arab Emirates. The draft took 90 players from a pool of 462 registered cricketers, across six categories and 18 rounds.

He arrives with something most Supplementary picks do not have: a Test cap. He made his Test debut against Zimbabwe at Harare on 20 October 2025, and he had 77 first-class wickets to his name by July 2026.

A leg-spinner with a Test debut behind him

The surname needs sorting out before anything else, because three different Gurbaz names sit in the same Paktia squad. Rahmanullah Gurbaz is the captain and the Afghan Icon pick, a wicketkeeper-batter with nine ODI centuries, the most by an Afghan. Masoodulrahman Gurbaz is a Silver pick, also a wicketkeeper-batter. Khalil Ahmad Gurbaz is neither: he is a right-arm leg-break bowler, a Test cricketer who bats right-handed and bowls leg-spin.

The stats sites list him in short form as Khalil Gurbaz, which is the same player under a shorter name, not a fourth man. Any listing that calls him a wicketkeeper has mixed him up with one of the other two.

His Test debut came against Zimbabwe at Harare on 20 October 2025, and his first-class record — 77 wickets by July 2026 — is the record of a bowler who has earned his place the long way, through domestic cricket rather than one breakout tournament.

A third leg-spinner, and a Test bowler’s patience

Paktia’s spin attack is already built on wrist spin — Hasaranga’s leg spin and Noor Ahmad’s chinaman — and Khalil Ahmad Gurbaz gives Gurbaz a third wrist-spinner, one who bowls leg-break and has done it in Test cricket.

For a Supplementary pick, that is a remarkable amount of experience to have in reserve. He is the bowler the captain can turn to when a pitch turns, when a game needs a wicket, or when one of the two frontline wrist-spinners needs a rest.

A new club, a blank record

Five teams chase four playoff places in APL 2026-27, so one side goes home after the league stage. Each team plays eight league matches — each opponent twice — and a win is worth two points. The table is empty because the season has not started: every team begins on zero points, and the first update comes after Match 1 on 27 December 2026, when the first ball is bowled at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM Dubai time) in the UAE. The final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027.

Paktia are a new club for 2026-27, not a continuation of the 2018 Paktia Panthers. The Afghanistan Premier League season starts on 27 December 2026 in the UAE, and Gurbaz’s APL debut, whenever Paktia hand it to him, will be his first appearance in the tournament.

Frequently asked questions

Is Khalil Ahmad Gurbaz the same player as Rahmanullah Gurbaz?

No. There are three different Gurbazes in the Paktia squad: Rahmanullah, the captain and wicketkeeper-batter; Masoodulrahman, his younger brother, also a wicketkeeper-batter; and Khalil Ahmad, a right-arm leg-break bowler with a Test cap.

When did Khalil Ahmad Gurbaz make his Test debut?

20 October 2025, against Zimbabwe at Harare. He had 77 first-class wickets by July 2026.

Which draft round did Paktia pick Khalil Ahmad Gurbaz in?

Supplementary — he was one of Paktia's two Supplementary picks, alongside Sediqullah Pacha. No salary was published for the Supplementary category.
