Mujeeb Ur Rahman — Bowler

Mujeeb Ur Rahman joins Akcel United Kabul as their first Gold pick of the 2026 draft — a right-arm off-break bowler with 101 ODI wickets, 89 T20I wickets, and an APL season already behind him from 2018.

Mujeeb was still a teenager when he took 5 for 50 against Zimbabwe in 2018 — the youngest five-wicket haul in ODI history. It set the pattern for everything that followed: a bowler who reads batters early, varies his pace and trajectory without changing his action, and takes wickets in the powerplay, where most spinners are only trying to survive. He has 101 ODI wickets in 75 matches and 89 T20I wickets in 70.

He is one of a handful of Gold picks with an APL season already behind him, having played in the first season in 2018. That matters for a new club: Kabul’s squad is full of players meeting for the first time, and Mujeeb is one of the few who knows what this league feels like. Born in Khost on 28 March 2001, he bowls right-arm off break and bats right-handed.

Kabul took him with their first Gold pick at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$40,000 — the clearest signal of how the squad was built. The attack comes first: Mujeeb in the powerplay and the middle overs, Sharafuddin Ashraf and Imran Tahir beside him, and the quicks at either end.

Where he fits at Akcel United Kabul

Mujeeb Ur Rahman plays for Akcel United Kabul in APL 2026-27, captained by Azmatullah Omarzai and coached by Shane McDermott, with Nikhil Chopra as mentor. The squad of nineteen was set at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026 — 90 players picked from 462 across 18 rounds.

Mujeeb is the spinner Shane McDermott builds the middle overs around — and the one most likely to bowl in the powerplay, where his variations are hardest to line up. With Sharafuddin Ashraf’s left-arm spin and Imran Tahir’s leg-spin beside him, Kabul can attack with spin from both ends. The season starts on Sunday 27 December 2026 in the UAE; APL will follow every Kabul match, with fixtures on the APL Schedule and the standings on the APL Points Table. The full squad is on the APL Players page.

Five teams chase four playoff places in a double round-robin, so every team plays eight league matches — each opponent twice — and one side goes home after the league stage. The final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Kabul are a new club with no APL record yet, and that is the point: everything this squad does in 2026-27 will be a first.
