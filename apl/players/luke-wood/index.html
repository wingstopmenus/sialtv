A debut eight years in the making

Luke Wood is Balkh Zwanan’s left-arm fast-medium bowler — a Diamond pick at $65,000 and one of three Englishmen in the squad. The 31-year-old from Sheffield made his England debut in 2022, and APL 2026-27, starting 27 December 2026 in the UAE, is his debut in the league.

Wood was 23 when the first APL season was played in October 2018 — a Nottinghamshire seamer who had not yet played international cricket. His name appears in none of the five 2018 squads, so 2026 is his APL debut, eight years after the league began. When Balkh Zwanan picked him at the Dubai draft, it was a debut eight years in the making, not a return.

He brings what Balkh’s attack needs most: a left-arm angle. Ghazanfar, Qais Ahmad and Salamkheil give captain Ibrahim Zadran three different kinds of spin, but Wood is the seamer who leads the pace attack, swinging the new ball and bowling the hard overs at the death. He plays under Zadran, with Mickey Arthur as head coach and Tillakaratne Dilshan as mentor, in a squad with a strong English flavour — Moeen Ali came in as the Platinum pick and Thomas Moores as a Silver pick.

Left-arm pace is a scarce resource in any T20 draft, and Balkh paid Diamond money for it. Wood’s job is simple to describe and hard to do: take early wickets, keep the powerplay quiet, and give Arthur a seamer he can trust at both ends of an innings.

Where he fits at Balkh Zwanan

Wood leads Balkh Zwanan’s seam attack, and the role matters because of what sits behind him. Balkh’s spin is deep — Ghazanfar, Qais Ahmad, Salamkheil, Zia ur Rehman Akbar — so the seamers do not need to bowl long spells; they need to strike early and close late. Wood, Christopher Wood and Abdullah Ahmadzai share that work, and Wood’s left arm gives Arthur a different look from the two right-armers.

Balkh play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, and the top four reach the playoffs. The final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. APL Schedule will carry Balkh’s dates once the Afghanistan Cricket Board publishes the fixture list, and the season in full is on APL.

Frequently asked questions

Has Luke Wood played in the APL before?

No. He was 23 and still a county cricketer at Nottinghamshire when the first season was played in 2018, and his name appears in none of the five squads that year.

What did Balkh Zwanan pay for Luke Wood?

He was taken in the Diamond round of the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, at the Diamond price of $65,000.

Is Luke Wood related to Christopher Wood?

No. Both are English left-arm seamers in the same Balkh squad, but they are not related — Luke Wood is from Sheffield, Christopher Wood from Basingstoke.
