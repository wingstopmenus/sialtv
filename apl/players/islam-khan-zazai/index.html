A domestic batter gets his franchise chance

Islam Khan Zazai’s name reached a wider audience on 3 November 2026, when Nangarhar Cosmic picked him in the Emerging category at the APL player draft in Dubai. The draft, held at the Le Méridien Dubai Hotel and Conference Centre, completed all five squads for the revived league’s first season: 90 players were selected from a pool of 462 registered cricketers, across six categories and 18 rounds. Zazai was one of Nangarhar’s three Emerging picks.

He is a right-handed batter, born in Paktia on 2 December 2002, and he has come through Afghanistan’s domestic system with Kabul Region, Band-e-Amir Region and Pamir Legends. That is the path most Afghan franchise players take: years of regional cricket, then a draft pick, then a first season under lights. For Zazai, 2026-27 is that first season.

For Cosmic, he is batting depth with something to prove. The top order is full of overseas stars and established internationals, so Zazai’s chance will come when the schedule bites or when form dips — and the league’s playing conditions, which let a side include one emerging player in its XI, mean his chance may come sooner than he expects.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic’s Emerging group is three strong — Faisal Khan Shinozada, Bahar Ali Shinwari and Islam Khan Zazai — and Zazai is the domestic-system batter of the three. Captain Rahmat Shah leads the side, Toby Radford coaches it, and Mark Boucher mentors it.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, with four playoff places on offer. The Afghanistan Premier League season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Zazai’s first APL season starts then.
