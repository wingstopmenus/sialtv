Khalid Ahmadzai — Wicketkeeper-batter

Khalid Ahmadzai is in the Akcel United Kabul squad for APL 2026-27 — a 19-year-old wicketkeeper-batter, an Emerging pick at the Dubai draft, and one of the youngest players in the whole league.

Ahmadzai’s name reached a wider audience on 3 November 2026, when Akcel United Kabul picked him in the Emerging category at the APL player draft in Dubai. He is 19, born in Kabul on 16 March 2007, a right-handed wicketkeeper-batter who has come through Afghanistan’s Under-19 setup and the country’s domestic youth teams.

His calling card so far is a highest Youth ODI score of 132 against UAE Under-19 in 2025 — the innings of a keeper who bats properly, not one who keeps first and hopes with the bat. His major teams so far are Afghanistan Under-19, the Pamir Legends, the Mis-e-Ainak Knights and the Maiwand Champions: a young career spent moving up Afghanistan’s age-group and domestic ladder, one rung at a time.

Kabul took him in the Emerging category for a reported US$10,000. He is the second wicketkeeper in the squad behind Johnson Charles, which is exactly the role a 19-year-old keeper needs: learn from a two-time World Cup winner, keep in the nets every day, and be ready when the chance comes. APL 2026-27 is his debut season in the league.

Where he fits at Akcel United Kabul

Khalid Ahmadzai plays for Akcel United Kabul in APL 2026-27, captained by Azmatullah Omarzai and coached by Shane McDermott, with Nikhil Chopra as mentor. The squad of nineteen was set at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026 — 90 players picked from 462 across 18 rounds.
