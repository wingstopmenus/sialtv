Two five-fors before his twentieth birthday

Allah Mohammad Ghazanfar is the youngest frontline spinner in the APL — a 20-year-old off-spinner from Paktia who took two ODI five-wicket hauls inside his first 11 ODIs. Balkh Zwanan took him in the Diamond round of the Dubai draft for $65,000, and APL 2026-27 is his debut in the league.

Ghazanfar was 12 years old when the first APL season was played in Sharjah in October 2018, so the 2026 season is his APL debut. His rise since has been rapid: a T20 debut for Mis Ainak Knights in the Shpageeza in July 2022, then an ODI debut against Ireland in Sharjah on 7 March 2024 — thirteen days before his 18th birthday — and two ODI five-wicket hauls inside his first 11 ODIs, 6 for 26 against Bangladesh and 5 for 33 against Zimbabwe.

That is why Balkh spent Diamond money on him. At 6 ft 2 in, he gets bounce most off-spinners do not, and his mystery variations have already troubled international batters. He bowls in an attack that also includes Qais Ahmad’s leg-breaks and Mohammad Waqar Salamkheil’s wrist spin, so captain Ibrahim Zadran can attack through the middle overs three different ways — and Balkh Zwanan can defend totals without leaning on their seamers.

He is still only 20, and the APL is the next step in a career that has moved fast: domestic T20 at 16, ODI cricket at 17, two five-fors before 19. Mickey Arthur, Balkh’s head coach, has built attacks around young mystery spinners before, and Ghazanfar is the bowler this squad is built around.

Where he fits at Balkh Zwanan

Ghazanfar is the bowler Balkh Zwanan’s season is built around. In a double round-robin where every team plays eight league matches, a spinner who takes wickets in the middle overs is worth more than one who merely contains — and his ODI record says he takes them. He will bowl with Qais Ahmad and Salamkheil around him, and with Luke Wood’s left-arm pace at the other end, Arthur has an attack with a different question for every batter.

The season starts on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM Dubai time) in the UAE, and ends with the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027. APL Schedule will carry Balkh’s dates once the Afghanistan Cricket Board publishes the fixture list, and the season in full is on APL T20.
