Shamim Hossain — Batter

Shamim Hossain joins Akcel United Kabul as a Gold pick of the 2026 draft — a 26-year-old left-handed middle-order batter from Bangladesh with 52 T20I caps behind him.

Shamim’s job is the hardest one in T20 batting: come in with wickets down or overs running out, and score quickly from ball one. He has done it for Bangladesh 52 times in T20Is, scoring 621 runs with a best of 51 against Ireland in 2023, and his T20 best is an unbeaten 81 in the Bangladesh Premier League in 2025–26 — the innings of a finisher, not a grafter.

Born in Chandpur on 2 September 2000, he bats left-handed and bowls right-arm off break, which gives his captain a part-time option if a left-hander needs tying down. He is 26, in the middle of his international career, and Kabul is his first APL season: the league gets a batter who has played World Cups and franchise cricket, and Shamim gets a stage where his finishing can decide a five-team race.

Kabul took him in the Gold category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$40,000. He will bat in the middle order, behind Charles, Short and Zazai — the place where Kabul’s innings will either accelerate or stall, and where a left-hander who hits the ball cleanly is worth more than his average suggests.
