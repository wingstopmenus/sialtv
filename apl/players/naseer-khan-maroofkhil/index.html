A captain at 20, and a different angle for Rashid

Naseer Khan Maroofkhil was born on 30 January 2006. He is a slow left-arm orthodox bowler who bats left-handed, and he joins Kandahar Warriors for APL 2026-27 as an Emerging pick from the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$10,000.

He captained Afghanistan at the Under-19 World Cup in 2024, and the pick came the day after he was named in the Afghanistan A squad, captained by Ikram Alikhil, for the Rising Stars Asia Cup in Doha — a sign that the national selectors rate him among the country’s most promising young cricketers.

The Emerging category is reserved for young Afghan players who have not yet established themselves at senior level. He is one of three Emerging picks in the Kandahar squad, alongside Numan Shah Agha and Usman Khan, and his senior international career has not yet begun.

What Kandahar are buying is variety and control. A slow left-arm orthodox bowler gives Rashid Khan a different angle in the attack: a spinner who turns the ball away from right-handed batters and ties down an end while the leg-spin operates at the other.

The third spinner in the room

Kandahar’s spin attack is the best in the league on paper — Rashid Khan’s leg-spin, Mohammad Nabi’s off-spin — and Naseer gives it a third angle. His first-class record backs up the promise, and his captaincy record says he thinks about the game, not just bowls in it.
