Shakib Al Hasan — two decades of cricket in one Diamond pick

Shakib Al Hasan brings two decades of international cricket to Nangarhar Cosmic in APL 2026-27. The Bangladesh all-rounder is a Diamond pick who has topped the world all-rounder rankings and owns the most wickets in T20 World Cup history.

The most complete cricketer in the squad

Nangarhar Cosmic took Shakib in the second Diamond round at the Dubai draft on 3 November 2026, at the Diamond price of $65,000 — a signing the ACB called a major overseas capture. He plays under captain Rahmat Shah, with Toby Radford as head coach and Mark Boucher as mentor, and the season starts on 27 December 2026 in the UAE.

The record is the longest in the squad. Shakib is a left-hand batter and a slow left-arm orthodox bowler, born in Magura, Bangladesh, on 24 March 1987, and he has spent much of his career at the top of the world all-rounder rankings. His 50 wickets are the most by any bowler in T20 World Cup history, he scored 606 runs in eight matches at the 2019 World Cup, and his highest Test score is 217 against New Zealand in 2017.

This is his APL debut. Shakib did not play in the first APL season in 2018 — his name is absent from all five squads — so 2026-27 is his first season in the league, eight years later. For Cosmic, his value is balance: he bats in the middle order and bowls his four overs, which lets Radford play an extra batter without losing a bowling option.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic’s middle order is where games will be won and lost, and Shakib sits at the heart of it. Around him are Hales and Rahmat at the top, Kamindu Mendis in the middle, and a bowling attack led by Junaid Siddique’s pace and Zahir Khan’s wrist spin.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, with four playoff places on offer. The Afghanistan Premier League 2026 season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Shakib’s four overs of left-arm spin could be the most valuable in the side.
