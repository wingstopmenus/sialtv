The Test-debut seven-for, back in the APL

Sharifi was Nangarhar Cosmic’s second pick of the Gold round at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported $40,000. He joins a squad captained by Rahmat Shah, coached by Toby Radford and mentored by Mark Boucher — and he arrives with a connection to the coach already in place: Radford was head coach of Dhaka Capitals when Sharifi played his first overseas franchise season in the Bangladesh Premier League in 2025-26.

He is a right-arm fast-medium bowler and a right-hand batter, born in Khost on 17 October 1998, and his calling card is a Test debut few bowlers can match: 7 for 97. That kind of new-ball threat is exactly what Cosmic need in a league where most sides lean on spin through the middle overs.

This is his second spell in the APL. In the first season, in Sharjah in October 2018, he played six matches for Paktia Panthers and took 11 wickets at 16.18 — a strike rate that made him one of the tournament’s most effective seamers. Eight years on, he returns to the league as a Gold pick with a Test seven-for behind him.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic’s bowling leans on experience: Junaid Siddique’s pace, Zahir Khan’s wrist spin, Aftab Alam’s seam, and Sharifi’s fast-medium at the top. Shakib Al Hasan’s left-arm spin ties the attack together, and Sharifi gives Radford a wicket-taker with the new ball.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, with four playoff places on offer. The APL season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Sharifi’s 2018 record says he knows how to take wickets in this league.
