A famous surname, and four years of domestic cricket

Masoodulrahman Gurbaz was born on 6 December 2005. He bats right-handed, keeps wicket, and Paktia picked him in the Silver category at the Dubai draft on 3 November 2026, for a reported $20,000.

He was one of Paktia’s five Silver picks, and his selection put him in a nineteen-player squad led by his own older brother, Rahmanullah Gurbaz, for a season starting on 27 December 2026 in the United Arab Emirates. The draft took 90 players from a pool of 462 registered cricketers, across six categories and 18 rounds.

The family connection is the first thing most readers will notice, and it is real: the captain is his older brother, and the two will share a dressing room for the first time in franchise cricket. But the pick was not a favour. He arrives with four years of domestic cricket behind him, a first-class hundred to his name, a Bangladesh Premier League stint with Chattogram Royals, and a 75 off 44 balls in the ILT20 Development Tournament in September 2026 that showed what Abu Dhabi Knight Riders saw in him.

Domestic cricket, and a 75 that travelled

Gurbaz’s cricket so far has been played in Afghanistan’s domestic system: Kabul Eagles, Amo Region, Boost Region, Amo Sharks, Pamir Legends and Maiwand Champions, with a Bangladesh Premier League stint for Chattogram Royals on top. He has spent the previous two years trying to force his way into the Afghanistan side, and the APL is the stage where that case gets made in front of a wider audience.

The innings that travelled furthest was the 75 off 44 balls in the ILT20 Development Tournament in September 2026. It was the kind of knock that gets a young keeper-batter noticed — quick runs, at the top of the order, against bowlers he had never faced before — and it is the reason Paktia took a wicketkeeper-batter who can cover for their captain behind the stumps, not just a famous surname.

He was twelve years old when the league was first played in 2018. The 2026-27 season is his APL debut.

Cover behind the stumps, and a second keeper

Paktia’s captain keeps wicket and opens the batting, and that is a lot of work for one player across eight league matches. A second wicketkeeper-batter in the squad gives Gurbaz the option to rest the gloves, or to play both keepers when the balance of the side demands it.

Beyond that, he is a young batter with a first-class hundred and a 75 off 44 in a development tournament behind him. The Silver round exists for exactly this kind of player — young Afghan talent whose domestic numbers demand a bigger stage.

Three Gurbazes in one squad — do not mix them up

The format is a double round-robin: five teams, eight league matches each, two against every opponent, and the top four go through to the playoffs. A win is worth two points, and one side goes home after the league stage. Nothing is on the board yet — every team starts on zero, and the first points come after Match 1 on 27 December 2026, when the first ball is bowled at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM Dubai time) in the UAE. The season ends with the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027.

Paktia’s 2026-27 squad contains three players called Gurbaz, and this page is about only one of them. The first is the captain, Rahmanullah Gurbaz, born 28 November 2001: the Afghanistan international wicketkeeper-batter and Paktia’s Afghan Icon pick. The second is the subject of this page, his younger brother. The third is Khalil Ahmad Gurbaz, a right-arm leg-break bowler and Test cricketer — a different player again. The Afghanistan Premier League season starts on 27 December 2026.
