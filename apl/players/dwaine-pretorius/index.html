A record no other South African owns

Dwaine Pretorius was born on 29 March 1989 in Randfontein, South Africa. He is a right-handed batter and a right-arm medium-fast bowler, and he joins Kandahar Warriors for APL 2026-27 as a Silver pick from the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$20,000.

His record is the one that defines him: 5 for 17 against Pakistan at Lahore in February 2021 remains the best T20I figures ever recorded by a South African. He was also his country’s joint-highest wicket-taker at the 2021 T20 World Cup, with nine wickets, and he led the bowling charts at the CPL in 2023 with 20 wickets.

He arrives as a title winner, too. He took 4 for 26 in the 2023 CPL final as Guyana Amazon Warriors won their first title, and he was in the Chennai Super Kings squad that won the IPL in 2023. His ODI debut came on 25 September 2016 against Ireland.

He is making his APL debut in 2026. He was not part of the first season in Sharjah in October 2018 — that month he was named in the Jozi Stars squad for the first Mzansi Super League in South Africa instead. At 37 he is one of the most experienced overseas players in the Kandahar squad, and his job is clear: bowl the hard overs.

Dwaine Pretorius in numbers

The season has not started, so there are no APL 2026-27 numbers for Dwaine Pretorius yet. The first ball is on Sunday 27 December 2026, and his figures for this season will be added here after Match 1.

His career marks are the standard: best T20I figures of 5 for 17 against Pakistan, nine wickets at the 2021 T20 World Cup, 20 wickets as the leading bowler at the CPL in 2023, and 4 for 26 in the CPL final that won Guyana Amazon Warriors their first title.

The seam option in a spin attack

Kandahar’s attack is built around Rashid Khan’s spin and Mohammad Nabi’s off-spin, so Pretorius gives the side what neither of them can: a seam option at the start and at the death. With Muhammad Amir and Tymal Mills sharing the new ball, Pretorius is the bowler Ravi Bopara turns to when the ball stops swinging and the game needs holding together.

His batting lengthens the lower order, too. A side that can bat to eight — Nabi, Pretorius, Rashid, Janat — does not panic when it loses early wickets, and in a league where four of five teams reach the playoffs, that depth is worth points across eight league matches.

The APL T20 season runs from 27 December 2026 to the final on 20 January 2027, and at 37 Pretorius is playing for the kind of late-career title that defines a journeyman’s story. He has already won a CPL and an IPL — the APL would complete a rare set.

Frequently asked questions

What is Dwaine Pretorius’ role for Kandahar Warriors?

Bowling all-rounder. He bowls right-arm medium-fast and bats right-handed, and he was picked in the Silver category at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026.
