Imran Tahir — Bowler

Imran Tahir, the leg-spinner who took 173 ODI wickets for South Africa and won the IPL Purple Cap at 40, is Akcel United Kabul’s Silver pick for APL 2026-27.

Tahir was not there the first time round. When the inaugural APL season was played in Sharjah in October 2018, his name appears in none of the five squads — and eight years later he arrives anyway, at 47, as one of the most recognisable leg-spinners the game has known. Born in Lahore on 27 March 1979, he played for South Africa from 2011 to 2019, taking 173 ODI wickets in 107 matches with best figures of 7 for 45 against West Indies in 2016.

The number that explains him best is not an ODI figure but an IPL one: 26 wickets in 2019, the Purple Cap for Chennai Super Kings, won at the age of 40. Leg-spin is supposed to be a young man’s art — long run-ups, big revolutions, hard landings — and Tahir has simply refused to stop. His googly is still the ball batters plan for and still miss, and his celebration, the sprint down the pitch, is as famous as the bowling.

Kabul took him in the Silver category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$20,000. He bowls right-arm leg break with a googly and bats right-handed. APL 2026-27 is his first season in the league, and he will bowl the middle overs beside Mujeeb Ur Rahman and Sharafuddin Ashraf — three spinners, three different directions of turn, in the same attack.

Where he fits at Akcel United Kabul

Imran Tahir plays for Akcel United Kabul in APL 2026-27, captained by Azmatullah Omarzai and coached by Shane McDermott, with Nikhil Chopra as mentor. The squad of nineteen was set at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026 — 90 players picked from 462 across 18 rounds.

Tahir bowls the middle overs, where his leg-spin and googly give Kabul a wicket-taking option when batters are set. He is also the oldest player in the squad by a distance — 47, against a side full of teenagers and twenty-somethings — and in a new club, that experience in the dressing room matters as much as the overs. The season starts on Sunday 27 December 2026 in the UAE; APL T20 will follow every Kabul match, with fixtures on the APL Schedule and the standings on the APL Points Table. The full squad is on the APL Players page.

Five teams chase four playoff places in a double round-robin, so every team plays eight league matches — each opponent twice — and one side goes home after the league stage. The final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Kabul are a new club with no APL record yet, and that is the point: everything this squad does in 2026-27 will be a first.
