Dushmantha Chameera — Bowler

Dushmantha Chameera brings genuine pace to Akcel United Kabul in APL 2026-27 — a Diamond pick, a five-for in every international format, and the third Sri Lankan to 100 T20I wickets.

Chameera was 26 when the first APL season was played in October 2018 — a Sri Lanka international who had already played in a World Cup and made his Test debut. His name did not appear in that season, so APL 2026-27 is his first in the league, eight years after he could plausibly have played in it. He arrives as a finished article: a right-arm fast bowler with a five-wicket haul in Tests, ODIs and T20Is.

The most recent of those came at home, in Pallekele in 2026 — 5 for 24 against England, his maiden T20I five-for. It also carried him to 100 T20I wickets, making him only the third Sri Lankan to reach the mark. His best Test figures, 5 for 47 against New Zealand in Hamilton in 2015, are a decade old now, which is itself the point: Chameera has been doing this at the top level for a very long time.

Born in Ragama on 11 January 1992, he bowls right-arm fast and bats right-handed. For Kabul he is the pace partner to Fazalhaq Farooqi’s left-arm swing — one end swing, one end speed — and on UAE pitches where the ball can skid on, that combination is the attack’s sharpest edge.

Where he fits at Akcel United Kabul

Dushmantha Chameera plays for Akcel United Kabul in APL 2026-27, captained by Azmatullah Omarzai and coached by Shane McDermott, with Nikhil Chopra as mentor. The squad of nineteen was set at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026 — 90 players picked from 462 across 18 rounds.

He and Farooqi give Shane McDermott two quicks who attack in completely different ways, and behind them sit Mujeeb Ur Rahman and Imran Tahir to squeeze the middle overs.
