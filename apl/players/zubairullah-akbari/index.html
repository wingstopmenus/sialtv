Two hundreds that earned a draft pick

At the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, Nangarhar Cosmic picked Zubairullah Akbari in the Supplementary category — no salary published for the round. He joins a squad captained by Rahmat Shah, coached by Toby Radford and mentored by Mark Boucher, and the season starts on 27 December 2026 in the UAE.

Akbari is a batting all-rounder: a left-hand batter who bowls slow left-arm orthodox, born in Nangarhar on 1 May 2000. His T20I debut came on 7 October 2023 against India, in the Asian Games gold-medal match, and he has four T20Is for Afghanistan. At domestic level he has played for Speen Ghar Region, Speen Ghar Tigers, Nangarhar Province, Hindukush Strikers and Dhaka Capitals.

The two innings that earned him the pick were both hundreds under pressure. He made 124 not out off 54 balls in the Kabul Premier League eliminator on 19 October 2025, and 134 off 133 balls in the List A final on 25 July 2024. For Cosmic, he is a left-hand option in the middle order and a second left-arm spinner alongside Shakib — a Supplementary pick who can cover two jobs.
