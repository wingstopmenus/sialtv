The keeper who was there at the very start

Afsar Khan Zazai is Afghanistan’s first Test cricketer — Test cap number one — and he keeps wicket for Balkh Zwanan in APL 2026-27. The 33-year-old right-hand wicketkeeper-batter has 10 Tests behind him, and he played in the very first APL season, for Kabul Zwanan in 2018.

Zazai joins Balkh Zwanan as a Gold pick of the 2026 draft, signed at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026 for a reported $40,000. He is a 33-year-old right-hand wicket-keeper batter with 10 Tests for Afghanistan behind him, and he brings something no other Gold pick in the league can offer: a man who was there at the very start of Afghanistan’s Test story, as the country’s first Test cap.

He is not new to this league. Zazai played in the first APL season in October 2018, for Kabul Zwanan — two matches, six runs and two catches. Against Kandahar Knights on 16 October he kept wicket in a side that carried some of the biggest names of that first season, and eight years later he returns to a league that folded after that one year and has only now come back.

Balkh are captained by Ibrahim Zadran and coached by Mickey Arthur, with Tillakaratne Dilshan as mentor, and Zazai gives them a specialist keeper and a calm head in the middle order. He does not bowl — his job is the gloves and the runs — and in a squad with a second keeper in Thomas Moores, his experience is the difference between the two.

Where he fits at Balkh Zwanan

Zazai’s competition is Thomas Moores, the English keeper taken in the Silver round, and the contest is a genuine one: Moores is the younger man, Zazai is the one with 10 Tests and a first APL season behind him. Balkh Zwanan can pick on form, on conditions, or on the balance of the overseas places — and a side that plays eight league matches in under a month will need both keepers ready.

Behind the stumps, his value is steadiness. Balkh’s attack is spin-heavy — Ghazanfar, Qais Ahmad, Salamkheil — and a keeper who has stood up to wrist-spin in Test cricket is not a luxury. The season starts on Sunday 27 December 2026 in the UAE, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Frequently asked questions

Why is Afsar Khan Zazai significant in Afghanistan cricket?

He is Afghanistan’s first Test cap — the country’s Test cap number one — with 10 Tests behind him. He was there at the very start of Afghanistan’s Test story.

Did Afsar Zazai play in the 2018 APL?

Yes, for Kabul Zwanan — two matches, six runs and two catches, including the match against Kandahar Knights on 16 October 2018.

Who is Balkh Zwanan’s other wicketkeeper?

Thomas Moores, the English keeper from Nottinghamshire, taken in the Silver round. Zazai and Moores compete for the gloves in APL 2026-27.
