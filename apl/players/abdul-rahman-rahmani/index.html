Abdul Rahman Rahmani — Bowler

Abdul Rahman Rahmani is a 24-year-old Afghan seamer who bowls right-arm medium-fast, and he joins Akcel United Kabul for APL 2026-27 as a Silver pick from the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026.

Rahmani made his ODI debut for Afghanistan on 11 July 2023 against Bangladesh, and has three ODI caps — a small number that still puts him ahead of most 24-year-old seamers in the country. He bowls right-arm medium-fast and bats right-handed, and his best work so far has come in Afghanistan’s second string: five wickets in Afghanistan A’s Emerging Asia Cup title win in October 2024.

The domestic numbers back up the selection. His best Shpageeza spell in 2025 was 3 for 33 against Speen Ghar Tigers on 20 July 2025 — the kind of figures that say a bowler can hold his length under pressure, not just take wickets when the game is already won. Born in Kabul on 22 November 2001, he is an Afghan seamer in a squad that leans heavily on spin, and that is precisely his value.

Kabul took him in the Silver category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$20,000. APL 2026-27 is his debut season in the league. He will not bowl the new ball — Farooqi and Chameera own that — but in a double round-robin where every team plays eight league matches, the third and fourth seamers decide whether the attack stays fresh in the second half of the season.
