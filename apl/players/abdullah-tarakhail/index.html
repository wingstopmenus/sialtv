A debut on Saturday, a draft pick on Monday

Abdullah Tarakhail took a wicket with his second ball in international cricket — 2 for 14 on T20I debut against Zimbabwe at Sharjah on 1 November 2026. Two days later, Balkh Zwanan took him in the Silver round of the APL draft, and APL 2026-27 is his debut in the league.

Tarakhail joins Balkh Zwanan as a Silver pick of the 2026 draft, taken at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026 for a reported $20,000. He is 25, a right-arm fast-medium bowler who bats in the middle order, and he arrives two days after making his international debut — a T20I against Zimbabwe at Sharjah on 1 November 2026 in which he took 2 for 14, his first wicket coming with his second ball.

That is the sharpest possible introduction. Two days between a first international wicket and a franchise contract is not a coincidence — it is a bowler announcing himself at exactly the right moment, in front of exactly the right people. The draft took 90 players from a pool of 462 across 18 rounds, and Tarakhail was one of the last names anyone needed to think twice about.

He is new to this league. Tarakhail did not play in the first APL season in October 2018 — he was 17 at the time, and his first senior match did not come until April 2019, so his name appears in none of the five 2018 squads. Balkh are captained by Ibrahim Zadran and coached by Mickey Arthur, with Tillakaratne Dilshan as mentor, and Tarakhail gives them a wicket-taking seamer who has already done the job for his country.

Where he fits at Balkh Zwanan

Tarakhail is the seamer Balkh Zwanan can throw the new ball to without a second thought. Luke Wood leads the pace attack with his left arm, Christopher Wood brings sixteen seasons of county craft, and Tarakhail brings the thing neither of them can: a bowler who has just taken international wickets, this month, and arrives with the confidence that comes with it.

His middle-order batting is a bonus Arthur will use — a bowling all-rounder who can bat gives a side length without weakening the attack.
