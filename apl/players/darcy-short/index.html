D'Arcy Short — Batter

D’Arcy Short joins Akcel United Kabul as a Gold pick of the 2026 draft — a 36-year-old left-handed opener from Australia with one of the most explosive records in Big Bash history.

Short’s calling card is BBL|07. Across 102 BBL matches he has 3,138 runs, and his List A best is 257 against Queensland in 2018, an innings that shows the ceiling when he gets going.

He is 36, the second-oldest overseas player in the squad after Imran Tahir, and the experience is the point: Kabul’s top order is full of power, and Short has spent a decade showing how to use it.

He will open with Johnson Charles or Imran Mir, and the plan writes itself: two left-handers or a left-right pair at the top, both capable of clearing the ropes from ball one.
