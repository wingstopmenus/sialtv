APL 2026-27 Players — All 95 Profiles & Teams

Last updated: 14 November 2026

95 players

Every player, team by team

SialTV lists all 95 players of APL 2026-27 — every squad member with a profile on this site, grouped by team. The season has not started yet, so there are no APL 2026-27 runs or wickets to show: the roles beside each name come from the verified squad lists set at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, and players whose role is not listed there are shown as squad members until they play. Five teams of 19, 24 matches, and the first ball is bowled on Sunday 27 December 2026.

The squads are led by five Afghanistan captains: Azmatullah Omarzai takes Akcel United Kabul into the revived league, Ibrahim Zadran leads Balkh Zwanan, Rashid Khan captains Kandahar Warriors, Rahmat Shah leads Nangarhar Cosmic, and Rahmanullah Gurbaz captains Paktia. Around them are some of the biggest names in the short format — Muhammad Amir and Tymal Mills with the new ball, Shakib Al Hasan and Moeen Ali as all-rounders, Alex Hales and Colin Munro at the top of the order. Every name below links to a full profile — role, batting and bowling style where verified, career context and the team’s fixtures.
