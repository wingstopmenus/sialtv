APL 2026-27 Players — All 95 Profiles & Teams

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Every player, team by team

SialTV lists all 95 players of APL 2026-27 — every squad member with a profile on this site, grouped by team. The season has not started yet, so there are no APL 2026-27 runs or wickets to show: the roles beside each name come from the verified squad lists set at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, and players whose role is not listed there are shown as squad members until they play. Five teams of 19, 24 matches, and the first ball is bowled on Sunday 27 December 2026.

The squads are led by five Afghanistan captains: Azmatullah Omarzai takes Akcel United Kabul into the revived league, Ibrahim Zadran leads Balkh Zwanan, Rashid Khan captains Kandahar Warriors, Rahmat Shah leads Nangarhar Cosmic, and Rahmanullah Gurbaz captains Paktia. Around them are some of the biggest names in the short format — Muhammad Amir and Tymal Mills with the new ball, Shakib Al Hasan and Moeen Ali as all-rounders, Alex Hales and Colin Munro at the top of the order. Every name below links to a full profile — role, batting and bowling style where verified, career context and the team’s fixtures.

Frequently asked questions

How many players are in APL 2026-27?

95 players have a profile on SialTV — 19 for each of the five teams: Akcel United Kabul, Balkh Zwanan, Kandahar Warriors, Nangarhar Cosmic and Paktia. Every squad was set at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, and every name on this page links to that player’s full profile.

When does APL 2026-27 start?

On Sunday 27 December 2026, with the first ball at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM in Dubai). The league stage is a double round-robin, the top four reach the playoffs, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027.

Who captains each team?

Azmatullah Omarzai (Akcel United Kabul), Ibrahim Zadran (Balkh Zwanan), Rashid Khan (Kandahar Warriors), Rahmat Shah (Nangarhar Cosmic) and Rahmanullah Gurbaz (Paktia). All five are Afghanistan internationals, and each captain’s name is marked on this page. Rashid Khan, the Kandahar captain, is the most established T20 bowler in the world.

Where are the full squads?

On each team page — verified 2026-27 squads with roles where confirmed, set at the Dubai draft on 3 November 2026 — and on the APL Teams page, which lists all five sides with their captains and coaches. Fixtures and results are on the schedule page.
