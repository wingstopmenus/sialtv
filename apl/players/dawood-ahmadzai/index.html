Dawood Ahmadzai — Bowler

Dawood Ahmadzai arrived at the Afghanistan Premier League by the longest road of any player in the 2026 draft — a leg-break bowler, born in Afghanistan, who plays international cricket for France.

Kabul took him in the Silver category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, for a reported US$20,000, completing a 19-man Akcel United Kabul squad led by captain Azmatullah Omarzai. His full name is Mouhammad Dawood Ahmadzai, and his story is unlike anyone else’s in the squad: born in Afghanistan on 25 February 1994, he plays his international cricket for France.

He made his T20I debut for France on 5 August 2021 against Norway in Krefeld, and has since played 52 T20Is for them, taking 62 wickets with his leg-breaks. That is a serious record at any level — more than a wicket a match, from a bowler who turns the ball away from right-handers and has spent five years as his country’s frontline spinner.

For Kabul he is a different kind of option again: not Mujeeb’s off-breaks, not Sharafuddin’s left-arm orthodox, not Tahir’s googly, but a leg-break bowler of his own, right-handed with the bat. In a squad built around spin, Ahmadzai is the depth that lets Shane McDermott keep attacking even when a frontline spinner has an off day.
