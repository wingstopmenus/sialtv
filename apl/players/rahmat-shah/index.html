The captain who runs Afghanistan — and Nangarhar

Rahmat Shah is the obvious centre of Nangarhar Cosmic. He is Afghanistan’s Test and ODI captain, a job he has held since 22 July 2026, and he arrived at Cosmic through the only swap in the whole draft: in the leaders’ draft on 25 October, Rahmanullah Gurbaz was first named for Nangarhar and Rahmat for Paktia, and on 3 November the two changed places.

The numbers explain why a franchise builds around him. Rahmat has 1,043 Test runs at 45.34, with a highest score of 234 against Zimbabwe in 2024, and 4,300 ODI runs at 35.83, including 143 not out against Ireland in 2026. He is a right-hand top-order batter who bowls right-arm leg-break, and he has been an Afghanistan regular since his ODI debut against Scotland in March 2013. His Test debut came against India in June 2018, and his T20I debut against India in January 2024.

He already knows this league. Rahmat played for Nangarhar Leopards in the first APL in October 2018, playing all eight league matches, scoring 87 runs and taking six wickets. His best day came against Kabul Zwanan on 12 October 2018, when 8 off 6 and 3 for 25 made him Player of the Match in a 15-run win. He sat out the semi-final, when Nangarhar were bowled out for 64 by Balkh Legends and lost by 171 runs. Eight years on, he returns to the same city’s colours as captain.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic are a new club with a blank record, and Rahmat is the man the whole project leans on. Toby Radford coaches the side, Mark Boucher mentors it, and the top order — Hales, Rahmat, Darwish Rasooli and Kamindu Mendis — is the strongest part of the squad on paper.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, and four of the five sides reach the playoffs. The Afghanistan Premier League season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Rahmat’s own form at number three will decide how far this side goes.
