Two hundred and seventy wickets, one county, one debut

Christopher Wood is Balkh Zwanan’s one-county man — a left-arm fast-medium seamer who has spent his entire career at Hampshire, sixteen seasons and counting, with 270 career T20 wickets. He was a Supplementary pick in the last round of the Dubai draft, and APL 2026-27 is his debut in the league.

Balkh Zwanan took Christopher Wood in Supplementary Round 2 at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, the last round of a draft in which 90 players were picked from a pool of 462 across six categories and 18 rounds. He and leg-spinner Qais Ahmad, the player of the 2018 final, were the two Supplementary picks who completed Balkh’s 19-man squad. No fee is published for Supplementary picks, so none is listed here.

Wood was 28 when the first APL season was played in Sharjah in October 2018, and his name appears in none of the five squads from that year. He was already a Hampshire white-ball regular by then, but the overseas places went elsewhere, so 2026 is his APL debut — eight years after the tournament began, and sixteen seasons into a county career spent entirely with one club.

His record is the quiet kind: 270 career T20 wickets in 251 matches (to 25 September 2026), 228 of them for Hampshire (to 5 June 2026), three Blast titles with the county, and a maiden first-class hundred — 105 not out at Grace Road in 2012 — that says he can bat a little too. He is 36, born in Basingstoke on 27 June 1990, and he arrives in a squad with a strong English flavour: Moeen Ali came in as the Platinum pick and Luke Wood — no relation, and a fellow left-armer — as the Diamond pick.
