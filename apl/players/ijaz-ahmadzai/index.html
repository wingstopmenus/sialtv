The first name Paktia called in the Gold round

Ijaz Ahmad Ahmadzai was born in Kabul on 25 June 2003. He bats right-handed, bowls right-arm medium, and he is a batting all-rounder who does his best work when a game is slipping away.

Paktia took him as the first pick of the Gold round at the Dubai draft on 3 November 2026, for a reported $40,000, ahead of Farmanullah Safi, Colin Munro, David Wiese and Hashmatullah Shahidi. He joins a side captained by Rahmanullah Gurbaz, coached by Azhar Mahmood and mentored by Waqar Younis, with the season starting on 27 December 2026 in the UAE.

He was fifteen when the first APL season was played in Sharjah in October 2018, and his name appears in none of the five squads of that season. The 2026-27 season is his APL debut.

Two fifties, two rescues, two wins

Ahmadzai made his T20I debut against Ireland at Sharjah on 15 March 2024, and he had played seven T20Is for 145 runs before the Zimbabwe series in October 2026. That series is the reason Paktia wanted him.

Days before the draft, in Afghanistan’s home T20I series against Zimbabwe at Sharjah, he made two fifties in two innings. The first, on 27 October, was 52 off 22 balls — a fifty off 20 balls, the joint-fastest T20I fifty ever made by an Afghan, level with Azmatullah Omarzai and Mohammad Nabi. The second, on 29 October, was 52 off 27. Both came with Afghanistan in trouble — 66 for 5 in the first match, 38 for 5 in the second — and both ended in wins. It was his first international cricket in almost a year.

His domestic numbers say the same thing. His List A average of 38.16 shows he can build an innings as well as finish one, and his domestic T20 strike rate of 138.88 — 174.19 at the 2026 Shpageeza — shows the tempo he brings.

A finisher for the lower middle order

Gurbaz gives Paktia its power at the top, Hasaranga its world-class spin, and Munro and Wiese their overseas experience. What Ahmadzai offers is different: a 23-year-old who bats in the lower middle order, scores quickly, and can offer a few overs of right-arm medium when the captain needs them.

If the Zimbabwe series was a glimpse, Paktia will hope the APL is where the rest of the world meets him. The Gold round exists for exactly this kind of player — young, in form, and ready for a bigger stage.

A new club, a blank record

The format is a double round-robin: five teams, eight league matches each, two against every opponent, and the top four go through to the playoffs. A win is worth two points, and one side goes home after the league stage. Nothing is on the board yet — every team starts on zero, and the first points come after Match 1 on 27 December 2026, when the first ball is bowled at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM Dubai time) in the UAE. The season ends with the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027.

Paktia are a new club for 2026-27, not a continuation of the 2018 Paktia Panthers, so Ahmadzai has no old APL record to defend. Every appearance he makes will be a first, in the APL season that starts on 27 December 2026.
