The rarest kind of bowler in the squad

Zahir Khan Pakteen, born in Kabul on 20 December 1998, is a left-hand batter and a left-arm wrist spinner — a chinaman bowler, which makes him one of the rarest kinds of bowler in world cricket. Nangarhar Cosmic picked him in the Silver category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026, alongside Ismatullah Alam, Mohammad Yamin Ahmadzai, Amir Jangoo and Shafiqullah Ghafari.

Wrist spin that turns the ball away from the right-hander is a scarce skill, and it is why franchises keep coming back to him: he signed with Rajasthan Royals for the 2018 Indian Premier League season, and he has played in the ILT20 for Dubai Capitals. A left-arm wrist spinner gives a captain a different angle through the middle overs, and in T20 that difference is often the wicket that breaks a partnership.

For Cosmic, he is the second front-line leg-spinner in the squad alongside Shafiqullah Ghafari, and the contrast between the two — Zahir’s left-arm wrist spin against Ghafari’s right-arm leg-break — gives Toby Radford two ways to attack the middle order. With Shakib Al Hasan’s left-arm orthodox also in the side, Nangarhar can bowl spin from both ends for long stretches.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic’s attack is built around variety: Junaid Siddique’s pace, Aftab Alam’s seam, Shakib’s left-arm spin, and Zahir’s wrist spin. Captain Rahmat Shah leads the side, Toby Radford coaches it, and Mark Boucher mentors it.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, with four playoff places on offer. The APL season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Zahir’s wrist spin could be the surprise weapon of the season.
