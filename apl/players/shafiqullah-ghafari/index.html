A second leg-spinner for Cosmic’s attack

Nangarhar Cosmic picked Shafiqullah Ghafari in the Silver category at the APL player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026. The Silver category carries a reported salary of $20,000. He is one of five Silver picks in the Nangarhar squad, alongside Zahir Khan, Ismatullah Alam, Mohammad Yamin Ahmadzai and Amir Jangoo, and the pick gives Nangarhar a second front-line leg-spinner to go with Zahir Khan.

Ghafari is a bowling all-rounder: a right-hand batter who bowls right-arm leg-break, born on 8 October 2001. His name first travelled beyond Afghanistan at the Under-19 World Cup in 2020, where he took 16 wickets — a haul that marked him out as a wrist spinner worth following. Leg-spin is the hardest skill to play in T20, and a side that can bowl it from both ends has a real weapon.

For Cosmic, the pairing with Zahir Khan is the point. Ghafari’s right-arm leg-break turns the ball the other way from Zahir’s left-arm wrist spin, so Toby Radford can attack right-handers and left-handers with a different kind of spin in each over. With Shakib Al Hasan’s left-arm orthodox alongside them, Nangarhar’s spin group is the deepest in the league on paper.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic’s bowling is built on spin variety: Shakib’s left-arm orthodox, Zahir’s left-arm wrist spin, and Ghafari’s right-arm leg-break, with Junaid Siddique’s pace and Aftab Alam’s seam up front. Captain Rahmat Shah leads the side, Toby Radford coaches it, and Mark Boucher mentors it.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, with four playoff places on offer. The APL season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027.
