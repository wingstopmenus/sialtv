The keeper who was there the first time round

Ikram Alikhil is one of the few players in the 2026-27 squads who was there the first time round. He kept wicket for Balkh Legends in the inaugural APL season in Sharjah in October 2018, playing all nine matches as the champions’ first-choice keeper while Kamran Akmal sat on the bench. His batting barely got a look in — he faced only 37 balls in the whole tournament, scoring 25 runs in six innings.

Since then he has become an Afghanistan regular. Alikhil is a left-hand batter, born in Kabul on 29 September 2000, and his highest ODI score is 86 against West Indies at Headingley in 2019. He made his ODI debut against Ireland in March 2019, has 94 Test runs at 18.80 in three Tests, and has been Afghanistan’s Test vice-captain since 1 October 2026.

For Cosmic, he is the local player most likely to hold a place all season. He keeps wicket and bats in the middle order, and in a side full of overseas stars he is the finisher — the one who closes games out if the top order fires, and the one who holds them together if it does not. Nangarhar picked him fourth in the Gold round, for a reported $40,000.

Where Nangarhar stand

Nangarhar Cosmic have two wicketkeeper-batters in the squad — Alikhil and West Indian Amir Jangoo — and Alikhil’s 2018 title gives him the edge for the gloves. Captain Rahmat Shah leads the side, Toby Radford coaches it, and Mark Boucher mentors it.

Cosmic play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams, with four playoff places on offer. The Afghanistan Premier League 2026 season opens on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time, and the final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027. Alikhil already knows what it takes to win this league.
