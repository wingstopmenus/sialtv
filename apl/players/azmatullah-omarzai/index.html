Azmatullah Omarzai — All-rounder

Azmatullah Omarzai captains Akcel United Kabul into APL 2026-27 — a fast bowler who bats in the middle order, the franchise’s Afghan Icon, and the ICC Men’s ODI Cricketer of the Year for 2024. He leads a brand-new club into a brand-new season.

Omarzai was 18 when he made his Twenty20 debut in the first APL season, named in the Paktia Panthers squad in 2018. Eight years later he returns to the league as a captain, not a prospect — and as one of the most complete cricketers Afghanistan has produced. He bowls right-arm fast-medium, bats in the middle order, and fields like a man who expects to be involved in every ball.

The 2024 season made his reputation. He scored 417 ODI runs in the calendar year and was named the ICC Men’s ODI Cricketer of the Year, the first Afghan to take the award. His highest ODI score is an unbeaten 149, and his best ODI bowling is 5 for 58 — numbers that show both halves of his game in the same sentence. He made his ODI debut against Ireland on 21 January 2021, his T20I debut against Bangladesh on 3 March 2022, and his Test debut against Zimbabwe on 26 December 2024.

Then came October 2026, and a T20I hundred — 116 — that underlined how far his batting has travelled since that teenage APL debut. For Kabul, that matters as much as the new ball. Omarzai is the player who lets Shane McDermott balance the side: a fifth bowler who is also a genuine middle-order batter, so the team can carry an extra spinner or an extra seamer without weakening the batting.
