Nineteen wickets at Under-19 level — now the APL

Abdul Aziz Khan is a 19-year-old seamer from Paktia who took 19 Under-19 ODI wickets before Balkh Zwanan picked him in the Emerging category. He bowls right-arm medium, bats right-handed, and APL 2026-27 — starting 27 December 2026 in the UAE — is his first season of senior franchise cricket.

Abdul Aziz Khan is in the Balkh Zwanan squad for APL 2026-27, picked in the Emerging category at the player draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026. He is one of three Emerging picks for Balkh, alongside Salaam Khan Ahmadzai and Azizullah Miakhil, and one of fifteen Emerging players picked across the five franchises.

The draft took 90 players from a pool of 462 over eighteen rounds, and the Emerging category — a reported $10,000 salary — is the league’s way of putting young Afghan domestic talent on the same stage as the overseas stars. His record so far is at Under-19 level: 19 U19 ODI wickets, with a best of 4 for 28.

The season starts on 27 December 2026 in the UAE, and for Abdul Aziz Khan it will be an APL debut: he did not play in 2018, when he was eleven years old, and he has no senior franchise record anywhere yet. The squad he has joined is not a development side. Balkh Zwanan is captained by Ibrahim Zadran, the national opener, coached by Mickey Arthur and mentored by Tillakaratne Dilshan.

Where he fits at Balkh Zwanan

Abdul Aziz Khan’s season is the same as his fellow Emerging picks’: learn from the seniors, push in the nets, and be ready when the chance comes. Balkh Zwanan have a deep seam attack — Luke Wood, Christopher Wood, Abdullah Ahmadzai, Abdullah Tarakhail — so his games will be earned, not given. But eight league matches in under a month, with travel and quick turnarounds, is exactly the schedule where a fourth or fifth seamer gets his game.

What he offers is control and a wicket-taking record at the level he has played: 19 Under-19 ODI wickets is not a small sample. The final is on Wednesday 20 January 2027.
