Format

Format — double round-robin, top four to the playoffs

Five teams play a double round-robin: every team meets each of the other four twice, for eight league matches per team and 20 league matches in all. The top four teams on the APL Points Table reach the playoffs, and the season ends with the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027. A win is worth 2 points.

The playoff format beyond that — which teams meet in which playoff, and on which dates — has not been announced yet. The three playoff matches and the final are listed in the table above as soon as the details are confirmed.
