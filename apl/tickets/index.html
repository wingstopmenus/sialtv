APL 2026-27 Tickets — Availability & Updates

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Tickets, not yet announced

No ticket prices, sellers or on-sale dates have been announced for APL 2026-27 in the information SialTV has verified. This page says so plainly rather than guessing — and it will list the official seller and the prices as soon as the league announces them.

Until then, do not buy from unauthorised sellers or resale listings. Tickets bought outside the official channel can be refused at the gate, and SialTV never lists unauthorised sellers.

Where to check

The Afghanistan Cricket Board’s official channels and the APL’s official channels are the first places ticketing news appears. This page is checked against the league’s announcements and updated when ticketing opens. SialTV also carries the season’s APL Schedule and the APL Live Scores page, so the dates below are the ones to plan around.

Plan your visit

The season runs from Sunday 27 December 2026, with the first ball at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM Dubai time), to the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027, all in the UAE. The grounds have not been announced yet — the fixture list, with grounds and start times, will be added to the schedule page once the ACB publishes it.

If you are travelling for a match, check the fixture date and the ground on the schedule page first — the UAE has several grounds, and the same weekend can have matches at more than one of them.
