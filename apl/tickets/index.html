Tickets, not yet announced

No ticket prices, sellers or on-sale dates have been announced for APL 2026-27 in the information SialTV has verified. This page says so plainly rather than guessing — and it will list the official seller and the prices as soon as the league announces them.

The Afghanistan Cricket Board’s official channels and the APL’s official channels are the first places ticketing news appears. This page is checked against the league’s announcements and updated when ticketing opens. Plan your visit

The season runs from Sunday 27 December 2026, with the first ball at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM Dubai time), to the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027, all in the UAE. The grounds have not been announced yet — the fixture list, with grounds and start times, will be added to the schedule page once the ACB publishes it.
