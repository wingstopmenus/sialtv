APL 2026-27 Teams — Captains, Coaches & Squads

Five teams play APL 2026-27, from 27 December 2026 to 20 January 2027 in the UAE. Every squad has 19 players, picked at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026. Here are all five — captains, coaches and draft facts — in one place.

The draft — 3 November 2026, Dubai

Each squad has 19 players, picked at the draft in Dubai on 3 November 2026 — 90 players in 18 rounds, plus the five Afghan Icon captains, one per team. The five captains are also the Icon players: Azmatullah Omarzai, Ibrahim Zadran, Rashid Khan, Rahmat Shah and Rahmanullah Gurbaz.

The first Platinum pick of the draft was Muhammad Amir, taken by Kandahar Warriors. The full squads are listed on the individual team pages; the captains, coaches and draft facts for all five teams are listed above.

What each team plays for

A win is worth 2 points, and with five teams chasing four playoff places, one side goes home after the league stage. Every team plays eight league matches — each opponent twice — for 20 league matches in all, then three playoffs and the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027. The trophy for 2026-27 is reported as the Koh-E-Zafar. The full fixture list is on the APL Schedule page once the ACB publishes it, and the standings, once play starts, are on the APL Points Table page.

The last APL season — 2018

The only completed APL season so far was played in Sharjah from 5 to 21 October 2018. Balkh Legends won the final, beating Kabul Zwanan by 4 wickets (Kabul Zwanan 132/9, Balkh Legends 138/6). The 2026-27 clubs are new — they do not inherit the 2018 titles, and Balkh Zwanan is not the same side as the 2018 champions Balkh Legends.
