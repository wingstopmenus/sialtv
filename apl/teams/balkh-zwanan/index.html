Season so far

Balkh is the one city in the APL with a championship in its past, and Balkh Zwanan do not own it. The 2018 champions were Balkh Legends, who beat Kabul Zwanan by 4 wickets in the Sharjah final. That was a different club. Zwanan carry the city name, not the trophy, and they start 2026-27 with a blank record: played 0, won 0.

What they do carry is the most experienced coach in the league. Mickey Arthur has coached Pakistan, Sri Lanka and South Africa at international level, and he works with a captain who already runs a national side: Ibrahim Zadran is Afghanistan’s T20I captain. Between them, the dugout and the dressing room have seen most of what a T20 season can throw at a team.

The draft gave Arthur a side built from the top down. Balkh used the second pick of the Platinum round on England all-rounder Moeen Ali, then took Allah Mohammad Ghazanfar and Luke Wood in Diamond, Gulbadin Naib, Sediqullah Atal and UAE captain Muhammad Waseem in Gold, and used their Wildcard on fast bowler Abdullah Ahmadzai. The last name in was Qais Ahmad, the player of the match in the 2018 final with 5 for 18 — the only man in this squad who has won an APL final, for a different club.

What to watch

The batting has three openers — Zadran, Atal and Waseem — and only two opening places, which is the kind of problem coaches like to have. Moeen Ali bats in the middle order and bowls off-spin, Gulbadin Naib does the same job with seam, and Colin Ackermann adds another all-round option. On paper, this is the deepest batting group in the league.

The spin is just as strong. Ghazanfar’s mystery spin, Qais Ahmad’s leg-breaks and Mohammad Waqar Salamkheil’s wrist spin give Arthur three different ways to take wickets in the middle overs, and Luke Wood’s left-arm pace leads the seam attack. The question is the same one every drafted side faces: nineteen players who have never played together, with eight league matches to become a team.

Watch Qais Ahmad. He took 15 wickets in the 2018 season — second only to Isuru Udana — and his 5 for 18 won the final for Balkh Legends. Eight years later he is back in Balkh colours, for a different club, and the city will expect him to do it again.

Points table

Five teams chase four playoff places, so one side goes home after the league stage. Each team plays eight league matches — every opponent twice — and a win is worth 2 points. Sixteen points is the maximum; four wins should be enough to stay in the race, and net run rate decides the ties. The table below is empty because the season has not started: every team begins on 0 points, and the first update comes after Match 1 on 27 December.

Balkh’s edge should be experience. Arthur has won tournaments with drafted squads before, Zadran already captains his country, and Moeen Ali has played in every major T20 league there is.
