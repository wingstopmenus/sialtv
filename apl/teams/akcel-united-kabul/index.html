A new club from Kabul, built on pace and spin

Akcel United Kabul were one of the first three identities unveiled when the revived league was launched in August 2026, alongside Balkh Zwanan and Kandahar Warriors. The club is new in every sense that matters: new owners, new badge, new squad. Draft paperwork has also called the same club Akcel Kabul United — a word-order difference, not a second team.

The Kabul name has APL history, but this club does not inherit it. The 2018 side from the city was Kabul Zwanan, captained by Rashid Khan, and it finished runner-up to Balkh Legends in Sharjah. That club is not this club. Its results stay with it, and Akcel United Kabul start 2026-27 with a blank record: played 0, won 0.

What the new side does have is a captain who does two jobs. Azmatullah Omarzai is the Afghan Icon and a fast bowler who bats in the middle order, so Kabul’s balance starts with him. Around him, the draft built an attack first: Johnson Charles in Platinum, Fazalhaq Farooqi and Dushmantha Chameera in Diamond, and Mujeeb Ur Rahman with the first Gold pick. The batting — D’Arcy Short, Shamim Hossain, Hazratullah Zazai — has power at the top and questions to answer below it.

What Kabul need to reach the playoffs

Five teams chase four playoff places, so one side goes home after the league stage. Every team plays eight league matches — each opponent twice — and a win is worth 2 points. Sixteen points is the maximum; in practice, four wins should be enough to stay in the race, and net run rate decides the ties. The table below is empty because the season has not started: every team begins on 0 points, and the first update comes after Match 1 on 27 December.

There is no home ground to lean on — all matches are in the UAE, and the grounds are still to be announced — so Kabul’s season will be decided on neutral pitches, by an attack that has to take wickets every game. Kabul’s fixtures — dates still to be announced

The Afghanistan Cricket Board has not published the full fixture list, so Kabul’s dates, opponents and grounds are all still to be announced. No predicted schedule is shown here — the dates circulating online are not official. What is confirmed is the shape of the season: first ball on Sunday 27 December 2026 at 5:30 PM Afghanistan time (5:00 PM Dubai time) in the UAE, 20 league matches, three playoffs and the final on Wednesday 20 January 2027.

Kabul play eight league matches, two against each of the other four teams. Their fixtures will be added to this page the day the ACB publishes them, and the full list will be on the APL Schedule page.

Where Kabul look strong — and where the questions are

The attack is the argument for this team. Fazalhaq Farooqi’s left-arm pace, Dushmantha Chameera’s speed, Mujeeb Ur Rahman’s off-breaks and Imran Tahir’s leg-spin give McDermott four different kinds of threat, and Omarzai’s own fast bowling means the captain can hold an end while the specialists rotate. On UAE pitches, where the ball has gripped for spinners in past tournaments, that depth matters more than a single star.

The question is the batting. Johnson Charles and D’Arcy Short have opened in leagues around the world, and Hazratullah Zazai can change a game in six balls — but none of them has batted together before, and the middle order leans on young Afghan names: Uzairullah Niazai, Khalid Ahmadzai and Keramatullah, all Emerging picks. If the top three fire, Kabul can beat anyone. If they fall early, the new club will find out quickly how deep its batting really is.

Watch Curtis Campher, too. The Ireland all-rounder was a Supplementary pick, the last round of the draft, and he is the kind of signing that decides tight games — a seam bowler who bats in the top six and fields everywhere. In a five-team league where one side misses the playoffs, those are the margins.

Frequently asked questions

Who captains Akcel United Kabul?

Azmatullah Omarzai, Afghanistan’s all-rounder and the franchise’s Afghan Icon. He is a fast bowler who bats in the middle order, and he captains the side in its first season.

Who coaches Akcel United Kabul?

Shane McDermott is head coach, with Nikhil Chopra as mentor. McDermott’s job in the first season is to turn a drafted squad — nineteen players who have never played together — into a team before the first ball on 27 December.

Who are Akcel United Kabul’s overseas players?

Johnson Charles (West Indies), Dushmantha Chameera (Sri Lanka), D’Arcy Short (Australia), Shamim Hossain (Bangladesh), Imran Tahir (South Africa) and Curtis Campher (Ireland). The rest of the 19-man squad is Afghan.

When do Akcel United Kabul play their first match?

Their first match date has not been announced — the ACB has not published the full fixture list. The season starts on Sunday 27 December 2026, and Kabul’s fixtures will be added here as soon as they are confirmed.
