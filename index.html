About SialTV

SialTV is an independent informational website for cricket league schedules, live scores, points tables and where to watch. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, club, board or federation. The site never links to pirated streams or betting content.

Coverage is written by Asad Sial, Saleem Sial, Arshad Sial, Shakir Ali and Waseem Sial. See all authors. More about SialTV · Contact us if you spot a mistake.

  • Checked, not guessed

    Scores, fixtures, squads and broadcasters are checked against official and reliable sources. Anything unconfirmed is marked as such.

  • A hub for every league

    Each league has its own schedule, points table, results, teams and official broadcasters for that season.

  • Named editors

    Every page is written by a named editor, so you can see who is responsible for the coverage.
