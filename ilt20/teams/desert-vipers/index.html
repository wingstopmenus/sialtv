Season 5

Season so far

Desert Vipers won the 2025-26 season, the first ILT20 title in the club’s history, and Season 5 starts with them as the team everyone has to beat. The headline change is at the top: Shadab Khan, Pakistan’s leg-spinning all-rounder and former vice-captain, takes the captaincy for Season 5.

The champions kept their core. Fakhar Zaman and Shimron Hetmyer give the batting its punch, Shadab Khan and Mujeeb Ur Rahman run the middle overs, and Naseem Shah leads the pace attack. James Foster stays as head coach, so the title-winning dressing room has barely changed.

The base is Dubai, where six of the ten league matches are played, including the opener. The Vipers play MI Emirates twice in the first nine days — the opener on 21 November and the return in Abu Dhabi on 28 November — so the champions will know early whether the new captain has settled.

Season 5

What to watch

The strength is balance. There is a match-winner in every part of this side: Babar at the top, Hetmyer in the middle, Naseem with the new ball, Mujeeb and Shadab through the middle. Few ILT20 squads cover all four phases of a T20 game as neatly.

The question is the captaincy. Shadab is new to leading this group, but not to the job — he has captained Pakistan in T20Is and led Islamabad United to the PSL title in 2024. The Vipers won last season with a settled formula; changing the captain of a champion side is a gamble, and the first fortnight — opener, then Sharjah Warriorz, Dubai Capitals and the MI Emirates return — will show whether the gamble has paid off.

Watch the opener. Desert Vipers against MI Emirates is a repeat of the 2025-26 final’s storyline in the very first match of the season, and it is the first time Shadab leads the champions. A win sets the tone for the defence; a loss hands the initiative straight to the runners-up.

Season 5

Points table

There is no points table yet — the season starts on 21 November. The format is simple: six teams, each plays ten league matches, a win is worth 2 points, and the top four reach the playoffs. In a six-team league, five or six wins usually books a playoff place; four wins leaves a side depending on net run rate and other results. As champions, the Vipers’ target is the top two, which go straight to Qualifier 1 on 16 December.

The full standings, once play starts, are on the ILT20 Points Table page, and every result is on the ILT20 Results page. SialTV will update this page after every match day, through the final.
