Season so far

Dubai Capitals are the 2025 champions, and Season 5 is their defence of a title won only one season ago — except the side that won it has been rebuilt. Faf du Plessis leads the team, Hemang Badani coaches it, and the squad is built for Dubai’s pitches: power at the top, pace through the middle, and two of the best left-arm quicks in the league.

Phil Salt and Marcus Stoinis are the headline names alongside du Plessis. Salt sets the tempo in the powerplay, Stoinis finishes games, and Dasun Shanaka and Rovman Powell give the middle order its muscle. With the ball, Mustafizur Rahman and Noor Ahmad are the two bowlers opposition teams plan around.

The base is Dubai, and the schedule favours the Capitals more than any other side: seven of their ten league matches are in Dubai, including five of the first six. The season starts at home against Abu Dhabi Knight Riders on 22 November.

What to watch

The strength is the top order. Du Plessis, Salt and Stoinis are three of the most destructive batters in this league, and on Dubai’s true pitches they can take a game away in six overs. If two of them fire in the same match, the Capitals are very hard to beat.

The question is the death bowling. Mustafizur is as good as anyone at the death, but the support cast — Luke Wood, Matthew Potts, Mark Adair — has to hold its nerve when the Capitals defend small totals. Last season’s title was won with the bat; this season the bowlers will have to win at least two games on their own.

Watch the Dubai run. Five of the first six matches are at home, and a strong start there puts the Capitals top of the table before they travel. Drop two of those home games and the title defence starts to look like hard work.

Points table

There is no points table yet — the season starts on 21 November. The format is simple: six teams, each plays ten league matches, a win is worth 2 points, and the top four reach the playoffs. In a six-team league, five or six wins usually books a playoff place; four wins leaves a side depending on net run rate and other results. With seven home matches in Dubai, the Capitals’ path to the playoffs runs through their own ground.

The full standings, once play starts, are on the ILT20 Points Table page, and every result is on the ILT20 Results page. SialTV will update this page after every match day, through the final.

Frequently asked questions

Who captains Dubai Capitals?

Faf du Plessis. The South Africa batter captains the side in Season 5, with Hemang Badani as head coach.

When did Dubai Capitals win the ILT20?

In 2025. They are one of four past champions in the league, along with Gulf Giants (2023), MI Emirates (2024) and Desert Vipers (2025-26).

When is Dubai Capitals’ first match?

Match 3 against Abu Dhabi Knight Riders, on Sunday 22 November 2026, evening session, in Dubai.

How many home matches do Dubai Capitals play?

Seven of their ten league matches are in Dubai. The other three are in Sharjah (one) and Abu Dhabi (two).
