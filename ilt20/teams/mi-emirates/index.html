Season 5

Season so far

MI Emirates have been to the final in two of the last three seasons — champions in 2024, runners-up in 2025-26 — and Season 5 is about going one step further than last season. The change is in the dugout: Mark Boucher takes over as head coach.

Nicholas Pooran keeps the captaincy and keeps the most destructive middle order in the league around him: Jonny Bairstow, Sherfane Rutherford and Romario Shepherd, with Rashid Khan as the bowler every opponent plans for. Chris Jordan and Fazalhaq Farooqi give the pace attack its experience.

The base is Dubai, but the schedule is the toughest of any side’s: only three of the ten league matches are in Dubai, six are in Abu Dhabi and one is in Sharjah. The opener is in Dubai, the return against the Vipers is in Abu Dhabi on 28 November.

Season 5

What to watch

The strength is the middle order and the leg-spin. Pooran and Bairstow can chase anything, Shepherd and Rutherford finish games, and Rashid Khan wins matches in the middle overs like no other bowler in this league. When Rashid takes two wickets, MI Emirates usually win.

The question is the travel. Six matches in Abu Dhabi is the heaviest away load in the league, and the side that won in 2024 did it with Dubai as a fortress. Boucher’s first job is to make this team win away from home — starting with the Abu Dhabi return against the Vipers on 28 November.

Watch the opener. Desert Vipers against MI Emirates is the champions against the runners-up on the first night of the season. It is the only match of the first round that pairs last season’s two finalists, and the winner takes an early hold on the top of the table.

Season 5

Points table

There is no points table yet — the season starts on 21 November. The format is simple: six teams, each plays ten league matches, a win is worth 2 points, and the top four reach the playoffs. In a six-team league, five or six wins usually books a playoff place; four wins leaves a side depending on net run rate and other results. MI Emirates’ path is the away-heavy one: six of ten in Abu Dhabi, so the playoff maths depends on winning on the road.

The full standings, once play starts, are on the ILT20 Points Table page, and every result is on the ILT20 Results page. SialTV will update this page after every match day, through the final.

When did MI Emirates win the ILT20?
