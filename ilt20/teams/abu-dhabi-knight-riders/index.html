Season so far

Abu Dhabi Knight Riders are the only side in the ILT20, along with Sharjah Warriorz, never to have won the league. Four seasons have come and gone without a title, and Season 5 is built around a simple idea: put the most experienced cricketers in the tournament together and let them win the close games this side has so often lost.

Jason Holder takes the captaincy. The West Indies all-rounder leads a squad that mixes proven T20 winners — Kane Williamson, Sunil Narine, Moeen Ali, Finn Allen and Colin Munro — with UAE players who know these grounds. Dwayne Bravo, who won as much as any T20 cricketer ever has, is the head coach.

The base is Abu Dhabi, and the schedule is kind to the home side: six of the ten league matches are in Abu Dhabi, starting with MI Emirates on 26 November. The other four are split between Dubai and Sharjah. If the Knight Riders are going to end the wait, the home stretch is where it has to happen.

What to watch

The strength of this squad is its middle. Williamson steadies an innings, Munro and Allen can take a game away in the powerplay, and Narine plus Moeen give Bravo two world-class spinners who also bat. On the slow, low pitches the UAE can produce in December, that is a genuine edge.

The question is the pace attack. Beyond Holder himself there is no established overseas quick in the verified squad, so Muhammad Rohid and the rest of the seam group have to hold up their end. If the quicks leak runs, the spinners will be bowling under scoreboard pressure instead of setting the terms.

Watch the first two games. Dubai Capitals on 22 November and MI Emirates on 26 November are both sides with title-winning pedigree. Two good results there and the Knight Riders stop being the nearly-men of this league; two losses and the old story writes itself again.

Points table

There is no points table yet — the season starts on 21 November. The format is simple: six teams, each plays ten league matches, a win is worth 2 points, and the top four reach the playoffs. In a six-team league, five or six wins usually books a playoff place; four wins leaves a side depending on net run rate and other results. The Knight Riders’ path runs through their six home matches in Abu Dhabi: win most of those and the maths takes care of itself.

Frequently asked questions

Who captains Abu Dhabi Knight Riders?

Jason Holder. The West Indies all-rounder captains the side in Season 5, with Dwayne Bravo as head coach.

Have Abu Dhabi Knight Riders ever won the ILT20?

No. They have played all four seasons so far without winning the title. Season 5 is their fifth attempt.

Match 3 against Dubai Capitals, on Sunday 22 November 2026, evening session, in Dubai.
