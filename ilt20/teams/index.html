Season 5

Four champions, two chasers

Four of the six teams have won this league. Desert Vipers are the defending champions after winning 2025-26, Dubai Capitals won in 2025, MI Emirates in 2024 and Gulf Giants won the first season in 2023. Abu Dhabi Knight Riders and Sharjah Warriorz have never lifted the trophy — Season 5 is their next chance.

Every team plays 10 league matches, each opponent twice, and the top four reach the playoffs. The full fixture list is on the ILT20 Schedule page, and the standings, once play starts, are on the ILT20 Points Table page.

Season 5

What each team plays for

A win is worth 2 points, and with six teams chasing four playoff places, two sides go home after the league stage. The league stage runs from 21 November to 14 December, then Qualifier 1 on 16 December, the Eliminator on 17 December, Qualifier 2 on 18 December in Sharjah, and the final on 20 December in Dubai. SialTV follows every team through the season.

Season 5

Frequently asked questions

How many teams play in the ILT20?

Six teams: Desert Vipers, MI Emirates, Dubai Capitals, Gulf Giants, Abu Dhabi Knight Riders and Sharjah Warriorz. Each team plays 10 league matches.

Who are the defending ILT20 champions?

Desert Vipers won the 2025-26 season, so they defend the title in Season 5. Shadab Khan captains them in Season 5.

Which ILT20 team has won the most titles?

No team has won it twice. Desert Vipers (2025-26), Dubai Capitals (2025), MI Emirates (2024) and Gulf Giants (2023) have one title each. Abu Dhabi Knight Riders and Sharjah Warriorz have none yet.
