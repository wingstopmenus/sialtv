Pace with a skiddy action

Williams is not the tallest fast bowler, but his action is quick through the crease and the ball hurries onto the bat — which is why batters so often mistime him. He bowls a heavy length, uses the short ball well, and his best spells come when he attacks the stumps rather than searching for swing.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm fast-medium
  • Born: 1 October 1993, Vredenburg, South Africa

For MI Emirates he is the pace option who can bowl anywhere in the innings. Farooqi takes the new ball, Jordan closes, and Williams is the man Pooran can turn to when a partnership needs breaking in the middle overs or when a batter is set and needs to be rushed.

South Africa and the IPL

Williams made his South Africa debut in 2021 and has played Tests, ODIs and T20Is, taking wickets in all three formats. He has been a fixture of the Titans in South African domestic cricket and played in the IPL for Delhi Capitals, where his ability to bowl at the death was tested against the best T20 batters in the world.

He has also played in the SA20 and the ILT20 before, so the UAE's pitches are familiar ground. Season 5 is a chance to lock down a regular place in a strong attack — and with four overseas pace options in the squad, every performance counts.
