The title-winning captain, now the senior opener

Fakhar is a left-handed opener who bowls slow left-arm orthodox on occasion, and he is one of the most important players in Vipers history. He captained the side to the ILT20 title in Season 4, leading from the front as the champions beat MI Emirates by 46 runs in the final on 4 January 2026.

His most famous innings came in the 2017 Champions Trophy final, when he scored 114 against India at The Oval to win Pakistan the trophy. He has since become one of Pakistan's most destructive white-ball openers, with a double hundred in ODI cricket — the first by a Pakistani — and a T20 game built on power and timing. In Season 5, he hands the captaincy to Babar Azam and goes back to what he does best: opening the batting and hitting the ball hard.

Career so far

Fakhar made his Pakistan debut in 2017 and announced himself with that Champions Trophy final hundred — 114 off 106 balls against India. He has since scored more than 3,000 ODI runs, including a double hundred against Zimbabwe in 2018, the first by a Pakistani in the format.

He has played in the Pakistan Super League for Lahore Qalandars, where he is one of the league's leading run-scorers, and in the ILT20 for the Vipers, where he captained the side to the title in Season 4. His T20 game is built on power at the top of the order: he hits the ball hard, scores quickly, and takes the game on from the first ball. At 36, he is the senior statesman of the Vipers' batting — and still one of its most dangerous players.

The batting around him

The Vipers' batting is built around Babar Azam at the top, with Fakhar as the left-handed partner who can match him for power. Shimron Hetmyer provides the middle-order muscle, Harry Tector and Dan Lawrence add class, and Andries Gous keeps wicket and bats in the top six.

Fakhar's role is to give the innings its pace: if he gets going in the powerplay, the Vipers' total takes care of itself. His partnership with Babar — two of Pakistan's greatest white-ball batters opening together — is one of the most exciting opening pairs in the league, and it is the partnership the champions will build their title defence around.
