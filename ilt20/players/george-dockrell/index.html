Control in the middle overs

Dockrell's job is the one every T20 side needs and few players do well: bowl four overs in the middle, go for under seven an over, and take a wicket while doing it. His left-arm spin is accurate, his arm ball is a weapon, and his experience means he reads batters quickly — he knows when to flight it and when to fire it in.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Slow left-arm orthodox
  • Born: 22 July 1992, Dublin, Ireland

For the Warriorz he is one of five spin options, and his left-arm orthodox is the variation the attack needs: Rashid turns it one way, Nabi and Raza the other, and Dockrell brings it back into the right-hander. That variety is what makes a spin attack hard to line up.

A decade and more with Ireland

Dockrell made his Ireland debut in 2010 as a teenager and has played in three T20 World Cups and two ODI World Cups, including the famous win over England at the 2011 World Cup. He has played county cricket for Somerset and in franchise leagues around the world, and he has been a fixture of the Ireland side through its rise from associate to Full Member.

He is also a useful middle-order batter who has scored runs for Ireland in all formats. Season 5 is another stage for a player who has given Irish cricket more than a decade of service — and who still has plenty to offer a franchise side.

How long has George Dockrell played for Ireland?

What is George Dockrell's bowling style?
