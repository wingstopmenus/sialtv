The Scot who broke through in Zimbabwe

McMullen's breakthrough came at the 2023 ODI World Cup Qualifier in Zimbabwe, where he scored a hundred against Oman and took wickets against full-member sides — the kind of all-round performance that makes scouts sit up. He bats right-handed in the middle order and bowls right-arm medium-fast, and he does both jobs with the same directness: hit the ball hard, hit the pitch hard.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium-fast
  • Born: 18 October 1999, Durban, South Africa

For the Warriorz he is the seam-bowling all-rounder who balances the spin-heavy attack. When the pitches do not turn, or when the opposition has left-handers who handle spin well, McMullen is the bowler Vince can turn to — and his batting means the side never runs out of hitters.

From Durban to Scotland to the franchise circuit

McMullen was born in Durban and moved to Scotland, where he came through the club game and into the national side. Since the 2023 qualifier he has played in the ILT20, the SA20 and The Hundred, and he has been a regular for Scotland in ODIs and T20Is — including at the 2024 T20 World Cup.

He also plays in the NPL for Sudurpaschim Royals, which makes him one of the few players in world cricket playing two franchise leagues in the same season. Season 5 in the ILT20 is his next stage, and at 27 he is entering his best years as an all-rounder.
