The Fizz, back where he belongs

Mustafizur Rahman announced himself in 2015 with a debut series against India in which his off-cutters made world-class batters look ordinary, and he has been Bangladesh's premier fast bowler ever since. His weapon is deception: a cutter that looks like a fast ball until it grips and slows, a slower ball that arrives a fraction late, and a yorker that appears when the batter least expects it. He has taken more than 100 T20 international wickets for Bangladesh and has played in the IPL for Sunrisers Hyderabad, Chennai Super Kings and Delhi Capitals.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: Dubai Capitals
  • Batting: Left-handed
  • Bowling: Left-arm fast-medium
  • Born: 6 September 1995

He was one of four players the Capitals pre-signed before the auction, which is the highest compliment a franchise can pay: the champions did not want to risk losing him. In a league where the best death bowler often decides the title, keeping Mustafizur was the first decision of their Season 5.

The death-overs bank

Mustafizur's job is the last four overs, and the Capitals' planning starts there. Du Plessis knows he has four overs of cutters and yorkers to close any game, which changes how he uses the rest of the attack: the new-ball bowlers can attack, the spinners can bowl in the middle, and the total the side defends is always smaller than it looks because the last four overs are the hardest to score from in the league.

His left-arm angle adds to the problem for right-handed batters, who make up most of every opposition top order. Bowling across them, cutting the ball away, and then slipping in the yorker is a sequence that has worked for ten years, and there is no sign of it stopping.

The bowler the title defence depends on

The Capitals won the 2025 title with the bat, and their own analysis of this season is blunt: the bowlers have to win at least two games on their own. Mustafizur is the bowler most likely to do it. A spell of 4 for 20 at the death turns a lost game into a won one, and he has produced those spells in World Cups, in the IPL, and in this league.
