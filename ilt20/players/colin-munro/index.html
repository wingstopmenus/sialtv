The powerplay destroyer

Munro is a left-handed opener who bats with a simple plan: hit the ball hard, hit it early, and let the bowlers worry. He was the first batter in history to score three T20I centuries, and his strike rate in international T20 cricket has always been among the best in the world.

At 39 he is no longer a New Zealand regular, but his franchise record has stayed strong — he has played in the Caribbean Premier League, the Big Bash, the Pakistan Super League and the ILT20, and he was part of Trinbago Knight Riders' 2026 CPL side. The Knight Riders bought him for $40,000, a low price for a man with his record, and the bet is obvious: if Munro and Finn Allen both fire in the powerplay, no total is safe. He can also bowl right-arm medium if the captain needs an over.

Career so far

Munro made his New Zealand debut in 2012 and played 65 T20Is, scoring three hundreds — against Bangladesh, West Indies and India — and holding the record as the first man to the mark. He also played 57 ODIs and a Test, and was part of New Zealand's 2019 World Cup final side.

His franchise career has taken him around the world: Auckland at home, Trinbago Knight Riders in the CPL, Islamabad United and Karachi Kings in the PSL, Perth Scorchers in the BBL, and the Knight Riders family in both the Caribbean and Abu Dhabi. His most famous innings remain his T20I hundreds, all scored at strike rates above 150, and his 2018 hundred against West Indies — 104 off 53 balls — is still one of the great T20I knocks by a New Zealander.

The batting around him

Two of the most aggressive openers in world cricket will take the new ball for the Knight Riders — Allen and Munro — with Williamson the anchor who makes sure a fast start does not become a collapse.

Behind them sit Andre Fletcher, Michael Pepper and Moeen Ali, so the batting runs deep — but the plan starts with Allen and Munro. If the two openers give the Knight Riders 60 in the first six overs, the middle order can play with freedom. Munro's job is to make that happen, and his record says he has done it for a decade.
