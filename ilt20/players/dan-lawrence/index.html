The versatile England all-rounder

Lawrence is a right-handed batter and right-arm off-break bowler who has played Test cricket for England, and he was a direct signing for the Vipers before the Season 5 auction. He is one of four players the champions kept without going to auction, alongside Andries Gous, Mujeeb Ur Rahman and Shimron Hetmyer.

His value is in his versatility: he can open the batting, bat in the middle order, bowl off-spin, and field anywhere. He has played for Essex and Surrey in English domestic cricket, and he has been a regular in The Hundred. In the Vipers' title-winning Season 4 campaign, he was a key member of the side that beat MI Emirates in the final, and his all-round game gives Babar Azam options in every phase of a match.

Career so far

Lawrence made his England Test debut in 2021 against Sri Lanka and has played more than ten Tests, scoring a hundred against West Indies in 2022. He has also played ODI cricket for England and has been a regular in the county game for Essex and Surrey.

His T20 game has grown steadily: he is a clean striker of the ball, a useful off-spinner, and a brilliant fielder. He has played in The Hundred for London Spirit and has been part of the Vipers' ILT20 side since Season 4, where his versatility made him a regular in the title-winning team. At 29, he is entering his prime as a cricketer — and the Vipers have kept him for the title defence.

The all-round group around him

The Vipers' all-round depth is the envy of the league. Shadab Khan is Pakistan's premier leg-spinning all-rounder, Mujeeb Ur Rahman is a mystery spinner who can bat, and Lawrence is the English all-rounder who can do a bit of everything.

His role is to bat in the middle order, bowl four overs of off-spin when the captain needs them, and field in the deep. In a side full of stars, Lawrence is the cricketer who does the jobs nobody else wants to do — and does them well. If the Vipers need balance, Lawrence is the man who gives it to them.
