The American who hit the World Cup headlines

Krishnamurthi's moment came in Chennai in February 2026, when he smashed an unbeaten 68 off 33 balls against Namibia at the T20 World Cup — reaching fifty in just 23 balls, the third-fastest by an associate batter in World Cup history. He hit six sixes, including three in one over, and was named Player of the Match.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Slow left-arm orthodox
  • Born: 2 June 2003, Corvallis, Oregon, USA

For MI Emirates he is a middle-order batter who can also bowl four overs of left-arm spin. That two-way value matters in a squad that has to balance overseas slots: Krishnamurthi can bat at five or six, bowl in the middle overs, and field sharply — three jobs from one player.

From Karnataka to California to the ILT20

Krishnamurthi was born in Corvallis, Oregon, played age-group cricket for Karnataka in India, and moved with his family to the San Francisco Bay Area in 2020. He studies computer science at San Jose State University while playing professional cricket — for the USA, for the San Francisco Unicorns in Major League Cricket, and now for MI Emirates.

He made his ODI debut for the USA against Nepal in September 2021 and his T20I debut against Oman in February 2025. He played in the ILT20 for MI Emirates in the 2025-26 season, so Season 5 is a return, not a debut — and after his World Cup, he arrives with a reputation to build on.
