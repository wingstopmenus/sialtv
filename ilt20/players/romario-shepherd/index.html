Pace and power in one player

Shepherd's bowling is built on hostility. He runs in hard, hits the pitch hard, and his bouncer is a genuine weapon — which is why he is used at the times when batters are trying to attack. He has taken wickets for West Indies in T20Is and ODIs, and his economy in the middle overs is better than his raw pace suggests.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium-fast
  • Born: 26 November 1994, Guyana

With the bat he is a finisher. A right-hander who clears the ropes easily, he bats in the lower middle order and has turned games for Guyana Amazon Warriors in the CPL and for West Indies with late cameos. For MI Emirates he is the player who can win a game in either discipline on the same night.

From Guyana to the IPL

Shepherd comes from Guyana and made his West Indies debut in 2019. He has played in the CPL since his teens, in the IPL for Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Lucknow Super Giants, and in the ILT20 for MI Emirates before — he knows this league, these grounds and this dressing room.

His international career has grown steadily: he has played Tests as well as white-ball cricket for West Indies, and his all-round value keeps him in demand in every franchise league. Season 5 is another chance to show why teams keep coming back for him.
