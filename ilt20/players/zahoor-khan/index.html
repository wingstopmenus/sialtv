The UAE seamer who knows these grounds

Zahoor's value is local knowledge. He has bowled on the UAE's pitches for the national side for years — in ODIs and T20Is, in World Cup qualifiers and in the ILT20 — and he understands how they change under lights, when the ball stops swinging and when it starts to grip. That knowledge is worth as much as pace on these surfaces.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium-fast

He bowls right-arm medium-fast, hits a consistent length, and uses the slower ball well at the death. For MI Emirates he is the UAE bowler who can hold an end while the overseas stars attack from the other, and he has done that job in this league before.

A decade with the UAE

Zahoor made his UAE debut in 2017 and has been a regular in the national side ever since, playing in the 2022 T20 World Cup and in a string of qualifiers. He has also been part of MI Emirates squads in earlier ILT20 seasons, so the dressing room, the coaches and the grounds are all familiar.

In a squad that leans heavily on overseas pace, Zahoor is the local bowler who makes the balance work. He will not always make the XI — the competition for pace places is fierce — but when the conditions suit seam or when a game needs a steady hand, he is the man Pooran turns to.

The MI Emirates around him
