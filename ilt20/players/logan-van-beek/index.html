The Super Over hero

Van Beek's name is forever linked to one over. At the 2023 ODI World Cup Qualifier in Zimbabwe, the Netherlands tied with West Indies and van Beek smashed 30 runs off Jason Holder in the Super Over — the most runs ever scored in a Super Over in ODI cricket — and then took two wickets with the ball to win the game. It was the innings that took the Netherlands to the World Cup.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium-fast
  • Born: 7 September 1990, Christchurch, New Zealand

For the Warriorz he is the seam-bowling all-rounder who does the unglamorous jobs: bowl in the powerplay, bowl at the death, bat at seven or eight and score quickly. He has done those jobs for the Netherlands for more than a decade, and he does them without fuss.

From Christchurch to the Netherlands

Van Beek was born in Christchurch and played for Canterbury before moving to the Netherlands, where he qualified through his Dutch grandparents. He made his Netherlands debut in 2014 and has played in two ODI World Cups and in T20 World Cups, as well as in the BBL for the Sydney Sixers and in county cricket for Leicestershire.

He is also a former New Zealand Under-19 international, which makes him one of the few players in the league to have represented two countries at age-group or senior level. Season 5 is another stage for a player who has made a career out of being reliable when it matters most.
