The batter who pulled out

Donald is a right-handed batter from Swansea who plays for Glamorgan, and he was named in the Vipers' original Season 5 squad at the auction on 1 October 2026. He is an aggressive top-order batter who has scored hundreds in first-class, List A and T20 cricket for his county.

His ILT20 chance did not last. On 16 October 2026, the Vipers announced that Donald had pulled out of the squad for national duty and that Pakistan batter Hassan Nawaz would replace him. The change was confirmed by the franchise and by the league, and the updated Vipers squad lists Nawaz in Donald's place. This page records Donald's original selection and the replacement, so the record of the Season 5 squad is clear.

Career so far

Donald came through Glamorgan's academy and made his debut as a teenager, scoring a hundred on his first-class debut. He has since become one of the most destructive batters in English domestic cricket, with a T20 strike rate among the best in the county game.

He has also played in The Hundred, where his power-hitting has made him a regular pick. He has been one of the most destructive batters in the county game, with a T20 strike rate among the best in the format. The Vipers' decision to buy him at the Season 5 auction was a sign of how highly his power-hitting is rated — even if national duty meant he never took the field for the champions.

The batting he would have joined

The Vipers' batting is built around Babar Azam at the top, with Fakhar Zaman and Shimron Hetmyer providing the power and Harry Tector and Dan Lawrence adding class in the middle order. Donald would have fitted into that line-up as an aggressive top-order option.

His replacement, Hassan Nawaz, is a different kind of batter — a Pakistan international who played for the Vipers in Season 4 and knows the dressing room. The champions' batting is no weaker for the change, but Donald's power-hitting would have added another dimension to a side that already has plenty of it.
