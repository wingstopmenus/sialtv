The young power-hitting keeper

Pepper is a right-handed wicketkeeper-batter from England who plays for Essex, and his game is built on power at the top of the order. He has played in The Hundred for Southern Brave and has a reputation as one of the hardest hitters in English domestic cricket.

His most famous innings came in the 2022 T20 Blast, when he smashed 86 off 32 balls for Essex against Middlesex, and he has since become a regular opener in white-ball cricket. The Knight Riders bought him for $40,000 at the Season 5 auction — four times what they paid for Lorcan Tucker — which says plenty about how they rate his batting. If the Knight Riders want a keeper who can open and clear the ropes in the powerplay, Pepper is the pick.

Career so far

Pepper came through Essex's academy and made his debut in 2018, establishing himself as the county's white-ball wicketkeeper. He has played in The Hundred for Southern Brave and has been one of the most consistent power-hitters in the T20 Blast.

His T20 record is built on strike rate rather than volume: he scores quickly, hits sixes, and takes the game on from the first ball. He has also kept wicket at a high standard for Essex, which is why the Knight Riders see him as a genuine top-order option rather than just a keeper. The ILT20 is his first major overseas franchise league, and it is a natural step for a 28-year-old who has dominated English domestic white-ball cricket.

The keeping group around him

Of the Knight Riders' three keepers, Pepper is the youngest and the most aggressive with the bat. Fletcher has the World Cup medal and the experience, Tucker has the international record — and Pepper has the powerplay game.

If the Knight Riders' top order needs a jolt — if Finn Allen falls early, or if the side wants two openers who can both clear the ropes — Pepper is the man. His keeping is good enough to hold the gloves for 20 overs, and his batting is good enough to open. That combination is why the franchise paid $40,000 for him.
