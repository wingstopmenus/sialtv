A left-armer for the new ball

Luke Wood's game is built on the angle: a left-armer bowling across right-handed batters, swinging the new ball back in, and then using the short ball when the pitch allows. He has played T20 internationals for England and has been a regular in the Vitality Blast for Lancashire, where his powerplay bowling has been his calling card. He has also played in the Pakistan Super League and the Big Bash, so the franchise circuit is familiar ground.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: Dubai Capitals
  • Batting: Left-handed
  • Bowling: Left-arm fast-medium
  • Born: 2 August 1995

Left-arm pace is a scarce resource in any T20 squad, and the Capitals have only one of them. That makes Wood's role clear even before the season starts: he is the bowler du Plessis turns to when the side needs a different angle at the top, or a wicket in the powerplay. His batting is a bonus — a left-hander who can hit a few at the end.

Support act to Mustafizur

Mustafizur Rahman is the Capitals' death bowler and the attack's leader, but even the best death bowler needs someone to set the game up. Wood's job is the first six overs: swing the new ball, take a wicket or two, and leave the middle-order batters facing scoreboard pressure. He will share the new ball with Mark Adair or Matthew Potts, and the three of them give du Plessis a pace attack with three different angles.

At $40,000 he is a mid-priced signing, which reflects his role: not the star, but the bowler the stars depend on. If Wood takes early wickets in Dubai, the Capitals' spinners bowl with runs on the board. If he goes for runs, the pressure moves to the middle overs.

The pace group behind the spinners

Dubai Capitals' attack is often described as spin-led, and Noor Ahmad and Aayan Afzal Khan deserve that billing, but the pace group matters just as much in Dubai. The pitches there are true and the outfield is fast, so new-ball swing is the pace bowlers' best weapon — and Wood is the only left-armer who provides it. His spells will decide whether the Capitals defend totals or chase them.
