The leg-spinner who learned from YouTube

Muhammad Zuhaib Zubair's story is the kind that makes the ILT20 matter. He learned leg-spin by watching videos of Shane Warne on YouTube, then refined it at the Sharjah Cricket Academy under former West Indies leg-spinner Devendra Bishoo. In the 2024 ILT20 he announced himself with 11 wickets for Gulf Giants — the highest tally in his side, ahead of seasoned internationals like Chris Jordan and Jamie Overton — including four wickets against Sharjah Warriorz in a 79-run win.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: Dubai Capitals
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Leg-break googly
  • Born: 31 December 2003

He made his international debut for the UAE soon after, playing his first ODI against Canada on 28 February 2024 and his first T20 international against Scotland on 11 March 2024. His bowling is leg-break with a googly, and his batting is right-handed and useful down the order. He is 22, he is a UAE international, and he is a leg-spinner — three facts that make him one of the most valuable local players in the league.

From the Giants to the champions

Zuhaib's move from Gulf Giants to Dubai Capitals is one of the quiet stories of the Season 5 auction. The Giants were the side he broke through with; the Capitals are the side that paid $26,000 to take him. For the champions, he is the third spinner in a group led by Noor Ahmad and Aayan Afzal Khan, and the one who offers a different problem: a leg-spinner's googly, where the other two bowl left-arm spin. That variety matters on Dubai's pitches, where batters face spin from both ends.

His likely role is as the change-up spinner — the bowler du Plessis turns to when the left-armers need a rest, or when a right-handed batter is struggling against the turning ball. Leg-spin is the hardest art to bowl and the hardest to face, and the Capitals have bought one of the league's best young exponents of it.

The spin twins, split up

For two seasons, Zuhaib and Aayan Afzal Khan were the UAE spin twins at Gulf Giants — the young pair who bowled together and won games together, taking six wickets for 38 runs between them in one match in 2024. Now they are together again, but in Dubai Capitals colours, after the Capitals signed them both. The reunion is one of the subplots of Season 5: the two bowlers who learned the league together, now bowling for the champions.
