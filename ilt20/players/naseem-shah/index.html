The fastest bowler in the league

Naseem is a right-arm fast bowler who bats right-handed, and he is one of the most exciting quicks in world cricket. He made his Test debut for Pakistan at 16 in 2019, and he has since become a regular in all three formats, with a Test hat-trick to his name.

His most famous innings with the bat came in the 2022 Asia Cup, when he hit two sixes off the last two balls to beat Afghanistan and send Pakistan to the final. With the ball, his pace and swing have troubled the best batters in the world, and his new-ball partnership with Shaheen Shah Afridi is one of the most feared in international cricket. The Vipers bought him at the Season 5 auction to lead their pace attack, and his job is to take wickets in the powerplay and set the tone for the champions' title defence.

Career so far

Naseem made his Pakistan Test debut in November 2019 against Australia, at 16, and took a hat-trick against Bangladesh in February 2020 — the youngest bowler in history to take a Test hat-trick. He has since played more than 20 Tests and 30 ODIs, and he has been a regular in Pakistan's white-ball sides.

He has played in the Pakistan Super League for Quetta Gladiators and Islamabad United, and in the ILT20 for the Vipers, where his pace has made him a fan favourite. His most famous moment with the bat — two sixes off Fazalhaq Farooqi to beat Afghanistan in the 2022 Asia Cup — is still talked about in Pakistan. At 23, he is entering his prime as a fast bowler — and the Vipers have signed him for the biggest season in their history.

The pace attack around him

The Vipers' pace attack is the strongest in the league. Naseem is the spearhead, David Payne is the left-arm swing bowler, Khuzaima Tanveer took 17 wickets in the title-winning Season 4 campaign, and Saleem Safi is a 140kph Afghan quick.

Naseem's role is to take the new ball, bowl fast, and take wickets in the powerplay. Six of the Vipers' ten league games are in Dubai, where pace has often decided matches, and a bowler who can bowl at 145kph and swing the new ball is worth his weight. If the Vipers defend their title, Naseem will be at the centre of it.
