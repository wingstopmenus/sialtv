The USA all-rounder in demand

Hassan Khan plays international cricket for the United States, where he has been one of the side's most reliable all-rounders since his debut. He has played franchise cricket in the Pakistan Super League and in North America's T20 leagues, and his game travels well: left-arm spin that is hard to score against on slow pitches, and batting that can rebuild an innings or finish one. His value is that he never has only one job.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: Dubai Capitals
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Slow left-arm orthodox

The $90,000 fee made him one of the Capitals' bigger auction buys outside the headline names, which tells you how the champions rate multi-skilled players. In a league where overseas slots are limited, an all-rounder who covers two disciplines is worth more than a specialist who covers one. Hassan Khan is that player for the Capitals.

Where he fits in the eleven

Hassan Khan's most likely role is the second spinner and lower-middle-order batter, competing with Aayan Afzal Khan for the local-spin overs and with Karim Janat for the all-round place. His left-arm spin is a different problem from Noor Ahmad's wrist-spin, which matters: du Plessis can pair them, or use Hassan Khan as the control bowler while Noor attacks from the other end. With the bat he is the insurance policy behind Stoinis, Shanaka and Powell.

He is also a strong fielder, and in T20 cricket that is not a footnote. The Capitals' outfield in Dubai is fast and the boundaries are short, so a fielder who saves ten runs is worth as much as a batter who scores them. Hassan Khan's all-round package is built for exactly that kind of ground.

Spin depth, and a different angle

Dubai Capitals' spin attack has two left-armers with very different methods: Noor Ahmad turns the ball both ways with wrist-spin, Hassan Khan and Aayan Afzal Khan bowl finger spin with control. That gives the captain options on every pitch — attack with Noor, hold with Hassan Khan, or bowl both together and squeeze the middle overs dry.
