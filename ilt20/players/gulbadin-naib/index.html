The senior Afghan all-rounder

Naib is a right-handed middle-order batter and right-arm medium-fast bowler who has been part of Afghanistan's rise from Associate cricket to the top table. He captained Afghanistan at the 2019 World Cup and was a key member of the side that reached the semi-finals of the 2024 T20 World Cup.

His T20 game is built on versatility: he can bat anywhere from five to eight, bowl in the powerplay or at the death, and field anywhere. The Knight Riders paid $75,000 for him at the Season 5 auction — the second-highest Afghan price in their squad — and he is one of four Afghans in the side, which matters with two required in every playing eleven. In a squad full of big names, Naib is the cricketer who does the jobs nobody else wants to do.

Career so far

Naib made his Afghanistan debut in 2011 and has played more than 100 T20Is, along with 80-plus ODIs. He captained his country at the 2019 World Cup in England, and his 2024 T20 World Cup was a career highlight: he took 4 for 20 against Australia in the Super Eight win that sent Afghanistan to the semi-finals.

He has played franchise cricket around the world — in the Bangladesh Premier League, the Lanka Premier League, the Caribbean Premier League and the ILT20 — and his all-round numbers in T20 cricket are consistently strong: over 2,000 runs and more than 100 wickets in the format. At 35, he is no longer the future of Afghan cricket; he is its memory, and the Knight Riders are buying that experience.

The all-round group around him

The Knight Riders are stacked with all-rounders. Jason Holder captains the side, Moeen Ali is one of the great T20 all-rounders of his generation, Sunil Narine opens the bowling and the batting, and Naib is the fourth member of that group.

That depth is the side's greatest strength — and its selection headache. Naib's edge is his seam bowling: on Abu Dhabi surfaces, where the Knight Riders play six of their ten league games, a medium-fast bowler who can swing the new ball and hit the deck at the death is worth his weight. If the Knight Riders need balance, Naib is the man who gives it to them.
