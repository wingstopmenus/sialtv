Season 5

Dominica's left-hander

Alick Athanaze comes from Dominica, a small island that has produced some of West Indies' most stylish batters, and his game fits that tradition: a high elbow, a still head, and drives through the off side that look effortless. He played for West Indies Under-19s, then broke into the senior side in all three formats, scoring a half-century on his one-day debut and showing the temperament to bat long when the situation demands it.

  • Role: Batter
  • Team: Gulf Giants
  • Batting: Left-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm off-break
  • Born: 17 December 1998

His T20 game is still developing, which is why the Giants could sign him for $10,000. But the talent is not in doubt: he has scored first-class hundreds, he has opened for West Indies, and he has the technique to play the moving ball as well as the short one. In a league full of power hitters, Athanaze is a different kind of batter — and that is exactly why the Giants wanted him.

Season 5

The left-handed option

The Giants' top order is stacked with right-handers: Buttler, Klaasen, Miller, Livingstone and Gurbaz all bat right-handed. Athanaze is the left-hander who breaks that up, and his role is to be the batter who forces opposition captains to change their plans. Left-right combinations at the crease make bowling and fielding harder, and in a short tournament those small edges matter.

His most likely place is as reserve batter and tactical pick — the player who comes in when the Giants want a left-hander against a particular attack, or when an injury opens a place. At $10,000 he is the cheapest overseas batter in the squad, which makes his selection an easy call when the team needs one.

Season 5

Batting depth without end

Gulf Giants' batting is the strongest in the league on paper, and Athanaze extends it one place further: even the reserve batters in this squad have international hundreds behind them. That depth is the Giants' identity — a side that does not stop batting until the tail, and that can chase any total because the next player in is as good as the last one out.
