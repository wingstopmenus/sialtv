Mumbai's all-rounder

Shubham Ranjane has been part of Mumbai's domestic set-up for years, scoring runs in the middle order and bowling medium pace in the Ranji Trophy and in India's white-ball competitions. His IPL stint with Rajasthan Royals in 2019 gave him a taste of franchise cricket, and he has played in the United States' Minor League Cricket since, where his all-round game has made him a regular. He is not a star — he is a professional, the kind of player who does his job without fuss and is valued by every captain he plays for.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: Gulf Giants
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium-fast

The Giants signed him for $10,000 as squad depth, and his value is his versatility: he can bat at six or seven, bowl two or three overs, and field anywhere. In a squad full of stars, the professionals who fill the gaps are the ones who make the team work.

The fourth all-rounder

Ranjane's role is to be the Giants' fourth all-rounder behind Omarzai, Livingstone and Wiese, and the player who gives Jos Buttler options when the stars need a rest. His most likely path into the eleven is as a replacement all-rounder if one of the overseas players is unavailable, or as a tactical pick on a pitch where an extra medium-pacer is worth more than a specialist. His medium pace is the key: it is a different problem from the Joseph brothers' speed, and on a slow Dubai pitch it can be harder to score against.

At $10,000 he is the cheapest overseas signing in the squad, which makes his selection an easy call when the team needs one. His Season 5 is about being ready — and about showing that a decade of domestic cricket counts for something at this level.

All-round depth behind the stars

Gulf Giants' all-round depth is what makes their batting work: Omarzai, Livingstone, Wiese, Campher and Ranjane can all bat and bowl, which means the side never has to choose between a batter and a bowler. Ranjane is the quietest of that group, and the one whose contribution is hardest to measure — a wicket here, a quick 20 there, a catch in the deep.
