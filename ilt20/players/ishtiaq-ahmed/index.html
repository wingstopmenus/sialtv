Saudi Arabia's spearhead

Ishtiaq — also listed as Ishtiaq Ahmad — is a right-arm medium-fast bowler who bats right-handed, and he is Saudi Arabia's most experienced international bowler. He has played more than 50 T20Is for his country, taking 74 wickets with a best of 5 for 27 against Kuwait.

His October 2026 was a career highlight. In Saudi Arabia's Sub-Regional Asia B Qualifier win for the 2028 T20 World Cup, he took seven wickets — among the top five wicket-takers in the tournament — including 4 for 25 against Hong Kong, for which he was named player of the match. The Vipers bought him at the Season 5 auction after a season with Gulf Giants, and he is one of six Saudi players in the league this season. At 33, he is the oldest bowler in the Vipers squad — and one of its most proven wicket-takers.

Career so far

Ishtiaq made his Saudi Arabia T20I debut in October 2021 against Malaysia and has been a fixture of the side ever since, playing in the Gulf T20I Championship, the Asian T20 Cup and the T20 World Cup qualifiers. His T20I record — 74 wickets in more than 50 matches at an average of 16.78 — is outstanding for an Associate bowler.

He played for Gulf Giants in ILT20 Season 4, where he learnt what franchise cricket demands, and his move to the Vipers for Season 5 puts him in the champions' squad. His best T20I figures, 5 for 27 against Kuwait, show he can win games on his own — and his qualifier performances in October 2026, including 4 for 25 against Hong Kong, show he is in the form of his life.

The pace attack around him

Few attacks in the league can match the Vipers for pace: Naseem is one of the fastest bowlers in the world, Payne swings the new ball, Tanveer took 17 wickets in the title season and Safi bowls at 140kph — and Ishtiaq's role is to be the experienced seamer who can bowl in the powerplay and at the death, and who knows how to win games for an Associate nation. He is unlikely to play every game — the champions' pace attack is settled — but T20 tournaments are long, and a bowler who has just taken seven wickets in a qualifier is never far from a game.
