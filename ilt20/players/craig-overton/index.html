The Somerset workhorse

Overton's game is built on reliability. He bowls a heavy length, gets bounce from his height, and takes wickets in English conditions year after year — more than 400 first-class wickets for Somerset, and a county championship winner's medal. He is the kind of bowler captains trust with the ball when the game is tight and the batters are set.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm fast-medium
  • Born: 10 April 1994, Barnstaple, England

With the bat he is a genuine lower-order hitter. He has scored first-class hundreds for Somerset and has won games from No. 8, which means the Warriorz batting does not end at seven. For a side built around spin, Overton is the seam option who can bowl long spells and then add 30 quick runs.

Tests for England, and a twin in the game

Overton made his England Test debut in 2017 and has played in the Ashes, taking wickets against Australia. His twin brother Jamie is also an England international and a fast bowler, and the two have played together for Somerset — one of the few sets of twins to have played first-class cricket together.

He has also played in The Hundred for Welsh Fire and in franchise cricket around the world. The ILT20 is his next stage, and at 32 he brings the experience of a player who has bowled in every situation the game can produce.
