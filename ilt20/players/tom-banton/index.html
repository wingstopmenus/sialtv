The opener who hits it clean

Banton's batting is built on timing and power. A right-hander who opens for Somerset, he has scored hundreds in the T20 Blast and in The Hundred, and his strike rate in franchise cricket is among the best of any English opener. He hits the ball cleanly down the ground and over the ropes, and he does it from the first over.

  • Role: Wicketkeeper
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm off break
  • Born: 11 November 1998, Chiltern, England

For the Warriorz he is the first-choice wicketkeeper and a top-order batter who can take the powerplay apart. With Vince at the other end, the opening pair has two right-handers who score quickly and put pressure on the bowlers from ball one — and Banton's keeping means the side does not need to carry a specialist gloveman.

From Somerset to England and back

Banton made his England debut in 2019 and has played ODIs and T20Is, including a hundred against Ireland in an ODI. He has played in the IPL for Kolkata Knight Riders, in the BBL for Brisbane Heat, and in the ILT20 before — including for MI Emirates, where he opened with Muhammad Waseem.

He has also had to rebuild his game after losing his England place, and his recent seasons for Somerset have been his best: big runs in the Blast, a Hundred hundred, and a recall to the England set-up. Season 5 is another chance to show why he was once called the most exciting young batter in England.
