A Jersey batter on the big stage

Tribe came through Jersey's age-group cricket and made his T20I debut for the island at 17. What sets him apart from most associate batters is the weight of runs behind him: a List A average near 60, an ODI hundred against Papua New Guinea, and a first-class double century — 206 for Glamorgan against Northamptonshire in September 2025, the first by a Jersey player in the County Championship.

  • Role: Batter
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm off break
  • Born: 29 March 2004, Jersey

For MI Emirates he is a top-order option who can bat through an innings rather than just hit through it. That matters in a squad full of power-hitters: Tribe is the player who can hold one end while Pooran and Bairstow attack from the other, and his off-breaks give Pooran an extra bowling option if a match needs one.

From St Helier to franchise cricket

Tribe's rise has been quick. He signed with Glamorgan, impressed for the England Lions — top-scoring with 64 not out against the Pakistan Shaheens in February 2026 — and was then drafted by the Paarl Royals for the SA20, where he opened the batting and made 51 against MI Cape Town. Welsh Fire signed him for The Hundred in March 2026.

The ILT20 is the next step in that climb. Franchise cricket keeps asking him the same question — can he do it against better bowling, on slower pitches, under lights — and so far he keeps answering yes. A strong Season 5 would put him firmly in the conversation for higher honours.
