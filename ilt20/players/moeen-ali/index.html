The World Cup winner

Moeen is a left-handed batter and right-arm off-break bowler who has won the World Cup in both white-ball formats with England — the 2019 ODI World Cup and the 2022 T20 World Cup — and he retired from international cricket in 2024 as one of England's most decorated cricketers.

His T20 value is in his versatility: he can open the batting, bat in the middle order, bowl in the powerplay or at the death, and field anywhere. He has played in the Indian Premier League for Chennai Super Kings, where he won the title in 2021 and 2023, and in the Big Bash, the CPL and the Pakistan Super League. The Knight Riders paid $80,000 for him at the Season 5 auction, and he is the most decorated player in their squad. He also played for Arabian Aces in the Abu Dhabi T10 in November 2026, so he arrives in the UAE already in match rhythm.

Career so far

Moeen made his England debut in 2014 and played 68 Tests, 138 ODIs and 92 T20Is, scoring more than 6,000 international runs and taking over 300 wickets. He was England's first-choice spinner in the 2019 World Cup win and a key member of the 2022 T20 World Cup triumph in Australia.

His franchise career has been just as successful: two IPL titles with Chennai Super Kings, where he was a regular in the side that won in 2021 and 2023, and stints in the Big Bash, the CPL and the PSL. His most famous innings include a 53-ball hundred for England against South Africa in 2017 and a match-winning 63 in the 2022 T20 World Cup semi-final. At 39, he is no longer an England regular — but he remains one of the most sought-after T20 all-rounders in the world.

The all-round group around him

No side in the league has more all-rounders than the Knight Riders. Holder captains it, Narine is a great of the format, Naib is a former international captain, Tromp is the youngster — and Moeen is the most decorated of them all.

His role is to bat in the middle order, bowl four overs of off-spin, and bring a winner's calm to a side that has never won this league. When the Knight Riders reached Qualifier 2 last season and fell short, it was experience they lacked at the finish; Moeen has finished more big games than anyone in the squad. If the Knight Riders are in a semi-final again, he is the man you want in the middle.
