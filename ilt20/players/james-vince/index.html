The captain who sets the tone

Vince's batting is easy on the eye and hard to bowl at. A right-hander with a full range of shots through the off side, he has scored thousands of runs for Hampshire as captain and opener, and his T20 game is built on timing rather than muscle — which is why he scores quickly without ever looking like he is trying.

  • Role: Batter
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium
  • Born: 14 March 1991, Cuckfield, England

As captain he is calm and clear. He has led Hampshire for years and the Warriorz since they were formed, and his job in Season 5 is the same as ever: score runs at the top, set the field with the spin attack in mind, and keep a squad full of stars pulling in the same direction. A first title for the Warriorz would be his as much as anyone's.

From the 2019 World Cup to the ILT20

Vince was part of the England squad at the 2019 ODI World Cup, and he has played Tests, ODIs and T20Is for his country. He has played in the IPL, the BBL — where he captained the Sydney Sixers to two titles — the PSL and the ILT20, and he is one of the most experienced franchise captains in the world.

His BBL record is the proof: two titles as captain of the Sixers, and a reputation as a leader who gets the best out of his players. The Warriorz have never won the ILT20; Vince has won franchise titles before, and that is exactly why they keep him as captain.
