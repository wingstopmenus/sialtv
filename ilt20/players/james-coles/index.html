The all-rounder who does both jobs properly

Coles is not a batter who bowls a bit, or a bowler who bats a bit. He has scored eight first-class hundreds for Sussex — including an unbeaten 224 against Glamorgan in June 2026 — and he bowls genuine left-arm spin that can open the bowling or tie down the middle overs. In T20 cricket he strikes at close to 150 and has taken wickets at under 27 apiece.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Slow left-arm orthodox
  • Born: 2 April 2004, Aylesbury, England

For MI Emirates that two-way value is gold in a league that limits overseas players. A batting all-rounder who can bowl four overs lets Pooran pick an extra specialist somewhere else, and Coles can bat anywhere from three to six depending on what the match needs.

The Hundred bidding war

Coles became one of the stories of the 2026 Hundred auction when London Spirit paid £390,000 for him — among the highest fees ever paid for an uncapped domestic player — after a bidding war with four other teams. Sussex head coach Paul Farbrace has called him a genuine all-rounder who wants to be in the thick of things.

The England call-up followed in June 2026, for the T20I series against India. He had already played for the England Lions and in the SA20 for Sunrisers Eastern Cape, where he hit a match-winning 45 to take them into the final. The ILT20 is his next franchise stage — and at 22, he is playing like a man in a hurry.
