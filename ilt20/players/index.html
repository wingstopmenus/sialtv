ILT20 Season 5 Players — All 131 Profiles & Teams

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Every player, team by team

SialTV lists all 131 players of ILT20 Season 5 — every squad member with a profile on this site, grouped by team. The season has not started yet, so there are no Season 5 runs or wickets to show: the roles beside each name come from the verified squad lists, and players whose role is not listed there are shown as squad members until they play. Six teams, 34 matches, three grounds — and the first ball is bowled in Dubai on Saturday 21 November 2026.

The squads are led by six very different captains: Babar Azam takes the Desert Vipers into the season as defending champions, Nicholas Pooran captains MI Emirates after winning the title in 2024, and James Vince leads Sharjah Warriorz in their chase for a first trophy. Every name below links to a full profile — role, batting and bowling style where verified, career context and the team’s fixtures.

Frequently asked questions

How many players are in ILT20 Season 5?

131 players have a profile on SialTV — 23 for Gulf Giants, and 22 each for Desert Vipers, Dubai Capitals, MI Emirates and Sharjah Warriorz, and 20 for Abu Dhabi Knight Riders.

When does ILT20 Season 5 start?

On Saturday 21 November 2026, with Desert Vipers against MI Emirates in Dubai in the evening session. The final is on Sunday 20 December 2026, also in Dubai.

Who captains each team?

Babar Azam (Desert Vipers), Faf du Plessis (Dubai Capitals), Jos Buttler (Gulf Giants), Jason Holder (Abu Dhabi Knight Riders), Nicholas Pooran (MI Emirates) and James Vince (Sharjah Warriorz).

Where are the full squads?

On each team page — verified Season 5 squads with roles where confirmed — and on the ILT20 Teams page, which lists all six sides.
