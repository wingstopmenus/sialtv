The leg-spinner who switched sides

Arab Gul is a right-arm leg-break bowler who bats right-handed, and his move from MI Emirates to the Knight Riders is one of the more interesting switches of the Season 5 auction. He played seven ILT20 matches for MI Emirates in Season 4, taking six wickets at an economy of 6.37 with a best of 2 for 19 against the Knight Riders themselves.

That economy rate matters. In a league where wrist-spin wins games, a 21-year-old leg-spinner who goes at barely over six an over is a genuine asset, and the Knight Riders paid $80,000 for him — the joint-second-highest Afghan price in their squad. With two Afghan players required in every playing eleven, and with Qais Ahmad and Abdullah Tarakhail also in the squad, Gul has to earn his spot; but his Season 4 record says he can.

Career so far

Gul — full name Arab Gul Momand — came through Afghanistan's Under-19 system, playing at the 2024 Under-19 World Cup, and has played domestic cricket for Band-e-Amir Region, Speen Ghar Tigers and Boost Defenders.

His international debut came in T20I cricket for Afghanistan, where he took 5 wickets in two matches including 4 for 8 against Japan. In the ILT20 he played for MI Emirates in Season 4, taking six wickets in seven games, and he was part of the Emirates side that reached the final against Desert Vipers. The Knight Riders' $80,000 bid at the Season 5 auction made him one of the most expensive Afghan players in the league — and a leg-spinner the champions will have to plan for when the two sides meet.

The spin group around him

Spin is where the Knight Riders can out-bowl anyone. Narine is the great mystery spinner, Qais is the established Afghan leg-spinner, and Tariq's off-spin won games for Desert Vipers last season — Gul's job is to be the second wrist-spinner — the one who bowls when Narine rests, or the one who partners Qais in the middle overs on a turning Abu Dhabi surface. Six of the Knight Riders' ten league games are in Abu Dhabi, where spin has often decided matches, and a leg-spinner who can also bat a little is exactly the profile this squad needs.
