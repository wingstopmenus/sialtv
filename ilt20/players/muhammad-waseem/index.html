The UAE captain who opens for his franchise

Waseem is the heartbeat of UAE cricket. He captains the national T20I side, he is the country's highest-ranked T20 batter, and for MI Emirates he does the job he does for his country: open the batting and set the tone. His ILT20 record for the franchise — 871 runs in 33 matches before Season 5, at a strike rate above 140 — is the record of a player who belongs at this level, not one who is making up numbers.

  • Role: Batter
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium
  • Born: 12 February 1996, Pakistan

He bats right-handed and scores all around the wicket, with a particular strength through the off side. In a squad full of overseas stars, Waseem is the local player the team is built around, and his 59 off 42 balls against Gulf Giants in the 2025-26 season showed he can anchor an innings as well as attack in it.

From debut to captaincy

Waseem was born in Pakistan and made his UAE T20I debut against Namibia in October 2021. He was appointed UAE captain in March 2023, and he has since scored three T20I hundreds and more than 3,300 T20I runs — numbers that put him among the most productive associate batters in the world. In January 2026 he was named captain of the UAE squad for the T20 World Cup.

For MI Emirates, his value goes beyond runs. He knows the UAE grounds better than any overseas player in the squad, he understands the pitches, and he is the link between the local players and the stars. When the season opener comes around in Dubai, he will be the player the home crowd is watching.

The MI Emirates around him

Waseem is the UAE's captain and MI Emirates' most reliable ILT20 batter, the opener who sets the tone and the man this squad is partly built around.

His 871 ILT20 runs in 33 matches are the most by any UAE player for the franchise, and this season he can push past 1,000. He has scored three T20I hundreds and more than 3,300 T20I runs since his UAE debut against Namibia in October 2021, and he was appointed UAE captain in March 2023.
