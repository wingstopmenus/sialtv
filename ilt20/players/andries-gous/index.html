The USA keeper-batter

Gous is a right-handed wicketkeeper-batter who was born in South Africa and now plays for the United States, and he was one of the stories of the 2024 T20 World Cup. His 80 not out against South Africa — his country of birth — in Antigua was one of the innings of the tournament, and it announced the USA as a serious cricket nation.

He was a direct signing for the Vipers before the Season 5 auction, alongside Dan Lawrence, Mujeeb Ur Rahman and Shimron Hetmyer, and he is the side's first-choice keeper. His job is to keep wicket for 20 overs and bat in the top six, where his power-hitting and his ability to play spin make him a dangerous middle-order batter. In a side full of stars, Gous is the keeper who holds it together.

Career so far

Gous played domestic cricket in South Africa for Free State and Knights before moving to the United States, where he became a regular in Major League Cricket for Washington Freedom. He made his USA debut in 2024 and was a key member of the side that reached the Super Eight of the T20 World Cup.

His 80 not out against South Africa in the Super Eight was the highlight, but his tournament as a whole was strong: he kept wicket cleanly, batted in the top order, and showed he could score against the best attacks in the world. He has since played for the Vipers in the ILT20, where his keeping and his batting have made him a regular in the side that won the title in Season 4.

The batting around him

Babar leads it, Fakhar and Hetmyer provide the power, Tector and Lawrence add the class — and Gous fits into that line-up as the keeper-batter who can bat anywhere from three to six.

His value is in his flexibility: if the top order fires, he can finish an innings; if it fails, he can rebuild it. And behind the stumps, he is the man the bowlers trust — Naseem Shah, Mujeeb Ur Rahman and Shadab Khan all need a keeper who can stand up to the stumps and take the chances that come. Gous is that keeper.
