The spinner who bowls to take wickets

Ghazanfar is not a containing spinner. He flights the ball, looks for drift and dip, and his stock delivery turns away from the right-hander sharply enough to beat the bat or find the edge. That is why MI Emirates want him in the side: on Dubai and Abu Dhabi surfaces that can grip, a spinner who attacks is worth more than one who merely survives.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm off break
  • Born: 20 March 2006, Afghanistan

His role is the middle-overs squeeze with teeth. Pooran can throw him the ball after the powerplay and ask for a breakthrough rather than four quiet overs, and if the top order has already been broken, Ghazanfar can bowl at batters who are new to the crease and unsure of the pace of the pitch.

Six wickets against Bangladesh

Ghazanfar's name travelled fast after his six-wicket haul against Bangladesh in an ODI in Sharjah in November 2024 — a performance that showed he could do it against a full international side, not just at age-group level. He had already been talked about in Afghanistan cricket as the next spin prospect after Rashid Khan, and that night made the talk real.

Since then he has been picked up by franchise sides around the world, including in the IPL, and he arrives in the ILT20 with a reputation to protect rather than to build. The league's batters will have watched the Bangladesh game; the challenge for Ghazanfar is to stay ahead of them with variations they have not seen yet.

The MI Emirates around him
