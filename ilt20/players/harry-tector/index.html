Ireland's Mr Consistent

Tector is a right-handed batter who bowls right-arm off-break, and he is Ireland's most reliable run-scorer. He has scored ODI hundreds against Bangladesh and Zimbabwe, and his average in the format is among the best of any Irish batter in history.

His game is built on timing and placement rather than power, which makes him the perfect foil for the Vipers' power-hitters. Where Fakhar Zaman and Shimron Hetmyer hit the ball hard, Tector scores through the gaps, rotates the strike, and builds an innings. The Vipers bought him at the Season 5 auction to bat in the middle order, and his job is to be the man who holds an innings together when the big names fall.

Career so far

Tector made his Ireland debut in 2019 and has since become the side's most consistent batter across formats. He has played more than 50 ODIs, scoring hundreds against Bangladesh and Zimbabwe, and he has been a regular in Ireland's T20I side.

He has played domestic cricket for Leinster Lightning and in the English county system, and his T20 game has grown steadily: he strikes at a healthy rate in the middle order and is a clean, reliable fielder. The ILT20 is his first major franchise league, and it is a natural step for a 26-year-old who has spent five years proving himself for Ireland. Note: Tector also has a player page in the Nepal Premier League section of this site — this page covers his ILT20 career with Desert Vipers only.

The batting around him

Babar at the top, Fakhar and Hetmyer for power, Lawrence for all-round depth — and Tector fits into that line-up as the middle-order anchor — the batter who can rebuild an innings or finish one, depending on what the game needs.

His value is in his consistency: when the Vipers lose early wickets, Tector is the man who steadies the ship; when they need 60 off the last five overs, he is the man who can score them. In a side full of stars, he is the quiet professional who makes the whole thing work.
