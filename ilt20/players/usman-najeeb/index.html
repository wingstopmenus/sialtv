A squad place earned, twice

Usman Najeeb was part of the Dubai Capitals set-up in the 2025-26 season, and the Capitals used their right-to-match card to keep him for Season 5 at the 1 October 2026 auction. That is the key fact about him: franchises do not spend right-to-match cards on players they do not rate. The card is used on the players a side wants to keep, and the Capitals chose to keep him.

What a right-to-match card means

The right-to-match card lets a franchise match the highest bid for a player from its previous squad and keep him. It is a limited resource — sides use it only on players they genuinely want — and the Capitals used one of theirs on Usman Najeeb at $10,000. That tells you more than any profile could: the coaches and the captain have seen him in training and in the development competition, and they want him around for another season.

His path into the eleven is the same as every squad player's: be ready, train well, and take the chance when an injury or a tactical change opens a place. Dubai Capitals play seven of their ten league matches at home, so any debut is likely to come in Dubai, on pitches he knows from training.

The squad players who decide seasons

A season of ten matches in 24 days is decided by the whole squad, not just the eleven. Players get injured, form dips, and the sides that stay in the top four are the ones whose 15th or 18th player can step in without the side falling apart. Usman Najeeb is part of that depth for the champions — the group behind the stars that keeps the squad functioning through a long month.
