Kuwait's record-breaking opener

Sandaruwan is a right-handed opening batter who bowls right-arm off-break on occasion, and his T20I record for Kuwait is remarkable: more than 2,000 runs in 80 matches, two hundreds, 12 fifties, and a strike rate of 145.82.

He was born in Colombo, played domestic cricket in Sri Lanka for Saracens Sports Club, and moved to Kuwait for work — where he became the national side's most important batter. His T20I debut in January 2019, against Maldives, brought a match-winning 68 not out, and three days later he scored 103 off 59 balls against Bahrain. The Knight Riders bought him for $10,000 at the Season 5 auction, and he is one of six Kuwait players in the league this season. At 34, he is the oldest player in the Knight Riders squad — and one of its most proven run-scorers.

Career so far

Sandaruwan made his List A debut for Saracens in December 2012 and his first-class debut two months later, before moving to Kuwait. He made his Kuwait T20I debut in January 2019 and has been a fixture of the side ever since, playing in the 2018-19 ICC World Twenty20 Asia Qualifier and in the Sub-Regional Asia A Qualifier for the 2028 T20 World Cup in October 2026.

His T20 numbers are the story: 2,094 runs in 80 T20Is at an average of 27.19, with two hundreds and 12 fifties, and a strike rate that has stayed above 145 across his career. He has also scored more than 1,000 runs in T10 cricket at a strike rate of 189. The ILT20 is his first major franchise league, and it is a reward for seven years of carrying Kuwait's batting.

Season 5

The batting around him

Allen and Munro will take the new ball for the Knight Riders, with Williamson at three and Fletcher and Pepper behind them — and Sandaruwan's place in that line-up is as the backup opener — the man who steps in if Allen or Munro is rested or injured, and the batter who knows how to score quickly on UAE grounds. His T10 record, more than 1,000 runs at a strike rate of 189, says he can score fast enough for any format. If the Knight Riders need a replacement opener, they have one of the most experienced T20I openers in Associate cricket waiting.
