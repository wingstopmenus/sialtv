The breakout star of the Knight Riders family

De Silva is a right-arm medium-fast bowler who bats right-handed and fields brilliantly, and his story is one of the best in this season's squad. Born in Galle, he played for Sri Lanka Under-19s at the 2020 World Cup and for Sri Lanka A, then moved to the United States in 2023 to join his family — giving up hopes of a Sri Lanka cap.

Three years later he was the breakout star of Major League Cricket 2026 for Texas Super Kings, taking 13 wickets in ten innings at an economy of 8.70, including 4 for 28 against San Francisco Unicorns. That form caught the eye of Dwayne Bravo, the Knight Riders' head coach, who took him to Trinbago Knight Riders for the CPL — and then to Abu Dhabi for $100,000. He can bowl at 140kph with a slightly slingy action, and he is exactly the kind of athletic, multi-skilled cricketer modern T20 sides want.

Career so far

De Silva made his List A debut for Colombo Cricket Club in October 2021 and his T20 debut on Sri Lanka's tour of England in May 2022, against Surrey. He was named in the Sri Lanka A squad for the Australia A series that June.

The move to the USA reset his career. After local tournaments and the Minor League, his MLC debut for Texas Super Kings in 2026 turned into a breakthrough season: 13 wickets in ten games, a best of 4 for 28, and a reputation as a new-ball bowler who hits the deck hard. Trinbago Knight Riders signed him for the 2026 CPL, where he played alongside the Knight Riders family he now joins in Abu Dhabi. He has also been named in the Arabian Aces squad for the Abu Dhabi T10 in November 2026 — a busy month that ends with his ILT20 debut.

The attack around him

The Knight Riders' seam attack has experience at the top and youth behind it. Jason Holder and Gulbadin Naib are the senior all-rounders, Sunil Narine and Qais Ahmad control the middle with spin, and de Silva joins Jakeem Pollard as the young quicks pushing for games.

De Silva's edge over the other young seamers is that he has already done it in a franchise league — MLC 2026 was not a promise, it was 13 wickets. If he takes the new ball in Abu Dhabi, where the Knight Riders play six of their ten league games, his pace and bounce could make him one of the finds of Season 5.
