His first ILT20 season

Babar is a right-handed batter who bowls right-arm off-break, and he joins the Vipers for Season 5. He was bought at the Season 5 auction for the champions' title defence, and his arrival is the headline signing of the whole league.

His record is extraordinary: more than 4,000 T20I runs for Pakistan, a former world number one in both ODI and T20I batting, and a batter who has scored hundreds in every format. He captained Pakistan to the 2022 T20 World Cup final and the 2021 semi-final, and he has played in the Pakistan Super League for Karachi Kings and Peshawar Zalmi, where he is the league's all-time leading run-scorer. In the ILT20, his job is to lead the champions — and to score the runs that win them a second straight title.

Career so far

Babar made his Pakistan debut in 2015 and has since become one of the most consistent batters in world cricket. He has scored 19 ODI hundreds, three T20I hundreds, and nine Test hundreds, and he was the fastest batter in history to 5,000 ODI runs.

He captained Pakistan in all three formats from 2019 to 2024, leading the side to the 2022 T20 World Cup final at the MCG, and he has been a regular in franchise leagues around the world — the PSL, the BBL, the CPL and now the ILT20. His T20 game is built on timing and placement rather than power, and his cover drive is one of the most beautiful shots in the game. At 32, he is at the peak of his powers — and the Vipers have signed him for the biggest season in their history.

The side he joins

Babar's squad is the strongest in the league. Fakhar Zaman and Shimron Hetmyer provide the power at the top, Harry Tector adds class in the middle order, Shadab Khan and Mujeeb Ur Rahman run the spin attack, and Naseem Shah leads the pace attack.

What the side needs from Babar is runs — and plenty of them. The Vipers won the title in Season 4 under Sam Curran's captaincy, beating MI Emirates by 46 runs in the final, and Babar's job is to help defend that title. Six of the ten league games are in Dubai, where the Vipers are at home, and the opener against MI Emirates on 21 November is a repeat of the final. If Babar scores runs, the champions will be hard to stop.
