The all-rounder who bet on Dubai

Pahal is a right-handed batter and right-arm medium bowler who plays as a bowling all-rounder, and his story is one of the best in the Vipers squad. He spent eight years in and out of Haryana's Ranji Trophy side and was a net bowler for Rajasthan Royals in the IPL — then, when the opportunities dried up at home, he followed a friend's advice and moved to Dubai to play cricket.

Eighteen months later he was part of the Vipers' ILT20 title-winning squad. He earned his place through the development tournament, where he was named player of the competition with 157 runs and 12 wickets, and the Vipers paid his $10,000 base price at the first ILT20 auction. He appeared twice in the main tournament, took a catch in the final win over MI Emirates, and in September 2026 he took 4 for 26 in the development semi-final against Sharjah Warriorz to send the Vipers to a second straight development final.

Career so far

Pahal played eight years of first-class cricket for Haryana, where he was a regular in the Ranji Trophy side, and he was a net bowler for Rajasthan Royals in the IPL. His move to Dubai in 2025 changed his career: he landed a place in the Vipers' development side, was named player of the tournament, and earned a contract with the main squad.

His development-tournament numbers tell the story: 157 runs and 12 wickets in the first tournament, and 4 for 26 in the 2026 semi-final against Sharjah Warriorz. He has also played in the Emirates D10 and D20 competitions, scoring 99 runs in the D10 in January 2026. At 33, he is the oldest player in the Vipers squad — and one of its most inspiring stories.

The all-round group around him

The Vipers' all-round depth is the envy of the league. Shadab Khan is Pakistan's premier leg-spinning all-rounder, and Nilansh Keswani is the young UAE left-armer.

Pahal's role is to be the experienced all-rounder who can bat in the lower order, bowl four overs of medium pace, and field brilliantly. He is unlikely to play every game — the champions' side is settled — but T20 tournaments are long, and a 33-year-old who has already won a title with this club is never far from a game.
