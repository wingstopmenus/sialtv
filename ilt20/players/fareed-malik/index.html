Left-arm pace, and a different angle

Fareed's value starts with his angle. A left-armer coming over the wicket to a right-hander brings the ball back in and takes it across the left-hander — two problems from one action — and his pace is genuine. He bowls with the new ball and at the death, and his yorker is a weapon in the last overs.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Left-handed
  • Bowling: Left-arm fast-medium
  • Born: 10 August 1994, Nangarhar, Afghanistan

For the Warriorz he is the left-arm option in a pace attack that also has Milne's speed and Gleeson's experience. Left-arm pace changes the geometry of an innings: batters have to adjust their lines, and that adjustment is where wickets come from.

Afghanistan in all three formats

Fareed made his Afghanistan debut in 2018 and has played Tests, ODIs and T20Is, taking wickets in all three formats. He has played in the BBL for Melbourne Stars, in the PSL and in the ILT20 before, so the franchise circuit is familiar ground — and he knows the UAE's pitches from Afghanistan's many games there.

He comes from Nangarhar, the province that has produced so many of Afghanistan's fast bowlers, and he is part of the generation that took Afghan cricket from associate status to a Test nation. Season 5 is another chapter in that story.

The Sharjah Warriorz around him

Fareed is the left-arm angle in the pace attack: Milne is fast, Gleeson is clever, and Fareed brings the line neither of them has.

The Afghan made his international debut in 2018 and has played Tests, ODIs and T20Is, as well as the BBL for Melbourne Stars, the PSL and the ILT20 before. The angle does the work for him, one action and two problems for the batter, and it comes with genuine pace rather than just swing.

Frequently asked questions

What is Fareed Malik's bowling style?

Left-arm fast-medium. He swings the new ball and bowls yorkers at the death for Afghanistan and Sharjah Warriorz.

Has Fareed Malik played Test cricket?

Yes — he has played for Afghanistan in Tests, ODIs and T20Is since his debut in 2018.

Is Fareed Malik the same player as Fareed Ahmad?

Yes — he is also known as Fareed Ahmad. Both names refer to the same Afghanistan fast bowler.
