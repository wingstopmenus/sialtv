Season 5

A development-pathway bowler

Anudeep Chenthamara is listed as a bowler, and his route to the Capitals squad ran through the ILT20 Development Tournament, where the six franchises run development sides and the best performers earn senior contracts. He was part of that system before Season 5, and the Capitals' decision to sign him again at the auction is the clearest sign of what the pathway is for: players who prove themselves in the development competition get a second look.

What is certain is the opportunity: a place in the champions' 22-man squad, training every day alongside Mustafizur Rahman, Noor Ahmad and Mark Adair, and a season in which a single good performance in the nets or a debut can change a career.

Season 5

What Season 5 can do for him

For a development bowler, the value of a season is not only minutes on the field. Anudeep spends the tournament working with the Capitals' overseas pace and spin attack, learning how Mustafizur plans a death over and how Noor Ahmad reads a batter, and waiting for the match where the champions need a fresh bowler. Dubai Capitals play seven of their ten league matches at home in Dubai, so any chance he gets is likely to come on pitches he knows.

His route into the eleven runs through the bowling group: if the Capitals rest a seamer on a turning pitch, or if an injury opens a place, the development bowlers are first in line. The Capitals have shown before that they trust the pathway — Anudeep's continued presence in the squad is the evidence.

Season 5

The local depth behind the overseas attack

Dubai Capitals' attack is built on overseas names, but the league's rules mean the UAE players in the squad matter just as much: every eleven needs its local core. Anudeep sits in that group with Aayan Afzal Khan, Zuhaib Zubair and Haider Ali, the bowlers who give the champions cover across spin and seam. It is a competitive group, and places in it are earned in training as much as in matches.
