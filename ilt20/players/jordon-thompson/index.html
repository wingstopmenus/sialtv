The all-rounder who never goes missing

Thompson's game is built on effort. He bowls hard into the pitch, hits the bat hard in the lower middle order, and fields like every run matters — because to him it does. He has more than 140 T20 wickets and over 1,300 T20 runs, and his best T20 moments have come when Yorkshire needed someone to hold their nerve: a brilliant 20th over against Surrey in the 2022 Blast quarter-final, then an 18-ball fifty at Finals Day.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Left-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium-fast
  • Born: 9 October 1996, Leeds, England

For MI Emirates he is the seam-bowling all-rounder who links the attack together. He can bowl in the powerplay, in the middle or at the death, and he can bat at seven or eight and win a game from there. That flexibility is why he keeps getting picked.

Champion in 2024, new county in 2026

Thompson was part of the MI Emirates side that won the ILT20 in 2024, so he knows what a title-winning dressing room in this league feels like. In June 2025 he agreed a three-year deal with Warwickshire, leaving Yorkshire — the club he joined as a nine-year-old — at the end of the 2025 season, a move he described as the main contract of his career.

He also played in the BBL for Hobart Hurricanes, in the PSL for Karachi Kings and in The Hundred for four different sides. The ILT20 is a familiar stage for him now, and Season 5 is a chance to win it for a second time with the same franchise.

The MI Emirates around him

Thompson is one of the few players in this squad who knows exactly what the last week of this tournament feels like, because he won the ILT20 with MI Emirates in 2024.

The Yorkshire all-rounder bats left-handed, bowls right-arm medium-fast, and has more than 140 T20 wickets and over 1,300 T20 runs behind him. He gives the side effort in every department: hard lengths with the ball, hard hitting low down the order, and fielding that treats every run as if it matters.

Frequently asked questions

Is it Jordon or Jordan Thompson?

The ILT20 squad list spells it Jordon, but his registered name is Jordan Thompson. This profile uses the squad-list spelling for its title and slug, as the other pages do.

Did Jordon Thompson win the ILT20 before?

Yes — with MI Emirates in 2024. He was part of the title-winning side and returns to the same franchise for Season 5.

Which county does Jordon Thompson play for now?

Warwickshire, after signing a three-year deal in June 2025 to leave Yorkshire, his boyhood club.
