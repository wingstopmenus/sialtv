The international keeper

Tucker is a right-handed wicketkeeper-batter from Dublin, and he is Ireland's established first-choice behind the stumps. He scored a Test hundred against Bangladesh in 2023 — 108 in Mirpur — and has kept wicket for Ireland in all three formats.

His ILT20 move is a first: the Knight Riders bought him for $10,000 at the Season 5 auction, and he arrives as the most internationally proven keeper in the squad. Fletcher has the World Cup medal, Pepper has the youth and the power, but Tucker has the combination — a keeper who bats in the top six for his country and has done it against Test attacks. If the Knight Riders want a keeper-batter who can bat at five and keep for 20 overs, Tucker is the pick.

Career so far

Tucker made his Ireland debut in 2016 and has played more than 50 T20Is, along with ODIs and Tests. His Test hundred against Bangladesh in April 2023 — 108 off 162 balls in Mirpur — was only the second Test hundred by an Irish batter.

He has played domestic cricket for Leinster Lightning and in the English county system, and his T20 game has grown steadily: he strikes at a healthy rate in the middle order and is a clean, reliable keeper. The ILT20 is his first major franchise league, and it is a natural step for a 30-year-old international keeper who has spent a decade proving himself for Ireland.

Season 5

The keeping group around him

Three keepers are fighting for one spot, and Tucker is the international regular of the three — Fletcher has the World Cup medal, Pepper has the powerplay game.

Tucker's edge is his all-round game: he keeps tidily, bats in the top six for Ireland, and has a Test hundred to his name. If the Knight Riders pick their keeper on batting alone, Tucker and Pepper are the two in the frame; if they pick on experience, it is Tucker or Fletcher. Either way, the side will not be short of options behind the stumps.
