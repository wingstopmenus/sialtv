The spinner every batter fears

Rashid's genius is his speed through the air. He bowls leg-breaks and googlies at a pace that gives batters almost no time to read him, and his record — more than 600 T20 wickets, the most by any bowler in the format's history — is built on that one fact: even the best players in the world struggle to pick him.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm leg break
  • Born: 20 September 1998, Afghanistan

For MI Emirates he is the middle-overs weapon. Pooran can bowl him at any stage — in the powerplay, through the middle, or at the death — and he will take wickets. He is also a dangerous lower-order hitter who has won games with the bat, and one of the best fielders in the side.

From Nangarhar to the top of the world

Rashid made his Afghanistan debut as a teenager in 2015 and within two years was the No. 1 ranked T20I bowler in the world. He has played in the IPL for Sunrisers Hyderabad and Gujarat Titans, in the BBL for Adelaide Strikers, in The Hundred and the PSL, and he has captained Afghanistan in all three formats.

He is also the player Afghan cricket is built around: the first global superstar the country produced, and the reason a generation of Afghan children took up leg-spin. MI Emirates have had him before, and bringing him back for Season 5 is the clearest sign of their intent — they want the best spinner in the world bowling for them, not against them.

Frequently asked questions

How many T20 wickets does Rashid Khan have?

More than 600 — the most by any bowler in T20 history. He has also been ranked the No. 1 T20I bowler in the world.

What is Rashid Khan's bowling style?

Right-arm leg break, bowled unusually quickly through the air, with a googly that is almost impossible to pick.

Has Rashid Khan captained Afghanistan?

Yes — he has captained Afghanistan in Tests, ODIs and T20Is, and he remains the face of Afghan cricket around the world.
