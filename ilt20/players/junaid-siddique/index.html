The UAE seamer who knows Sharjah

Siddique's value is local knowledge. He has bowled on the UAE's pitches for the national side for years — in ODIs and T20Is, in World Cup qualifiers and at the 2022 T20 World Cup — and he understands how Sharjah's surface behaves: slow, low, and kinder to bowlers who hit the pitch hard and vary their pace than to those who search for swing.

  • Role: Bowler
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium-fast
  • Born: 6 December 1992, Multan, Pakistan

He bowls right-arm medium-fast, hits a consistent length, and has a good slower ball. For the Warriorz he is the UAE bowler who can hold an end while the overseas stars attack from the other, and with five of their ten league games at home in Sharjah, his knowledge of the ground is a real asset.

From Multan to the UAE side

Siddique was born in Multan in Pakistan and made his UAE debut in 2019. He has since become a regular in the national side, playing in the 2022 T20 World Cup in Australia and in a string of qualifiers, and he has been part of ILT20 squads in earlier seasons.

In a squad that leans heavily on spin, Siddique is the local seamer who makes the balance work. He will not always make the XI — the competition for pace places is fierce — but when the conditions suit seam or when a game needs a steady hand, he is the man Vince turns to.
