Zimbabwe's match-winner

Raza's 2022 was one of the great individual years in T20 cricket. He scored runs and took wickets at the T20 World Cup in Australia — including a match-winning spell against Pakistan — and he was named in the ICC's T20I team of the year. He bats in the middle order with power and timing, and his off-spin is a wicket-taking option, not a containing one.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm off break
  • Born: 24 April 1986, Sialkot, Pakistan

For the Warriorz he is the all-rounder who can win a game in either discipline. He can come in at four or five and score a fifty, or bowl four overs in the middle and take two wickets, or do both on the same night — which he has done for Zimbabwe more times than any other player in their history.

From Sialkot to Harare to the franchise circuit

Raza was born in Sialkot in Pakistan and moved to Zimbabwe, where he studied and came through the domestic game before making his international debut in 2013. He has played in the IPL for Punjab Kings, in the PSL for Lahore Qalandars, in the BBL and the ILT20, and he has captained Zimbabwe in white-ball cricket.

He is also Zimbabwe's most capped T20I player and their most reliable performer in big games. Season 5 is another stage for a player who has made a career out of carrying his team — and who will be expected to do the same for the Warriorz.
