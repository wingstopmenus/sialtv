The Guyanese power-hitter

Hetmyer is a left-handed batter from Guyana who hits the ball as hard as anyone in world cricket, and he was a direct signing for the Vipers before the Season 5 auction. He is one of three players the champions kept without going to auction, alongside Andries Gous and Mujeeb Ur Rahman.

His most famous achievement came in 2016, when he captained West Indies to the Under-19 World Cup title in Bangladesh, beating India in the final. He has since become one of the most destructive middle-order batters in T20 cricket, with a strike rate above 140 in the format and a reputation for clearing the ropes at will. The Vipers kept him for the title defence because his power-hitting wins games — and because he knows what it takes to win this league.

Career so far

Hetmyer made his West Indies debut in 2017 and has played more than 50 T20Is, scoring a hundred against England in 2019. He has played in the Indian Premier League for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals and Rajasthan Royals, where he has been a regular in the side that reached the 2024 final.

His franchise career has taken him to the CPL for Guyana Amazon Warriors, the BBL for Sydney Sixers, and the ILT20 for the Vipers, where he was part of the title-winning Season 4 squad — though injury curtailed his time with the side. His most famous innings include a 139 off 106 balls for West Indies against India in 2019. At 29, he is one of the most feared finishers in T20 cricket — and the Vipers have kept him for the title defence.

The batting around him

Babar and Fakhar open it, Tector adds the class — and Hetmyer fits into that line-up as the finisher — the left-handed batter who can come in at five or six and score 50 off 20 balls.

His value is in his power: when the Vipers need 60 off the last five overs, Hetmyer is the man who can score them. His injury in Season 4 meant he did not play as much as the side would have liked, but his return for Season 5 — as a direct signing — says everything about how the franchise rates him.
