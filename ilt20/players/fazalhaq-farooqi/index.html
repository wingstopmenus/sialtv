The new-ball threat

Farooqi's job is the first two overs. A left-armer who can swing the new ball back into the right-hander and away from the left-hander, he attacks the stumps from the start, and his early wickets change the shape of an innings: a side that loses two in the powerplay cannot set the pace it planned.

  • Role: Bowler

He is also a bowler for the big games. His T20 World Cup performances for Afghanistan — including a five-wicket haul against Uganda in the 2024 tournament — showed he does not shrink when the stage grows, and MI Emirates will want that same nerve in the opener against the defending champions.

From Baghlan to the IPL

Farooqi comes from Baghlan in Afghanistan and broke into the national side as a teenager. He has since played in the IPL for Sunrisers Hyderabad, in the BBL and in the ILT20, and his T20I record for Afghanistan is built on early wickets rather than containment — exactly the currency a franchise values most.

His left-handed batting is a bonus, not a plan: he bats low and swings hard. The real value is the ball in his left hand at the start of the innings, and MI Emirates have built their attack around giving him that ball as often as the match allows.
