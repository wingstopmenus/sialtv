Season 5

The UAE's next wicketkeeper

Suri is an opening batter and wicketkeeper who has come through the UAE's age-group system and into the senior side faster than almost anyone. He made his T20I debut against Afghanistan in December 2023 at 18, his ODI debut against Canada in February 2024, and he has scored runs in both formats — including 78 against the USA in ODIs and 77 against Kuwait in T20Is.

  • Role: Wicketkeeper
  • Team: Sharjah Warriorz
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Born: 4 June 2005

For the Warriorz he is the third wicketkeeper in the squad alongside Banton and Rew, and the local one. That matters: he knows the Sharjah ground, he knows the conditions, and he is the player the home crowd will be watching when he gets his chance.

Season 5

From the Under-19 Asia Cup to the ILT20

Suri's breakthrough came at the Under-19 Asia Cup in December 2023, where he was the UAE's joint top-scorer with 163 runs in five matches — including 75 against Sri Lanka — as the UAE beat Pakistan and Sri Lanka on their way to the final. He was then drafted by the Desert Vipers for the ILT20, and for Season 5 he moves to the Warriorz.

His younger brother Uddish is also a rising UAE cricketer, and the two dream of playing together for their country. For now, Tanish's job is to keep improving: open the batting for the UAE, keep wicket cleanly, and use his ILT20 season to learn from Banton and Rew.

The Sharjah Warriorz around him

Suri is the UAE's brightest young wicketkeeper-batter: a right-handed opener who made his senior international debut at 18.

His breakthrough came at the Under-19 Asia Cup in December 2023, where he was the UAE's joint top-scorer with 163 runs in five matches, including 75 against Sri Lanka. He made his T20I debut against Afghanistan in December 2023 at 18, his ODI debut against Canada in February 2024, and he was signed by the Warriorz for $10,000 at the Season 5 auction.
