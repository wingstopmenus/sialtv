Season 5

A development pacer earns his place

Haider Rarraq's route to the Gulf Giants squad ran through the ILT20 Development Tournament, where he was one of the standout UAE pacers — a young quick who impressed enough to earn a $50,000 deal with the Giants for the previous season, and then a new $10,000 contract for Season 5. The development competition is the league's pathway for UAE-based players, and Rarraq is one of its success stories: a bowler who turned development performances into a senior contract, twice.

Little about his game is published beyond the squad and auction lists, and his batting and bowling styles are not confirmed in the records available — so this page does not state them. What is certain is the opportunity: a place in a 23-man squad, training alongside Alzarri Joseph and Shamar Joseph, and a season in which a single good performance can change a career.

Season 5

What Season 5 can do for him

For a young pace bowler, the value of a season is not only minutes on the field. Rarraq spends the tournament working with the Giants' overseas attack, learning how the Joseph brothers plan a new-ball spell and how Blessing Muzarabani uses his height, and waiting for the match where the Giants need a fresh seamer. With six of the Giants' ten league matches in Dubai, any chance he gets is likely to come on pitches he already knows.

His route into the eleven runs through the pace group: if the Giants rest a seamer in a packed schedule, or if an injury opens a place, the development bowlers are first in line. The franchise has shown it trusts the pathway — Rarraq's continued presence in the squad is the evidence.

Season 5

The local pace behind the Joseph brothers

Gulf Giants' pace attack is built on the Joseph brothers, but the league's rules mean the UAE bowlers in the squad matter just as much: every eleven needs its local core. Rarraq sits in that group with Matiullah Khan and Awais Ahmad, the seamers who give the Giants cover when the overseas quicks need a rest. It is a competitive group, and the bowlers in it earn their places in the nets.

Frequently asked questions

Is Haider Rarraq the same player as Haider Razzaq?

The names refer to the same UAE pace bowler; some reports spell it Haider Razzaq. The Season 5 squad list spells it Haider Rarraq, and this page follows that spelling.

How did Haider Rarraq reach Gulf Giants?

Through the ILT20 Development Tournament, where he was a standout UAE pacer. He was signed to the senior squad for the previous season and again for Season 5 at the auction for $10,000.

When is Gulf Giants' first match?

Match 2 against Sharjah Warriorz on Sunday 22 November 2026, in the afternoon session, in Sharjah.
