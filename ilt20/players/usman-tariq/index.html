The off-spinner with the unusual action

Tariq is a right-arm off-break bowler who bats right-handed, and his action — a side-arm delivery with a distinctive pause — has made him one of the most discussed bowlers in the Pakistan Super League, where he plays for Quetta Gladiators.

His ILT20 record is strong. He played for Desert Vipers in the title-winning Season 4 campaign, taking wickets in the middle overs, and his off-spin was good enough to hold a place in a championship side. The Knight Riders paid $80,000 for him at the Season 5 auction, and he is the kind of bowler who wins games in the UAE: an off-spinner who bowls in the powerplay, takes wickets in the middle overs, and goes at barely six an over. In a squad that already has Sunil Narine and Qais Ahmad, Tariq gives the captain a third spin option — and a different one.

Career so far

Tariq came through Pakistan's domestic system and made his name in the PSL with Quetta Gladiators, where his unusual action and his ability to bowl in the powerplay made him a regular. He has also played in the ILT20 for Desert Vipers.

His most famous performance came in the PSL, where his side-arm off-spin troubled some of the best batters in the world, and his ILT20 record has been just as strong: he dismissed Sanjay Krishnamurthi for Desert Vipers against MI Emirates in December 2025, in a one-run thriller, and he was part of the Vipers' title-winning Season 4 squad. At 29, he is entering his prime as a T20 spinner — and the Knight Riders have signed him at the right time.

The spin group around him

Narine, Qais and Gul give the Knight Riders mystery, leg-spin and youth — and Tariq is the off-spinner who completes the set.

His role is to bowl in the powerplay and the middle overs, take wickets with his variations, and give the captain control when the fast bowlers need a rest. Six of the Knight Riders' ten league games are in Abu Dhabi, where spin has often decided matches, and a side with four frontline spinners is a side that can win on any surface.
