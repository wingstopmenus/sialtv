The Champions Trophy hero

Azmatullah Omarzai's performance against England in the 2025 Champions Trophy — 5 for 58 with the ball and 67 with the bat — was the innings that made the cricket world learn his name. It was not a one-off: he had already been Afghanistan's most improved player for two years, a seam-bowling all-rounder who bats in the top six and bowls at the death, and the Champions Trophy simply showed it on the biggest stage. He was named the ICC's ODI Cricketer of the Year for 2024, and he has been a regular in franchise leagues since.

  • Role: All-rounder
  • Team: Gulf Giants
  • Batting: Right-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium-fast
  • Born: 24 March 2000

The Giants kept Omarzai before the auction rather than risk losing him to another side — retention is a franchise saying, in the plainest way, that it needs the player. In a squad full of batting stars, Omarzai is the player who does the jobs nobody else does — the death overs, the rebuilding innings, the fielding in the deep.

The all-rounder who covers everything

Omarzai's role is to be the Giants' most versatile player: he bats at five or six, bowls three or four overs of medium pace, and fields anywhere. His bowling is the key — he can take the new ball, bowl in the middle, or close the innings, which gives Jos Buttler a bowler he can use in any phase. His batting is the bonus: a clean hitter who can rebuild an innings or finish one, as he showed against England.

In a squad where the batting stars take the headlines, Omarzai is the player the captain trusts most. He is the one Buttler will turn to when the game needs managing rather than hitting, and the one who will bowl the over that decides a close match.

The balance the batting stars need

Gulf Giants' batting is the best in the league on paper, but batting alone does not win tournaments — the side needs bowlers who can defend totals and all-rounders who can fill the gaps. Omarzai is the player who makes the balance work: his four overs mean the Giants can play an extra batter, and his batting means they never run out of hitters. Without him, the side would be a batter short or a bowler short; with him, it is neither.

Was Azmatullah Omarzai retained by Gulf Giants?

What did Azmatullah Omarzai do in the 2025 Champions Trophy?
