The left-hander who attacks from ball one

Perera bats like there is no tomorrow. A left-hander with a full range of shots square of the wicket, he has always scored quickly at the top of the order for Sri Lanka, and his best innings have a habit of becoming the story of the match. His most famous remains the unbeaten 153 against South Africa at Durban in 2019, one of the great Test innings — but his T20 game is built on the same fearlessness.

  • Role: Wicketkeeper
  • Team: MI Emirates
  • Batting: Left-handed
  • Bowling: Right-arm medium
  • Born: 17 August 1990, Kalutara, Sri Lanka

In this squad he gives Pooran a left-handed option at the top, which matters against teams stacked with right-arm pace. He can open, he can keep wicket, and he has played in the IPL, the BBL and the ILT20 before, so the league holds no surprises for him.

A decade at the top for Sri Lanka

Perera made his Sri Lanka debut in 2013 and has played more than 100 T20Is, as well as Tests and ODIs including the 2019 World Cup. He has captained Sri Lanka in white-ball cricket and has been a fixture of their top order through several generations of the side.

Injuries have interrupted him at times, but his record when fit has never been in doubt. MI Emirates are not signing potential with Perera — they are signing a player who has done it at the highest level for over a decade, and who still bats with the same intent he had on debut.

What is Kusal Perera's batting style?

Left-handed. He is a wicketkeeper-batter who usually opens the batting and scores quickly in the powerplay.

What is Kusal Perera's most famous innings?

His unbeaten 153 against South Africa at Durban in 2019, which won a Test Sri Lanka had no right to win — one of the great innings in Test history.
