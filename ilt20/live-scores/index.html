Live scores, kept honest

SialTV follows every ILT20 Season 5 match from Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi. While a match is in progress, the score on this page is refreshed from verified live sources, and the time of that last refresh is printed next to the score — so a score from ten minutes ago is shown as ten minutes old, never as ball-by-ball. Between matches the page shows the latest result and the next fixture, with the points table alongside.

There is no live feed yet because the season has not started. Once a match ends, the final result — and the points table it changes — is written into the page, so the numbers here and on the ILT20 Results page always agree. For the full fixture list, the ILT20 Schedule page has every match, date and session.

What comes next

Match 1 is Desert Vipers against MI Emirates on Saturday 21 November 2026, in the evening session at Dubai International Stadium. Match 2 follows on Sunday 22 November — Sharjah Warriorz against Gulf Giants in Sharjah, in the afternoon — and Match 3 the same evening, Dubai Capitals against Abu Dhabi Knight Riders in Dubai.

Thirty league matches run to Monday 14 December, then the playoffs: Qualifier 1 on 16 December, the Eliminator on 17 December, Qualifier 2 on 18 December in Sharjah, and the final on 20 December in Dubai.
