ILT20 Season 5 — Schedule, Teams & Points Table

The DP World ILT20 returns for Season 5 on 21 November 2026, with 6 teams playing 34 matches across Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi. Defending champions Desert Vipers open the season against MI Emirates in Dubai, and the final is on 20 December.

The league stage runs from 21 November to 14 December 2026. The playoffs run from 16 to 18 December — Qualifier 1 in Abu Dhabi on 16 December, the Eliminator in Dubai on 17 December, Qualifier 2 in Sharjah on 18 December — and the final is on Sunday 20 December in Dubai. All 34 matches are in Dubai, Sharjah or Abu Dhabi.

Venues

Three grounds host Season 5. Dubai International Stadium hosts 15 matches, including the opener and the final. Sharjah Cricket Stadium hosts 8 matches, including Qualifier 2. Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi hosts the remaining 11, including Qualifier 1.

Where to watch the ILT20

Watch the ILT20 only through its official broadcasters. SialTV never links to pirated streams.

In India, the ILT20 is on Zee TV and ZEE5. Broadcasters for Pakistan, the UAE and Gulf, the UK and the USA are still to be confirmed for Season 5 and will be added here once announced.

Frequently asked questions

When does ILT20 Season 5 start?

Season 5 starts on Saturday 21 November 2026 with Desert Vipers against MI Emirates in Dubai, and ends with the final on Sunday 20 December 2026, also in Dubai.

How many teams play in the ILT20?

Six teams: Desert Vipers, MI Emirates, Dubai Capitals, Gulf Giants, Abu Dhabi Knight Riders and Sharjah Warriorz. Each team plays 10 league matches.

Who are the defending champions?

Desert Vipers won the 2025-26 season, so they are the defending champions in Season 5. Babar Azam captains them for his first ILT20 season.

Where are ILT20 matches played?

At three grounds in the UAE: Dubai International Stadium (15 matches), Sharjah Cricket Stadium (8 matches) and Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi (11 matches).

Where can I see the full ILT20 schedule?

On the ILT20 schedule page, which lists all 30 league matches and the four playoffs with dates, sessions and venues. Exact start times are still to be announced.
