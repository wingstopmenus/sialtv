ILT20 Season 5 — Schedule, Teams & Points Table

The DP World ILT20 returns for Season 5 on 21 November 2026, with 6 teams playing 34 matches across Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi. Defending champions Desert Vipers open the season against MI Emirates in Dubai, and the final is on 20 December.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Season opener — Saturday 21 November 2026

Match 1: Desert Vipers vs MI Emirates
Dubai International Stadium, Dubai — evening session (Gulf Standard Time)

Desert Vipers are the defending champions, and Babar Azam captains them for his first ILT20 season. MI Emirates are captained by Nicholas Pooran.

Exact start times per match are published on the full ILT20 site. This hub shows the session (afternoon or evening) for each fixture.

ILT20 Season 5 points table

The season has not started yet, so there is no points table for Season 5. The table will appear here after the first match on 21 November, and will be updated after every match day. A win is worth 2 points.

Venues

Three grounds host Season 5. Dubai International Stadium hosts 15 matches, including the opener and the final. Sharjah Cricket Stadium hosts 8 matches, including Qualifier 2. The remaining matches are in Abu Dhabi.

Frequently asked questions

When does ILT20 Season 5 start?

Season 5 starts on Saturday 21 November 2026 with Desert Vipers against MI Emirates in Dubai, and ends with the final on Sunday 20 December 2026, also in Dubai.

How many teams play in the ILT20?

Six teams: Desert Vipers, MI Emirates, Dubai Capitals, Gulf Giants, Abu Dhabi Knight Riders and Sharjah Warriorz. Each team plays 10 league matches.

Who are the defending champions?

Desert Vipers won the 2025-26 season, so they are the defending champions in Season 5. Babar Azam captains them for his first ILT20 season.

Where are ILT20 matches played?

At three grounds in the UAE: Dubai International Stadium (15 matches), Sharjah Cricket Stadium (8 matches) and Abu Dhabi.
