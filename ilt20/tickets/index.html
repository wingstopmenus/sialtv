ILT20 Season 5 Tickets — Availability & Updates

Tickets on sale

Tickets for DP World ILT20 Season 5 are on sale. Sales opened on Saturday 17 October 2026, and there are two official places to buy them: the league’s own ticketing site, tickets.ilt20.ae, and Platinumlist (ilt20.platinumlist.net), the league’s ticketing partner. Ticketing news appears first on the league’s official site, ileaguet20.com.

All 34 matches are covered, from the opener on Saturday 21 November to the final on Sunday 20 December 2026, at three grounds — Dubai International Stadium, Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi and Sharjah Cricket Stadium. Tickets are priced in UAE dirhams (AED). Buy only through the two official channels: tickets bought outside them can be refused at the gate, and resale is not allowed. This page lists the published prices and the buying rules; it does not sell tickets, and SialTV takes no bookings.

Ticket prices

Prices published by the league for Season 5, in UAE dirhams. League matches are the 30 league-stage games; the playoffs are Qualifier 1, the Eliminator and Qualifier 2.

On standard tickets, a family of two adults and two children pays AED 60 for a league match (2 × 20 + 2 × 10) and AED 80 for the final (2 × 30 + 2 × 10). Sky Boxes and VIP Suites are listed by the seller, but their prices are still to be announced, so they are TBC here. Prices vary by match and seating category — check the final amount on the listing before paying.

Buying a ticket

Buy online through tickets.ilt20.ae or Platinumlist at ilt20.platinumlist.net — both sell from the same price ladder, and both show the ground, the date and the match number before payment. Physical box offices in Dubai and Sharjah open on Saturday 14 November 2026 for fans who would rather buy in person.

The booking shows the match, the date, the ground and the ticket type; check all four against the ILT20 Schedule before paying. A ticket usually covers one match, so on double-header days check whether it covers both games before you pay. Purchases are capped at 10 tickets per match day, and resale is not allowed. Keep the booking confirmation and any QR code safe, and do not post it online.

For a payment or delivery problem, the seller named on the booking confirmation is the one to contact — SialTV cannot retrieve a Platinumlist order or authorise a refund.

At the ground

Season 5 runs from Saturday 21 November to Sunday 20 December 2026 at three grounds: Dubai International Stadium hosts 15 matches, including the opener and the final; Sharjah Cricket Stadium hosts 8, including Qualifier 2 on 18 December; Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi hosts 11, including Qualifier 1 on 16 December. Afternoon sessions start at 2:00 PM GST (3:00 PM PKT); evening sessions start at 6:30 PM GST (7:30 PM PKT).

If you are travelling for a match, check the fixture date and the ground on the schedule page first — Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi are three different cities, and the same weekend can have matches at two of them. A ticket bought for one ground does not cover a match at another. Gate opening times, bag rules and parking arrangements have not been announced for Season 5 yet — they are TBC.
