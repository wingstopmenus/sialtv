ILT20 Season 5 Tickets — Availability & Updates

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Tickets, not yet announced

No ticket prices, sellers or on-sale dates have been announced for ILT20 Season 5 in the information SialTV has verified. This page says so plainly rather than guessing — and it will list the official seller and the prices as soon as the league announces them.

Until then, do not buy from unauthorised sellers or resale listings. Tickets bought outside the official channel can be refused at the gate, and SialTV never lists unauthorised sellers.

Where to check

The league’s official site is the first place ticketing news appears: ileaguet20.com. This page is checked against the league’s announcements and updated when ticketing opens. SialTV also carries the season’s ILT20 Schedule and the ILT20 Live Scores page, so the dates and venues below are the ones to plan around.

Plan your visit

Season 5 runs from Saturday 21 November to Sunday 20 December 2026 at three grounds: Dubai International Stadium hosts 15 matches, including the opener and the final; Sharjah Cricket Stadium hosts 8, including Qualifier 2 on 18 December; Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi hosts 11, including Qualifier 1 on 16 December. Exact start times are still to be announced — each fixture is listed as an afternoon or evening session.

If you are travelling for a match, check the fixture date and the ground on the schedule page first — Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi are three different cities, and the same weekend can have matches at two of them.
