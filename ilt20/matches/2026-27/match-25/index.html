Dubai Capitals vs Gulf Giants, Thursday, 10 December 2026 — Evening session in Dubai

The 25th match of ILT20 Season 5 brings Dubai Capitals and Gulf Giants together at Dubai International Stadium on Thursday 10 December 2026, in the evening session. It is the first of the final six league matches, and it opens the last weekend of the league stage with two former champions meeting at the ground both call home.

Both sides arrive at this match with the table still to be shaped — the season opens on 21 November, so no points exist yet. What is already clear is the shape of the run-in: Dubai Capitals will have played seven league matches before this one and will have three left, including this game, while Gulf Giants will have played eight and will have two left. A win here is worth two points, and in a six-team league where only four go through, two points in the final week carry more weight than two points in the first.

The two captains set the tone for the contest. Faf du Plessis leads Dubai Capitals, the 2025 champions, and Jos Buttler leads Gulf Giants, the inaugural champions of 2023. The scorecard will appear on this page after the match.

How both sides arrive in Dubai

The season has not started yet, so there is no Season 5 form to read — no results, no points and no net run rate exist for either side. What can be said before a ball is bowled is who leads each team, who coaches them, and what they have won before.

Du Plessis captains a Capitals side rebuilt around himself, Phil Salt and a returning Marcus Stoinis, with Hemang Badani as coach. The rebuild follows a title: Dubai Capitals won the ILT20 in 2025, and the squad for Season 5 keeps the core of that winning group while adding Stoinis back into the mix. Salt at the top and Stoinis in the middle give the batting two very different gears, and the bowling leans on Mustafizur Rahman and Noor Ahmad.

Gulf Giants, coached by Simon Helmot, have loaded their top order for this season. Buttler opens the batting and captains, and behind him sit Heinrich Klaasen, David Miller and Liam Livingstone — four names who can each win a match on their own day. The Giants won the first ILT20 title in 2023 and have not won one since, so this season is a chance to add a second title to the first.

The run-in and the race for the top four

Four teams qualify for the playoffs, and the seeds decide everything that follows: the top two meet in Qualifier 1 on 16 December, with the winner going straight to the final, while the third and fourth seeds meet in the Eliminator on 17 December, where the loser goes home. Every league win is worth two points, and with only six league matches left after Match 24, there is no time left to recover from a dropped game.

For Dubai Capitals, this is the first of three matches in five days — Gulf Giants here, MI Emirates on Sunday 13 December, and Abu Dhabi Knight Riders on Monday 14 December. Three wins from those three would be a perfect finish to the league stage; even two would leave them well placed. For Gulf Giants, the run-in is shorter and sharper: this match, then Desert Vipers on Sunday evening. Two matches, four points available, and no margin for a slip.

The wider picture is simple. After Match 30 on Monday 14 December, the league stage ends, Tuesday 15 December is a rest day, and the playoffs begin on Wednesday 16 December. Whatever the table looks like after this match, both sides will know exactly what they still need — and neither will want to go into the final weekend needing favours from other results.

The players who can decide it

Every name below links to that player’s profile. No Season 5 numbers exist yet — the season starts on 21 November — so these are the names to follow for what they bring into the season, not for form they have already shown in it.

Faf du Plessis captains Dubai Capitals and opens the batting, so his start sets the innings. Beside him, Phil Salt is the batter who can take the game away in the powerplay, and Marcus Stoinis returns to the side as the all-rounder who bats in the middle order and bowls in the middle overs. With the ball, Noor Ahmad gives the Capitals a wicket-taking spinner for the Dubai surface.

For Gulf Giants, Jos Buttler is the name the Capitals will plan around — captain, opener, and the most destructive batter in the Giants squad. Behind him, Heinrich Klaasen and David Miller are the two middle-order batters who punish spin and finish innings, while Liam Livingstone can change a game with bat or ball. Tabraiz Shamsi leads the Giants’ spin attack.

The contest inside the contest is the powerplay. Salt and du Plessis against the Giants’ new-ball bowlers, and Buttler against Mustafizur Rahman and Noor Ahmad, will decide whether either side gets the fast start that Dubai so often rewards.

The most recent meeting came in the 2025-26 season, on 21 December 2025, when Dubai Capitals won by 6 wickets. The meeting before that, on 6 December 2025, went the Giants’ way by 4 wickets. The two sides have also met in a playoff before: in Qualifier 2 of the 2024 season, Dubai Capitals won by 9 wickets to reach the final.

What the record does not show is a settled pattern. The Giants have won the majority of the meetings, but the Capitals have won the two that mattered most recently in the playoffs — and the 101-run win for the Giants in January 2023 remains the biggest margin between the two sides.

When does Dubai Capitals vs Gulf Giants start?
