Sharjah Warriorz vs Gulf Giants, Sunday, 22 November 2026 — afternoon session, Sharjah

The 2nd match of the DP World ILT20 Season 5 is Sharjah Warriorz against Gulf Giants on Sunday, 22 November 2026, in the afternoon session, at the Sharjah Cricket Stadium in Sharjah. It is the first match of the day at a ground hosting 8 matches this season.

James Vince keeps the captaincy, and Adrian Birrell stays on as coach. The Warriorz attack is the part of the squad that stands out on paper: Adil Rashid's leg-spin, Mohammad Nabi's experience, and Waqar Salamkheil, last season's leading bowler award winner. If the attack fires in Sharjah, the Warriorz can defend almost any total.

Jos Buttler captains the Giants, and Simon Helmot coaches a top order that reads like a list of the format's biggest names: Buttler himself, Heinrich Klaasen, David Miller and Liam Livingstone. The Giants won the first ILT20 in 2023 and have spent three seasons trying to win it again. Their batting is built to chase down anything Sharjah offers.

Two points, and the first marker of the season

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Each side plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs.

Two points are on offer, and for both sides the opening weekend is about setting a tone. The Warriorz play their first two matches at home in Sharjah, so a win here would give them a platform before the season's travel begins. The Giants, meanwhile, open away from Dubai and would love to take two points back to their base.

Early-season games count double in a ten-match league season because the table moves so fast: lose the first two and a side is already chasing the top four. Neither team can afford to treat this as a warm-up. The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December, and every point from November counts toward them.

The players who can decide it

Every name below links to that player's profile.

James Vince captains the Warriorz and opens their batting. He has held the role before, and a strong start from him in front of a home crowd would settle the Warriorz order for the rest of their Sharjah run.

Adil Rashid is the Warriorz' most reliable wicket-taker, and Sharjah's slower surface suits his leg-spin. Against a Giants top order that attacks spin, his four overs are the contest inside the contest.

Jos Buttler captains the Giants and opens their batting. He is one of the most feared openers in the format, and if he bats through the powerplay, the Giants' total — or chase — changes shape entirely.

Heinrich Klaasen bats in the Giants' middle order and is among the cleanest strikers of spin in the tournament. How he handles Adil Rashid in the middle overs is likely to decide how far the Giants' innings goes.

Past meetings between these two sides

Eight meetings, and the Giants lead 5–3, but the most recent game went the other way: the Warriorz won by 11 runs on 15 December 2025, after the Giants had won the two meetings before that, both by 6 wickets. That December win was the Warriorz' first in this fixture since January 2025.

Giants lead the head-to-head 5–3 across 8 past meetings, from the 2023 season to last season.
