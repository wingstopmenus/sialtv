Jos Buttler captains the Giants, and the top order is the reason this side is feared: Buttler, Heinrich Klaasen, David Miller and Liam Livingstone. Simon Helmot's side has won six of nine meetings with the Capitals, and their batting is built to take this game away in the last five overs.

Faf du Plessis captains the Capitals, with Hemang Badani coaching a rebuilt side around Faf, Phil Salt and a returning Marcus Stoinis. The 2025 champions have won only three of nine against the Giants, but they won the most recent meeting, by 6 wickets on 21 December 2025.

Two points, and the first marker of the season

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Each side plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs.

Two points are on offer, and for the Capitals a win would confirm that the record has turned their way after December's win. For the Giants, a win would restore the old order in this fixture and keep them among the early leaders. The top four reach the playoffs from 16 December.

By the end of the second Saturday, every side will have played three or four matches, and the table will already separate the contenders from the chasers. A team that wins here moves toward the top four; a team that loses it is left needing wins against the sides above them.

David Miller bats in the Giants' middle order and finishes innings. If the Giants are chasing, his calm at the death is the quality the Capitals will fear most in the last five overs.

Past meetings between these two sides

Nine meetings, and the Giants lead 6–3. The recent run is split: the Giants won by 4 wickets on 6 December 2025, and the Capitals answered with a 6-wicket win on 21 December 2025. Before that, the Capitals had won by 5 wickets in January 2025 — the Giants have never lost three in a row in this fixture.

Giants lead the head-to-head 6–3 across 9 past meetings, from the 2023 season to last season.
