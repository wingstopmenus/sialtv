Abu Dhabi Knight Riders vs Dubai Capitals, Monday, 14 December 2026 — Evening session in Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi Knight Riders host Dubai Capitals at Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi on Monday 14 December 2026, in the evening session, in the 30th and final league match of the ILT20 Season 5. When this match ends, the league stage is over, the top four are settled, and the playoffs begin two days later.

The Knight Riders will have played nine league matches before this one, with this as their tenth and last. Dubai Capitals will also have played nine — against Gulf Giants, MI Emirates and now the Knight Riders in the space of five days — with this as their tenth and last. Two points are on offer, and they are the last two points of the league stage. Whatever the table looks like before this match, both sides will know exactly what a win would do to their seed.

Jason Holder captains the Knight Riders at their home ground in Abu Dhabi, with Dwayne Bravo as coach. Faf du Plessis captains Dubai Capitals, the side that won the title in 2025. The scorecard will appear on this page once the match is played.

How both sides arrive in Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi Knight Riders have no ILT20 title after four seasons, and Holder’s captaincy is the fresh start they have built Season 5 around. The squad mixes experience — Kane Williamson, Sunil Narine, Moeen Ali — with Finn Allen at the top of the order and Andre Fletcher behind him. A win in the final league match, at home, would be the strongest possible way to carry momentum into the playoffs.

Dubai Capitals won the title in 2025, and Hemang Badani has rebuilt the side around du Plessis, Phil Salt and a returning Marcus Stoinis. Their run-in is the busiest of any side: three matches in five days, and this is the third of them. If the first two went their way, this match could be about seeding rather than survival; if they did not, it could be the match that decides whether they play on Wednesday or go home.

The run-in and the race for the top four

This is the last league match, so the stakes are as clear as they ever get. Four teams qualify for the playoffs, and the seeds are settled by the table after this match. The top two meet in Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December, with the winner going straight to the final and the loser getting a second chance in Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December. The third and fourth seeds meet in the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December, where the loser’s season ends. Two points here could move either side up a seed — and a seed is worth a second chance.

For the Knight Riders, a win at home in the final league match would also settle a score: Dubai Capitals have won eight of the ten meetings between these sides, including both meetings in the 2025-26 season. For the Capitals, a win would complete a perfect final week — three wins from three matches in five days — and would send the 2025 champions into the playoffs as the side nobody wants to meet.

After this match, Tuesday 15 December is a rest day. The playoffs begin on Wednesday 16 December with Qualifier 1 in Abu Dhabi, followed by the Eliminator in Dubai on Thursday, Qualifier 2 in Sharjah on Friday, and the final in Dubai on Sunday 20 December.

Jason Holder captains Abu Dhabi Knight Riders and leads the attack as the side’s all-rounder, while Kane Williamson anchors the batting and Finn Allen provides the fast start at the top. Sunil Narine is still the spinner sides plan their innings around, and Moeen Ali offers a match-winning option with bat or ball.

Dubai Capitals lean on the same names: Faf du Plessis opens and captains, Phil Salt attacks from the other end, and Marcus Stoinis is back as the middle-order all-rounder. Down the order, Rovman Powell supplies the power, and Noor Ahmad is the spinner the Knight Riders must handle.

The Knight Riders’ one win in the fixture’s recent history came in the Eliminator of the 2025-26 season, when they beat the Capitals by 50 runs — a reminder that the head-to-head counts for little once the playoffs begin, and that this match is a rehearsal for what could follow.
