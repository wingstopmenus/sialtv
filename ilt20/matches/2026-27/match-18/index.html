Desert Vipers vs Sharjah Warriorz, Friday, 4 December 2026 — evening session at Sharjah Cricket Stadium, Sharjah

Match 18 of 34 in ILT20 Season 5. Second meeting of the season — the vipers hosted the first, in match 4 on 23 november.

The 18th match of the Desert Vipers season is an evening session at the Sharjah Cricket Stadium on Friday, 4 December 2026. It is the second meeting between these sides in Season 5 — the Vipers hosted the first, in Match 4 on 23 November — so the Warriorz get their home return ten days later.

The Vipers have played six league matches by now and the Warriorz five, which makes this a Friday-night game with the table already meaning something. A win is worth two points, with one point each for a no-result, and the top four after 30 league matches reach the playoffs.

The exact start time has not been confirmed, so this page lists the evening session only. The scorecard will appear here once the match is played.

How both sides arrive in Season 5

Desert Vipers are the defending champions, with Babar Azam taking the armband for his first ILT20 season and James Foster coaching. Their squad has Fakhar Zaman and Shimron Hetmyer with the bat, Shadab Khan and Mujeeb Ur Rahman with the ball, and Naseem Shah leading the pace attack. Eight wins from nine against Sharjah says the champions know this fixture well.

Sharjah Warriorz are still chasing a first title, with James Vince keeping the captaincy and Adrian Birrell coaching. Their attack is their best argument: Adil Rashid and Waqar Salamkheil, last season's leading bowler award winner, lead a group that also includes Adam Milne and Mohammad Nabi. At home in Sharjah, with the crowd behind them, the Warriorz have their best chance yet to break the pattern.

The points on offer and the playoff race

Match 18 of 30 league games. The top four qualify for the playoffs.

Six games in for the champions and five for the hosts, this is the point where the league stage starts to punish teams that lose at home. A Warriorz win would be their second over the Vipers ever, and it would come against the defending champions in front of their own crowd. A Vipers win would keep the champions on course for the top two — and extend a run that already spans four seasons.

The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, followed by the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah and the final on Sunday 20 December in Dubai. The top two after the league stage go straight to Qualifier 1, so the Vipers' Friday-night points count double in effect.

The players who can decide it

Every name below links to that player's profile. Squads are as announced for Season 5.

Fakhar Zaman opens for the defending champions, and his powerplay hitting is the fastest way the Vipers take a game away from a side. Sharjah's bowlers have to find a way past him early, because once Zaman is set the total tends to run away.

Shadab Khan is the Vipers' middle-overs controller with bat and ball, and his leg-spin is aimed at a Warriorz middle order that has to score quickly at home. Shadab's four overs are where the champions usually turn a contest into a comfortable win.

Sikandar Raza is the Warriorz' all-rounder and the player most likely to change this fixture's story on his own. His batting in the middle order and his bowling give Sharjah two ways to hurt the champions, and the home crowd will expect both.

Tom Banton opens for the Warriorz, and his job is to give Sharjah the fast start their record against the Vipers has so rarely had. If Banton takes the powerplay, the champions are suddenly defending rather than dictating — and that is new territory for this fixture.

Desert Vipers lead this fixture 8–1 from nine meetings across the four previous seasons, with no abandoned games. It is the most one-sided head-to-head in the league, and the Warriorz' only win came in the first season, in 2023.

The last two meetings both went to the Vipers in the 2025-26 season: Match 22 on 20 December 2025 by 4 wickets, and Match 28 on 26 December 2025 by 5 wickets. The Vipers also won the Qualifier 2 meeting between the sides in February 2025 by 7 wickets, on their way to the title.
