The 23rd match of the Dubai Capitals season is an evening session at the Dubai International Stadium on Tuesday, 8 December 2026. The Capitals have played six league matches by now and the Warriorz seven, with only a few league games left after this one. A win is worth two points, with one point each for a no-result, and the top four after 30 league matches reach the playoffs.

Dubai Capitals are the 2025 champions, rebuilt around Faf du Plessis, Phil Salt and a returning Marcus Stoinis, with Hemang Badani coaching. Their squad has Rovman Powell and Dasun Shanaka in the middle order, and Mustafizur Rahman and Noor Ahmad with the ball. The overall record against Sharjah is poor — two wins from eight — but both of those wins came last season, and the most recent was a 63-run win.

Sharjah Warriorz are still chasing a first title, with James Vince keeping the captaincy and Adrian Birrell coaching. Their attack is the calling card: Adil Rashid and Waqar Salamkheil, last season's leading bowler award winner, lead a group that also includes Adam Milne and Mohammad Nabi. Five wins from eight against the champions says the Warriorz know how to beat this side — the question is whether last season broke the pattern for good.

The points on offer and the playoff race

Match 23 of 30 league games. The top four qualify for the playoffs.

Six games in for the champions and seven for the visitors, this is the final week of the league stage, where every point is fought over. A Capitals win would make it three straight over Sharjah and keep the 2025 champions in the top-four race; a Warriorz win would restore the old order in this fixture and push the hosts toward the bottom half.

The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, followed by the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah and the final on Sunday 20 December in Dubai. With only seven league matches left after this one, there is no time left to recover from a home defeat.

The players who can decide it

Noor Ahmad is the Capitals' wrist-spin option, and his variations are built for the middle overs against a Warriorz side that has historically scored freely against Dubai. If Noor ties down the middle order, the Capitals' pace attack gets a total it can defend.

Mustafizur Rahman leads the Capitals' death bowling, and his cutters at the end of the innings are the reason the champions won both meetings last season. Sharjah's finishers know what is coming — stopping it is another matter.

Adam Milne leads the Warriorz pace attack, and his new-ball speed is the first test for a Capitals top order built around Faf du Plessis and Phil Salt. An early Milne wicket would put the champions on the back foot in front of their own crowd.

Brandon McMullen is one of the Warriorz' middle-order batters, and his hitting gives Sharjah a way to post a total even when the top order falls early. Against a Capitals attack that has beaten them twice in a row, McMullen's innings could be the one that breaks the run.

Sharjah Warriorz lead this fixture 5–2 from eight meetings across the four previous seasons, with one game ending in no result — Match 17 on 26 January 2023. The overall record is the Warriorz' best against any side in the league, and it was built in the first three seasons.

The last two meetings both went to the Capitals in the 2025-26 season, and neither was close: Match 20 on 19 December 2025 by 63 runs, and Match 27 on 24 December 2025 by 6 wickets. The question on Tuesday is whether last season was a blip or a changing of the guard in this fixture.
