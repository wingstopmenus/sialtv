Dubai Capitals vs Desert Vipers, Wednesday, 25 November 2026 — evening session, Dubai

The 6th match of the DP World ILT20 Season 5 is Dubai Capitals against Desert Vipers on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, in the evening session, at the Dubai International Stadium in Dubai. It is the evening game at a ground hosting 15 matches this season.

This is the sixth match of the season and the third in Dubai, where 15 of the season's 34 matches are played. The Capitals opened against Abu Dhabi Knight Riders on Sunday, while the Vipers played in Sharjah on Monday. The full scorecard will appear on this page once the match is played.

Faf du Plessis captains the Capitals, with Hemang Badani coaching a rebuilt side: Phil Salt opens, Marcus Stoinis returns, and the bowling has Mustafizur Rahman and Noor Ahmad. The 2025 champions know this fixture well — they have won six of the ten meetings with the Vipers.

Babar Azam captains the defending champions, and the Vipers' last two meetings with the Capitals tell their own story: they won both league games last season, by 4 wickets and by 5 wickets, after losing the 2025 final by 4 wickets. James Foster's side will feel this fixture has turned their way.

Two points, and the first marker of the season

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Each side plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs.

Two points are on offer, and for the Capitals a win would be a reminder that the 2025 final was no accident. For the Vipers, a win would confirm the shift: three wins in a row over the Capitals, and the title defence gathering pace. The top four reach the playoffs from 16 December.

Both sides will have played twice before this game, so the table will already have a shape. A team that wins here moves toward the early leaders; a team that loses it is left with at most one win from three and a run of away games to come. In a ten-match season, that gap is hard to close.

The players who can decide it

Every name below links to that player's profile.

Faf du Plessis captains the Capitals and opens their batting. He led them to the 2025 title, and against the side that took the crown from them he will want to set the tone from the first over.

Marcus Stoinis returns to the Capitals and gives them a middle-order batter who can also bowl at the death. His all-round game is built for exactly this kind of night: a big game in Dubai against the champions.

Babar Azam captains the defending champions, and his calm at the crease is the counterweight to the Capitals' pace attack. If he bats through the middle overs, the Vipers' total will be hard to chase under lights.

Mujeeb Ur Rahman opens the Vipers' spin attack and bowls in the powerplay, where his variations trouble even set batters. Against the Capitals' aggressive openers, his first two overs could decide the shape of the innings.

Past meetings between these two sides

Ten meetings, and the Capitals lead 6–4, but the momentum has flipped. The Capitals won the 2025 final by 4 wickets, then lost both league meetings last season — by 4 wickets on 2 December 2025 and by 5 wickets on 14 December 2025. The Vipers have won the last two meetings in a row.

Capitals lead the head-to-head 6–4 across 10 past meetings, from the 2023 season to last season.
