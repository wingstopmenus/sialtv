Abu Dhabi Knight Riders vs Sharjah Warriorz, Sunday, 29 November 2026 — afternoon session, Abu Dhabi

The 11th match of the DP World ILT20 Season 5 is Abu Dhabi Knight Riders against Sharjah Warriorz on Sunday, 29 November 2026, in the afternoon session, at the Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi. It is the first match of the day at a ground hosting 11 matches this season.

This is the eleventh match of the season and the first of two on Sunday, with Desert Vipers against Gulf Giants following in the evening in Dubai. Abu Dhabi hosts 11 matches this season, and this is the third of them. The full scorecard will appear on this page once the match is played.

How both sides arrive in Season 5

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 21 November 2026, so there is no Season 5 form yet. What follows is what each side brings into the tournament.

Jason Holder captains the Knight Riders and Dwayne Bravo coaches them, a new leadership pair for a side still chasing its first title. The squad has Kane Williamson and Sunil Narine, and Finn Allen at the top, and their home ground is where they must turn close games into wins.

James Vince captains the Warriorz, with Adrian Birrell coaching, and the attack is the strength: Adil Rashid, Mohammad Nabi and Waqar Salamkheil, last season's leading bowler award winner. The Warriorz won the most recent meeting in this fixture, by 4 wickets on 22 December 2025.

Two points, and the first marker of the season

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Each side plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs.

Two points are on offer, and in a fixture this level they matter more than usual: the winner takes the lead in the head-to-head and a place among the early leaders, while the loser falls behind a direct rival for the top four. The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December.

Both sides will have played three matches before this game, so the table will already have a shape. A team that wins here can afford a slip later; a team that loses it is left with at most one win from four and a run of games against the champions to come. In a ten-match season, that is a hard place to recover from.

The players who can decide it

Every name below links to that player's profile.

Jason Holder captains the Knight Riders and gives them balance in both disciplines. At home in Abu Dhabi, his own performance with bat and ball will set the standard for a side trying to turn close games into wins.

Finn Allen opens for the Knight Riders and scores quickly when he gets going. Against a Warriorz attack built on spin, his first six overs could decide whether the home side bat on the front foot.

James Vince captains the Warriorz and opens their batting. He has led them to wins in this fixture before, and a strong start from him on Sunday afternoon would put the Knight Riders under pressure from the first over.

Waqar Salamkheil is last season's leading bowler award winner, and he leads the Warriorz attack. His wrist-spin on Abu Dhabi's surface is the spell the Knight Riders' middle order will have to survive.

Past meetings between these two sides

Eight meetings, and the record is level at 4–4 — the only tied head-to-head of the opening week. The recent run is split down the middle: the Warriorz won by 4 wickets in January 2025, the Knight Riders by 39 runs in December 2025, and the Warriorz again by 4 wickets later that month.

The head-to-head is level at 4–4 across 8 past meetings, from the 2023 season to last season.
