Qualifier 1, Wednesday, 16 December 2026 — Evening session in Abu Dhabi

Qualifier 1 opens the ILT20 Season 5 playoffs at Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi on Wednesday 16 December 2026, in the evening session. Seed 1 meets Seed 2, and the winner goes straight to the final in Dubai on Sunday 20 December. The loser is not out — they get a second chance in Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December — but the winner’s reward is four days of rest and a direct route to the title match.

The two teams are not known yet. The league stage ends with Match 30 on Monday 14 December, and the seeds are decided by the points table after that match: the top two seeds meet here, and the third and fourth seeds meet in the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December. Tuesday 15 December is a rest day between the league stage and the playoffs. The scorecard will be added to this page after the match.

This is the first of four playoff matches in five days. The ILT20 playoffs end with the final at Dubai International Stadium on Sunday 20 December, and the winner of this match will already have one foot in that game.

How the playoffs work

Four matches turn four qualifiers into one champion. The top four teams from the league stage go through, seeded one to four by the points table after 30 matches. The seeds are not decided yet — the league stage ends with Match 30 on Monday 14 December, and the table will only be final after that match.

Qualifier 1, on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, pairs Seed 1 with Seed 2. The winner goes straight to the final on Sunday 20 December. The loser is not out: they get a second chance in Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah. The Eliminator, on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, pairs Seed 3 with Seed 4, and the loser’s season ends there. The winner of the Eliminator meets the loser of Qualifier 1 in Qualifier 2, and the winner of that match takes the second place in the final.

Two rest days break up the playoff week. Tuesday 15 December sits between the league stage and the playoffs, and Saturday 19 December sits between Qualifier 2 and the final. Every playoff match is played in the evening session; the exact start times are still to be announced.

The final berth, and what losing costs

The winner of Qualifier 1 goes directly to the final on Sunday 20 December in Dubai, with four days to prepare and no further match to win. That is the best reward the playoff format offers, and it is why finishing first or second in the league matters more than finishing third or fourth: only the top two seeds play for a direct final place.

The loser’s path is longer but still open. They meet the winner of the Eliminator in Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah, and a win there takes them to the final as well. But they will have played an extra match, with one day less to prepare, against a side that has already won a knockout game. The difference between winning and losing Qualifier 1 is not elimination — it is rest, preparation, and the shape of the week.

The final itself is played at Dubai International Stadium on Sunday 20 December, after a rest day on Saturday 19 December. The defending champions are Desert Vipers, who beat MI Emirates by 46 runs in the 2025-26 final; the 2025 champions were Dubai Capitals, the 2024 champions MI Emirates, and the inaugural champions of 2023 were Gulf Giants.

The road to the final

Seed 1 and Seed 2 have the shortest road. They meet in Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, and the winner goes directly to the final in Dubai on Sunday 20 December. The loser drops to Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah, where a win still takes them to the final — so a top-two seed carries a second chance that no other seed has.

Seed 3 and Seed 4 have the longest road. They meet in the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, and the loser goes home. The winner must then beat the loser of Qualifier 1 in Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December, and then beat the winner of Qualifier 1 in the final on Sunday 20 December. That is three wins in four days to lift the title, with no margin for a defeat anywhere along the way.

No team is assigned to any of these paths yet. The seeds will be known only after Match 30 on Monday 14 December, and this page will name the teams once the league table is final.

No head-to-head yet — the teams are not decided

There is no head-to-head to write for this match yet, because the two teams are not known. The seeds are decided by the league stage, which ends with Match 30 on Monday 14 December. Once the top four are settled, this page will name the two sides and their past meetings. Until then, the only honest statement is that the pairing is still to be decided.
