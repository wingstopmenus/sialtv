Dubai Capitals vs MI Emirates, Wednesday, 2 December 2026 — evening session at Dubai International Stadium, Dubai

Match 16 of 34 in ILT20 Season 5. First meeting of the season between these sides.

The 16th match of the Dubai Capitals season is an evening session at the Dubai International Stadium on Wednesday, 2 December 2026. It is the first meeting between these sides in Season 5, and the second game of the day — the Knight Riders host the Vipers in Abu Dhabi in the afternoon.

Dubai Capitals have played four league matches by now and MI Emirates five, so this is a mid-table meeting with the table already taking shape. A win is worth two points, with one point each for a no-result, and the top four after 30 league matches reach the playoffs.

How both sides arrive in Season 5

Dubai Capitals are the 2025 champions, rebuilt around Faf du Plessis, Phil Salt and a returning Marcus Stoinis, with Hemang Badani coaching. Their squad has Rovman Powell and Dasun Shanaka in the middle order, and Mustafizur Rahman and Noor Ahmad with the ball. They have won five of ten against MI Emirates, and they will not need reminding that the 2024 final went the other way.

MI Emirates are the 2024 champions, runners-up in January, now coached by Mark Boucher and captained by Nicholas Pooran. Their squad has Jonny Bairstow and Kusal Perera at the top, Rashid Khan and Fazalhaq Farooqi with the ball, and Romario Shepherd and Sherfane Rutherford to finish. They have also won a final against these opponents, in 2024, so the rivalry cuts both ways.

The points on offer and the playoff race

Match 16 of 30 league games. The top four qualify for the playoffs.

Two former champions, level at 5–5, meeting for the first time this season: this is the kind of game that decides who spends December in the top two and who spends it chasing. The Capitals have played one game fewer than MI Emirates, so a win would put them level or better with a game in hand.

The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, followed by the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah and the final on Sunday 20 December in Dubai. The top two after the league stage go straight to Qualifier 1, which is why games like this one — against a direct rival — carry more than two points.

The players who can decide it

Faf du Plessis captains the Dubai Capitals and opens the batting, so the first ten overs of this game run through him. His side rebuilt around him this season, and against an MI Emirates attack led by Rashid Khan, the captain's own innings sets the tone.

Phil Salt is the Capitals' other opener, and his powerplay hitting is built for Dubai's true surface. If Salt and du Plessis both fire, MI Emirates are chasing a total rather than setting one — and that changes the whole shape of the evening.

Jonny Bairstow is one of the most destructive openers in the MI Emirates squad, and his battle with the Capitals' new-ball bowlers is the first contest inside the contest. A fast start from Bairstow puts the 2024 champions in front early.

Romario Shepherd gives MI Emirates the late-overs hitting and the hard overs with the ball that decide close games. In a fixture where five of ten meetings have gone each way, the side whose finishers land the last blows usually walks off with the points.

Dubai Capitals and MI Emirates are level at 5–5 from ten meetings across the four previous seasons, with no abandoned games. It is the most even head-to-head in the league, and the two sides have met in an Eliminator and in two finals.

The last two meetings both went to MI Emirates in the 2025-26 season: Match 18 on 17 December 2025 by 7 runs, and Match 29 on 27 December 2025 by 8 wickets. MI Emirates have also won both knockout meetings between the sides — the 2023 Eliminator by 8 wickets and the 2024 final by 45 runs.
