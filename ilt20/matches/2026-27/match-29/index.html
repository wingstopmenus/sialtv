Gulf Giants vs Desert Vipers, Sunday, 13 December 2026 — Evening session in Dubai

Gulf Giants meet Desert Vipers at Dubai International Stadium on Sunday 13 December 2026, in the evening session, in the 29th match of ILT20 Season 5. It is the second match of a two-game Sunday, it closes the Vipers’ league stage, and it is the Giants’ last league match of the season.

The Vipers will have played nine league matches before this one, having faced Abu Dhabi Knight Riders the day before; this is their tenth and final league game. The Giants will also have played nine, having faced Dubai Capitals on Thursday; this is their tenth and final league game too. Two points are on offer, and for both sides this is the last chance to add to their total before the table is final.

Jos Buttler captains Gulf Giants, the inaugural champions of 2023. Babar Azam captains Desert Vipers, the defending champions. After the match, the full scorecard will appear here.

How both sides arrive in Dubai

Neither side has played a Season 5 match yet, so form is not something this preview can measure — the season starts on 21 November. The known facts are the captains, the coaches and the titles each side has already won.

TeamCaptainCoachILT20 titles
Gulf GiantsJos ButtlerSimon Helmot2023
Desert VipersBabar AzamJames Foster2025-26

Gulf Giants, coached by Simon Helmot, built this season around a top order of Buttler, Heinrich Klaasen, David Miller and Liam Livingstone. The Giants won the first ILT20 title in 2023, beating these same Vipers by 7 wickets in the final, and have not won one since. A win here would be the ideal way to close the league stage: against the defending champions, at the ground both sides call home.

Desert Vipers, coached by James Foster, are the defending champions, winners of the 2025-26 title in January. Babar Azam captains for his first ILT20 season, and the squad carries the champions’ core — Fakhar Zaman and Shimron Hetmyer with the bat, Naseem Shah and Shadab Khan with the ball. The Vipers play two matches in two days to close the league stage, and this is the second of them.

The run-in and the race for the top four

This is the last league match for both sides, so the maths is at its simplest: win, and add two points to the total; lose, and finish on what the first nine matches gave. Four teams qualify for the playoffs. Qualifier 1 on 16 December pairs the top two seeds, and its winner goes straight to the final while its loser gets a second chance in Qualifier 2. Seeds 3 and 4 meet in the Eliminator on 17 December, where the loser’s season ends.

For the Giants, a win here could lift them into the top two, depending on the afternoon result between MI Emirates and Dubai Capitals. For the Vipers, a win would complete a two-from-two final weekend after Saturday’s match against Abu Dhabi Knight Riders — and would send the defending champions into the playoffs with the momentum of a closing win.

After this match, one league match remains: Abu Dhabi Knight Riders against Dubai Capitals on Monday 14 December, the final league game of the season. After this match, Tuesday 15 December is a rest day, and the playoffs begin the next day.

The players who can decide it

Every player named below links to their profile. The season starts on 21 November, so these are not form picks from Season 5 — they are the players whose ability and experience make them the ones to watch in a final league match.

Jos Buttler captains Gulf Giants and opens the batting, so the home side’s innings will follow his start. Behind him, Heinrich Klaasen and David Miller are the middle-order batters who punish spin and finish innings, while Liam Livingstone can change a game with bat or ball. David Wiese adds experience to the lower order, and Tabraiz Shamsi leads the spin attack.

For Desert Vipers, Babar Azam captains and bats at the top, Fakhar Zaman opens beside him, and Shadab Khan gives the side wickets in the middle overs. Naseem Shah takes the new ball, and Mujeeb Ur Rahman adds mystery spin for the Dubai surface.

The fixture has a history of close finishes — the 2025-26 season alone produced a tied match decided by a Super Over — so neither side will expect this one to be settled early, even with so much at stake.

The most recent meeting came in the 2025-26 season, on 12 December 2025, when Desert Vipers won by 8 wickets. The meeting before that, on 8 December 2025, was tied — and the Vipers won the Super Over. The two sides have met in a final: in February 2023, Gulf Giants beat Desert Vipers by 7 wickets to win the first ILT20 title, and in Qualifier 1 of the same season, the Vipers had won by 19 runs.

The Vipers have won the majority of the ten meetings, including the last four in a row across the 2025 and 2025-26 seasons. The Giants’ last win in the fixture came in February 2023, in that first final — so a win here would end a run of four straight defeats to these opponents.
