Two points, and the first marker of the season

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Each side plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs.

Two points are on offer, and for MI Emirates they would be worth more than that: a win here levels the early season series and proves the new coaching set-up can beat the champions. For the Vipers, a win would make it three victories in a row over MI Emirates and keep the title defence on track.

By the second Saturday the table will have a clear shape, and the sides at the top will be the ones that won their return fixtures. Each team plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs from 16 December. Neither side can afford to drop a game against a direct rival for those places.

The players who can decide it

Nicholas Pooran captains MI Emirates and bats at the heart of their order. He has the power to win this game on his own, and the Vipers will plan their middle overs around keeping him from facing too many balls.

Fazalhaq Farooqi leads the MI Emirates pace attack with the new ball. Against a Vipers top order built around Babar Azam and Fakhar Zaman, an early wicket from him would change the whole shape of the innings.

Shadab Khan gives the Vipers a leg-spinning all-rounder in the middle order, and his four overs against MI Emirates' powerful batting are the spell the champions will build their defence around.
