MI Emirates vs Sharjah Warriorz, Sunday, 6 December 2026 — afternoon session at Zayed Cricket Stadium, Abu Dhabi

Match 20 of 34 in ILT20 Season 5. First meeting of the season between these sides.

The 20th match of the MI Emirates season is an afternoon session at the Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi on Sunday, 6 December 2026. It is the first meeting between these sides in Season 5, and the first game of the day — the Vipers play the Capitals in Dubai in the evening.

Both sides have played six league matches by now, which puts this game two-thirds of the way through the league stage. A win is worth two points, with one point each for a no-result, and the top four after 30 league matches reach the playoffs.

The exact start time has not been confirmed, so this page lists the afternoon session only. The scorecard will appear here once the match is played.

MI Emirates are the 2024 champions, runners-up in January, now coached by Mark Boucher and captained by Nicholas Pooran. Their squad pairs Pooran with Jonny Bairstow and Kusal Perera at the top, Rashid Khan and Fazalhaq Farooqi with the ball, and Romario Shepherd and Sherfane Rutherford as the finishers. Six wins from nine against Sharjah says the champions know how to handle this opponent.

Sharjah Warriorz are still chasing a first title, with James Vince keeping the captaincy and Adrian Birrell coaching. Their attack is the calling card: Adil Rashid and Waqar Salamkheil, last season's leading bowler award winner, lead a group that also includes Adam Milne and Mohammad Nabi. The Warriorz won the most recent meeting between these sides, by 6 runs, so they travel to Abu Dhabi with proof that the gap is closable.

The points on offer and the playoff race

Match 20 of 30 league games. The top four qualify for the playoffs.

With ten league matches left after this one, the table is about to split into teams that control their own fate and teams that need help. Both sides have played six matches, so a win here is worth more than two points in effect — it is a win taken directly off a rival for the same playoff places.

The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, followed by the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah and the final on Sunday 20 December in Dubai. The top two after the league stage go straight to Qualifier 1, which is why the champions will want this one banked early.

The players who can decide it

Every name below links to that player's profile. Squads are as announced for Season 5.

Kusal Perera is one of the most experienced openers in the MI Emirates squad, and his fast starts are how the champions put games beyond reach early. Against a Warriorz attack built around spin, Perera's aggression in the powerplay is the first contest of the afternoon.

Fazalhaq Farooqi leads the MI Emirates pace attack, and his new-ball overs are where the champions usually set the tone. If Farooqi removes the Warriorz openers cheaply, the middle order is left to rebuild against Rashid Khan — and that is a hard place to bat from.

Mohammad Nabi is the Warriorz' most experienced all-rounder, and his off-spin and late-order hitting give Sharjah two ways to stay in a game. Against the champions, Nabi's calm in the middle overs is worth as much as any single wicket.

Waqar Salamkheil was last season's leading bowler award winner, and his wrist-spin is the Warriorz' sharpest weapon. On an Abu Dhabi surface that has helped spinners before, his four overs could be the difference between a close loss and a famous win.

MI Emirates lead this fixture 6–3 from nine meetings across the four previous seasons, with no abandoned games. The record looks comfortable, but the last two meetings tell a different story: the sides split them, and both were decided by single-digit margins.

The last two meetings came in the 2025-26 season. MI Emirates won Match 6 on 7 December 2025 by 4 runs, then the Warriorz won Match 14 on 14 December 2025 by 6 runs. Two games, ten runs between them across both results — this fixture has become too close to call, whatever the overall record says.
