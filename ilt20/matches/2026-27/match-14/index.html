Gulf Giants vs Sharjah Warriorz, Tuesday, 1 December 2026 — evening session at Sharjah Cricket Stadium, Sharjah

Match 14 of 34 in ILT20 Season 5. Second meeting of the season — sharjah warriorz hosted the first, in match 2 on 22 november.

The 14th match of the Gulf Giants season is an evening session at the Sharjah Cricket Stadium on Tuesday, 1 December 2026. It is the second meeting between these sides in Season 5 — the Warriorz hosted the first, in Match 2 on 22 November — so both camps already know what the other side looks like this year.

Both teams have played four league matches by now, which makes this a genuine mid-table test rather than an early-season guess. A win is worth two points, with one point each for a no-result, and the top four after 30 league matches go through to the playoffs.

Gulf Giants are the inaugural champions, from 2023, and their Season 5 squad is built around a top order most sides would envy: Jos Buttler captains, with Heinrich Klaasen, David Miller and Liam Livingstone behind him. Simon Helmot coaches, and the bowling has Alzarri Joseph, Shamar Joseph and Tabraiz Shamsi. If the top order fires, few totals are out of reach.

Sharjah Warriorz are still chasing a first title, with James Vince keeping the captaincy and Adrian Birrell coaching. Their attack is the calling card: Adil Rashid and Waqar Salamkheil, last season's leading bowler award winner, lead a group that also includes Adam Milne and Mohammad Nabi. The batting has Tom Banton and Sikandar Raza to match the Giants' firepower.

The points on offer and the playoff race

Match 14 of 30 league games. The top four qualify for the playoffs.

Four games in, the table stops forgiving slow starts. Both sides have played four matches, and a win here is the difference between sitting in the playoff places and sitting in the chasing pack with ten days of the league stage still to go. The return-leg nature of the fixture adds an edge: whoever lost Match 2 gets an immediate chance to level the season series.

The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, followed by the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah and the final on Sunday 20 December in Dubai. Two points in Sharjah on 1 December count exactly the same as two points in the final week.

The players who can decide it

Jos Buttler captains the Gulf Giants and opens the batting, which makes him the first problem the Warriorz have to solve. His side has won five of eight against Sharjah, and when Buttler bats deep the Giants' totals tend to follow him.

Heinrich Klaasen is the middle-order batter who turns good Giants totals into match-winning ones. With Buttler, Miller and Livingstone around him, Klaasen rarely bats under scoreboard pressure — and that is when he is at his most destructive.

James Vince keeps the Warriorz captaincy and sets the tone at the top of their order. His side won the most recent meeting between these teams, by 11 runs in December, and a second straight win over the Giants would be a statement about where Sharjah stand this season.

Adil Rashid leads the Warriorz attack, and on a Sharjah surface his four overs are the ones the Giants' big hitters have to survive rather than attack. Alongside Waqar Salamkheil, he gives Sharjah a spin pair most teams would rather avoid in the middle overs.

Gulf Giants lead this fixture 5–3 from eight meetings across the four previous seasons, with no abandoned games between the sides. The record is closer than the headline suggests: the Warriorz won the most recent meeting, and the two sides split their two games last season.

The last two meetings came in the 2025-26 season: the Giants won Match 10 on 10 December 2025 by 6 wickets, then the Warriorz won Match 16 on 15 December 2025 by 11 runs. Both games were at Sharjah, and both were decided in the closing overs — a pattern this return leg could easily repeat.
