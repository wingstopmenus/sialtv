MI Emirates vs Gulf Giants, Monday, 7 December 2026 — evening session at Zayed Cricket Stadium, Abu Dhabi

Match 22 of 34 in ILT20 Season 5. Second meeting of the season — the giants hosted the first, in match 5 on 24 november.

The 22nd match of the MI Emirates season is an evening session at the Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi on Monday, 7 December 2026. It is the second meeting between these sides in Season 5 — the Giants hosted the first, in Match 5 on 24 November — so the return comes with the first game still fresh in both camps.

Both sides have played seven league matches by now, with only three league games left after this one. A win is worth two points, with one point each for a no-result, and the top four after 30 league matches reach the playoffs.

The exact start time has not been confirmed, so this page lists the evening session only. The scorecard will appear here once the match is played.

How both sides arrive in Season 5

MI Emirates are the 2024 champions, runners-up in January, now coached by Mark Boucher and captained by Nicholas Pooran. Their squad pairs Pooran with Jonny Bairstow and Kusal Perera at the top, Rashid Khan and Fazalhaq Farooqi with the ball, and Romario Shepherd and Sherfane Rutherford as the finishers. Four wins from ten against the Giants says this is a fixture they respect, not one they own.

Gulf Giants are the inaugural champions, from 2023, and their Season 5 squad is built around a top order most sides would envy: Jos Buttler captains, with Heinrich Klaasen, David Miller and Liam Livingstone behind him. Simon Helmot coaches, and the bowling has Alzarri Joseph, Shamar Joseph and Tabraiz Shamsi. The Giants lead the head-to-head 5–4, and they will want to keep it that way with the final week coming.

The points on offer and the playoff race

Match 22 of 30 league games. The top four qualify for the playoffs.

Seven games each and three to go after this: the league stage is nearly done, and a win here is the difference between a top-two finish and a scramble through the Eliminator. The sides split their two meetings last season, so neither can claim this fixture — but the winner on Monday takes the season series and the momentum into the final week.

The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, followed by the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah and the final on Sunday 20 December in Dubai. The top two go straight to Qualifier 1, which is why a win against a direct rival in the final week is worth more than the two points on the table.

The players who can decide it

Every name below links to that player's profile. Squads are as announced for Season 5.

Sherfane Rutherford is the MI Emirates finisher, and his late hitting is the reason the champions can chase totals that look too big. Against a Giants attack with Joseph pace and Shamsi spin, Rutherford's last five overs are where this game is most likely won or lost.

Chris Jordan is the MI Emirates death bowler, and his yorkers at the end of the innings are aimed squarely at a Giants middle order full of finishers. If Jordan's last two overs go for under twenty, the champions have usually done enough.

Azmatullah Omarzai is the Giants' all-rounder, and his batting in the middle order and his bowling give the inaugural champions two ways to hurt MI Emirates. Omarzai's versatility is the reason the Giants can balance their side without weakening either discipline.

Shamar Joseph leads the Giants' pace attack with genuine speed, and an evening game in Abu Dhabi is his kind of stage. If Joseph removes the MI Emirates openers early, the champions' middle order is left to rebuild under scoreboard pressure.

Gulf Giants lead this fixture 5–4 from ten meetings across the four previous seasons, with one game abandoned — Match 18 on 27 January 2023, which never got going. The sides have never been separated by more than one win in this fixture.

The last two meetings were split in the 2025-26 season: the Giants won Match 3 on 4 December 2025 by 6 wickets, then MI Emirates won Match 26 on 23 December 2025 by 8 wickets. A split again this season would leave the head-to-head exactly where it started — and this game decides who takes the season series.
