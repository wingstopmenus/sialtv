Final — ILT20 Season 5 Playoff Preview

Final, Sunday, 20 December 2026 — Evening session in Dubai

The final of ILT20 Season 5 is played at Dubai International Stadium on Sunday 20 December 2026, in the evening session. The winner of Qualifier 1 meets the winner of Qualifier 2, and the winner of this match lifts the title. There is no second chance left — one evening in Dubai decides the champions.

The two teams are not known yet. The league stage ends with Match 30 on Monday 14 December, and the playoffs run from Wednesday 16 December: Qualifier 1 in Abu Dhabi, the Eliminator in Dubai on Thursday, and Qualifier 2 in Sharjah on Friday. Saturday 19 December is a rest day before the final. The scorecard will be published on this page once the final is played.

Dubai hosts 15 of the season’s 34 matches, including the opener on 21 November and this final — the same ground where the season began, and where it will end. The ILT20 title has been won by four different teams in four seasons, and Season 5 will either add a fifth name or give one of them a second title.

How the playoffs work

The title is decided by four playoff matches. The top four teams from the league stage qualify, seeded one to four by the points table after 30 matches. The seeds will only be known after Match 30, the last league match, on Monday 14 December.

Qualifier 1, on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, is Seed 1 against Seed 2, and the winner goes straight to the final. The loser plays again in Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December in Sharjah. The Eliminator, on Thursday 17 December in Dubai, is Seed 3 against Seed 4 — the loser is eliminated, and the winner goes to Qualifier 2. The winner of Qualifier 2 takes the second final place, and meets the Qualifier 1 winner here.

There are two rest days in the playoff week: Tuesday 15 December, between the league stage and the playoffs, and Saturday 19 December, between Qualifier 2 and the final. Every playoff match is played in the evening session, and the exact start times are still to be announced.

The final berth, and what losing costs

The final is the only match of the season where the prize is the title itself. The winner of Qualifier 1 arrives with four days of rest since Wednesday 16 December; the winner of Qualifier 2 arrives with one day of rest since Friday 18 December, and possibly with two knockout wins already behind them in the week. Rest against momentum is the shape of every ILT20 final played under this format, and this one is no different.

The four past finals show how open this tournament is. Gulf Giants won the first title in 2023, beating Desert Vipers by 7 wickets. MI Emirates won in 2024, beating Dubai Capitals by 45 runs. Dubai Capitals won in 2025, beating Desert Vipers by 4 wickets. Desert Vipers won in 2025-26, beating MI Emirates by 46 runs. Four seasons, four different champions — and no team has yet won the title twice.

Whoever wins on Sunday will also settle the season’s story. A win for the Qualifier 1 winner would reward the side that finished top of the league and took the direct route. A win for the Qualifier 2 winner would complete the longest road in the tournament — three knockout wins in five days, or two for a top-two seed that lost Qualifier 1. Either way, the champions will have earned the title in the hardest week of the season.

The road to the final

The winner of Qualifier 1 took the shortest road: one win on Wednesday 16 December in Abu Dhabi, then four days to prepare for this final. The winner of Qualifier 2 took the longer road. If they were the loser of Qualifier 1, they won again on Friday 18 December in Sharjah to reach the final. If they were the winner of the Eliminator, they won on Thursday 17 December in Dubai and again on Friday in Sharjah — two knockout wins in two days — to reach the final.

Both roads end here, at Dubai International Stadium, on Sunday 20 December. The rest day on Saturday 19 December gives the Qualifier 2 winner one day to recover before the final, and gives the Qualifier 1 winner a fifth day since their last match. The final is a single match, in the evening session, with the title on the line and no further game to fall back on.

The teams will be named on this page once Qualifier 2 is played on Friday 18 December. Until then, the only honest statement is that the two finalists are the winners of Qualifier 1 and Qualifier 2 — and that one of them will be crowned ILT20 Season 5 champions on Sunday night.

No head-to-head yet — the teams are not decided

The two finalists are not known yet, so there is no head-to-head for the final. The winner of Qualifier 1 and the winner of Qualifier 2 will be decided by Friday 18 December, and this page will name them — and their past meetings — once Qualifier 2 is played.

Frequently asked questions

When is the ILT20 Season 5 final?

The final is played in the evening session on Sunday 20 December 2026 at Dubai International Stadium, Dubai. It is Match 34, the last match of the season, played after a rest day on Saturday 19 December. An exact start time is still to be confirmed.

Who plays in the final?

The winner of Qualifier 1 plays the winner of Qualifier 2. Neither team is known yet — Qualifier 1 is played on Wednesday 16 December, the Eliminator on Thursday 17 December, and Qualifier 2 on Friday 18 December. This page will name the two finalists once Qualifier 2 is played.

Who are the defending champions?

Desert Vipers. They won the 2025-26 title in January, beating MI Emirates by 46 runs in the final. Four different teams have won the four ILT20 titles so far — Gulf Giants in 2023, MI Emirates in 2024, Dubai Capitals in 2025 and Desert Vipers in 2025-26 — and no team has won it twice.
