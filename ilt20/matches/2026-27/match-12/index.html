Desert Vipers vs Gulf Giants, Sunday, 29 November 2026 — evening session, Dubai

The 12th match of the DP World ILT20 Season 5 is Desert Vipers against Gulf Giants on Sunday, 29 November 2026, in the evening session, at the Dubai International Stadium in Dubai. It is the evening game at a ground hosting 15 matches this season.

This is the twelfth match of the season and the second on Sunday, following Abu Dhabi Knight Riders against Sharjah Warriorz in the afternoon. Dubai hosts 15 matches this season, and this is the fifth of them. After this game the league takes its first breath before Match 13 on Monday. Babar Azam captains the defending champions, and the Vipers' record in this fixture is the story of the match: seven wins from ten, including a tied game in December 2025 that they won in the Super Over. James Foster's side has made a habit of winning the close ones against the Giants.

Jos Buttler captains the Giants, and the top order is the reason this side is feared: Buttler, Heinrich Klaasen, David Miller and Liam Livingstone. The inaugural champions have won only three of ten against the Vipers, but their batting can take any game away in the last five overs.

Two points, and the first marker of the season

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Each side plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs.

Two points are on offer, and for the Giants they would be worth more than that: a win in Dubai against the champions would end the first week on a high and prove their batting-heavy build can beat the best. For the Vipers, a win would keep the title defence on track and the record intact.

Twelve matches in nine days is a fast start, and by Sunday night the table will have a clear shape. The top four reach the playoffs from 16 December, and the sides that end the first week in the top four are usually the sides still there in December. Neither team will want to close the week with a defeat.

The players who can decide it

Babar Azam captains the defending champions, and his calm at the crease is the counterweight to the Giants' pace attack. If he bats through the middle overs, the Vipers' total will be hard to chase under lights in Dubai.

Joe Clarke bats at the top for the Vipers and scores quickly when he gets going. Against a Giants attack that includes Alzarri Joseph and Shamar Joseph, his start in the powerplay could set up the whole innings.

Jos Buttler captains the Giants and opens their batting. He is the most dangerous opener in this fixture, and the Vipers will plan their first six overs around keeping him quiet.

Tabraiz Shamsi bowls wrist-spin for the Giants, and his four overs in the middle of the innings are the spell the Vipers' middle order must survive. Against Babar Azam, his variations are the contest inside the contest.

Past meetings between these two sides

Ten meetings, and the Vipers lead 7–3. One of the ten, on 8 December 2025, finished level and was settled by a Super Over in the Vipers' favour, and they won the other meeting that month by 8 wickets. The Giants' last win in this fixture came in the early seasons — the Vipers have won the last five meetings.

Vipers lead the head-to-head 7–3 across 10 past meetings, from the 2023 season to last season.
