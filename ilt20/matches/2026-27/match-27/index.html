Desert Vipers vs Abu Dhabi Knight Riders, Saturday, 12 December 2026 — Evening session in Dubai

Desert Vipers meet Abu Dhabi Knight Riders at Dubai International Stadium on Saturday 12 December 2026, in the evening session, in the 27th match of the International League T20 Season 5. The defending champions against the side still chasing a first title — and a match that could shape both seeds at the top of the table.

The Vipers will have played eight league matches before this one, with one more to come against Gulf Giants on Sunday evening. The Knight Riders will also have played eight, with one more to come against Dubai Capitals on Monday — the last league match of the season. Two points are on offer, as in every league match, and with only four league matches left after this one, neither side can treat it as a free hit.

Babar Azam captains the Vipers for his first ILT20 season, taking the armband as the defending champions begin their title defence’s final week. Jason Holder captains the Knight Riders, with Dwayne Bravo as coach. The full scorecard will be added here once the match is over.

How both sides arrive in Dubai

With the season still to start, neither side carries Season 5 form into this match — there are no results to read and no points on the board. The table below sets out what is known before the first ball: captain, coach and titles.

TeamCaptainCoachILT20 titles
Desert VipersBabar AzamJames Foster2025-26
Abu Dhabi Knight RidersJason HolderDwayne BravoNone yet

The Vipers are the defending champions, winners of the 2025-26 title in January, when they beat MI Emirates by 46 runs in the final. Babar Azam takes the captaincy for his first ILT20 season, and James Foster coaches a squad that still carries the champions’ core: Fakhar Zaman and Shimron Hetmyer with the bat, Naseem Shah and Shadab Khan with the ball. The title defence has been the story of their season from the first match.

Abu Dhabi Knight Riders, coached by Dwayne Bravo, have no ILT20 title after four seasons, and Holder’s captaincy is the fresh start they have built the season around. The squad mixes experience — Kane Williamson, Sunil Narine, Moeen Ali — with Finn Allen at the top of the order. A win in Dubai would be their strongest statement yet that this is the season the wait ends.

The run-in and the race for the top four

Four teams qualify for the playoffs, and the difference between finishing first or second and finishing third or fourth is a second chance: the top two meet in Qualifier 1 on 16 December, and the loser of that match gets another game in Qualifier 2 on 18 December. The third and fourth seeds meet in the Eliminator on 17 December, where the loser goes home. Two points here could be the difference between a seed with a safety net and a seed without one.

For the Vipers, the run-in is two matches in two days — the Knight Riders here, then Gulf Giants on Sunday evening. For the Knight Riders, the run-in is this match and then Dubai Capitals on Monday 14 December, the final league match of the season. Both sides will know by Sunday morning whether they need a win in their last game or whether they can already start planning for the playoffs.

After this match, three league matches remain: MI Emirates against Dubai Capitals and Gulf Giants against Desert Vipers on Sunday 13 December, and Abu Dhabi Knight Riders against Dubai Capitals on Monday 14 December. Tuesday 15 December is a rest day before the playoffs begin.

The players who can decide it

All names below link to the player’s profile. With no Season 5 matches played, the players below are picked for what they have done before and what their teams ask of them — not for numbers from this season, which do not exist yet.

Babar Azam captains Desert Vipers and bats at the top, so his innings will set the shape of the champions’ total. Beside him, Fakhar Zaman is the opener who can score quickly from the first ball, while Shadab Khan gives the side wickets in the middle overs and runs down the order. With the new ball, Naseem Shah is the bowler the Knight Riders will plan around, and Mujeeb Ur Rahman adds mystery spin.

For Abu Dhabi Knight Riders, Jason Holder captains and leads the attack as the side’s all-rounder, while Kane Williamson anchors the batting and Finn Allen provides the fast start at the top. Sunil Narine remains the spinner every opposition plans for, and Moeen Ali can turn a match with bat or ball.

The meeting of Babar and Williamson — two of the most technically assured batters in the tournament — is the subplot to watch, but the match may be decided earlier, by whether Allen and Fakhar give their sides the powerplay start Dubai so often rewards.

The most recent meeting came in the 2025-26 season, on 16 December 2025, when Abu Dhabi Knight Riders won by 1 run — the closest finish between the two sides. The meeting before that, on 5 December 2025, went the Vipers’ way by 2 wickets. The very first meeting, in January 2023, was a Vipers win by 111 runs, still the biggest margin in the fixture.

The Vipers have won the majority of the eight meetings, but the Knight Riders have won three, including two of the four meetings in the 2024 season. Neither side has beaten the other twice in a row since the Vipers won the first two meetings in 2023.
