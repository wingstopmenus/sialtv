Playoffs — four matches, 16 to 20 December

The top four teams on the points table qualify for the playoffs. Qualifier 1 and the Eliminator are separated by a day, Qualifier 2 is in Sharjah on Friday 18 December, and 19 December is a rest day before the final in Dubai on Sunday 20 December.

The teams in each playoff match depend on the final league standings — they are confirmed once the league stage ends on Monday 14 December. The ILT20 Points Table shows who is in line for each place.

Venues — three grounds, 34 matches

Dubai International Stadium hosts 15 matches, including the opener on 21 November and the final on 20 December. Sharjah Cricket Stadium hosts 8 matches, including Qualifier 2. Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi hosts the remaining 11 matches, including Qualifier 1 on 16 December and the first Abu Dhabi match on 26 November.

How to follow the season

SialTV will update the ILT20 Results page after every match and the points table after every match day. During play, the ILT20 Live Scores page carries the latest score. In India, matches are on Zee TV and ZEE5. Broadcasters for Pakistan, the UAE and Gulf, the UK and the USA are still to be confirmed for Season 5.

Frequently asked questions

When does ILT20 Season 5 start?

On Saturday 21 November 2026, with Desert Vipers against MI Emirates at Dubai International Stadium, in the evening session. The season ends with the final on Sunday 20 December, also in Dubai.

When is the ILT20 final?

Sunday 20 December 2026, at Dubai International Stadium, in the evening session. The finalists are the winners of Qualifier 1 and Qualifier 2.

How many matches are in ILT20 Season 5?

Thirty-four: 30 league matches, then four playoffs — Qualifier 1 on 16 December, the Eliminator on 17 December, Qualifier 2 on 18 December and the final on 20 December.

What time do ILT20 matches start?

Exact clock times have not been announced yet. Each fixture is listed here as an afternoon or evening session, in Gulf Standard Time. The times will be added once the league announces them.

Where are ILT20 matches played?

At three grounds in the UAE: Dubai International Stadium (15 matches), Sharjah Cricket Stadium (8 matches) and Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi (11 matches).
