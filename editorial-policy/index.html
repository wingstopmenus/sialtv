Editorial Policy

How SialTV checks its facts, how it corrects mistakes, and what it will never publish.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

How facts are checked

Dates, times, scores, squads, points tables and broadcasters are taken from official league sources or reliable, verifiable records, and checked before publication. When two sources disagree, the page follows the most authoritative one and notes the difference. Anything that cannot be confirmed is shown as to be announced, never guessed.

Corrections

Mistakes are corrected as soon as they are found, and the page's last-updated date is changed at the same time. Readers and rights holders can report an error through the Contact Us page.

What SialTV never publishes

SialTV never links to pirated streams, never publishes betting content, and never presents a predicted fixture list as if it were official. The site is an independent informational website, not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, club, board or federation.

Independence

No league, team or broadcaster pays for coverage on SialTV, and no advertiser influences what is published. Editorial decisions are made on usefulness to the reader alone.
