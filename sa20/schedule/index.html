SA20 Season 5 Schedule — Fixtures, Dates & Venues

SA20 Season 5 runs from Sunday 17 January to Sunday 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches across six grounds in South Africa. Sunrisers Eastern Cape, the three-time champions, open the season against Pretoria Capitals at St George’s Park — and the final is at Newlands on 21 February. Every start time is in South African Standard Time (SAST, UTC+2).

SA20 Season 5 fixtures — all 30 league matches

Every league match is at one of six grounds, and every team plays ten — five at home, five away. Four Saturdays carry two matches, a 1:00 PM start followed by a 5:30 PM start: 23 and 30 January, 6 and 13 February. Sunday matches start at 3:30 PM; the rest start at 5:30 PM. Times are in SAST (UTC+2) — 5:30 PM SAST is 8:30 PM in Pakistan, 3:30 PM is 6:30 PM, and 1:00 PM is 4:00 PM.

Playoffs — four matches, 16 to 21 February

The top four teams on the points table reach the playoffs. Qualifier 1 is at Boland Park on Tuesday 16 February, the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 are at the Wanderers on 17 and 19 February, and the final is at Newlands on Sunday 21 February.

Venues — six grounds, 34 matches

Each of the six grounds hosts exactly five league matches. Newlands in Cape Town hosts six in all, with the final on 21 February. The Wanderers in Johannesburg hosts seven, more than any other ground — five league matches plus the Eliminator and Qualifier 2. Boland Park in Paarl hosts six, with Qualifier 1 on 16 February. St George’s Park in Gqeberha hosts the opener on 17 January; SuperSport Park in Centurion and Kingsmead in Durban host five league matches each.

How to follow the season

SialTV will update the SA20 Results page after every match and the points table after every match day. During play, the SA20 Live Scores page carries the latest score. In South Africa matches are on SuperSport, in Pakistan on Apex, in India on JioStar, in the UK on Sky Sports, in Australia on Fox Sports and in North America on Willow by Cricbuzz. Broadcasters for the UAE and Middle East and the rest of the world are still to be confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

When does SA20 Season 5 start?

On Sunday 17 January 2027, with Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals at St George’s Park, Gqeberha, at 3:30 PM SAST. The season ends with the final on Sunday 21 February, at Newlands in Cape Town.

When is the SA20 final?

Sunday 21 February 2027, at Newlands in Cape Town, starting at 3:30 PM SAST. The finalists are the winners of Qualifier 1 and Qualifier 2.

How many matches are in SA20 Season 5?

Thirty-four: 30 league matches, then four playoffs — Qualifier 1 on 16 February, the Eliminator on 17 February, Qualifier 2 on 19 February and the final on 21 February.

What time do SA20 matches start?

Most matches start at 5:30 PM SAST. Sunday matches start at 3:30 PM, and four Saturdays — 23 and 30 January, 6 and 13 February — have a 1:00 PM match before the evening match. SAST is UTC+2, so 5:30 PM SAST is 8:30 PM in Pakistan.

Where are SA20 matches played?

At six grounds across South Africa: Newlands in Cape Town, the Wanderers in Johannesburg, St George’s Park in Gqeberha, Kingsmead in Durban, SuperSport Park in Centurion and Boland Park in Paarl.
