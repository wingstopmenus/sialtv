The story so far

Paarl Royals have never won the SA20, but they have never been short of talent either. Their 19-player squad was already complete before the auction on 7 October, so like Durban’s Super Giants they bought nobody and watched the bidding from the sidelines. Azmatullah Omarzai and Joe Root were the pre-signings, with Root as the wildcard pick.

David Miller has captained the side since the first season, and Trevor Penney has been head coach since Season 3. Seventeen players were retained, including Miller, Dan Lawrence, Sikandar Raza, Mujeeb Ur Rahman and Kyle Verreynne, along with Ottniel Baartman, Bjorn Fortuin and Hardus Viljoen. Lhuan-dre Pretorius, JJ Basson and Nqobani Mokoena keep under-23 places.

What to expect: a balanced side that plays five home games at Boland Park — and knows the ground better than anyone, because Boland Park also hosts Qualifier 1 on 16 February. The season starts at home, against Joburg Super Kings on Monday 18 January, and the last league game is away to the champions on 13 February.

What to watch

The spin is the strength. Mujeeb Ur Rahman, Gudakesh Motie, Sikandar Raza and Bjorn Fortuin give Penney four different spin options, and on a Boland Park surface that grips, that attack can strangle a chase. Omarzai and Delano Potgieter add seam-bowling all-round balance.

The batting has class and power: Joe Root’s control, David Miller’s finishing, Dan Lawrence’s aggression and Lhuan-dre Pretorius’ youth. Root as the wildcard is the signing that could lift the whole order — if he plays the season in full.

What to watch: the home games. Five league matches at Boland Park, plus Qualifier 1 on the same ground if they finish in the top two. Paarl Royals have never had a clearer path to a home playoff — they just have to earn it first.

Standings and the playoff race

No matches have been played yet, so there is no table to show — and this page will not invent one. The first standings appear after Match 1 on 17 January 2027, and SialTV will update this page after every match day.

The format is simple: 30 league matches, then four playoff games. The top four teams after the league stage reach the playoffs — Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, and the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands. The points a win is worth in 2027 are still to be confirmed — the official playing conditions had not been published at the 3 November 2026 check — so exact qualification maths cannot be stated yet. What is certain is the shape of the race: ten matches, four playoff places, and no room for a slow start.
