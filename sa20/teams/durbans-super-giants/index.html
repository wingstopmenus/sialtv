The story so far

Durban’s Super Giants have never won the SA20, but no side in Season 5 looks more settled on paper. Their 19-player squad was already complete before the auction on 7 October, so they bought nobody and sat out the bidding while the other teams filled their remaining places. Sam Curran was the one pre-signing.

The retained group of 18 keeps the core of last season’s side: captain Aiden Markram, Jos Buttler, Devon Conway, Kane Williamson and Heinrich Klaasen as the wildcard, along with Gerald Coetzee, Liam Livingstone and Sunil Narine. It is a squad full of players who have won T20 leagues around the world — just not this one, together.

What to expect: a side that should be there in February. Markram has captained the side since Season 4 and Klusener has been head coach since the first season, so there is no learning curve. The first home game is against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at Kingsmead on Thursday 21 January, and the last league match is against Joburg Super Kings at Kingsmead on 13 February.

What to watch

The batting is the best on paper in the league. Buttler, Conway, Williamson, Klaasen, Markram and Livingstone in one top six is a line-up most international sides would envy, and Curran and Narine bat deep enough that the side rarely runs out of hitters.

The bowling has variety too: Coetzee’s pace, Narine’s mystery spin, Simon Harmer’s control and Kwena Maphaka’s youth. The question is the same one that has followed this side for four seasons — whether a group of stars becomes a team when the pressure comes in February.

What to watch: the home run. Five home league games at Kingsmead, including the champions on 21 January and Joburg Super Kings to finish on 13 February. If Durban’s win their home games, the playoffs take care of themselves.

Standings and the playoff race

No matches have been played yet, so there is no table to show — and this page will not invent one. The first standings appear after Match 1 on 17 January 2027, and SialTV will update this page after every match day.

The format is simple: 30 league matches, then four playoff games. The top four teams after the league stage reach the playoffs — Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, and the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands. The points a win is worth in 2027 are still to be confirmed — the official playing conditions had not been published at the 3 November 2026 check — so exact qualification maths cannot be stated yet. What is certain is the shape of the race: ten matches, four playoff places, and no room for a slow start.
