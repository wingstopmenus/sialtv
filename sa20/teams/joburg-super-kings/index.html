The story so far

Joburg Super Kings have never won the SA20. Four seasons, no title — and Season 5 starts with a question nobody at the club has answered yet: who captains the side? Faf du Plessis was released on 5 August 2026, then bought back at the auction on 7 October for R2.7 million. Some listings name him as captain, but the club has not confirmed it, so this page does not present him as captain.

The auction was the busiest of any team. Six places were filled: Faf du Plessis, 18-year-old Jason Rowles for R2.9 million — the most expensive signing of the auction — Dayyaan Galiem, Kieron Pollard, Blessing Muzarabani and Andries Gous. Pollard has since withdrawn from Season 5, so 18 players are available. Jason Holder came in as the pre-signing and Jamie Overton as the wildcard.

What to expect: a side with experience everywhere and a leadership question hanging over it. Rilee Rossouw, James Vince, Akeal Hosein and Jason Holder were all retained, and the batting has du Plessis, Rossouw, Vince and Donovan Ferreira. The first home game is against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at the Wanderers on Saturday 23 January.

What to watch

The batting is deep and proven. Faf du Plessis, Rilee Rossouw and James Vince have all made runs in this league before, Donovan Ferreira can finish an innings, and Jason Holder and Jamie Overton bat deep enough that the tail starts late. On the Wanderers’ quick pitch, this top order can post big totals.

The bowling has variety: Akeal Hosein’s left-arm spin, Blessing Muzarabani’s height, Duan Jansen’s left-arm pace and Holder’s experience. The worry is the death overs, where Joburg have leaked runs in past seasons — and Pollard’s withdrawal takes away one of the finishers they had planned around.

What to watch: the captaincy call. Whoever takes the job inherits a squad that should make the playoffs on paper, and the answer will shape everything — the batting order, the bowling changes, the mood. Until it is named, every Joburg preview starts with the same sentence.

Standings and the playoff race

No matches have been played yet, so there is no table to show — and this page will not invent one. The first standings appear after Match 1 on 17 January 2027, and SialTV will update this page after every match day.

The format is simple: 30 league matches, then four playoff games. The top four teams after the league stage reach the playoffs — Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, and the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands. The points a win is worth in 2027 are still to be confirmed — the official playing conditions had not been published at the 3 November 2026 check — so exact qualification maths cannot be stated yet. What is certain is the shape of the race: ten matches, four playoff places, and no room for a slow start.

Frequently asked questions

Who captains Joburg Super Kings?

Nobody has been confirmed for Season 5. Faf du Plessis was released on 5 August 2026 and bought back at the auction on 7 October for R2.7 million, but the club has not named him — or anyone else — as captain.

Has Kieron Pollard left Joburg Super Kings?

Pollard was bought at the auction for R1.5 million but has since withdrawn from Season 5, so he will not play for Joburg Super Kings this season.

Who is Joburg Super Kings’ most expensive signing?

Jason Rowles, signed for R2.9 million at the 7 October 2026 auction. The 18-year-old all-rounder was the most expensive signing of the whole auction.

When is Joburg Super Kings’ first match?
