The story so far

Pretoria Capitals came closer than ever in 2026. They reached the final, where Sunrisers Eastern Cape beat them, and they are still chasing a first title. Season 5 gives them an immediate chance to measure themselves against the champions: the opener, on Sunday 17 January, is away at St George’s Park against the same side.

Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, and Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4. The squad was built around 12 retained players — Maharaj, Dewald Brevis, Lungi Ngidi and Andre Russell as the wildcard, plus Sherfane Rutherford and Connor Esterhuizen — then Phil Salt and Matthew Short as pre-signings, and five auction buys led by Rassie van der Dussen for R500,000.

What to expect: a side that knows the final is the target, not the surprise. Brevis and Salt can set a game alight at the top, Russell and Rutherford finish it, and Ngidi leads the attack. The first home game is against Durban’s Super Giants at SuperSport Park on Tuesday 19 January.

What to watch

The batting has two gears: power and more power. Phil Salt and Dewald Brevis at the top, Matthew Short and Sherfane Rutherford through the middle, Andre Russell to finish — on a good Centurion pitch this side can chase or set anything. Temba Bavuma, bought for R200,000, adds calm when the power hitters fail.

The bowling leans on Lungi Ngidi and Keshav Maharaj’s control, with Russell and Keemo Paul as the change-up options. The risk is the death overs: if the quicks have an off day, there is not much mystery spin to fall back on.

What to watch: the opener. Pretoria Capitals against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on 17 January is a final rematch on day one, and a win away from home would announce that last season’s runners-up are coming back for the trophy, not just the playoffs.

Standings and the playoff race

No matches have been played yet, so there is no table to show — and this page will not invent one. The first standings appear after Match 1 on 17 January 2027, and SialTV will update this page after every match day.

The format is simple: 30 league matches, then four playoff games. The top four teams after the league stage reach the playoffs — Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, and the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands. The points a win is worth in 2027 are still to be confirmed — the official playing conditions had not been published at the 3 November 2026 check — so exact qualification maths cannot be stated yet. What is certain is the shape of the race: ten matches, four playoff places, and no room for a slow start.
