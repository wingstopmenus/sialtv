Sunrisers Eastern Cape are the team to beat in SA20 Season 5. They have won this league three times — 2023, 2024 and 2026 — they open the season at home against Pretoria Capitals, and Tristan Stubbs captains them with Adrian Birrell coaching since the first season.

The story so far

No team has owned the SA20 like Sunrisers Eastern Cape. Three titles in four seasons — 2023, 2024 and 2026 — and the only final they lost in that run was 2025, against MI Cape Town. They won the title back in 2026, and they defend it in Season 5 with the same captain and coach who have been there from the start.

Tristan Stubbs has captained the side since Season 4, and Adrian Birrell has coached it since the first season. The squad around them is settled: Quinton de Kock, Matthew Breetzke, Anrich Nortje and Marco Jansen as the wildcard were all retained, and the pre-signings added Mitchell Marsh — playing his first SA20 season — and Bangladesh leg-spinner Rishad Hossain. The auction filled the last two places with Nandre Burger and Craig Overton.

What to expect: the champions defending at home. Season 5 opens at St George’s Park on Sunday 17 January against Pretoria Capitals, the side they beat in the 2026 final, and five of their ten league games are at home in Gqeberha. Nobody in this league knows how to win it better.

What to watch

The core is the strength. De Kock, Breetzke, Stubbs and Jansen have played together for years, and the bowling has Nortje’s pace, Adam Milne’s experience, Jansen’s left-arm swing and Rishad Hossain’s leg-spin. This is a side that knows its roles and trusts them.

Mitchell Marsh is the new name to watch. Australia’s T20 captain plays his first SA20 season, and his power at the top of the order could be the difference in tight games. Craig Overton and Nandre Burger add seam depth, so the attack stays fresh across ten league games.

What to watch: the opener. Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals is a repeat of the 2026 final, played at the same ground, and a win on 17 January would tell the rest of the league that the champions have not moved an inch.

Standings and the playoff race

No matches have been played yet, so there is no table to show — and this page will not invent one. The first standings appear after Match 1 on 17 January 2027, and SialTV will update this page after every match day.

The format is simple: 30 league matches, then four playoff games. The top four teams after the league stage reach the playoffs — Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, and the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands. The points a win is worth in 2027 are still to be confirmed — the official playing conditions had not been published at the 3 November 2026 check — so exact qualification maths cannot be stated yet. What is certain is the shape of the race: ten matches, four playoff places, and no room for a slow start.

Frequently asked questions

How many SA20 titles have Sunrisers Eastern Cape won?

Three — in 2023, 2024 and 2026. They lost the 2025 final to MI Cape Town, then won the title back in 2026 by beating Pretoria Capitals.

Who captains Sunrisers Eastern Cape?

Tristan Stubbs. He has captained the side since Season 4. Adrian Birrell has been head coach since the first season.

Who are Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s new signings?

Mitchell Marsh and Rishad Hossain as pre-signings, then Nandre Burger (R500,000) and Craig Overton (R1.5 million) at the auction.

When is Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s first match?

Match 1, the season opener, against Pretoria Capitals at St George’s Park, Gqeberha, on Sunday 17 January 2027 at 3:30 PM SAST.
