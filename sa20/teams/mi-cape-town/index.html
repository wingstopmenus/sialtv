MI Cape Town — Squad, Fixtures & SA20 Season 5

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025, and they go into Season 5 as the team everyone has to beat. Rashid Khan still captains the side, Robin Peterson still coaches it, and the squad has been strengthened with Trent Boult, Wiaan Mulder and Shimron Hetmyer.

The story so far

MI Cape Town are the 2025 champions. They beat Sunrisers Eastern Cape in the 2025 final, ending the Sunrisers’ run of back-to-back titles, and they arrive in Season 5 with the same captain and coach who delivered that first trophy. Rashid Khan has captained the side since the first season; Robin Peterson has been head coach since Season 2.

The champions did not stand still. Will Jacks came in as the pre-signing, then the auction added Wiaan Mulder for R2.6 million, Trent Boult and Shimron Hetmyer for R1.5 million each, and three young players — Lehan Botha, Daniel Smith and Oliver Whitehead — for R200,000 each. Twelve players were retained, including Nicholas Pooran, Ryan Rickelton, Kagiso Rabada as the wildcard and George Linde.

What to expect: a side built to defend. The bowling has real depth — Rashid Khan, Kagiso Rabada, Trent Boult, Corbin Bosch and Dane Piedt — and the batting has Pooran, Rickelton, Reeza Hendricks and Hetmyer around it. The first test comes quickly: the champions’ first home game is against Joburg Super Kings at Newlands on Wednesday 20 January, and their last league match, against Pretoria Capitals at Newlands on 14 February, could decide a top-two finish.

What to watch

The bowling is the strength. Rashid Khan and Kagiso Rabada are two of the best T20 bowlers in the world, Trent Boult brings swing with the new ball, and Corbin Bosch, Dane Piedt and George Linde give Peterson options for every surface. On a good Newlands pitch this attack can defend almost anything.

The batting has power all the way down. Ryan Rickelton and Nicholas Pooran can take a game away in ten overs, Reeza Hendricks steadies the top order, and Hetmyer and Will Jacks add hitting in the middle. Wiaan Mulder, bought for R2.6 million, is the new piece — a seam-bowling all-rounder who can bat in the top six.

What to watch: whether the champions handle being hunted. Every side raises its game against the title holders, and MI Cape Town’s first two home games — Joburg Super Kings, then Paarl Royals — come before they have settled. A fast start at Newlands would set the tone for the defence.

Standings and the playoff race

No matches have been played yet, so there is no table to show — and this page will not invent one. The first standings appear after Match 1 on 17 January 2027, and SialTV will update this page after every match day.

The format is simple: 30 league matches, then four playoff games. The top four teams after the league stage reach the playoffs — Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, and the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands. The points a win is worth in 2027 are still to be confirmed — the official playing conditions had not been published at the 3 November 2026 check — so exact qualification maths cannot be stated yet. What is certain is the shape of the race: ten matches, four playoff places, and no room for a slow start.

Frequently asked questions

Who captains MI Cape Town?

Rashid Khan. He has captained MI Cape Town since the first SA20 season, and he led them to their first title in 2025.

When did MI Cape Town win the SA20?

In 2025. They beat Sunrisers Eastern Cape in the final for their first title. Sunrisers Eastern Cape won the title back in 2026.

Who are MI Cape Town’s new signings for Season 5?

Will Jacks as the pre-signing, then Wiaan Mulder (R2.6 million), Trent Boult and Shimron Hetmyer (R1.5 million each) at the auction, plus Lehan Botha, Daniel Smith and Oliver Whitehead.

When is MI Cape Town’s first match?

Match 4, against Joburg Super Kings at Newlands, Cape Town, on Wednesday 20 January 2027 at 5:30 PM SAST. Their first away game is at Boland Park against Paarl Royals on 24 January.
