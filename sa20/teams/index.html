SA20 Season 5 Teams — Captains, Coaches & Titles

Six teams play SA20 Season 5, from 17 January to 21 February 2027 across South Africa. Two of them have won this league; four are still chasing a first title. Here are all six — captains, coaches, home grounds and titles — in one place.

The six teams

MI Cape Town

Captain: Rashid Khan · Coach: Robin Peterson · Home: Newlands, Cape Town

The 2025 champions. Rashid Khan has captained the side since the first season.

Joburg Super Kings

Captain: to be announced · Coach: Albie Morkel · Home: Wanderers, Johannesburg

Still chasing a first title. Faf du Plessis was bought back at the 7 October 2026 auction, but no captain has been confirmed for Season 5. Kieron Pollard, also bought at the auction, has since withdrawn.

Sunrisers Eastern Cape

Captain: Tristan Stubbs · Coach: Adrian Birrell · Home: St George’s Park, Gqeberha

Three-time champions — 2023, 2024 and 2026. They open the season at home against Pretoria Capitals.

Durban’s Super Giants

Captain: Aiden Markram · Coach: Lance Klusener · Home: Kingsmead, Durban

Still chasing a first title. Their 19-player squad was complete before the auction, with Sam Curran as the pre-signing.

Pretoria Capitals

Captain: Keshav Maharaj · Coach: Sourav Ganguly · Home: SuperSport Park, Centurion

Still chasing a first title. They reached the 2026 final and lost to Sunrisers Eastern Cape.

Paarl Royals

Captain: David Miller · Coach: Trevor Penney · Home: Boland Park, Paarl

Still chasing a first title. Their 19-player squad was complete before the auction, and Boland Park also hosts Qualifier 1.

Two champions, four chasers

Two of the six teams have won this league. Sunrisers Eastern Cape have three titles — 2023, 2024 and 2026 — and lost the 2025 final to MI Cape Town, who won their first title that year. Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and lost to Sunrisers Eastern Cape. Durban’s Super Giants, Joburg Super Kings and Paarl Royals have never lifted the trophy — Season 5 is their next chance.

Every team plays 10 league matches, each opponent twice, and the top four reach the playoffs. The full fixture list is on the SA20 Schedule page, and the standings, once play starts, are on the SA20 Points Table page.

What each team plays for

The league stage runs from 17 January to 14 February, then Qualifier 1 on 16 February at Boland Park, the Eliminator on 17 February at the Wanderers, Qualifier 2 on 19 February at the Wanderers, and the final on 21 February at Newlands. The points a win is worth in 2027 are still to be confirmed — the official playing conditions had not been published at the 3 November 2026 check — so they are marked TBC on the points table until the league publishes them. Frequently asked questions

How many teams play in SA20?

Six teams: MI Cape Town, Joburg Super Kings, Sunrisers Eastern Cape, Durban’s Super Giants, Pretoria Capitals and Paarl Royals. Each team plays 10 league matches.

Who are the defending SA20 champions?

Sunrisers Eastern Cape won in 2026 — their third title — so they defend the title in Season 5. Tristan Stubbs captains them, and Adrian Birrell has coached them since the first season.

Which SA20 team has won the most titles?

Sunrisers Eastern Cape, with three: 2023, 2024 and 2026. MI Cape Town have one, from 2025. Durban’s Super Giants, Joburg Super Kings, Pretoria Capitals and Paarl Royals have none yet.
