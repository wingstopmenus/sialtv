Plan your visit

Season 5 runs from Sunday 17 January to Sunday 21 February 2027 at six grounds: Newlands in Cape Town, the Wanderers in Johannesburg, St George’s Park in Gqeberha, Kingsmead in Durban, SuperSport Park in Centurion and Boland Park in Paarl. Each ground hosts five league matches. The opener is at St George’s Park on 17 January, Qualifier 1 is at Boland Park on 16 February, the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 are at the Wanderers on 17 and 19 February, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February. Most matches start at 5:30 PM SAST; Sunday matches start at 3:30 PM, and four Saturdays have a 1:00 PM match first.

If you are travelling for a match, check the fixture date and the ground on the schedule page first — Cape Town, Johannesburg, Gqeberha, Durban, Centurion and Paarl are six different cities, and the same weekend can have matches at two of them.
