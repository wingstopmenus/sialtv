Kwena Maphaka — the young left-arm quick with real pace

Kwena Maphaka is back with Durban’s Super Giants for SA20 Season 5, retained in one of the squad’s under-23 places. The young left-arm fast bowler is one of the most exciting young quicks in South African cricket, and the Super Giants kept him.

An under-23 place, and a rare left-arm angle

Maphaka was one of the 18 players Durban’s retained, keeping an under-23 place in a 19-man squad. Left-arm pace is a rare commodity in any T20 league, and a young bowler who has it — with genuine speed — is worth holding on to.

He bowls left-arm fast, swings the new ball into right-handers, and hurries batters with bounce. That angle is awkward by itself: right-handers see the ball coming across them, left-handers see it leaving them, and both have to adjust. In a pace attack otherwise built on right-armers, Maphaka is the point of difference.

The attack around him sets the standard. Gerald Coetzee leads it, Daryn Dupavillon and Eathan Bosch support it, and the spin group is led by Sunil Narine. Maphaka does not have to carry the attack — he has to learn from it, stay ready, and take his chance when the schedule or an injury opens a door.

Pace, swing, and a left-arm angle nobody else has

Maphaka’s value is in what he does differently. Every other quick in the attack is right-armed — he is not.

A new-ball spell from a left-armer changes the whole start of an innings: the field settings change, the batters’ plans change, and the captain gets a wicket-taking option from a different angle. Maphaka’s job, when the chance comes, is simple: run in hard, swing the new ball, and let the pace do the talking.

A settled squad with room for a young quick

Durban’s Super Giants kept 18 players and bought nobody at the auction, so the attack Maphaka returns to is the one he already knows. Aiden Markram captains, Lance Klusener coaches, and Kingsmead in Durban hosts five home league games in Season 5.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Super Giants’ first home game against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on 21 January. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. If the season goes his way, Maphaka could be its breakout bowler.
