SA20 Season 5 · Sunrisers Eastern Cape

Mitchell van Buuren — the young batter kept at R200,000

Mitchell van Buuren is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained at a listed R200,000. The young domestic batter is the champions' low-cost cover in a top order full of established names.

See Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Retained at the lowest figure in the squad

Van Buuren was retained at R200,000, the smallest figure in the Sunrisers squad, and one of the 15 players the champions kept for Season 5. That is how franchise squads are built: the headline names take the money and the attention, and the cheaper retentions fill out the squad with players who can step in when injuries or international calls bite.

As a batter, he joins one of the strongest top orders in the league. Quinton de Kock, Matthew Breetzke and Jordan Hermann are ahead of him, so his first job is not to displace anyone — it is to learn from them, train hard, and be ready for the moment an injury or a rotation opens a door.

What he brings

Cover is van Buuren's first contribution. A 30-match league stage across six weeks tests every batting group, and teams that reach the playoffs are the ones whose cover batters can step in without the standard dropping. Van Buuren is that cover — a batter who can slot into the top or middle order.

He also brings a fresh eye. Young batters play without the weight of expectation, and in T20 cricket that freedom can be dangerous for bowling sides. If van Buuren gets a game, he will be playing with nothing to lose — and the champions kept him precisely because they believe that freedom can turn into runs.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Jordan Hermann is the other young batter in the squad. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
