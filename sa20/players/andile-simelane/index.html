Andile Simelane — the young all-rounder kept in the under-23 place

Andile Simelane is back with Durban’s Super Giants for SA20 Season 5, retained in one of the squad’s under-23 places. The young all-rounder is the club’s investment in the future, learning his trade in a dressing room full of internationals.

See the Super Giants squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

An under-23 place in a squad of stars

Simelane was one of the 18 players Durban’s retained, keeping an under-23 place in a 19-man squad. Those places exist for exactly this: a young player spending a full season inside a professional setup, training with the best and waiting for a chance.

As an all-rounder he offers the most useful kind of cover. A player who can bat and bowl gives the captain options on selection day, and in a 30-match league stage, with travel and quick turnarounds, cover players often get their chance sooner than anyone expects.

The dressing room around him sets the standard. He trains alongside Sunil Narine, Liam Livingstone and Sam Curran — three all-rounders who have won T20 leagues around the world — and alongside a pace attack led by Gerald Coetzee. For a young player, that is an education no academy can match.

Energy, versatility, and a chance to grow

Simelane’s value is flexibility. He can fill more than one gap, and in a long season, that is what gets a young player into the side.

Whether he bats in the lower order or bowls a few overs of pace, his job is the same: do the small things well and make the selectors remember his name. The Super Giants did not retain him to sit still — they retained him because they believe he can grow into a first-team player, and Season 5 is his chance to show it.

A settled squad with room for a young player

Durban’s Super Giants kept 18 players and bought nobody at the auction, so the squad Simelane returns to is the one he already knows. Aiden Markram captains, Lance Klusener coaches, and Kingsmead in Durban hosts five home league games in Season 5.

The SA20 season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Super Giants’ first home game against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on 21 January. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. If injuries or form open a door, Simelane is the young all-rounder waiting to walk through it.
