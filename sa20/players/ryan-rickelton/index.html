SA20 Season 5 · MI Cape Town

Ryan Rickelton — the opener-keeper the champions kept

Ryan Rickelton is back with MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The domestic wicketkeeper-batter opens the batting left-handed and keeps wicket, two jobs that make him one of the most valuable players in the squad.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Two jobs, one player, retained

Rickelton was retained because he solves two selection problems at once. He opens the batting, where his left-handed aggression gives the innings a fast start, and he keeps wicket, which frees up a place for an extra bowler or batter. In a 19-man squad, a player who does both is worth more than either skill on its own.

He bats with intent from the first ball. Left-handers who attack the new ball force bowlers to change their lines early, and Rickelton's powerplay hitting has set up many a MI Cape Town innings. When he gets going, the field spreads quickly and the bowlers run out of places to hide.

What he brings

The powerplay is Rickelton's territory. A fast start — 50 in the first six overs — changes the maths of the whole innings, letting the middle order play without scoreboard pressure. His left-handedness also matters: paired with a right-hander, he forces the bowling side into constant adjustments.

Behind the stumps, he is the keeper for one of the best spin attacks in the league. Standing up to Rashid Khan takes sharp hands and sharper concentration, and Rickelton's keeping lets the captain attack with his spinners knowing the stumping chance will be taken.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 and kept the core together, retaining Rickelton alongside Rashid Khan, Nicholas Pooran and Kagiso Rabada. Robin Peterson coaches the side at Newlands in Cape Town. Reeza Hendricks is the experienced opener who shares the top-order duties. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
