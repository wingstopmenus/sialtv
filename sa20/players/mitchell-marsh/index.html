Mitchell Marsh — the Australian captain playing his first SA20

Mitchell Marsh joins Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5 as a pre-signing, signed before the auction alongside Rishad Hossain. The Australia T20 captain plays his first SA20 season, and the champions signed him early for a reason.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A first SA20 season, signed before the auction

Marsh was one of two pre-signings Sunrisers made, the other being Rishad Hossain. Pre-signings are the players a club moves for first, before the bidding starts — and the champions chose Australia's T20 captain, a player who has led his country and won World Cups. It is the biggest name in the Sunrisers squad, and he was signed before anyone else could bid.

He is a top-order batter and seam bowler in the classic Australian mould: tall, powerful, and direct. Marsh hits the ball as hard as anyone in world cricket, and his bowling gives the captain a genuine sixth or seventh option. In a side chasing a fourth title, he is the kind of signing that lifts the whole dressing room.

What he brings

Power at the top is Marsh's first gift. He bats in the top order, takes on the new ball, and scores quickly against pace and spin alike. When he is set, the run rate climbs in a hurry — and his presence at the crease forces bowling sides to spend their best overs early, which leaves easier ones for the batters behind him.

He also brings leadership. Marsh has captained Australia in T20 cricket, and that experience matters in a dressing room led by a young captain in Tristan Stubbs. The two can share the load: Stubbs leads the side, Marsh leads by example, and the squad gets two captains for the price of one.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Tristan Stubbs is the captain Marsh supports. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

How did Mitchell Marsh join Sunrisers Eastern Cape?

As a pre-signing, agreed before the auction alongside Rishad Hossain. He was one of the club's two pre-signings.

Has Mitchell Marsh played SA20 before?

No. Season 5 is his first SA20 season. He arrives as Australia's T20 captain, signed early by the three-time champions.

Who captains Sunrisers Eastern Cape in SA20 Season 5?

Tristan Stubbs, who has captained the side since Season 4. Adrian Birrell is the head coach.
