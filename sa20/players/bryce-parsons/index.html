Bryce Parsons — the domestic all-rounder the Capitals kept

Bryce Parsons is back with Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The domestic all-rounder gives the side batting depth and bowling cover in a single squad place.

See the Capitals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Two skills, one squad place

Parsons was retained because he covers two jobs at once. He bats in the middle order and bowls spin — the combination every T20 captain wants, because it frees up a place for an extra specialist elsewhere.

As an all-rounder, he offers the most useful kind of cover. A player who can bat and bowl gives the captain options on selection day, and in a 30-match league stage, with travel and quick turnarounds, cover players often get their chance sooner than anyone expects.

Pretoria retained 12 players, and Parsons’s versatility is a big part of why he made the list. In a 19-man squad, the players who can do two things are the ones who give the coach room to react — to conditions, to injuries, to a match-up that only appears on the morning of the game.

Balance, depth, and a bowler who can bat

Parsons lengthens the batting and shortens the bowling worries. That is the whole point of a spin-bowling all-rounder.

His overs give Keshav Maharaj a second spin option to turn to, and his batting means the lower order never runs out of runs. He may not play every game, but in a long season, the all-rounders are the players whose turn always comes — and Parsons is ready for his.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. Parsons’s versatility could be a quiet edge in the title chase.
