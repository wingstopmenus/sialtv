Lehan Botha — the low-cost auction pick with a chance to grow

Lehan Botha joins MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5 as a domestic auction signing, bought for R200,000. The all-rounder is one of three players the champions picked up at the lowest auction price, and one of the squad's chances to grow a new name.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A R200,000 pick in a squad full of stars

Botha was bought alongside Daniel Smith and Oliver Whitehead, all three at R200,000 — the smallest price on MI Cape Town's auction list. That is how franchise squads are built: the headline signings take the money and the attention, and the cheaper picks fill out the squad with players who can step in when injuries or international calls bite.

As an all-rounder, Botha offers the most useful kind of cover. A player who can bat and bowl gives the captain options on team-selection day, and in a 30-match league stage, with travel and quick turnarounds, cover players often get their chance sooner than anyone expects.

What he brings

Botha's value is flexibility. He can slot into the lower middle order and bowl a few overs, which means Rashid Khan can pick him without unbalancing the side. For a young domestic player, that versatility is the fastest route into the XI — specialists wait for one vacancy, while all-rounders can fill several.

The learning curve is steep. He will train and travel with a squad that includes Rashid Khan, Kagiso Rabada and Trent Boult, and the dressing room alone is an education. If he gets a game, the job is simple: do the small things well, take the chance, and make the selectors remember his name.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town go into Season 5 as the 2025 champions, captained by Rashid Khan and coached by Robin Peterson at Newlands in Cape Town. The squad blends proven internationals with young domestic picks like Botha and Tristan Luus, who keeps an under-23 place. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
