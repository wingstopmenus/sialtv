JJ Basson — the young all-rounder keeping an under-23 place

A young all-rounder the Royals kept

JJ Basson was retained by Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, keeping one of the squad's under-23 places. The Royals retained 17 players in all, and Basson's place among them shows the franchise rates what he offers with bat and ball — young players are kept only when the coaches believe they can contribute, not just fill a slot.

The under-23 places matter in SA20. They are how franchises build for the future while staying competitive now, and Basson is one of three Royals in that bracket alongside Lhuan-dre Pretorius and Nqobani Mokoena. Season 5 is his chance to turn a retained place into regular cricket.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Basson gives the Royals a two-skills option. As an all-rounder he can be picked for either discipline on a given day, which lets the captain shape the side around the pitch and the opposition instead of carrying a specialist for every job.

His value is flexibility. If the Royals need an extra bowling option, he can give them overs; if the batting needs length, he can bat down the order. Young all-rounders who do both jobs honestly are rare, and the Royals clearly want to keep this one in their system.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Basson is part of a young core the Royals are building around, and Season 5 gives him a settled dressing room to grow in — the same captain, the same coach, and a squad that was not rebuilt at the auction. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park in Paarl.

Boland Park in Paarl is the Royals' home, hosting five league games and Qualifier 1 on 16 February. David Miller captains the side, as he has since the first season, and Trevor Penney is head coach. Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and after four seasons without a title, the Royals' wait is the story of their campaign.
