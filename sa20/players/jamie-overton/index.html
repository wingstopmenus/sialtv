Jamie Overton — the England all-rounder signed as the wildcard

Jamie Overton joins Joburg Super Kings for SA20 Season 5 as the side's wildcard pick. The England fast-bowling all-rounder was one of the Super Kings' additions around their 11 retained players, and he brings genuine pace and lower-order power.

Pace, power, and a wildcard worth using

Jamie Overton came in as the wildcard pick for Joburg Super Kings, the signing that completed the overseas group around Jason Holder, Akeal Hosein, Andries Gous, Blessing Muzarabani and James Vince. Wildcard picks are how SA20 sides add a star from outside the usual overseas pool, and Overton is a player built for T20 cricket.

Overton bowls fast and hits the ball hard, the two skills that make him a match-winner in the short format. His pace troubles batters in the middle overs, and his batting can change a game in the last five — the kind of player who wins you matches from nowhere.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Joburg Super Kings get a fast-bowling all-rounder who can do both jobs at a high level. Overton's pace gives the coach another strike option alongside Muzarabani and Holder, and his batting lengthens the order so the Super Kings can keep attacking even after losing early wickets.

He also gives the side a point of difference. Most of the Super Kings' overseas picks are batters or spinners; Overton is the overseas quick who bats. In a squad that needed pace and power, the wildcard was spent well.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

Overton is the Super Kings' wildcard pick, joining a squad built from 11 retained players, one pre-signing in Jason Holder, and six auction buys. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at the Wanderers in Johannesburg.

Joburg Super Kings are coached by Albie Morkel, and the captain for Season 5 is yet to be confirmed. Their home is the Wanderers in Johannesburg, which hosts five home league games. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Kings are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.
