Hardus Viljoen — the experienced quick back in the Royals attack

Hardus Viljoen is back with Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the domestic fast bowlers in a 19-player squad that was settled before the auction. The experienced quick adds genuine pace to an attack led by Ottniel Baartman.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Pace and experience at the top of the attack

Hardus Viljoen was one of 17 players Paarl Royals retained for SA20 Season 5. The domestic fast bowler has played franchise cricket around the world, and his experience is the point — a quick who has bowled in big games before does not need to learn how to handle the pressure.

Viljoen bowls with pace and bounce, and he can take the new ball or come back in the middle overs to break a partnership. Fast bowlers who can do both are the ones captains trust, and Viljoen's record in franchise cricket is why the Royals kept him.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a fast bowler who can set the tone with the new ball. Viljoen's pace makes batters hurry, and on South African pitches with bounce in them, a quick who hits the deck hard is a genuine threat in the powerplay.

He also gives the attack a senior head. With young quicks like Nqobani Mokoena learning their trade, Viljoen is the bowler they watch in training — and the one Miller turns to when the side needs a wicket.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Viljoen is part of the Royals' settled pace group, in a squad that did not change at the auction — 17 retained, two pre-signings, nobody bought. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park in Paarl.

Miller has led the Royals since Season 1 and Penney has coached them since Season 3, so this is a settled leadership group. Boland Park in Paarl is home, staging five home league games plus Qualifier 1 on 16 February. The season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Royals still looking for a first title.
