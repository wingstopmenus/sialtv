CJ King — the young all-rounder kept in the under-23 place

CJ King is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained in one of the squad's under-23 places and listed at R200,000. The young all-rounder is the champions' investment in the next generation, learning his trade in a title-winning dressing room.

An under-23 place in a champion squad

King was one of the 15 players Sunrisers retained, keeping an under-23 place in the squad. Those places exist for exactly this: giving a young player a full season inside a professional setup, training with the best and waiting for a chance. For an all-rounder, there may be no better classroom in South African cricket right now.

As an all-rounder, he offers the most useful kind of cover. A player who can bat and bowl gives the captain options on team-selection day, and in a 30-match league stage, with travel and quick turnarounds, cover players often get their chance sooner than anyone expects.

What he brings

Flexibility is King's first gift. He can slot into the lower middle order and bowl a few overs, which means Tristan Stubbs can pick him without unbalancing the side. For a young player, that versatility is the fastest route into the XI — specialists wait for one vacancy, while all-rounders can fill several.

He also brings the energy of a young player with everything to prove. Training and travelling with a squad that includes Mitchell Marsh, Quinton de Kock and Marco Jansen is an education in itself. If King gets a game, the job is simple: do the small things well, take the chance, and make the selectors remember his name.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. JP King holds the other under-23 all-rounder place in the same squad. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
