Temba Bavuma — the R200,000 buy with a captain’s brain

Temba Bavuma joins Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5 as a domestic auction signing, bought for R200,000. The top-order batter brings a Test captain’s calm and a wealth of international experience — for the smallest price on the Capitals’ auction list.

See the Capitals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A R200,000 buy, and a bargain at the price

Bavuma was bought alongside Keemo Paul, Ackeem Auguste and Josh Breed, all four at R200,000 — the smallest price on the Capitals’ auction list. For a batter who has captained South Africa, that price looks like good business.

He bats right-handed at the top of the order, where the job is to give the innings a start — and to read the game as it unfolds. Bavuma plays the ball late, picks the gaps, and rarely gives his wicket away. He is not the biggest hitter in the side, but he is one of the smartest batters in it.

His leadership is the bonus. Bavuma has captained South Africa in Tests and one-day cricket, so he brings a captain’s eye to a dressing room already led by Keshav Maharaj. Two captains in one squad is a luxury — and in a title chase, it is the kind of depth that matters.

Calm at the top, and a second captain in the room

Bavuma’s value is in the start he gives and the brain he brings. Both make the side better.

A solid opening partnership takes pressure off the middle order, and Bavuma’s calm lets the hitters around him play with freedom. With Salt and Short attacking at the top, Bavuma is the batter who can steady the innings when an early wicket falls — and the voice in the huddle who has been there before.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final.
