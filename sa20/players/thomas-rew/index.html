Thomas Rew — the young English keeper-batter back with the Royals

Thomas Rew is back with Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the overseas wicketkeeper-batters in a 19-player squad that was settled before the auction. The young Englishman is part of a Royals keeping group that also includes Kyle Verreynne, Lhuan-dre Pretorius and Asa Tribe.

A young keeper the Royals kept

Thomas Rew was one of 17 players Paarl Royals retained for SA20 Season 5. The overseas wicketkeeper-batter is a young English player the Royals clearly rate, because overseas places are precious in SA20 and franchises do not spend one on a keeper they do not believe in.

Rew's job is to keep wicket and add batting depth, competing for the gloves with Verreynne and Pretorius. Four keepers in one squad sounds like a lot, but it means the Royals always have a gloveman in form — and a young keeper learning from senior players around him.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a keeper-batter who can bat in the middle order and keep tidily behind the stumps. Rew's youth is part of the point: the Royals are keeping a player they expect to grow, and a season around Miller, Root and the senior keepers is a fast education.

He also gives the side cover. If a keeper is injured or out of form, the Royals do not have to change their balance — they have another gloveman ready. That depth is why squads carry more than one keeper, and Rew is part of it.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Rew is one of the overseas players the Royals kept, in a squad that was finished before auction day. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park in Paarl.

Paarl Royals are led by David Miller, who has captained the side since the first season, with Trevor Penney as head coach since Season 3. Their home is Boland Park in Paarl, which also hosts Qualifier 1 on 16 February. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Royals are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.
