Adam Milne — the fast bowler the champions kept

Adam Milne is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, one of the 15 players the club retained. The overseas fast bowler gives the champions genuine pace in an attack that already includes Anrich Nortje and Marco Jansen.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Pace, retained rather than replaced

Milne was retained, and for a fast bowler of his type that is a deliberate choice. He bowls right-arm fast, hits the pitch hard, and has spent years bowling at the death in franchise leagues around the world. Sunrisers kept three overseas players — Milne, James Coles and Lewis Gregory — and Milne is the quick among them.

His value is sharpest at the end of an innings. Death bowling is the hardest job in T20 cricket: the batters are set, the field is spread, and every mistake disappears over the rope. Milne's yorker and his slower-ball variations give Tristan Stubbs a proven option for the final overs.

What he brings

Pace is Milne's currency. He hurries batters with speed through the air and bounce off the pitch, and in a league where most attacks have one express bowler, Sunrisers have three — Milne, Nortje and Jansen. That depth means the captain can keep a fast bowler fresh for every phase of the innings.

He also brings experience of winning in franchise cricket. Milne has played T20 leagues across the world, and he knows what a title run looks like: the travel, the quick turnarounds, the pressure of knockout games. In a squad chasing a fourth title, that experience is worth as much as the pace.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Nandre Burger is the new left-arm seamer in the same attack. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

Was Adam Milne retained by Sunrisers Eastern Cape?

Yes. He was one of the 15 players Sunrisers retained for Season 5, so he did not go through the auction.

What is Adam Milne's role in the side?

He is an overseas fast bowler, valued for pace and death bowling in an attack led by Anrich Nortje and Marco Jansen.

How many SA20 titles have Sunrisers Eastern Cape won?

Three — in 2023, 2024 and 2026. They lost the 2025 final to MI Cape Town.
