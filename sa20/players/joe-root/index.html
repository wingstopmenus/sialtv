SA20 Season 5 · Paarl Royals

Joe Root — England's batting great, signed as the wildcard

Joe Root joins Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5 as the side's wildcard pre-signing, signed before the auction alongside Azmatullah Omarzai. The England batter is one of the finest players of his generation, and his arrival is the biggest signing of the Royals' off-season.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Class, calm, and a wildcard worth having

Joe Root was a pre-signing for Paarl Royals, brought in as the wildcard pick before the auction. Wildcard picks are how SA20 sides add a star from outside the usual overseas pool, and Root is the biggest name the Royals have ever signed — a batter with more than a decade at the top of the game.

Root's batting is built on timing and placement rather than brute force, which makes him a different kind of T20 batter to most of the Royals' power-hitters. He finds gaps, runs hard, and rarely gets out cheaply — the calm head at the top of an order full of hitters.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a batter who makes everyone around him better. Root's presence at the crease takes pressure off Miller and the finishers, because the scoreboard keeps moving even when the big shots are not coming — and his experience in big matches is exactly what a side chasing a first title needs.

He also gives the Royals a new way to win. On slow or two-paced pitches where the power-hitters struggle, Root's touch and timing can be the difference. A side that can win both ways — by power and by placement — is a side that can win a tournament.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Root is one of two new faces in the Royals squad — the other is Azmatullah Omarzai — and both were pre-signings, so the Royals never had to bid for them. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park in Paarl.

Paarl Royals are led by David Miller, who has captained the side since the first season, with Trevor Penney as head coach since Season 3. Their home is Boland Park in Paarl, which also hosts Qualifier 1 on 16 February. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Royals are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.

Frequently asked questions

How did Joe Root join Paarl Royals?

As a pre-signing, signed before the auction as the wildcard pick, along with Azmatullah Omarzai. The Royals' 19-player squad was already complete before auction day, so they bought nobody.

What is Joe Root's role for Paarl Royals?

He is a batter in the wildcard slot. The England star bats at the top of the order, bringing timing, placement and big-match experience to a side full of power-hitters.

Who captains Paarl Royals in SA20 Season 5?

David Miller, who has captained the side since the first season. Trevor Penney is head coach, a role he has held since Season 3.
