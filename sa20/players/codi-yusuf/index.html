Codi Yusuf — the seamer who does the quiet overs

Codi Yusuf is back with Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The domestic bowler gives the attack a reliable seam option through the middle overs.

See the Capitals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Retained for reliability, not headlines

Yusuf was retained because he does a job every attack needs and few bowlers enjoy: the middle overs, when the ball has stopped swinging and the batters are set. He bowls a hard length, varies his pace, and makes scoring difficult without giving away free runs.

He bowls right-arm fast-medium, and his strength is control. Batters cannot simply line him up, because he changes his pace and his length just enough to keep them guessing. In a league where the headline bowlers take the new ball and the death, the bowler who holds the middle together is the one who makes the attack work.

The attack around him is full of variety. Lungi Ngidi and Lizaad Williams bring pace, Gideon Peters brings bounce, and Keshav Maharaj brings spin. Yusuf is the seamer who links those phases — the bowler the captain turns to when the game needs steadying.

Control through the middle, when games are won

Yusuf’s value is in the overs nobody remembers — until the total stays ten runs short.

A tight middle spell builds the pressure that brings wickets at the other end. Yusuf bowls those spells: dot balls, hard lengths, and the occasional wicket when a batter gets impatient. He will not always bowl the first over or the last, but the Capitals’ season will depend on bowlers like him doing the work in between.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. Yusuf’s job is unglamorous — and essential.
