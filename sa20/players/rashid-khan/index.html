SA20 Season 5 · MI Cape Town

Rashid Khan — the captain leading the champions again

Rashid Khan captains MI Cape Town in SA20 Season 5, retained as the leader of the side he has captained since the first season. The leg-spinning all-rounder led the club to its first SA20 title in 2025, and now he leads the defence of it.

Captain since day one, champion since 2025

Rashid has captained MI Cape Town since the first SA20 season, which makes him the longest-serving captain in the club's short history. The 2025 title was the payoff: a first championship for a side that had waited four seasons, won under a captain who had been there from the start. Keeping him was the easiest retention decision the club had to make.

He is listed as an all-rounder, and the label fits. The leg-spin is the main act — a googly that has troubled the best batters in the world for a decade — but his lower-order hitting has won games of its own. As captain he also sets the tone: calm, direct, and demanding of the high standards his own bowling sets.

What he brings

With the ball, Rashid is the attack's centre of gravity. Captains build their plans around his four overs: when he bowls, who he bowls to, and what the field does behind him. His economy strangles innings in the middle overs, and his wickets break partnerships just as they are forming.

As captain, he brings continuity. The same captain, the same coach in Robin Peterson, and 12 retained players mean MI Cape Town start Season 5 as a settled side rather than a new one. In a short tournament, that familiarity — knowing who bowls the 18th over, who fields where — is worth runs.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 under Rashid, with Robin Peterson as head coach since Season 2. Newlands in Cape Town is the home ground, hosting five home league games, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. Kagiso Rabada, the wildcard, leads the pace attack Rashid directs. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.
