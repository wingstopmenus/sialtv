James Coles — the overseas all-rounder kept at R1 million

James Coles is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained at a listed R1 million. The overseas all-rounder is one of three overseas players the champions kept, alongside Lewis Gregory and Adam Milne.

See Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Retained among the overseas core

Coles was retained at R1 million, the same figure as fellow overseas all-rounder Lewis Gregory. Sunrisers kept 15 players, and the overseas trio of Coles, Gregory and Adam Milne all stayed — a sign that the champions trusted the balance of last season's squad and saw no need to rebuild it.

He is a left-handed batting all-rounder who bowls left-arm spin, a combination that gives the captain options in every phase. Left-handers who can also bowl are rare in any squad, and Coles offers the kind of flexibility that lets a side change shape without changing personnel.

What he brings

With the bat, Coles adds left-handed variety to the middle order. His batting is built on timing and placement rather than brute power, which makes him a useful foil for the hitters around him — he rotates the strike, finds the gaps, and keeps the scoreboard moving when the big shots are not coming off.

With the ball, his left-arm spin gives Tristan Stubbs a control option through the middle overs. Paired with Senuran Muthusamy's left-arm spin and Rishad Hossain's leg-spin, Sunrisers can bowl long spells of spin without repeating the same look, and Coles is the overseas piece of that puzzle.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Lewis Gregory is the other retained overseas all-rounder. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
