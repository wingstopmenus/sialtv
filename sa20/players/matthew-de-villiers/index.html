Matthew de Villiers — the domestic batter the Super Kings kept in the order

Matthew de Villiers is back with Joburg Super Kings for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the domestic batters in an 18-player squad. He is part of a batting group that runs from Faf du Plessis and James Vince down through Rilee Rossouw and Leus du Plooy.

Batting depth the Super Kings did not want to lose

Matthew de Villiers was one of 11 players Joburg Super Kings retained for SA20 Season 5. The domestic batter's retention says the franchise trusts his place in the batting order — in a league where overseas stars take the headlines, the South African batters decide whether a side has depth or just a top three.

De Villiers' job is to make the batting order longer and harder to bowl through. A side that can keep scoring after its stars are out is a side that posts winning totals, and domestic batters who can hold an innings together are the players who make that possible.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Joburg Super Kings get a batter who strengthens the middle of the order. With du Plessis and Vince carrying the star power and Rossouw providing the power, de Villiers is one of the players who has to turn good starts into big totals — the unglamorous work that wins league games.

He also gives the Super Kings a South African option in a batting group full of big names. Local batters who know the Wanderers and the conditions across South Africa are worth keeping, and the Super Kings kept de Villiers rather than going back to the auction for a replacement.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

De Villiers benefits from the Super Kings' settled core — 11 players retained, then a pre-signing and six auction buys to finish the squad. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad, with the Super Kings' first home game against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at the Wanderers on 23 January 2027.

Joburg Super Kings are coached by Albie Morkel, and the captain for Season 5 is yet to be confirmed. Their home is the Wanderers in Johannesburg, which hosts five home league games. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Kings are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.
