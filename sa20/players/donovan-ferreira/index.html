Donovan Ferreira — the big-hitting keeper-batter back with the Super Kings

Donovan Ferreira is back with Joburg Super Kings for SA20 Season 5, retained as the domestic wicketkeeper-batter in an 18-player squad. He is a big hitter in the middle order who also keeps wicket, the two-skills package that lets a side play an extra bowler.

Power with the bat, gloves behind the stumps

Donovan Ferreira was one of 11 players Joburg Super Kings retained for SA20 Season 5. The domestic wicketkeeper-batter is known for clean, powerful hitting — the kind of batter who can clear the ropes at the Wanderers, where the ball flies at altitude.

Ferreira's keeping is the other half of his value. A keeper-batter who can hold a place on his batting alone is a bonus; a keeper who can also win a game in ten balls is a match-winner. The Super Kings kept him because he does both.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Joburg Super Kings get a finisher who can close out chases and a keeper who tidies up behind the stumps. Ferreira's power gives the middle order its punch, and his keeping means the Super Kings do not have to carry a specialist gloveman — that place can go to another bowler or batter.

He also gives the side a South African who knows the Wanderers. Johannesburg's altitude makes the ball travel, and a hitter who knows how far it goes there is a dangerous player to have in the last five overs.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

Ferreira is part of the Super Kings' retained core, in a squad that added a pre-signing and six auction buys around 11 kept players. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at the Wanderers in Johannesburg.

The Wanderers in Johannesburg hosts the Super Kings' five home league games. Albie Morkel coaches the side and the captain is TBC — no captain has been named for Season 5. The season begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Super Kings still waiting for a first SA20 title.
