SA20 Season 5 · Sunrisers Eastern Cape

Matthew Breetzke — the top-order batter valued at R6.1 million

Matthew Breetzke is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained at a listed R6.1 million — the highest figure in the squad. The domestic top-order batter is the most valued player in a side that has won the title three times.

See Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The squad's highest-valued player

Breetzke was retained, and the R6.1 million figure attached to him is the largest in the Sunrisers squad — ahead of Anrich Nortje at R5 million and Quinton de Kock at R2.4 million. Whatever the mechanism, the message is the same: the three-time champions rate their top-order batter as the player they could least afford to lose.

He bats right-handed at the top of the order, where his job is to give the innings its shape. Breetzke plays the ball late, scores all around the wicket, and has the temperament to bat through the powerplay and into the middle overs. In a side full of power-hitters, he is the batter who makes sure the innings has a spine.

What he brings

Stability at the top is Breetzke's gift. T20 openers are often judged on strike rate alone, but a batter who is still there after ten overs has done something more valuable: he has kept the innings intact, seen off the new ball, and let the hitters behind him play without fear.

He also brings a growing reputation. Breetzke has become one of the most talked-about young batters in South African cricket, and the R6.1 million figure is the market's way of saying so. Season 5 is his chance to turn that valuation into the runs that carry Sunrisers to a fourth title.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Quinton de Kock is the other senior batter at the top of the order. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
