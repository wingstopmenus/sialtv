Jason Holder — the West Indies all-rounder signed before the auction

Jason Holder joins Joburg Super Kings for SA20 Season 5 as the side's one pre-signing, signed before the auction. The West Indies seam-bowling all-rounder is one of the most complete cricketers in the T20 game, and his signing meant the Super Kings went into auction day with 12 players already in place.

A pre-signing who does everything

Jason Holder was the pre-signing for Joburg Super Kings, signed before the auction. Pre-signings are how franchises lock in the players they want most, and Holder's signing says the Super Kings wanted a seam-bowling all-rounder badly enough to secure him early.

Holder bowls tall seam with bounce and control, and he bats with power in the middle order. He has captained West Indies in all formats, and his game is built for big occasions — he has won T20 games with the ball, with the bat, and in the field.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Joburg Super Kings get a player who balances the side on his own. Holder's seam bowling gives the coach a fourth seamer who can take the new ball or bowl at the death, and his batting lengthens the order so the Super Kings can keep attacking even after losing early wickets.

He also brings leadership without the title. A former West Indies captain who has played franchise cricket around the world sets standards in training and in the field, and in a side with a new coach and a captain still to be named, that experience is worth as much as the runs and wickets.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

Holder is the Super Kings' only pre-signing — the rest of the squad was built from 11 retained players and six auction buys. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at the Wanderers in Johannesburg.

Joburg Super Kings are coached by Albie Morkel, and the captain for Season 5 is yet to be confirmed. Their home is the Wanderers in Johannesburg, which hosts five home league games. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Kings are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.

Frequently asked questions

How did Jason Holder join Joburg Super Kings?

As a pre-signing, signed before the auction. The Super Kings went into auction day with 12 players already in place, then bought six more to complete their 18-player squad.

What is Jason Holder's role for Joburg Super Kings?

He is an overseas seam-bowling all-rounder. He bowls tall seam and bats with power in the middle order, giving the side two skills in one player.

When does SA20 Season 5 start?

On Sunday 17 January 2027. The opener is Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals at St George's Park, starting at 3:30 PM SAST.
