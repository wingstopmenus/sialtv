Patrick Kruger — the batting all-rounder kept at R450,000

Patrick Kruger is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained at a listed R450,000. The domestic all-rounder bats in the middle order and bowls seam, the kind of two-skill player every T20 squad leans on.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Retained for balance in the middle

Kruger was retained at R450,000, one of the 15 players Sunrisers kept for Season 5. He is a batting all-rounder: his main job is runs in the middle order, and his bowling is the bonus that makes him selectable even when the batting order is full. Players like that rarely make headlines, but captains love them — they make the XI work.

He is a powerful hitter in the middle order, the kind of batter who can clear the boundary against spin and pace alike. When the top order fires, Kruger's job is to keep the momentum going; when it fails, his job is to stop the collapse. Either way, he bats in the overs where games are decided.

What he brings

Middle-order power is Kruger's main currency. The overs between 10 and 16 decide whether a side posts 160 or 190, and Kruger is built for them: strong through the leg side, quick between the wickets, and unafraid to take on the spinners. His hitting gives the finishers behind him a platform to launch from.

With the ball, he is the captain's flexible option. A few overs of seam in the middle of the innings, a change of pace when the batters are set — Kruger gives Tristan Stubbs a bowler he can use without weakening the batting. That flexibility is why he was retained.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Senuran Muthusamy is the spin-bowling all-rounder in the same group. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
