Sam Curran — the pre-signing who completed the squad

Sam Curran joins Durban’s Super Giants for SA20 Season 5 as the club’s pre-signing — the one player signed before the auction. His arrival completed the 19-man squad, which is why the Super Giants bought nobody on auction day.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Signed before the auction, not at it

Curran was Durban’s only pre-signing, which tells its own story. Pre-signings are the players a club moves for first, before the bidding starts — and the Super Giants chose an England all-rounder who can do both jobs at the highest level.

He bowls left-arm seam, swings the new ball, and is one of the best death bowlers in the game, with a yorker and a slower ball that batters struggle to pick. He bats left-handed in the lower order, and his hitting has won games of its own — including a Player of the Tournament award at a T20 World Cup.

His signing is also the reason the squad was complete before 7 October. With 18 players retained and Curran added early, the Super Giants sat out the auction entirely while the other teams filled their remaining places.

Left-arm seam, late hitting, and big-match calm

Curran’s value is in the two phases that decide T20 games: the new ball, and the last five overs.

With the ball, he gives Aiden Markram a left-arm option no one else in the attack offers — swing up front, control in the middle, and yorkers at the death. With the bat, he lengthens an already deep order, so the top order can take risks knowing there is hitting still to come. And in a side chasing a first title, his big-match experience is worth as much as either skill.

A settled squad, still chasing the first title

Durban’s Super Giants are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons. Curran’s pre-signing completed the squad — 18 retained plus him — and the club bought nobody at the auction. Aiden Markram captains, Lance Klusener coaches, and Kingsmead in Durban hosts five home league games.

The SA20 season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Super Giants’ first home game against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on 21 January.
