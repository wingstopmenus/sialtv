Lungi Ngidi — the fast bowler who leads the attack

Lungi Ngidi is back with Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The domestic fast bowler is the spearhead of the attack — the quickest bowler in the side, and the one the captain turns to when a wicket is needed.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Pace, bounce, and a retained place

Ngidi was retained, which in a squad of 19 is the strongest signal a club can send to a bowler. He is South Africa’s frontline fast bowler: right-arm fast, tall, and happiest when the batter is on the back foot.

His bouncer is his calling card — steep, quick, and hard to play on South African surfaces — but there is more to him than pace. Ngidi swings the new ball when the conditions allow, bowls a full, fast yorker at the death, and competes on every single delivery. Batters do not get a rest against him.

The attack around him gives him support. Lizaad Williams and Gideon Peters bring pace, Codi Yusuf brings control, and Keshav Maharaj brings spin.

Wickets with the new ball, and fire at the death

Ngidi’s job is the hardest in the side — take wickets in the powerplay, then come back and defend the total.

A wicket or two in the first four overs changes the whole shape of a T20 innings, and Ngidi is the bowler most likely to provide them. At the death, his pace and his yorker make him the bowler Keshav Maharaj trusts with the final overs. Between those two phases, he is the presence that makes the whole attack look faster.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. If the Capitals finally lift the trophy, Ngidi’s pace will be a big reason why.

Frequently asked questions

Was Lungi Ngidi retained by Pretoria Capitals?

Yes. He was one of the 12 players the club retained for Season 5, so he did not go through the auction.

What is Lungi Ngidi’s role in the side?

He is the spearhead of the pace attack, a right-arm fast bowler who takes wickets with the new ball and at the death.

Where do Pretoria Capitals play their home games?

At SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Durban’s Super Giants on 19 January 2027.
