Shimron Hetmyer — the power-hitter bought to finish games

Shimron Hetmyer joins MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5 as an overseas auction signing, bought for R1.5 million. The left-handed batter is one of the most destructive middle-order hitters in the game, and the champions bought him to win the overs that decide matches.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A R1.5 million bet on middle-order power

Hetmyer was one of six players MI Cape Town bought at the auction, at R1.5 million — the same price as Trent Boult, and second only to Wiaan Mulder's R2.6 million among the club's auction signings. It is a clear statement of intent: the 2025 champions wanted more firepower in the middle order, and they paid for a player whose reputation is built on clearing boundaries.

He bats left-handed and scores quickly against both pace and spin, which makes him hard to plan against. Bowlers cannot simply bowl one type of delivery and hope — Hetmyer hits the ball cleanly to all parts of the ground, and once he is set, the run rate climbs in a hurry.

What he brings

The middle overs are where Hetmyer earns his fee. Between the powerplay and the death, many innings stall; Hetmyer is the batter who keeps the scoreboard moving, taking on the spinners and turning a par total into a winning one. His presence also protects the finishers behind him, because the bowling side has to spend its best overs on him.

He pairs naturally with Nicholas Pooran, another left-handed power-hitter, in the same middle order. Two left-handers who both hit the ball hard force captains into awkward bowling changes — and when both are going, few attacks have an answer.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 and have added power rather than stood still. Rashid Khan captains, Robin Peterson coaches, and Newlands hosts five home league games. Nicholas Pooran is the other left-handed finisher in the middle order. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
