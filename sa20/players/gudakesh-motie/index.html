Gudakesh Motie — the West Indian left-armer back in the spin group

Gudakesh Motie is back with Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the overseas bowlers in a 19-player squad that was settled before the auction. The West Indies left-arm orthodox spinner gives the Royals a wicket-taking option through the middle overs.

Left-arm spin from the West Indies

Gudakesh Motie was one of 17 players Paarl Royals retained for SA20 Season 5. The overseas spinner bowls left-arm orthodox, turning the ball away from right-handers and tying batters down with accuracy — the kind of bowling that wins T20 games in the middle overs, when the batting side wants to accelerate.

Motie's strength is that he takes wickets without leaking runs. Spinners who can do both are gold in T20 cricket, because the middle overs decide most matches: a quiet spell builds pressure, and pressure brings wickets at the other end.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a spinner who can bowl in any phase. Motie can open the bowling against a left-hander, hold the middle overs, or come back at the death, and his left-arm angle gives Miller a different look alongside Mujeeb Ur Rahman's mystery spin and Bjorn Fortuin's domestic left-arm orthodox.

He also gives the Royals an overseas spinner who knows how to win T20 games. With three front-line spinners in the squad, the Royals can shape their attack around the pitch — and Motie is the one who turns the ball most.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Motie is one of the overseas players the Royals kept, in a squad that was finished before auction day. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park, where spin has a real part to play on Paarl pitches.

Paarl Royals are led by David Miller, who has captained the side since the first season, with Trevor Penney as head coach since Season 3. Their home is Boland Park in Paarl, which also hosts Qualifier 1 on 16 February. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Royals are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.
