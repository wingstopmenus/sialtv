Connor Esterhuizen — the keeper-batter who gives the side options

Connor Esterhuizen is back with Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The domestic wicketkeeper-batter gives the side a keeper who can bat in the middle order — and cover behind the stumps.

See the Capitals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A keeper who bats, and a batter who keeps

Esterhuizen was retained because he covers two jobs at once. He keeps wicket and he bats in the middle order, which means the Capitals get a keeper and a batter in a single domestic slot — and the overseas slots can go elsewhere.

His batting is built on clean striking and a calm head. In the middle order, the job is rarely glamorous: rebuild after an early wicket, rotate the strike against spin, and be there at the end when the finishers need a partner. Esterhuizen plays that role with a straight bat and a clear plan.

Behind the stumps, he gives the captain a keeper who understands spin. Standing up to Keshav Maharaj, taking the stumping chances, and keeping the field honest — Esterhuizen does the keeping work that lets the bowlers attack. With Phil Salt also in the squad, the Capitals have two keepers and can choose by conditions.

Cover behind the stumps, runs in the middle

Esterhuizen’s value is in the balance he gives the side. One player, two roles, and a team that can be built around him.

When Salt opens and keeps, Esterhuizen is the cover who can step in without the side losing its shape. When the conditions suit an extra batter, he is the middle-order option. That flexibility is what kept him in the retained group — and in a long season, it is what gets a player into the side.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final.
