JP King — the young all-rounder kept in the under-23 place

JP King is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained in one of the squad's under-23 places. The young all-rounder is the champions' second investment in the next generation, alongside his namesake CJ King.

See Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

An under-23 place, and a famous surname

King was one of the 15 players Sunrisers retained, keeping an under-23 place in the squad. He shares the under-23 all-rounder places with CJ King — two young players with the same surname, the same role, and the same chance to grow inside a title-winning setup. It is a coincidence the broadcasters will enjoy and the selectors will watch closely.

As an all-rounder, he offers the most useful kind of cover. A player who can bat and bowl gives the captain options on team-selection day, and in a 30-match league stage, with travel and quick turnarounds, cover players often get their chance sooner than anyone expects.

What he brings

Flexibility is King's first gift. He can slot into the lower middle order and bowl a few overs, which means Tristan Stubbs can pick him without unbalancing the side. For a young player, that versatility is the fastest route into the XI — specialists wait for one vacancy, while all-rounders can fill several.

He also brings competition for the other young all-rounder in the squad. CJ King and JP King will push each other in training all season, and that rivalry is healthy: it raises the standard of both, and it gives the captain two hungry options when a place opens up. If either gets a game, he will be playing with nothing to lose.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. CJ King holds the other under-23 all-rounder place in the same squad. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
