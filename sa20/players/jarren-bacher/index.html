Jarren Bacher — the young all-rounder keeping an under-23 place

A young all-rounder the Super Kings kept

Jarren Bacher was retained by Joburg Super Kings for SA20 Season 5, keeping one of the squad's under-23 places. The young all-rounder was one of 11 players the Super Kings kept, and his place among them shows the franchise rates what he offers with bat and ball — young players are kept only when the coaches believe they can contribute.

The under-23 places matter in SA20. They are how franchises build for the future while staying competitive now, and Bacher is the Super Kings' young all-rounder in that bracket. Season 5 is his chance to turn a retained place into regular cricket.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Bacher gives the Super Kings a two-skills option. As an all-rounder he can be picked for either discipline on a given day, which lets the coach shape the side around the pitch and the opposition instead of carrying a specialist for every job.

His value is flexibility. If the Super Kings need an extra bowling option, he can give them overs; if the batting needs length, he can bat down the order. Young all-rounders who do both jobs honestly are rare, and the Super Kings clearly want to keep this one in their system.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

Bacher is part of a Super Kings squad that changed more than most — 11 retained, one pre-signing in Jason Holder, and six auction buys. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at the Wanderers in Johannesburg.

The Wanderers in Johannesburg hosts the Super Kings' five home league games. Albie Morkel coaches the side and the captain is TBC — no captain has been named for Season 5. The season begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Super Kings still waiting for a first SA20 title.
