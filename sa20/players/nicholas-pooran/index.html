Nicholas Pooran — the keeper-batter who finishes games

Nicholas Pooran is back with MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The overseas wicketkeeper-batter is one of the most feared finishers in T20 cricket, and the champions made sure he stayed.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A finisher the champions refused to lose

Pooran was retained, which in a squad of 19 is the strongest signal a club can send. He is the kind of player other teams build their auction plans around, and MI Cape Town made sure he never reached the auction at all. His keeping gives the side a second skill; his batting gives it a match-winner.

He bats left-handed and hits the ball as cleanly as anyone in the league. Against spin he is brutal, stepping down the pitch and clearing the boundary; against pace he is just as dangerous, with the bat speed to punish anything short. Bowlers do not get a rest when Pooran is at the crease — every ball is a scoring ball.

What he brings

The finish is Pooran's domain. Chasing a total, or setting one, the last five overs are where he does his damage, turning a tight game into a comfortable win. His presence at the crease changes the bowling side's plans: the yorkers have to be perfect, because anything less disappears.

Behind the stumps, he gives Rashid Khan a keeper who understands spin. Standing up to the leg-spin, taking the stumping chances, and keeping the field honest — Pooran does the keeping work that lets the bowlers attack. It is a two-skill package, and it is why he is one of the first names on the team sheet.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 and kept their match-winners, retaining Pooran alongside Rashid Khan, Ryan Rickelton and Kagiso Rabada. Robin Peterson coaches the side at Newlands in Cape Town. Shimron Hetmyer is the new left-handed power-hitter alongside him. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

Was Nicholas Pooran retained by MI Cape Town?

Yes. He was one of the 12 players MI Cape Town retained for Season 5, so he did not go through the auction.

What is Nicholas Pooran's role in the side?

He is an overseas wicketkeeper-batter and middle-order finisher, one of the most destructive left-handed hitters in T20 cricket.

Who captains MI Cape Town in SA20 Season 5?

Rashid Khan, who has captained the side since the first season. Robin Peterson is the head coach.
