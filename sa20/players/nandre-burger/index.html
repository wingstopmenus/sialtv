Nandre Burger — the left-arm seamer bought for R500,000

Nandre Burger joins Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5 as an auction signing, bought for R500,000. The left-arm seamer was one of only two players the champions needed at the auction, and he fills a specific gap in the attack.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

One of two auction signings

Burger was bought for R500,000, and he was one of just two auction signings Sunrisers made — the other was Craig Overton at R1.5 million. When a club only needs two players, every signing is deliberate, and Burger was chosen for a reason: the attack needed a left-arm seamer, and he is one.

He bowls left-arm fast-medium, swinging the new ball and hitting the pitch hard. Left-armers are a scarce resource, and Burger's angle across right-handers gives Tristan Stubbs a different look with the new ball — especially at St George's Park, where the sea breeze can help the ball move.

What he brings

The new ball is where Burger earns his place. A left-armer who swings it early can take the wickets that change a powerplay, and Sunrisers have built their title-winning sides on exactly that: early wickets, then spin through the middle. Burger gives the captain a new-ball option he did not have.

He also brings depth to a pace group that already includes Anrich Nortje, Marco Jansen and Adam Milne. Four quicks of that quality, plus the seam-bowling all-rounders, mean Sunrisers can rotate through a 30-match league stage without dropping their standard — and that is how titles are won.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Anrich Nortje leads the pace attack Burger joins. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

How much did Sunrisers pay for Nandre Burger?

R500,000 at the auction. He was one of only two auction signings, alongside Craig Overton at R1.5 million.

What is Nandre Burger's role for Sunrisers?

He is a domestic left-arm fast-medium bowler, signed to give the attack a new-ball option and a different angle.

Who captains Sunrisers Eastern Cape in SA20 Season 5?

Tristan Stubbs, who has captained the side since Season 4. Adrian Birrell is the head coach.
