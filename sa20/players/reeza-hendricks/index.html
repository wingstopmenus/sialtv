Reeza Hendricks — the steady opener the champions kept

Reeza Hendricks is back with MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The domestic batter gives the champions a calm, experienced presence at the top of the order.

See MI Cape Town’s squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Experience at the top, kept not bought

Hendricks did not need an auction bid to stay — MI Cape Town retained him, which says plenty about how the club rates him. In a squad full of overseas power, a reliable domestic opener is the glue: he knows the conditions, he knows the bowlers, and he has faced the new ball in South African cricket for years.

He bats right-handed and plays the kind of innings that rarely makes headlines but often decides games. While the power-hitters around him take risks, Hendricks can anchor the start, rotate the strike, and be there when the innings needs rebuilding after an early wicket.

What he brings

The first job is a solid start. T20 openers are judged on more than strike rate — a 30-ball 35 that keeps the innings intact can be worth more than a flashy 20. Hendricks reads the new ball well and is hard to dislodge early, which lets the hitters behind him play with freedom.

He also brings balance to the batting order. With Ryan Rickelton and Nicholas Pooran in the same squad, MI Cape Town can mix left and right handers and keep the bowlers changing their lines. Hendricks is the right-handed anchor that combination is built around.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 and kept the core of that side together, retaining 12 players including Hendricks, Rashid Khan and Ryan Rickelton. The captain is Rashid Khan, the coach is Robin Peterson, and the home ground is Newlands in Cape Town. Ryan Rickelton is the other wicketkeeper-batter in the top order. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

Was Reeza Hendricks retained by MI Cape Town?

Yes. He was one of the 12 players MI Cape Town retained for Season 5, so he did not go through the auction.

What is Reeza Hendricks' role in the side?

He is a domestic batter, an experienced right-handed opener who gives the top order stability alongside the power-hitters.

Who captains MI Cape Town in SA20 Season 5?

Rashid Khan, who has captained the side since the first season. Robin Peterson is the head coach.
