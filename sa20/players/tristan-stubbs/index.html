SA20 Season 5 · Sunrisers Eastern Cape

Tristan Stubbs — the captain leading the three-time champions

Tristan Stubbs captains Sunrisers Eastern Cape in SA20 Season 5, retained as the leader of the side he has captained since Season 4. The wicketkeeper-batter led the champions to the 2026 title, and now he leads the defence of it.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Captain since Season 4, champion since 2026

Stubbs has captained Sunrisers since Season 4, and the 2026 title was the payoff: a third championship for the most successful club in SA20 history, won under a young captain who had grown into the job. Keeping him was the easiest retention decision the club had to make — and keeping 15 players around him was the next easiest.

He is a middle-order batter and wicketkeeper, and the combination defines his captaincy. He reads the game from behind the stumps, sets fields with a keeper's eye for detail, and bats in the overs where games are decided. His own hitting — clean, powerful, and fearless against spin — has won Sunrisers games from positions that looked lost.

What he brings

With the bat, Stubbs is the finisher. The middle order is where T20 games turn, and Stubbs is the batter Sunrisers trust to be there at the end — hitting the spinners over the rope, running hard between the wickets, and keeping the chase on track when the asking rate climbs. His keeping gives the side a second skill in the same player.

As captain, he brings continuity. The same captain, the same coach in Adrian Birrell, and 15 retained players mean Sunrisers start Season 5 as a settled side rather than a new one. In a short tournament, that familiarity — knowing who bowls the 18th over, who fields where — is worth runs.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Adrian Birrell has been head coach since the first season, and St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games. Mitchell Marsh, Australia's T20 captain, is the new senior voice alongside Stubbs. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
