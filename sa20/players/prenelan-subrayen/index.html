Prenelan Subrayen — the off-spinning all-rounder back with the Super Kings

Prenelan Subrayen is back with Joburg Super Kings for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the domestic all-rounders in an 18-player squad. He bowls off-spin and bats in the lower order, the two-skills package that lets a side play an extra bowler without weakening the batting.

Off-spin and batting from the domestic ranks

Prenelan Subrayen was one of 11 players Joburg Super Kings retained for SA20 Season 5. The domestic all-rounder is an off-spinner who can hold a batting place, which is exactly what T20 sides want from their local players — one pick, two jobs.

Subrayen's off-spin gives the coach control against left-handers and a wicket-taking option in the middle overs. His batting is built for the back half of an innings, where he can hit the ball hard and finish totals. Players who do both honestly are hard to find, and the Super Kings kept theirs.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Joburg Super Kings get balance from Subrayen. His off-spin means the coach does not have to lean on the overseas bowlers for every over, and his batting means the Super Kings can bat deep without weakening the bowling — the trade-off that decides most T20 selections.

He also gives the side a South African spinner who knows the conditions. Local players who have bowled at the Wanderers and across South Africa do not need to learn the pitches, and in a short season that head start matters.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

Subrayen is part of the Super Kings' retained core — 11 players kept, then a pre-signing and six auction buys to finish the squad. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at the Wanderers in Johannesburg.

Albie Morkel is head coach for Season 5, while the captaincy is still TBC. The Wanderers in Johannesburg is home, staging five league games, and the season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027. The Super Kings have not won a title in four seasons, and that wait frames their campaign.
