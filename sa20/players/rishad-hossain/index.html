Rishad Hossain — the leg-spinner signed before the auction

Rishad Hossain joins Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5 as a pre-signing, signed before the auction alongside Mitchell Marsh. The Bangladesh leg-spinner gives the champions another spin option — and a different one to the left-armers already in the squad.

See Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Signed early, for a specific job

Rishad was one of two pre-signings Sunrisers made, the other being Mitchell Marsh. Pre-signings are the players a club moves for first, before the bidding starts, and the champions chose a leg-spinner for a reason: their spin group was heavy on left-arm orthodox, and a wrist-spinner who turns the ball the other way changes the whole equation.

He bowls leg-spin with a big, brave loop, the style that invites the drive and then beats it. Leg-spinners are the wicket-takers of T20 cricket — they go for runs, but they take the wickets that break partnerships, and captains accept the trade. At St George's Park, where the surface can grip for spin, Rishad should enjoy himself.

What he brings

Wickets in the middle overs are Rishad's currency. When the powerplay ends and batters look to build, a leg-spinner who tosses the ball up forces them into decisions: defend and fall behind, or attack and risk a wicket. Either way, the bowling side wins the over.

He also gives Tristan Stubbs a match-up weapon. Against teams stacked with right-handers, a leg-spinner turning the ball away is a natural threat; and his googly keeps even the best players honest. Paired with Senuran Muthusamy's left-arm spin, Sunrisers can now attack from both sides of the wicket.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Senuran Muthusamy is the left-arm spinner in the same group. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

How did Rishad Hossain join Sunrisers Eastern Cape?

What kind of bowler is Rishad Hossain?

He is a Bangladesh leg-spinner, signed to give the champions a wrist-spin wicket-taking option through the middle overs.

How many SA20 titles have Sunrisers Eastern Cape won?
