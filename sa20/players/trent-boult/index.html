Trent Boult — the new-ball swing MI Cape Town bought

Trent Boult joins MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5 as an overseas auction signing, bought for R1.5 million. The left-arm quick brings one of the most feared new-ball spells in white-ball cricket to Newlands.

See MI Cape Town’s squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A proven new-ball bowler, bought at auction

Boult was one of six players MI Cape Town bought at the auction, and the joint-priciest of the overseas pair alongside Shimron Hetmyer at R1.5 million each. Wiaan Mulder, at R2.6 million, was the only auction signing who cost more. The message from the bidding was clear: the champions wanted a bowler who could take wickets before the batters settled.

Left-arm swing is a rare commodity in any T20 league, and Boult has built a career on it. He moves the new ball both ways at pace, which makes him dangerous in the first two overs and awkward for right-handers who like to free their arms early. At Newlands, where the breeze and the early moisture can help the ball move, he should be a handful.

What he brings

The powerplay is where Boult earns his place. A wicket or two in the first four overs changes the whole shape of a T20 innings, forcing the batting side to rebuild instead of attack. Boult has spent a decade doing exactly that for New Zealand and in franchise leagues around the world.

He also brings control at the death. Boult's yorker and his slower-ball variations give Rashid Khan a second proven option for the final overs, alongside Kagiso Rabada. With the captain's leg-spin holding the middle overs, MI Cape Town now have wicket-taking threats in every phase of the innings.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025, and the champions have strengthened rather than stood still. Rashid Khan captains, Robin Peterson coaches, and Newlands hosts five home league games. Wiaan Mulder was the biggest auction buy at R2.6 million, with Boult and Hetmyer next at R1.5 million each. The SA20 returns on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

How much did MI Cape Town pay for Trent Boult?

R1.5 million at the auction, the same price as Shimron Hetmyer. Only Wiaan Mulder, at R2.6 million, cost MI Cape Town more.

What is Trent Boult's role for MI Cape Town?

He is the left-arm new-ball bowler, signed to take wickets in the powerplay and bowl at the death alongside Kagiso Rabada.

Who captains MI Cape Town in SA20 Season 5?

Rashid Khan, who has captained the side since the first season. Robin Peterson is the head coach.
