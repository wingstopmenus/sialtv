Sikandar Raza — the Zimbabwe star back for another Royals season

Sikandar Raza is back with Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the overseas all-rounders in a 19-player squad that was settled before the auction. The Zimbabwe star bowls off-spin and bats in the middle order, and he has a habit of winning games on his own.

Experience, off-spin, and a habit of winning games

Sikandar Raza was one of 17 players Paarl Royals retained for SA20 Season 5. The overseas all-rounder is one of the most valuable T20 players in the world, because he does three jobs: he bowls four overs of off-spin, he bats in the middle order, and he fields brilliantly.

Raza's batting is built for the middle overs, where he can rebuild an innings or accelerate one, and his off-spin is a wicket-taking option rather than a filler. He has carried Zimbabwe's hopes for years, and that experience of being the main man in a side shows in how calmly he plays the big moments.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a match-winner who can turn a game with either skill. Raza's off-spin gives Miller control against right-handers, and his batting gives the Royals a player who can finish a chase or rescue an innings — the kind of player who wins you two or three games a season by himself.

He also brings leadership without the title. A senior overseas player who has captained his country sets standards in training and in the field, and in a squad chasing a first title, that experience is worth as much as the runs and wickets.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Raza is one of the overseas players the Royals kept, in a squad that was finished before auction day. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park, where his off-spin and middle-order batting are central to the Royals' plans.

Boland Park in Paarl is the Royals' home, hosting five league games and Qualifier 1 on 16 February. David Miller captains the side, as he has since the first season, and Trevor Penney is head coach. Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and after four seasons without a title, the Royals' wait is the story of their campaign.

Frequently asked questions

He is an overseas all-rounder, retained for SA20 Season 5. He bowls off-spin and bats in the middle order, and he is one of the side's match-winners.

Was Sikandar Raza retained by Paarl Royals?

When does SA20 Season 5 start?

On Sunday 17 January 2027. The opener is Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals at St George's Park, starting at 3:30 PM SAST.
