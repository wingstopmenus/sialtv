Will Jacks — the pre-signing who opens and bowls spin

Will Jacks joins MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5 as a pre-signing, the one player the club signed before the auction. The England all-rounder was secured early, so the champions went into auction day with 13 players already in place.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

01 — The player

Signed before the auction, not at it

Jacks was MI Cape Town's only pre-signing, which tells its own story. Pre-signings are the players a club moves for first, before the bidding starts — and the champions chose an England all-rounder who can open the batting and bowl off-spin. It is the kind of two-skill signing that shapes the rest of the squad build.

He bats right-handed and attacks from the first ball, which makes him a natural fit at the top of a T20 order. His off-spin is a genuine second skill rather than a filler: in a side already rich in wrist-spin through Rashid Khan, Jacks gives the captain a different spin option to turn to.

02 — What he brings

What he brings

At the top of the order, Jacks sets the tempo. A fast start in the powerplay takes pressure off the middle order and lets the finishers play without chasing the game. His aggressive style suits Newlands, where the ball comes onto the bat and the boundaries reward clean hitting.

With the ball, he gives Rashid Khan flexibility. An off-spinner who can bowl in the powerplay or through the middle overs lets the captain rest his frontline bowlers and still keep control. Jacks is the sort of player who can win a game with either skill, and that is why he was signed before anyone else.

03 — Where MI Cape Town stand

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025, and the champions built their Season 5 squad around early decisions — Jacks as the pre-signing, then 12 retentions and six auction buys. Rashid Khan captains, Robin Peterson coaches, and the home ground is Newlands in Cape Town. Rashid Khan leads the spin attack Jacks supports. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

How did Will Jacks join MI Cape Town?

As a pre-signing, agreed before the auction. He was the club's only pre-signing, taking the squad to 13 players before auction day.

What is Will Jacks' role for MI Cape Town?

He is an overseas all-rounder who opens the batting aggressively and bowls off-spin, giving the side two skills in one player.

Who captains MI Cape Town in SA20 Season 5?

Rashid Khan, who has captained the side since the first season. Robin Peterson is the head coach.
