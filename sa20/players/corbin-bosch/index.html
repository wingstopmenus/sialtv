Corbin Bosch — the seamer who does both jobs

Corbin Bosch is back with MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The seam-bowling all-rounder gives Rashid Khan a middle-overs option with the ball and extra batting depth down the order.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A retained all-rounder with two jobs to do

Bosch earned his place in the retained group by covering two roles at once. He bowls right-arm seam, usually through the middle overs when the ball has stopped swinging, and he bats low enough to finish an innings or rescue one. That kind of cover is exactly what a 19-man squad needs, because it lets the captain change the balance of the side without changing the player.

MI Cape Town kept 12 players from last season, and Bosch was among them alongside the big names — Rashid Khan, Ryan Rickelton, Nicholas Pooran and Kagiso Rabada. Retention is a quiet vote of confidence: the club looked at its squad, decided what it could not afford to lose, and Bosch made the list.

What he brings

With the ball, Bosch is the bowler a captain turns to when the powerplay ends and the game needs controlling. He hits a hard length, varies his pace, and is comfortable bowling at batters who are already set — the thankless overs that decide whether a total stays within reach.

With the bat, he lengthens the order. MI Cape Town already bat deep, but a number seven or eight who can clear the rope changes how the top order plays: the openers can take risks knowing there is hitting still to come. Bosch will not bat every game, but when he does, his runs tend to come at the moments that matter.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town go into Season 5 as the 2025 champions, with Rashid Khan still captain and Robin Peterson still head coach, playing at Newlands in Cape Town. Kagiso Rabada leads the attack, and Bosch is the seam-bowling cover around him. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

What role does Corbin Bosch play for MI Cape Town?

He is a seam-bowling all-rounder. He bowls right-arm seam through the middle overs and bats in the lower order, giving the side balance and batting depth.

Was Corbin Bosch retained for SA20 Season 5?

Yes. He was one of the 12 players MI Cape Town retained, alongside captain Rashid Khan, Ryan Rickelton, Nicholas Pooran and Kagiso Rabada.

When does SA20 Season 5 start?

On Sunday 17 January 2027, with Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals at St George's Park. The final is at Newlands on 21 February 2027.
