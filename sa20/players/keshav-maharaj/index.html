Keshav Maharaj — the captain leading the finalists again

Keshav Maharaj captains Pretoria Capitals in SA20 Season 5, retained as the leader of the side he has captained since Season 4. The left-arm spinner led the Capitals to the 2026 final, and now he leads them back to finish the job.

See the Capitals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Captain since Season 4, spinner since day one

Maharaj is the first name on the Capitals team sheet, and the one everything else is built around. He bowls left-arm orthodox spin, fields where the ball follows him, and captains a side that came within one game of the title last season.

His bowling is built on control and guile. Maharaj flights the ball, changes his pace, and dares the batter to hit over the top — and when they do, his drift and dip bring the stumping and the caught-and-bowled chances. He is South Africa’s leading Test spinner, and in T20 he is the bowler who ties down an end while the quicks attack from the other one.

Retention was never in doubt. Pretoria kept 12 players and added five at the auction, and Maharaj was the centre of that decision.

Control with the ball, calm in the big moments

The Capitals need Maharaj the bowler as much as Maharaj the captain. A tight spell from him builds the pressure that brings wickets at the other end.

When Maharaj bowls well, the order relaxes. Ngidi and Williams can attack with the new ball, Russell can wait for the death, and the fielders know the plan.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games — the first against Durban’s Super Giants on 19 January 2027.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. For Maharaj, the job is simple to state and hard to do: lead the spin, lead the room, and end the wait.
