Every player, team by team

SialTV lists all 113 players of SA20 Season 5 — every squad member with a profile on this site, grouped by team. Five squads carry 19 players and Joburg Super Kings carry 18, after Kieron Pollard — bought for R1.5 million at the auction on 7 October 2026 — withdrew from the season. He has no profile here, and the Joburg squad below lists the 18 who remain.

The season has not started yet — the first ball goes down on Sunday 17 January 2027 — so there are no runs or wickets to show beside the names. Each profile carries the player’s role, and the numbers arrive once play starts. The five captains are Rashid Khan, Tristan Stubbs, Aiden Markram, Keshav Maharaj and David Miller; Joburg Super Kings have not named theirs yet.

Frequently asked questions

How many players are in SA20 Season 5?

113 players have a profile on SialTV — 19 each for MI Cape Town, Sunrisers Eastern Cape, Durban’s Super Giants, Pretoria Capitals and Paarl Royals, and 18 for Joburg Super Kings.

Why does Joburg Super Kings have only 18 players?

Kieron Pollard was bought for R1.5 million at the auction and then withdrew from Season 5, so the squad lists 18. He has no player profile on this site.

Who are the captains in SA20 Season 5?

Rashid Khan (MI Cape Town), Tristan Stubbs (Sunrisers Eastern Cape), Aiden Markram (Durban’s Super Giants), Keshav Maharaj (Pretoria Capitals) and David Miller (Paarl Royals). Joburg Super Kings have not named a captain yet.

When does SA20 Season 5 start?

Sunday 17 January 2027, with Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals at St George’s Park, Gqeberha, at 3:30 PM SAST (6:30 PM PKT). The final is at Newlands on Sunday 21 February.

Where are the full squads?

On each team page — verified Season 5 squads with roles — and on the SA20 Teams page, which lists all six sides.
