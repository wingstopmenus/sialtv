Lutho Sipamla — the seamer kept at R600,000

Lutho Sipamla is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained at a listed R600,000. The domestic seamer gives the champions reliable pace cover in an attack full of express quicks.

See Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Retained for depth in a quick attack

Sipamla was retained at R600,000, one of the 15 players Sunrisers kept for Season 5. He is the depth seamer in a pace group led by Anrich Nortje, Marco Jansen and Adam Milne — the bowler who steps in when the schedule bites, when a quick needs a rest, or when conditions suit his style. Every title-winning squad needs one.

He bowls right-arm fast-medium, hitting the pitch hard and moving the ball off the seam. His style is honest and accurate: he bowls a hard length, asks the batter to play at nearly every ball, and lets the pitch do the work. At St George's Park, where the surface can offer the seamers something, that accuracy is a weapon.

What he brings

Control is Sipamla's currency. In an attack full of express pace, a bowler who lands six balls in the same spot and goes for few runs is the one who lets the captain attack from the other end. His overs build the pressure that brings wickets — for him, or for the quick at the other end.

He also brings readiness. A 30-match league stage across six weeks tests every bowling group, and teams that go deep are the ones whose cover bowlers can step in without the standard dropping. Sipamla has been retained because the champions trust him to do exactly that.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Adam Milne is the overseas quick in the same pace group. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
