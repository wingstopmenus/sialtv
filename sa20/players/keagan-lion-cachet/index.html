Keagan Lion-Cachet — the young domestic batter staying with the Royals

Keagan Lion-Cachet is back with Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the domestic batters in a 19-player squad that was settled before the auction. He is part of the South African core the Royals built their side around, rather than a new face bought at the auction.

A domestic batter the Royals kept

Keagan Lion-Cachet was one of 17 players Paarl Royals retained for SA20 Season 5. The domestic batter's retention shows the franchise wants continuity in its batting group — in a short league season, players who already know the dressing room, the captain and the plans are worth more than new names who need time to settle.

Lion-Cachet's job is to add batting cover and competition for places. Every squad needs batters pushing for a spot, because injuries and form change a team quickly across ten league games, and a retained batter who knows the set-up can step in without the side missing a beat.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a batter who already understands how Miller and Penney want the side to play. That matters more than it sounds: a new signing spends the first week learning the plans, while a retained player starts the season ready.

He also keeps the batting group honest. With Root, Miller and Verreynne taking the senior places, the younger domestic batters have to earn their cricket in training — and that competition is part of what makes a squad better across a season.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Lion-Cachet is part of a Royals squad that did not change at the auction — 17 retained, two pre-signings, nobody bought. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park in Paarl.

Miller has led the Royals since Season 1 and Penney has coached them since Season 3, so this is a settled leadership group. Boland Park in Paarl is home, staging five home league games plus Qualifier 1 on 16 February. The season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Royals still looking for a first title.

Frequently asked questions

What is Keagan Lion-Cachet's role for Paarl Royals?

He is a domestic batter, retained for SA20 Season 5. He provides batting cover and competition in a squad built around David Miller, Joe Root and Kyle Verreynne.

Was Keagan Lion-Cachet retained by Paarl Royals?

Yes. He was one of 17 players the Royals retained, so their 19-player squad was complete before the auction and they bought nobody on auction day.

Where do Paarl Royals play their home games?

Boland Park in Paarl. It hosts five home league games in Season 5, and it also stages Qualifier 1 on 16 February 2027.
