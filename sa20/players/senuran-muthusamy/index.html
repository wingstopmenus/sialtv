Senuran Muthusamy — the left-arm spinner kept at R1.5 million

Senuran Muthusamy is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained at a listed R1.5 million. The domestic all-rounder bowls left-arm orthodox spin and bats in the lower order, two skills that make him one of the squad's most useful players.

Two skills, one squad place

Muthusamy was retained at R1.5 million, one of the 15 players Sunrisers kept for Season 5. He was kept because he covers two jobs at once: he bowls left-arm spin through the middle overs and he bats well enough to matter at seven or eight. In a 19-man squad, a player who does both is worth more than either skill on its own.

He is a tall left-armer whose spin is built on bounce and accuracy rather than mystery. Batters cannot simply sweep him away, because the ball gets up on them; and when the surface at St George's Park starts to grip, his overs become some of the hardest to score from.

What he brings

With the ball, Muthusamy is the control spinner who lets the wrist-spinners attack. While Rishad Hossain hunts wickets with his leg-spin, Muthusamy holds an end, builds dot-ball pressure, and picks up the batters who try to hit their way out of trouble. It is unglamorous work, and it wins tournaments.

With the bat, he is the late-innings insurance. A quick 20 from number eight can be the difference between a par total and a winning one, and Muthusamy has the power to clear the rope against both pace and spin. His runs tend to come when the innings needs them most.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Rishad Hossain is the leg-spinner in the same spin group. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
