Evan Jones — the seam-bowling all-rounder in the middle

Evan Jones is back with Durban’s Super Giants for SA20 Season 5, one of the 18 players the club retained. The domestic all-rounder bowls seam and bats in the lower order, two skills that make him one of the squad’s most useful players.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Two skills, one squad place

Jones was retained because he covers two jobs at once. He bowls right-arm seam through the middle overs and he bats well enough to matter at seven or eight — the combination every T20 captain wants, because it frees up a place for an extra specialist elsewhere.

With the ball, he is the bowler who holds a length and makes batters earn their runs. With the bat, he is the late-innings hitter who can clear the rope when the innings needs a finish. Neither skill is the headline of the side, but together they make Jones the kind of player a captain picks when the balance of the XI is tight.

Durban’s kept 18 players, and Jones’s versatility is a big part of why he made the list.

Balance, depth, and a late finish

Jones lengthens the batting and shortens the bowling worries. That is the whole point of a seam-bowling all-rounder.

A quick 20 from number eight can be the difference between a par total and a winning one, and Jones has the power to produce it against both pace and spin. And when the captain needs four overs from a bowler who will not leak runs, Jones is there. His runs tend to come when the innings needs them most, and his overs tend to come when the game needs steadying.

A settled squad, still chasing the first title

Durban’s Super Giants are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons, and they kept the core together to do it — 18 retained players, plus Sam Curran as a pre-signing, and nobody bought at the auction. Aiden Markram captains and Lance Klusener coaches.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Giants play their home games at Kingsmead in Durban, five of them in the league stage.
