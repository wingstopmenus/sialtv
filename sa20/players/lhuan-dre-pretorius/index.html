Lhuan-dre Pretorius — the young keeper-batter the Royals are building around

Lhuan-dre Pretorius is one of the under-23 players Paarl Royals retained for SA20 Season 5, holding a young-player place in a 19-player squad that was settled before the auction. The wicketkeeper-batter is one of the most exciting young South African players in the league.

Youth, gloves, and a big future

Lhuan-dre Pretorius was retained by Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, keeping one of the squad's under-23 places. The young wicketkeeper-batter is one of three Royals in that bracket, alongside JJ Basson and Nqobani Mokoena, and his retention shows the franchise sees him as a player to build around, not just a squad filler.

Pretorius keeps wicket and bats with freedom, the combination every T20 side wants from a keeper. A keeper-batter who can open or bat in the middle order gives the captain options, and a young one who keeps improving is the kind of player a franchise holds on to early.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a keeper-batter who can change a game with the bat and tidy up behind the stumps. Pretorius is competing with Kyle Verreynne for the gloves, and that competition is good for both of them — and for the side, which always has a keeper in form.

His value is also in the future. The under-23 places exist so franchises can grow their own stars, and Pretorius is exactly the kind of player the system is built for. Season 5 is another step in a career that is moving fast.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Pretorius is part of the Royals' young core, in a settled squad with the same captain and coach as last season. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park in Paarl.

Paarl Royals are led by David Miller, who has captained the side since the first season, with Trevor Penney as head coach since Season 3. Their home is Boland Park in Paarl, which also hosts Qualifier 1 on 16 February. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Royals are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.
