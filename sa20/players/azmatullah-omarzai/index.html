Azmatullah Omarzai — the Afghanistan all-rounder signed before the auction

Azmatullah Omarzai joins Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5 as one of the side's two pre-signings, alongside Joe Root. The Afghanistan seam-bowling all-rounder was signed before the auction, which is how the Royals went into auction day with a full 19-player squad.

See Paarl Royals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A pre-signing who fills two jobs at once

Azmatullah Omarzai was a pre-signing for Paarl Royals, signed before the auction along with Joe Root as the wildcard pick. Pre-signings are how franchises lock in the players they want most, and Omarzai's signing says the Royals wanted a seam-bowling all-rounder badly enough to secure him early.

Omarzai bowls lively seam and bats with power in the middle order, the two-skills package that every T20 side wants. For Afghanistan he has become a fixture in all formats, and his game is built for South African pitches — bounce for his bowling, pace on the ball for his batting.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a player who balances the side. Omarzai's seam bowling gives Miller a sixth bowling option who can take the new ball or bowl at the death, and his batting lengthens the order so the Royals can keep attacking even after losing early wickets.

He also gives the Royals a point of difference. Most of their overseas picks are spinners or batters; Omarzai is the overseas seamer who does both jobs. In a squad full of spin, a seam-bowling all-rounder who hits the bat hard is the change of pace the attack needs.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Omarzai is one of two new faces in the Royals squad — the other is Joe Root — and both were pre-signings, so the Royals never had to bid for them. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park in Paarl.

Miller has led the Royals since Season 1 and Penney has coached them since Season 3, so this is a settled leadership group. Boland Park in Paarl is home, staging five home league games plus Qualifier 1 on 16 February. The season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Royals still looking for a first title.

Frequently asked questions

How did Azmatullah Omarzai join Paarl Royals?

As a pre-signing, signed before the auction along with Joe Root. The Royals' 19-player squad was already complete before auction day, so they bought nobody.

What is Azmatullah Omarzai's role for Paarl Royals?

He is an overseas seam-bowling all-rounder. He bowls lively seam and bats with power in the middle order, giving the side two skills in one player.

Who captains Paarl Royals in SA20 Season 5?

David Miller, who has captained the side since the first season. Trevor Penney is head coach, a role he has held since Season 3.
