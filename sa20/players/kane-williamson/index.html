Kane Williamson — the calm anchor in a side full of hitters

Kane Williamson is back with Durban’s Super Giants for SA20 Season 5, one of the 18 players the club retained. The overseas batter is the calmest head in the side — a top-order anchor in a batting line-up built for power.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Class, calm, and a retained place

Williamson was retained because he offers something no one else in the squad does: control. In a top order full of hitters, he is the batter who can bat through the innings, pick the gaps, and let the power come from the other end.

He bats right-handed and plays the ball late, with a technique built on timing and placement. He rarely hits the ball in the air without meaning to, rarely gives his wicket away, and rarely looks rushed — even when the required rate climbs. As a former New Zealand captain, he also brings a tactical brain the dressing room leans on.

His role is the anchor. While Buttler attacks at one end and Klaasen finishes at the other, Williamson holds the innings together in between — rotating the strike, finding the boundary when the bowlers miss, and being there at the end when the game is tight.

The batter who makes the hitters better

Williamson’s value is in what he lets others do. A steady partner frees the hitters to take risks.

A batting order works when its parts fit together, and Williamson is the piece that fits between Markram’s timing and Klaasen’s power. He reads the game as well as anyone in the league, and in a side chasing a first title, that reading of the game — when to push, when to wait — may matter as much as any boundary.

A settled squad, still chasing the first title

Durban’s Super Giants are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons, and they kept the core together to do it — 18 retained players, plus Sam Curran as a pre-signing, and nobody bought at the auction. Aiden Markram captains and Lance Klusener coaches.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Giants play their home games at Kingsmead in Durban, five of them in the league stage. Frequently asked questions

Was Kane Williamson retained by Durban’s Super Giants?

Yes. He was one of the 18 players the club retained for Season 5, so he did not go through the auction.

What is Kane Williamson’s role in the side?

He is an overseas batter and top-order anchor, valued for calm, timing and holding the innings together.

Where do Durban’s Super Giants play their home games?

At Kingsmead in Durban, which hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on 21 January 2027.
