Jason Smith — the middle-order all-rounder who stays

Jason Smith is back with MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The domestic all-rounder bats in the middle order and bowls seam, the kind of two-skill player every T20 squad leans on.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Retained for balance in the middle

Smith was retained because balance wins tournaments. He bats in the middle order, where he can rebuild an innings or accelerate one, and he bowls seam when the captain needs an over or two to break a partnership. Players like that rarely make headlines, but captains love them — they make the XI work.

He is a powerful hitter in the middle order, the kind of batter who can clear the boundary against spin and pace alike. When the top order fires, Smith's job is to keep the momentum going; when it fails, his job is to stop the collapse. Either way, he bats in the overs where games are decided.

What he brings

Middle-order power is Smith's main currency. The overs between 10 and 16 decide whether a side posts 160 or 190, and Smith is built for them: strong through the leg side, quick between the wickets, and unafraid to take on the spinners. His hitting gives the finishers behind him a platform to launch from.

With the ball, he is the captain's flexible option. A few overs of seam in the middle of the innings, a change of pace when the batters are set — Smith gives Rashid Khan a bowler he can use without weakening the batting. That flexibility is why he was retained.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 and kept the core together, retaining Smith alongside Rashid Khan, George Linde and Corbin Bosch in a strong all-rounder group. Robin Peterson coaches the side at Newlands in Cape Town. George Linde is the spin-bowling all-rounder in the same group. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

He is a domestic all-rounder who bats in the middle order and bowls seam, giving the side balance and power.
