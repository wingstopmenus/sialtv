SA20 Season 5 · MI Cape Town

Daniel Smith — the young keeper bought for R200,000

Daniel Smith joins MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5 as a domestic auction signing, bought for R200,000. The young wicketkeeper-batter is one of three players the champions picked up at the lowest auction price, and the cover behind two established keepers.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Cover behind two proven keepers

Smith was bought at R200,000, alongside Lehan Botha and Oliver Whitehead at the same price. His role in the squad is clear: he is the third wicketkeeper, behind Ryan Rickelton and Nicholas Pooran, and the cover if either is injured, rested, or called away. Every squad needs that cover, and the champions bought it cheaply.

As a wicketkeeper-batter, he offers the same two-skill package as the men ahead of him. He can keep wicket to pace and spin, and he can bat in the middle or lower order. For a young player, the job is to be ready — keepers get their chance suddenly, and the ones who take it are the ones who prepared as if they were playing every game.

What he brings

Depth is Smith's first contribution. A 30-match league stage is long, and wicketkeepers take a physical battering — crouching for 20 overs, diving, taking throws. Having a third keeper in the squad means the side never has to improvise behind the stumps, and that is worth more than it looks on paper.

He also brings hunger. Young players on small contracts play every training session like a trial, and that energy lifts a squad. If Smith gets a game, he will be playing with nothing to lose — and in T20 cricket, that is often when young players surprise everyone.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 and built their Season 5 squad with a mix of stars and young cover, including Smith at R200,000. Rashid Khan captains, Robin Peterson coaches, and Newlands hosts five home league games. Ryan Rickelton is the first-choice keeper Smith covers. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
