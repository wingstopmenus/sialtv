Jordan Hermann — the left-handed opener kept at R1.5 million

Jordan Hermann is back with Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5, retained at a listed R1.5 million. The domestic batter gives the champions a left-handed option at the top of the order, alongside Quinton de Kock.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A left-hander at the top, retained

Hermann was retained at R1.5 million, one of the 15 players Sunrisers kept for Season 5. He is a left-handed opener, and that matters more than it sounds: two left-handers at the top of the order force bowling sides to change their lines, their fields and their plans from the very first over.

He bats with the freedom of a young opener who trusts his game. Hermann scores quickly against pace and is not afraid to take on the spinners, which makes him a natural partner for de Kock — two left-handers who both like to attack can take a powerplay away from a bowling side in a hurry.

What he brings

Left-right variety is Hermann's first gift, even when he opens with another left-hander. Bowlers cannot settle into one line against him: he scores square of the wicket, pulls anything short, and drives through the covers when the ball is full. That range makes him hard to contain in the powerplay.

He also brings youth to the top order. Alongside Matthew Breetzke, Hermann is part of the young core Sunrisers have built around their senior players — a top order that can grow together over several seasons. Season 5 is his chance to turn promise into the runs that win a fourth title.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Mitchell van Buuren is the other young batter in the squad. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.
