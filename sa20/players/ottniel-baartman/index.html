Ottniel Baartman — the fast bowler Paarl Royals kept for Season 5

Ottniel Baartman is back with Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the domestic fast bowlers in a 19-player squad that was settled before the auction. He is part of a bowling group that mixes his pace with Bjorn Fortuin's spin and Mujeeb Ur Rahman's mystery.

The bowler the Royals trust at the death

Ottniel Baartman was one of 17 players Paarl Royals retained for SA20 Season 5, a sign of how much the franchise values a bowler who does his best work when the game is on the line. The domestic fast bowler has been a Royals regular, and keeping him meant the Royals went into the auction with their squad already complete.

Baartman bowls with pace and control at the death, the overs where T20 matches are won and lost. He backs his yorker and his slower ball when the batting side is swinging hard, and that nerve is why the Royals kept him while other teams were still shopping.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get a death bowler who can also take the new ball. Baartman's variations make scoring hard in the last four overs, and his experience means Miller can throw him the ball in any phase.

He also gives the attack balance. With Mujeeb Ur Rahman and Gudakesh Motie spinning through the middle, Baartman is the seamer the Royals trust to close games out — in a league where most sides hunt one more fast bowler, the Royals already had theirs.

Where Paarl Royals stand

For Baartman, the wait for a first title is personal as well as collective — he has been part of the Royals' four-season chase, and Season 5 is another chance to finish it. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park, where the Royals open their home campaign against Joburg Super Kings on 18 January 2027.

Paarl Royals are led by David Miller, who has captained the side since the first season, with Trevor Penney as head coach since Season 3. Their home is Boland Park in Paarl, which also hosts Qualifier 1 on 16 February. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Royals are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.
