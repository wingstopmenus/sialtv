Faf du Plessis — the star batter bought back for R2.7 million

Faf du Plessis is back with Joburg Super Kings for SA20 Season 5, bought for R2.7 million at the auction on 7 October 2026. The star batter was released on 5 August 2026, then repurchased by the Super Kings, in one of the stories of the auction.

Back in Johannesburg — bought back at the auction

Faf du Plessis returned to Joburg Super Kings at the auction for R2.7 million, after being released on 5 August 2026. The Super Kings were the busiest team at the auction, filling six places, and bringing du Plessis back was the headline — a player the franchise knows well, returning to familiar colours after his release.

Du Plessis is one of the finest T20 batters of his generation. He opens the batting or bats at three, scores quickly against pace and spin alike, and fields brilliantly — the complete package that makes him worth every rand of an auction fee. His return gives the Super Kings a senior batter to build the order around.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Joburg Super Kings get a batter who can win games in the powerplay and a senior player who sets the standard for the whole squad. Du Plessis's batting gives the top order its shape: he takes the new ball on and rarely wastes a start.

His experience is the other half of the signing. A player who has captained and won at the highest level brings calm to a dressing room that has a new coach in Albie Morkel and a captain still to be named. Whatever the leadership looks like on paper, du Plessis will be central to how this side plays.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

Du Plessis returns to a Super Kings side chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons, with Albie Morkel as head coach and the captaincy still TBC. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at the Wanderers in Johannesburg.

Albie Morkel is head coach for Season 5, while the captaincy is still TBC. The Wanderers in Johannesburg is home, staging five league games, and the season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027. The Super Kings have not won a title in four seasons, and that wait frames their campaign.

Frequently asked questions

How did Faf du Plessis rejoin Joburg Super Kings?

At the auction on 7 October 2026, for R2.7 million. He had been released on 5 August 2026, and the Super Kings bought him back.

What is Faf du Plessis's role for Joburg Super Kings?

He is a domestic batter, bought at the auction. He bats at the top of the order and is the senior batter the Super Kings build around.

Who captains Joburg Super Kings in SA20 Season 5?

The captain is TBC — no captain has been named for Season 5. Albie Morkel is head coach.
