Delano Potgieter — the domestic all-rounder doing both jobs for the Royals

Delano Potgieter is back with Paarl Royals for SA20 Season 5, retained as one of the domestic all-rounders in a 19-player squad that was settled before the auction. He bowls seam and bats in the middle order, the kind of two-skills player who lets a captain pick a longer batting line-up.

Seam, batting, and a place in every plan

Delano Potgieter was one of 17 players Paarl Royals retained for SA20 Season 5. The domestic all-rounder is a seam bowler who can hold a batting place on his own merits, which is exactly what T20 sides want from their local players — one pick, two jobs.

Potgieter's bowling gives Miller overs in the middle of the innings, when the game needs control rather than fireworks. His batting is built for the back half of an innings, where he can hit the ball hard and finish totals. Players who do both honestly are hard to find, and the Royals kept theirs.

What he brings to Paarl Royals

Paarl Royals get balance from Potgieter. His seam bowling means Miller does not have to lean on the overseas quicks for every over, and his batting means the Royals can bat deep without weakening the bowling — the trade-off that decides most T20 selections.

He also gives the side a South African all-rounder who knows the conditions. Local players who have bowled on Boland Park and across South Africa do not need to learn the pitches, and in a short season that head start matters.

Where Paarl Royals stand

Potgieter is part of the Royals' settled domestic core — the same captain, the same coach, and a squad that was finished before auction day. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at Boland Park in Paarl.

Boland Park in Paarl is the Royals' home, hosting five league games and Qualifier 1 on 16 February. David Miller captains the side, as he has since the first season, and Trevor Penney is head coach. Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and after four seasons without a title, the Royals' wait is the story of their campaign.
