Dayyaan Galiem — the seamer bought for R375,000 at the auction

Dayyaan Galiem joins Joburg Super Kings for SA20 Season 5, bought for R375,000 at the auction on 7 October 2026. The domestic seamer was one of six players the Super Kings filled their squad with on auction day, completing an 18-player group.

An auction buy who adds seam depth

Dayyaan Galiem was bought by Joburg Super Kings at the auction for R375,000, one of the six places the Super Kings filled on the day. The Super Kings were the busiest team at the auction, and Galiem's signing shows they wanted seam depth to go with the pace of Blessing Muzarabani and the all-rounders around him.

Galiem bowls seam with control, the kind of bowling that holds an end while the strike bowlers attack from the other. In T20 cricket, a seamer who can bowl tight overs in the middle of the innings is worth as much as a wicket-taker — dot balls build pressure, and pressure brings wickets.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Joburg Super Kings get a seamer who deepens the bowling group. Galiem does not have to lead the attack — Muzarabani, Holder and Overton carry that load — but he has to be ready when the captain calls, because T20 seasons always need a bowler to step up.

He also gives the side a domestic option in the pace attack. Local seamers who know South African pitches do not need to learn them, and a bowler bought for a modest fee who delivers regular overs is one of the best-value signings a franchise can make.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

Galiem is one of six auction buys who completed the Super Kings squad, alongside Faf du Plessis, Jason Rowles, Andries Gous and Blessing Muzarabani. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at the Wanderers in Johannesburg.

Joburg Super Kings are coached by Albie Morkel, and the captain for Season 5 is yet to be confirmed. Their home is the Wanderers in Johannesburg, which hosts five home league games. Season 5 begins on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Kings are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons.
