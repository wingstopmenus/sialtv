Dane Piedt — the off-spinner who knows Newlands best

Dane Piedt is back with MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The experienced domestic off-spinner gives Rashid Khan a steady, knowing hand in an attack full of pace and wrist-spin.

Experience, retained rather than replaced

Piedt was retained, and for a bowler of his type that is a deliberate choice. Off-spin in T20 is a control art: the bowler who flights the ball, changes his pace, and dares the batter to hit over the top. It takes nerve and experience, and Piedt has spent years bowling exactly that way in South African cricket.

He is also a Newlands bowler in the truest sense. He knows how the surface plays as a game wears on, when the ball starts to grip and when it skids through. In a home campaign of five league games at the ground, that local knowledge is a small edge that can matter in a tight finish.

What he brings

Control through the middle overs is Piedt's job. While Rashid Khan's leg-spin hunts wickets, Piedt can hold an end, keep the scoring rate down, and force batters to take risks against the bowlers the captain wants them to face. It is unglamorous work, and it wins tournaments.

He also gives the attack its full spin set. With Rashid's leg-spin, Kaber and Linde's left-arm spin, and Piedt's off-spin, MI Cape Town can match a spinner to any batter and any situation. Few sides in the league have that kind of variety in one squad.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 and kept their spin group intact, retaining Piedt alongside Rashid Khan, Thomas Kaber and George Linde. Robin Peterson is the head coach and Newlands in Cape Town is the home ground. Thomas Kaber is the left-arm spinner in the same group. The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

He is a domestic off-spinner, valued for control and experience through the middle overs at Newlands.
