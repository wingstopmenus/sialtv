The biggest buy of the auction — and a young all-rounder

Jason Rowles was bought by Joburg Super Kings at the auction for R2.9 million, the most expensive signing of the auction. The Super Kings were the busiest team on the day, filling six places, and Rowles was the headline buy — a young all-rounder the franchise paid more for than any other player in the sale.

Rowles offers batting and bowling in a single pick, the two-skills package that every T20 side wants from a young player. Paying the auction's top price for an under-23 all-rounder is a statement: the Super Kings are not buying him for one season, they are buying him for the future.

What he brings to Joburg Super Kings

Joburg Super Kings get a young all-rounder who can do both jobs. Rowles's batting lengthens the order and his bowling gives the coach another option, and at his age he will only get better — a season around Holder, du Plessis and the senior players is a fast education.

He also gives the side a story. Every auction has a player everyone talks about, and in Season 5 that player is Rowles. The price brings pressure, but it also brings belief — the Super Kings looked at every young player in the sale and decided he was the one.

Where Joburg Super Kings stand

Rowles is the Super Kings' biggest auction buy, in a squad built from 11 retained players, Jason Holder's pre-signing, and six auction signings. He will be part of the SA20 Season 5 squad at the Wanderers in Johannesburg.

Albie Morkel is head coach for Season 5, while the captaincy is still TBC. The Wanderers in Johannesburg is home, staging five league games, and the season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027. The Super Kings have not won a title in four seasons, and that wait frames their campaign.
