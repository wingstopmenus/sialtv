SA20 Season 5 · Durban's Super Giants

Aiden Markram — the captain chasing a first title

Aiden Markram captains Durban’s Super Giants in SA20 Season 5, retained as the leader of a squad that was complete before the auction began. The batter has led the side since Season 4, and this is the season the Super Giants want to finally turn promise into a trophy.

See the Super Giants squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Captain since Season 4, opener of the innings

Markram is the first name on the Super Giants team sheet, and the one everything else is built around. He bats at the top of the order, fields where the ball follows him, and bowls off-spin when the captain — himself — needs an over.

His batting is built on timing rather than muscle. Markram drives through the line, plays the ball late, and scores all around the wicket, which makes him hard to tie down in the powerplay and harder still once he is set. He is also South Africa’s white-ball captain, so the leadership at Durban’s is not a new job — it is the same job, in different colours.

Retention was never in doubt. Durban’s kept 18 players and bought nobody at the auction, and Markram was the centre of that decision.

Calm at the top, and a captain who leads by runs

The Super Giants need Markram the batter as much as Markram the captain. A big season from him at the top sets the tone for everyone who follows.

When Markram scores, the order relaxes. Jos Buttler and Kane Williamson can play their own games, Heinrich Klaasen can wait for the spinners, and the middle order never has to rebuild from nothing.

A settled squad, still chasing the first title

Durban’s Super Giants are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons. Markram has captained the side since Season 4, Lance Klusener has been head coach since the first season, and the home ground is Kingsmead in Durban, which hosts five home league games — the first against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on 21 January 2027.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Giants’ squad was already complete before the 7 October auction — 18 retained players plus the pre-signing of Sam Curran, so they bought nobody.
