Dewald Brevis — the young power-hitter they call Baby AB

Dewald Brevis is back with Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The domestic batter is one of the most exciting young hitters in world cricket — and the Capitals made sure he stayed.

See the Capitals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A young star, retained not bought

Brevis was retained, which in a squad of 19 is the strongest signal a club can send to a young player. He is the batter they call Baby AB, after AB de Villiers, and the nickname is not just about the look — it is about the shots.

He bats right-handed in the middle order and hits the ball as cleanly as anyone in the league. Against spin he is brutal, stepping down the pitch and clearing the boundary; against pace he is just as dangerous, with the bat speed to punish anything short. Bowlers do not get a rest when Brevis is at the crease — every ball is a scoring ball.

He also bowls leg-spin, which gives the captain a second skill in the same player. It is a bonus rather than the main act, but in a squad built around Keshav Maharaj’s left-arm spin, a leg-spinning batter adds a different look the opposition has to plan for.

The middle-order batter who changes games in ten balls

Brevis does not need time to get going. Ten balls can be enough to turn a tight game into a comfortable win.

That speed is what makes him so valuable. While other batters need twenty balls to find their rhythm, Brevis arrives ready to hit — which means the Capitals can leave their acceleration late and still post a winning total. With Andre Russell beside him, Pretoria have two of the most destructive middle-order hitters in the league in the same side.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. Brevis’s bat may be the loudest weapon in the title chase.
