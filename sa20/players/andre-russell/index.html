SA20 Season 5 · Pretoria Capitals

Andre Russell — the wildcard who wins games with bat and ball

Andre Russell is back with Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5, retained as the club’s wildcard pick. The all-rounder is one of the most feared T20 players in the world — a power-hitter who clears any boundary, and a fast bowler who takes wickets at the death.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The wildcard, and the most dangerous player in the squad

Russell was kept as Pretoria’s wildcard — the special retention slot reserved for a player a club cannot afford to lose. It is a fitting label for a player who can win a game with either skill, sometimes in the same over.

He bats right-handed in the lower middle order and hits the ball further than almost anyone in the game. Against pace he is brutal, against spin he is just as dangerous, and once he is set, the run rate climbs in a hurry. Bowlers do not get a rest when Russell is at the crease — every ball is a scoring ball.

With the ball, he is the fast bowler the captain turns to at the death. His yorker and his slower ball are hard to pick, and his pace hurries even the best batters.

The finisher, and the death bowler, in one player

Russell’s value is in the last five overs — with the bat, and with the ball. Few players decide both ends of a game.

Chasing a total, he is the batter who turns a lost game into a won one. Defending one, he is the bowler who defends it. In a side chasing a first title, he is the player the big moments belong to.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. If the Capitals finally lift the trophy, Russell will be at the centre of it.
