Sunil Narine — the mystery spinner who still puzzles batters

Sunil Narine is back with Durban’s Super Giants for SA20 Season 5, one of the 18 players the club retained. The overseas all-rounder is one of the greatest T20 bowlers of all time — a mystery spinner batters still cannot pick, more than a decade after he first appeared.

Mystery spin, and a retained place

Narine was retained, which in a squad of 19 is the strongest signal a club can send. He is the bowler every T20 side wants: four overs that go for almost nothing, wickets in the powerplay and in the middle, and a record in franchise leagues around the world that few can match.

He bowls off-spin with variations batters still struggle to read — the carrom ball, the knuckle ball, the one that goes straight on. His economy is the point: even when he does not take wickets, he strangles the scoring rate, and the pressure brings wickets at the other end. At Kingsmead, where the surface can grip, he is harder still.

With the bat, he is the pinch-hitter who can turn a game in the powerplay. Opening the batting and swinging from the first ball, Narine has won games with the bat that his bowling had already set up.

Four overs that change the game, and a bat that can too

Narine’s value is in the control he brings. Four overs at his economy rate changes the maths of the whole innings.

Captains build their plans around his four overs: when he bowls, who he bowls to, and what the field does behind him. Few players in the league offer both.

A settled squad, still chasing the first title

Durban’s Super Giants are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons, and they kept the core together to do it — 18 retained players, plus Sam Curran as a pre-signing, and nobody bought at the auction. Aiden Markram captains and Lance Klusener coaches.

The SA20 season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Giants play their home games at Kingsmead in Durban, five of them in the league stage. Frequently asked questions

Was Sunil Narine retained by Durban’s Super Giants?

Yes. He was one of the 18 players the club retained for Season 5, so he did not go through the auction.

What is Sunil Narine’s role in the side?

He is an overseas all-rounder who bowls mystery off-spin and can open the batting as a pinch-hitter.

Where do Durban’s Super Giants play their home games?

At Kingsmead in Durban, which hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on 21 January 2027.
