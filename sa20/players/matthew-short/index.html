Matthew Short — the pre-signing who opens and bowls spin

Matthew Short joins Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5 as a pre-signing, one of two players the club signed before the auction. The Australian all-rounder was secured early, so the Capitals went into auction day with 14 players already in place.

See the Capitals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Signed before the auction, not at it

Short was one of Pretoria’s two pre-signings, alongside Phil Salt. Pre-signings are the players a club moves for first, before the bidding starts — and the Capitals chose an Australian all-rounder who can open the batting and bowl off-spin.

He bats right-handed and attacks from the first ball, which makes him a natural fit at the top of a T20 order. His off-spin is a genuine second skill rather than a filler: in a side already rich in left-arm spin through Keshav Maharaj, Short gives the captain a different spin option to turn to.

His signing shaped the rest of the squad build. With Salt and Short signed early and 12 players retained, the Capitals needed only five more at the auction — and they bought exactly five, spending R500,000 on Rassie van der Dussen and R200,000 on each of the other four.

A fast start, and a second spin option

Short’s value is in the tempo he sets. A fast start in the powerplay takes pressure off the middle order.

At the top of the order, his aggressive batting lets the finishers play without chasing the game — and with Phil Salt beside him, the Capitals have two openers who both attack from the first ball. With the ball, his off-spin gives the captain flexibility: a different angle, a different challenge, and four overs that keep the attack fresh.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 season starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final.
