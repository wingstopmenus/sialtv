Sherfane Rutherford — the left-handed finisher from the Caribbean

Sherfane Rutherford is back with Pretoria Capitals for SA20 Season 5, one of the 12 players the club retained. The overseas batter is a left-handed power-hitter who finishes games — and the Capitals made sure he stayed.

See the Capitals squad ›

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A finisher the Capitals refused to lose

Rutherford was retained, which in a squad of 19 is the strongest signal a club can send. He is the kind of player other teams build their auction plans around, and Pretoria made sure he never reached the auction at all.

He bats left-handed in the middle order and hits the ball cleanly to all parts of the ground. Against spin he is strong, stepping down the pitch and clearing the boundary; against pace he is just as dangerous, with the bat speed to punish anything short. Bowlers do not get a rest when Rutherford is at the crease — every ball is a scoring ball.

He also brings West Indian experience to a dressing room that already has plenty of it. Andre Russell and Keemo Paul are in the same squad, and Ackeem Auguste — another West Indian — was bought at the auction. Rutherford does not arrive alone; he is part of a group that knows his game.

The left-handed finisher who changes games late

Rutherford’s value is in the last five overs. That is where games are won, and where he does his damage.

A left-hander in the middle order also changes the angles for every bowler, and paired with the right-handed Brevis and Russell, he makes the attack constantly adjust. When Rutherford is set and the Capitals need twenty off the last two overs, the odds shift — and the opposition knows it.

Finalists again, with a rematch to open

Pretoria Capitals reached the 2026 final and are still chasing a first title. Keshav Maharaj has captained the side since Season 4, Sourav Ganguly has been head coach since Season 4, and the home ground is SuperSport Park in Centurion, which hosts five home league games.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Capitals open it — against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at St George’s Park, a rematch of the 2026 final. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. Rutherford’s late hitting could be the difference in the tight games.
