Craig Overton — the seam all-rounder bought for R1.5 million

Craig Overton joins Sunrisers Eastern Cape for SA20 Season 5 as an auction signing, bought for R1.5 million. The England seam-bowling all-rounder was one of only two players the champions needed at the auction — and the most expensive of the two.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

One of two auction signings, and the pricier one

Overton was bought for R1.5 million, three times the R500,000 Sunrisers paid for Nandre Burger, the only other auction signing. When a club only needs two players, every signing is deliberate, and Overton was chosen for a reason: the champions wanted another seam-bowling all-rounder, and the England international fit the bill exactly.

He bowls right-arm seam, hits a hard length, and moves the ball just enough to ask questions; and he bats in the lower middle order, where his power can turn a good total into a winning one. In a squad already rich in all-rounders, Overton adds the kind of depth that wins long tournaments.

What he brings

With the ball, Overton is the reliable seam option between the new ball and the death. Nortje, Jansen and Milne take the glamour overs; Overton takes the ones in between, where games are quietly won or lost. He bowls long, accurate spells that keep the run rate honest and bring wickets when batters get impatient.

With the bat, he deepens an already strong order. A lower-order batter who can also bowl four overs lets Tristan Stubbs pick an extra bowler or an extra batter depending on the conditions — and in a tournament played across six venues, that flexibility is worth more than any single performance.

Where Sunrisers Eastern Cape stand

Sunrisers Eastern Cape have won the SA20 title three times, in 2023, 2024 and 2026, and they open Season 5 against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027 — a rematch of the 2026 final. Tristan Stubbs captains, Adrian Birrell coaches, and St George's Park in Gqeberha is the home ground. Lewis Gregory is the retained seam-bowling all-rounder in the same group. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with 34 matches ending in the final at Newlands on 21 February.

St George's Park in Gqeberha hosts five home league games in Season 5, starting with the season opener against Pretoria Capitals on 17 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

How much did Sunrisers pay for Craig Overton?

R1.5 million at the auction. He was one of only two auction signings, alongside Nandre Burger at R500,000.

What is Craig Overton's role for Sunrisers?

He is an overseas seam-bowling all-rounder who bats in the lower middle order and bowls right-arm seam through the middle overs.

Who captains Sunrisers Eastern Cape in SA20 Season 5?

Tristan Stubbs, who has captained the side since Season 4. Adrian Birrell is the head coach.
