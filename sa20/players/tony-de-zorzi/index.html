Tony de Zorzi — the left-hander at the top of the order

Tony de Zorzi is back with Durban’s Super Giants for SA20 Season 5, one of the 18 players the club retained. The domestic batter gives the top order a left-handed option — and a player who has opened for South Africa.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A left-handed opener, retained not bought

De Zorzi was retained because he offers something the top order needs: a left-hander. Paired with a right-hander at the other end, he changes the angles for every bowler and makes the attack constantly adjust its lines and fields.

He bats left-handed at the top of the order, drives elegantly through the off side, and pulls anything short. His game is built on timing rather than muscle, which makes him hard to tie down once he has seen a few balls — and he has the temperament to bat through the powerplay and beyond.

He has played for South Africa at the top of the order, so the step up to franchise cricket holds no surprises. In a side full of overseas stars, de Zorzi is the domestic batter who can match them — and Durban’s kept him rather than risking the auction.

A left-handed start, and a batter who can bat long

De Zorzi’s value is in the start he gives and the angles he creates. Both make the bowlers’ job harder.

A left-right opening pair is one of the simplest advantages in T20 cricket, and de Zorzi is the left-hand half of it. When he bats through the powerplay, the middle order — Buttler, Williamson, Klaasen — walks in with the platform already built. His runs may not always be the fastest, but they are often the ones the innings is built on.

A settled squad, still chasing the first title

Durban’s Super Giants kept 18 players and bought nobody at the auction, so the top order de Zorzi returns to is the one he already knows. Aiden Markram captains, Lance Klusener coaches, and Kingsmead in Durban hosts five home league games in Season 5.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, with the Super Giants’ first home game against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on 21 January. See the full SA20 Schedule for the fixtures, and the SA20 Players index for every squad. De Zorzi’s left-handed starts could be a quiet edge in the title chase.
