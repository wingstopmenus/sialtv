Wiaan Mulder — the most expensive buy in the champions' auction

Wiaan Mulder joins MI Cape Town for SA20 Season 5 as the club's biggest auction signing, bought for R2.6 million. The seam-bowling all-rounder cost more than any other player the champions bought, and he arrives to do two jobs at once.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

R2.6 million — the headline signing

Mulder was MI Cape Town's most expensive auction buy by a distance: R2.6 million, against R1.5 million each for Trent Boult and Shimron Hetmyer. When a club spends that much on one player, it is not buying cover — it is buying a first-team player, and the price says the champions see Mulder as part of their best XI.

He is a seam-bowling all-rounder in the classic mould. He bowls right-arm medium-fast, holds a length, and moves the ball just enough to ask questions; and he bats in the middle order, where his technique lets him build an innings rather than just swing at it. In a squad full of specialists, that two-skill balance is rare.

What he brings

With the ball, Mulder is the reliable seam option between the new ball and the death. Boult and Rabada take the glamour overs; Mulder takes the ones in between, where games are quietly won or lost. He bowls long, accurate spells that keep the run rate honest and bring wickets when batters get impatient.

With the bat, he deepens an already strong order. A middle-order batter who can also bowl four overs lets Rashid Khan pick an extra bowler or an extra batter depending on the conditions — and in a tournament played across six venues, that flexibility is worth more than any single performance.

Where MI Cape Town stand

MI Cape Town won their first SA20 title in 2025 and spent the most at the auction on Mulder, then added Boult and Hetmyer at R1.5 million each. Rashid Khan captains, Robin Peterson coaches, and Newlands hosts five home league games. Trent Boult is the other big-money auction signing in the same attack. The SA20 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February.

Newlands in Cape Town hosts five home league games in Season 5, the first against Joburg Super Kings on 20 January 2027. The season is 34 matches in all — 30 league games, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the final.

Frequently asked questions

How much did MI Cape Town pay for Wiaan Mulder?

R2.6 million at the auction — more than any other player the club bought, ahead of Trent Boult and Shimron Hetmyer at R1.5 million each.

What is Wiaan Mulder's role for MI Cape Town?

He is a domestic seam-bowling all-rounder who bats in the middle order and bowls right-arm medium-fast through the middle overs.

Who captains MI Cape Town in SA20 Season 5?

Rashid Khan, who has captained the side since the first season. Robin Peterson is the head coach.
