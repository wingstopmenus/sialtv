Heinrich Klaasen — the wildcard who destroys spin

Heinrich Klaasen is back with Durban’s Super Giants for SA20 Season 5, retained as the club’s wildcard pick. The wicketkeeper-batter is one of the most destructive hitters of spin bowling in world cricket — and the wildcard slot exists for players exactly like him.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The wildcard, and the most feared hitter of spin

Klaasen was kept as Durban’s wildcard — the special retention slot reserved for a player a club cannot afford to lose. It is a fitting label for a batter who has made a career of taking apart the world’s best spinners.

He bats right-handed in the middle order and hits the ball as cleanly as anyone in the league. Against spin he is brutal, stepping down the pitch and clearing the boundary with a still head and fast hands; against pace he is just as dangerous. Bowlers do not get a rest when Klaasen is at the crease — every ball is a scoring ball.

Behind the stumps, he gives the side a second skill. Standing up to Sunil Narine and Simon Harmer, taking the stumping chances, and keeping the field honest — Klaasen does the keeping work that lets the bowlers attack.

The middle-order batter who changes games in ten balls

Klaasen does not need time to get going. Ten balls can be enough to turn a tight game into a comfortable win.

That speed is what makes him so valuable. While other batters need twenty balls to find their rhythm, Klaasen arrives ready to hit — which means the Super Giants can leave their acceleration late and still post a winning total.

A settled squad, still chasing the first title

Durban’s Super Giants are still chasing a first SA20 title after four seasons, and they kept the core together to do it — 18 retained players, plus Sam Curran as a pre-signing, and nobody bought at the auction. Aiden Markram captains and Lance Klusener coaches.

The SA20 Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027, and the Super Giants play their home games at Kingsmead in Durban, five of them in the league stage.
