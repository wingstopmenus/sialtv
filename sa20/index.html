How Season 5 works

Six teams, ten league games each, every opponent twice — once at home, once away. The top four at the end of the league stage reach the playoffs, and four matches across six days in February decide the title.

StageDateTime (SAST)Venue
Qualifier 1 — 1st vs 2ndTue 16 Feb5:30 PMBoland Park, Paarl
Eliminator — 3rd vs 4thWed 17 Feb5:30 PMWanderers, Johannesburg
Qualifier 2Fri 19 Feb5:30 PMWanderers, Johannesburg
FinalSun 21 Feb3:30 PMNewlands, Cape Town

Qualifier 1 is first against second, and its winner goes straight to the final. The Eliminator is third against fourth, and its winner meets the loser of Qualifier 1 in Qualifier 2 for the other place in the final. Playoff pairings depend on the league standings, so the teams are TBC until the league stage ends on 14 February.

Six grounds across South Africa

Every match is at one of six grounds. The Wanderers in Johannesburg hosts the most matches — seven, including the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 — while Boland Park and Newlands host six each, and St George’s Park, Kingsmead and SuperSport Park host five each.
