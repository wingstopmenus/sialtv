Frequently asked questions

When does SA20 Season 5 start?

Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027 with Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals at St George's Park, Gqeberha, and ends with the final on Sunday 21 February 2027 at Newlands, Cape Town.

How many teams play in SA20?

Six teams: MI Cape Town, Joburg Super Kings, Sunrisers Eastern Cape, Durban's Super Giants, Pretoria Capitals and Paarl Royals. Each team plays 10 league matches.

Where are SA20 matches played?

At six grounds across South Africa: Newlands in Cape Town, the Wanderers in Johannesburg, St George's Park in Gqeberha, Kingsmead in Durban, SuperSport Park in Centurion and Boland Park in Paarl.

Where can I watch SA20 in Pakistan?

On Apex, the official broadcaster for Pakistan in Season 5. In India the league is on JioStar, in the UK on Sky Sports, and in Sub-Saharan Africa on SuperSport.
