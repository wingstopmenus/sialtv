SA20 Season 5 — Schedule, Teams & Points Table

SA20 returns for Season 5 on 17 January 2027, with 6 teams playing 34 matches across South Africa. Sunrisers Eastern Cape open the season against Pretoria Capitals at St George's Park, and the final is at Newlands on 21 February.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Season opener — Sunday 17 January 2027

Match 1: Sunrisers Eastern Cape vs Pretoria Capitals
St George's Park, Gqeberha — 3:30 PM SAST (6:30 PM PKT)

Sunrisers Eastern Cape are captained by Tristan Stubbs. Pretoria Capitals are captained by Keshav Maharaj and coached by Sourav Ganguly.

SA20 Season 5 points table

The season has not started yet, so there is no points table for Season 5. The table will appear here after the first match on 17 January 2027, and will be updated after every match day. A win is worth 2 points.

Frequently asked questions

When does SA20 Season 5 start?

Season 5 starts on Sunday 17 January 2027 with Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals at St George's Park, Gqeberha, and ends with the final on Sunday 21 February 2027 at Newlands, Cape Town.

How many teams play in SA20?

Six teams: MI Cape Town, Joburg Super Kings, Sunrisers Eastern Cape, Durban's Super Giants, Pretoria Capitals and Paarl Royals. Each team plays 10 league matches.

Where are SA20 matches played?

At six grounds across South Africa: Newlands in Cape Town, the Wanderers in Johannesburg, St George's Park in Gqeberha, Kingsmead in Durban, SuperSport Park in Centurion and Boland Park in Paarl.

Where can I watch SA20 in Pakistan?

On Apex, the official broadcaster for Pakistan in Season 5. In India the league is on JioStar, in the UK on Sky Sports, and in Sub-Saharan Africa on SuperSport.

Where can I see the full SA20 schedule and every scorecard?

On the official SA20 site, sa20.co.za, which carries the complete fixture list, squads and news for Season 5.
