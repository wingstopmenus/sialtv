Joburg Super Kings vs MI Cape Town, Wednesday, 10 February 2027 — Wanderers

The 25th match of SA20 Season 5 is Joburg Super Kings against MI Cape Town on Wednesday, 10 February 2027, at Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC).

The two sides already met in Match 4 on 20 January at Newlands, so this is the return meeting with the home side switched. For Joburg it is their fifth home league game of five — their last home game of the league stage — and for MI Cape Town it is their fourth away league game of five. It is the only SA20 game on Wednesday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

There is no Season 5 form yet — the season starts on 17 January — but by 10 February the table will be nearly final, and both sides will know what this game is worth.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

Match 25 is Joburg's last home game of the league stage, and the Wanderers crowd will want a win to sign off with. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs, so the final week of the league is where the cut is decided.

For Joburg, winning their fifth home game would finish their home season on a high and could be the result that takes them into the playoffs. For MI Cape Town, an away win in Johannesburg would settle their head-to-head with Joburg for the season and keep their own top-four push on track.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

James Vince is the opener who times the ball as sweetly as anyone in the competition. Vince's cover drives are a feature of a Joburg innings, and a fast start from him sets the tone.

Jason Holder is the tall all-rounder who bowls with bounce and bats with power. Holder's overs through the middle and at the death give Joburg control, and his late hitting can rescue an innings.

Rashid Khan leads MI Cape Town and bowls the leg-spin that has made him one of the most feared bowlers in T20 cricket. He takes wickets in the middle overs and rarely goes for runs, and as captain he sets the tone for the whole side.

Will Jacks is the opener who attacks from the first ball. Jacks can take a powerplay away from the bowling side in a handful of overs, and his off-spin gives MI Cape Town an extra option.

Wanderers Stadium

The Wanderers was built in 1956 to replace the Old Wanderers ground, and its first Test came that December against England. South Africans call it the Bullring, for its round shape and its intimidating atmosphere, and it hosted the 2003 World Cup final.

In Season 5, Wanderers hosts seven matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 — more than any other ground. The published capacity is 34,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
