Final, Sunday, 21 February 2027 — Newlands

The final is the last match of SA20 Season 5. It is played at Newlands Cricket Ground in Cape Town on Sunday 21 February 2027, with the first ball at 3:30 PM SAST (6:30 PM PKT, 1:30 PM UTC). The two sides are the winner of Qualifier 1 and the winner of Qualifier 2, and they will only be known once Qualifier 2 is played on Friday 19 February.

There is no second chance left. One match, one evening in Cape Town, decides the champions of Season 5. Newlands hosts six matches in Season 5, and the final is the biggest of them — the same ground where the season's last league game was played a week earlier, on Sunday 14 February.

How the playoffs work

Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and the top four reach the playoffs. The seeds are decided by the final points table after Match 30, the last league game, on Sunday 14 February.

Qualifier 1, on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, is 1st against 2nd, and the winner goes straight to the final. The Eliminator, on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, is 3rd against 4th — the loser is out, and the winner goes to Qualifier 2. Qualifier 2, on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, is the loser of Qualifier 1 against the winner of the Eliminator, and the winner takes the second final place. The final is on Sunday 21 February at Newlands.

There is one rest day in the playoff week: Thursday 18 February, between the Eliminator and Qualifier 2. The league stage ends on Sunday 14 February, and Monday 15 February is a rest day before the playoffs begin.

What’s at stake

The final is the only match of the season where the prize is the title itself. The winner of Qualifier 1 arrives with five days of rest since Tuesday 16 February; the winner of Qualifier 2 arrives with one day of rest since Friday 19 February, and possibly with two knockout wins already behind them in the week. Rest against momentum is the shape of every final played under this format, and this one is no different.

The two finalists will be named on this page once Qualifier 2 is played on Friday 19 February. Until then, the only honest statement is that the winners of Qualifier 1 and Qualifier 2 meet here — and that one of them will be crowned champions of SA20 Season 5 on Sunday night.

The road to the final

The winner of Qualifier 1 took the shortest road: one win on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, then five days to prepare for this final. The winner of Qualifier 2 took the longer road. If they were the loser of Qualifier 1, they won again on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers to reach the final. If they were the winner of the Eliminator, they won on Wednesday 17 February and again on Friday — two knockout wins in three days — to reach the final. Both roads end here, at Newlands, on Sunday 21 February.

Newlands Cricket Ground

Newlands opened in 1888, and its first Test followed in March 1889, when England beat South Africa by an innings and 202 runs. The ground sits beneath Table Mountain, and it staged games at the 2003 World Cup.

In Season 5, Newlands hosts six matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus the final on 21 February. The published capacity is 25,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

Frequently asked questions

When is the SA20 Season 5 final?

Sunday 21 February 2027, at Newlands Cricket Ground in Cape Town. The first ball is at 3:30 PM SAST, which is 6:30 PM in Pakistan, 7:00 PM in India, 1:30 PM in the UK and 1:30 PM UTC.

Who plays in the final?

The winner of Qualifier 1 plays the winner of Qualifier 2. Neither side is known yet — Qualifier 1 is played on Tuesday 16 February, the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February, and Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February. This page will name the two finalists once Qualifier 2 is played.

How do the playoffs work?

The top four sides from the league stage qualify. Qualifier 1 is 1st against 2nd, and the winner goes straight to the final. The Eliminator is 3rd against 4th, and the loser is out. Qualifier 2 is the loser of Qualifier 1 against the winner of the Eliminator, and the winner takes the second final place.

Where can I watch the final?

The match is shown by SuperSport in Sub-Saharan Africa, JioStar in India, Sky Sports in the UK, Fox Sports in Australia, Apex in Pakistan and Willow by Cricbuzz in North America. Broadcasters for the UAE and the Middle East, and for the rest of the world, are still to be confirmed.
