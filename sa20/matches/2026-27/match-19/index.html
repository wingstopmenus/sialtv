MI Cape Town vs Sunrisers Eastern Cape, Thursday, 4 February 2027 — Newlands

The 19th match of SA20 Season 5 is MI Cape Town against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on Thursday, 4 February 2027, at Newlands Cricket Ground in Cape Town. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC).

The two sides already met in Match 14 on 30 January at St George's Park, so this is the return meeting with the home side switched. For MI Cape Town it is their fourth home league game of five, and for the Sunrisers it is their fourth away league game of five. It is the only SA20 game on Thursday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

Match 19 sits in the middle of the league, and by now the shape of the top four is starting to form. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs, so dropping a home game at this stage of the season is expensive.

For MI Cape Town, a fourth home game at Newlands is a chance to bank points in front of their own crowd before the run-in. For the Sunrisers, an away win in Cape Town would be a statement — and it would settle the head-to-head between these two sides for the season, after the first meeting in Gqeberha.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Rashid Khan leads MI Cape Town and bowls the leg-spin that has made him one of the most feared bowlers in T20 cricket. He takes wickets in the middle overs and rarely goes for runs, and as captain he sets the tone for the whole side.

Kagiso Rabada is South Africa's spearhead with the new ball. His pace and bounce make the first over a contest in itself, and he is just as dangerous coming back at the death.

Quinton de Kock is the wicketkeeper-batter who opens for the Sunrisers and sets the tempo. When de Kock finds the gaps early, the powerplay belongs to his side.

Tristan Stubbs captains Sunrisers Eastern Cape and bats in the middle order, where his clean hitting turns good totals into winning ones. He is also the fielder opposition batters try hardest to avoid.

Newlands Cricket Ground

Newlands opened in 1888, and its first Test followed in March 1889, when England beat South Africa by an innings and 202 runs. The ground sits beneath Table Mountain, and it staged games at the 2003 World Cup.

In Season 5, Newlands hosts six matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus the final on 21 February. The published capacity is 25,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
