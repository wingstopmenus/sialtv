MI Cape Town vs Paarl Royals, Friday, 22 January 2027 — Cape Town

The 6th match of the SA20 Season 5 Season 5 season is MI Cape Town against Paarl Royals on Friday, 22 January 2027, at Newlands Cricket Ground in Cape Town. It is the 2nd home league game of five for MI Cape Town, and the 1st away league game of five for Paarl Royals. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 6 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides meet again in Match 9 on 24 January 2027 at Boland Park, Paarl. That return game has the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. It is the only SA20 game on Friday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). That is 9:00 PM IST and 3:30 PM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Kagiso Rabada — MI Cape Town’s spearhead with the new ball and at the death. His pace and bounce make him a threat on any surface, and in a tight finish he is the bowler the captain turns to first.

Will Jacks — MI Cape Town’s attacking opener, who can also bowl useful off-spin. He likes to take the powerplay on, and a fast start from him lets the middle order play with freedom.

Sikandar Raza — Paarl Royals’ all-rounder, a middle-order batter and off-spinner who does a bit of everything. He bowls tight overs, fields brilliantly, and has a habit of producing a match-turning moment when his side needs one.

Azmatullah Omarzai — Paarl Royals’ seam-bowling all-rounder, who hits the deck hard and strikes the ball cleanly down the order. He gives the Royals balance — a fourth or fifth bowling option and a finisher in one player.

Newlands Cricket Ground, Cape Town

Newlands Cricket Ground in Cape Town, Western Cape, is the home ground of MI Cape Town, who play their five home league games here. The published capacity is 25,000, and the ground hosts 6 SA20 games in Season 5 — five league matches plus the final on 21 February. Capacities on this site are published figures, and this one has not been reconfirmed by SA20 for 2027, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

For the visiting side, this is an away ground in every sense: a different city, a different crowd, and a surface the home side knows well. There are no Season 5 results at this ground yet to read into, so the first games here will set the pattern — and the side that adapts fastest to the conditions usually takes the points.
