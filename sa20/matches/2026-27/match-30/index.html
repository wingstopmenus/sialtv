MI Cape Town vs Pretoria Capitals, Sunday, 14 February 2027 — Newlands

The 30th match of SA20 Season 5 brings MI Cape Town and Pretoria Capitals together at Newlands Cricket Ground in Cape Town on Sunday, 14 February 2027. The first ball is at 3:30 PM SAST (6:30 PM PKT, 1:30 PM UTC).

The two sides already met in Match 27 on 12 February at SuperSport Park, so this is the return meeting with the home side switched — just two days later. For MI Cape Town it is their fifth home league game of five — their last home game of the league stage — and for Pretoria it is their fifth away league game of five, their last away game. It is the only SA20 game on Sunday, and the last league game of the season.

There is no Season 5 form to judge yet — the season starts on 17 January — but by 14 February the table will be all but final, and this game could decide which of these two sides goes into the playoffs.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

Match 30 is the last league game of the season, and it decides the final shape of the table. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs — so this game could be the difference between a playoff place and going home.

For MI Cape Town, a win in their last home game would finish their home season on a high and could be the result that takes them into the playoffs. For Pretoria, winning their last away game at Newlands — two days after hosting the same opponent — would be the statement result of their season, and it would settle the head-to-head in their favour.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Rashid Khan leads MI Cape Town and bowls the leg-spin that has made him one of the most feared bowlers in T20 cricket. He takes wickets in the middle overs and rarely goes for runs, and as captain he sets the tone for the whole side.

Nicholas Pooran is the wicketkeeper-batter who can clear any boundary in the ground. When he is set, MI Cape Town's total grows in a hurry, and bowlers have nowhere safe to bowl to him.

Andre Russell is the all-rounder who can win a match in a single over with bat or ball. Russell's power hitting is famous, and his pace at the death gives Pretoria a wicket-taking option when it matters most.

Keshav Maharaj captains Pretoria Capitals and bowls the left-arm spin that ties batters down. Maharaj's control in the middle overs builds the pressure that brings wickets at the other end.

Newlands Cricket Ground

Newlands opened in 1888, and its first Test followed in March 1889, when England beat South Africa by an innings and 202 runs. The ground sits beneath Table Mountain, and it staged games at the 2003 World Cup.

In Season 5, Newlands hosts six matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus the final on 21 February. The published capacity is 25,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

Frequently asked questions

When does MI Cape Town vs Pretoria Capitals start?

Match 30 is played on Sunday, 14 February 2027 at Newlands Cricket Ground in Cape Town. The first ball is at 3:30 PM SAST, which is 6:30 PM PKT, 7:00 PM IST and 1:30 PM UTC, and 3:30 PM local time in Cape Town. It is the 30th match of SA20 Season 5.

Who captains each side in Match 30?

MI Cape Town are captained by Rashid Khan. Pretoria Capitals are captained by Keshav Maharaj. Both sides will confirm any change before the season starts, and this page will be updated when they do.

Who are the players to watch?

Rashid Khan and Nicholas Pooran for MI Cape Town, and Andre Russell and Keshav Maharaj for Pretoria Capitals. Each of them has a profile linked in the players section above.

What happens after Match 30?

The league stage ends, the table is final, and the top four go into the playoffs. Qualifier 1 is at Boland Park on Tuesday 16 February, the Eliminator is at the Wanderers on Wednesday 17 February, Qualifier 2 is at the Wanderers on Friday 19 February, and the final is at Newlands on Sunday 21 February.
