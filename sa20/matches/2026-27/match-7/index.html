Durban's Super Giants vs Pretoria Capitals, Saturday, 23 January 2027 — Durban

The 7th match of the SA20 Season 5 season is Durban's Super Giants against Pretoria Capitals on Saturday, 23 January 2027, at Kingsmead in Durban. It is the 2nd home league game of five for Durban's Super Giants, and the 2nd away league game of five for Pretoria Capitals. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 7 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides already met in Match 3 on 19 January 2027 at SuperSport Park, Centurion. This game is the return meeting, with the home side switched. It is one of two games on Saturday: the other is Match 8, Joburg Super Kings against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at Wanderers, starting at 5:30 PM SAST, in a different city, so the two games cannot both be watched in person.

The first ball is at 1:00 PM SAST (4:00 PM PKT, 11:00 AM UTC). That is 4:30 PM IST and 11:00 AM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Sam Curran — Durban’s Super Giants’ left-arm all-rounder, who bowls at the start and the death and bats anywhere in the order. His variety with the ball and his nerve with the bat make him a player for the tight moments.

Jos Buttler — Durban’s Super Giants’ wicketkeeper-opener and one of the great white-ball batters. He can anchor an innings or finish it in a handful of overs, and his record in T20 leagues around the world needs no introduction.

Keshav Maharaj — Pretoria Capitals’ captain and left-arm spinner. He controls the middle overs with accuracy and drift, and as captain he has the job of turning a talented squad into a side that wins tight games.

Matthew Short — Pretoria Capitals’ opener and off-spinning all-rounder. He scores quickly at the top and gives the captain an extra bowling option, which lets the side balance its attack around him.

Kingsmead, Durban

Kingsmead in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, is the home ground of Durban's Super Giants, who play their five home league games here. For the visiting side, this is an away ground in every sense: a different city, a different crowd, and a surface the home side knows well. There are no Season 5 results at this ground yet to read into, so the first games here will set the pattern — and the side that adapts fastest to the conditions usually takes the points.
