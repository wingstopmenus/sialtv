Pretoria Capitals vs MI Cape Town, Friday, 12 February 2027 — SuperSport Park

The 27th match of SA20 Season 5 is Pretoria Capitals against MI Cape Town on Friday, 12 February 2027, at SuperSport Park in Centurion. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC).

The two sides meet again in Match 30 on 14 February at Newlands, with the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. For Pretoria it is their fifth home league game of five — their last home game of the league stage — and for MI Cape Town it is their fifth away league game of five, their last away game. It is the only SA20 game on Friday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

There is no Season 5 form to judge yet — the season starts on 17 January — but by 12 February the table will be nearly final, and this game is the first half of a two-match story that ends the league stage.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

Match 27 is the last home game of Pretoria's league season and the last away game of MI Cape Town's, and it is the first of two meetings in three days. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs.

For Pretoria, a home win at SuperSport Park would set up the Newlands return nicely and could be the result that takes them into the playoffs. For MI Cape Town, an away win in Centurion would take the pressure off the return game and settle the head-to-head in their favour before the last league match.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Dewald Brevis is the young batter known for the range of shots he plays. Brevis takes on spin and pace alike, and a quick start from him lifts the whole Pretoria innings.

Matthew Short is the opener who scores quickly and bowls useful off-spin. Short's powerplay runs set the tempo for Pretoria, and his overs give the captain room to shuffle the attack.

Nicholas Pooran is the wicketkeeper-batter who can clear any boundary in the ground. When he is set, MI Cape Town's total grows in a hurry, and bowlers have nowhere safe to bowl to him.

Kagiso Rabada is South Africa's spearhead with the new ball. His pace and bounce make the first over a contest in itself, and he is just as dangerous coming back at the death.

SuperSport Park

SuperSport Park opened in 1986 as Centurion Park, and its first international was a one-day game against India in 1992. Its first Test came in November 1995, against England, the match in which Shaun Pollock made his South Africa debut. It is the home of the Titans.

In Season 5, SuperSport Park hosts five league matches in Season 5, all of them Pretoria Capitals home games. The published capacity is 22,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
