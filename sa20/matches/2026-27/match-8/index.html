Joburg Super Kings vs Sunrisers Eastern Cape, Saturday, 23 January 2027 — Johannesburg

The 8th match of the SA20 Season 5 Season 5 season is Joburg Super Kings against Sunrisers Eastern Cape on Saturday, 23 January 2027, at Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg. It is the 1st home league game of five for Joburg Super Kings, and the 2nd away league game of five for Sunrisers Eastern Cape. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 8 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides meet again in Match 12 on 28 January 2027 at St George's Park, Gqeberha. That return game has the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. It is one of two games on Saturday: the other is Match 7, Durban's Super Giants against Pretoria Capitals at Kingsmead, starting at 1:00 PM SAST, in a different city, so the two games cannot both be watched in person.

The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). That is 9:00 PM IST and 3:30 PM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Jason Holder — Joburg Super Kings’ tall seam-bowling all-rounder. He takes wickets with the new ball, bowls hard overs at the death, and his power with the bat down the order has won games on its own.

Jamie Overton — Joburg Super Kings’ fast-bowling all-rounder. His pace with the ball troubles batters, and his hitting down the order gives the side late runs — a two-in-one player who changes the shape of a team.

Mitchell Marsh — Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s all-rounder, a powerful top-order batter who bowls useful medium pace. His experience of big T20 occasions makes him the player the Sunrisers lean on when a game is in the balance.

Anrich Nortje — Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s express fast bowler. Genuine pace is his weapon, and on a lively surface he can hurry even set batters — the kind of bowler who can take two wickets in an over and turn a match.

Wanderers Stadium, Johannesburg

Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg, Gauteng, is the home ground of Joburg Super Kings, who play their five home league games here. The published capacity is 34,000, and the ground hosts 7 SA20 games in Season 5 — five league matches plus the Eliminator on 17 February and Qualifier 2 on 19 February. Capacities on this site are published figures, and this one has not been reconfirmed by SA20 for 2027, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

For the visiting side, this is an away ground in every sense: a different city, a different crowd, and a surface the home side knows well. There are no Season 5 results at this ground yet to read into, so the first games here will set the pattern — and the side that adapts fastest to the conditions usually takes the points.

Frequently asked questions

When does Joburg Super Kings vs Sunrisers Eastern Cape start?

Match 8 is played on Saturday, 23 January 2027 at Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST, which is 8:30 PM PKT and 3:30 PM UTC. It is 9:00 PM IST in India and 3:30 PM UK in the UK, all on the same day.

Where is Match 8 played?

Where can I watch this match?

In South Africa matches are on SuperSport, in Pakistan on Apex, in India on JioStar, in the UK on Sky Sports, in Australia on Fox Sports and in North America on Willow by Cricbuzz. Broadcasters for the UAE and Middle East and the rest of the world are still to be confirmed, so check your local listings closer to the day.

Why is the Wanderers called the Bullring?

The Wanderers in Johannesburg is known as the Bullring, a nickname that comes from its steep stands and the noise the crowd makes. It is the home ground of Joburg Super Kings, and its published capacity is 34,000.
