Durban's Super Giants vs MI Cape Town, Saturday, 6 February 2027 — Kingsmead

The 21st match of SA20 Season 5 is Durban's Super Giants against MI Cape Town on Saturday, 6 February 2027, at Kingsmead in Durban. The first ball is at 1:00 PM SAST (4:00 PM PKT, 11:00 AM UTC).

The two sides already met in Match 17 on 2 February at Newlands, so this is the return meeting with the home side switched. For Durban it is their fourth home league game of five, and for MI Cape Town it is their third away league game of five. It is one of two games on Saturday: the other is Match 22, Paarl Royals against Sunrisers Eastern Cape at Boland Park, starting at 5:30 PM SAST in a different city, so the two games cannot both be watched in person.

Durban are led by Aiden Markram, who bats at the top of the order and captains from the front. MI Cape Town are led by Rashid Khan. This is the final stretch of the league, and although there is no Season 5 form yet — the season starts on 17 January — the table will be taking shape by the time this game is played, and both sides will know exactly what a win is worth.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

The final stretch of the league starts here, and the 1:00 PM start makes this the first game of a Saturday double-header. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs — so the margin for error is shrinking fast.

For Durban, a home win at Kingsmead would settle their head-to-head with MI Cape Town for the season and keep them in the top-four race. For MI Cape Town, an away win on the coast would do the same in reverse, and it would come against a side they only faced four days earlier in Cape Town.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Jos Buttler is the wicketkeeper-batter who has won T20 games on his own more times than most. Buttler reads a chase as well as anyone, and his range of shots makes every part of the ground a scoring area.

Heinrich Klaasen is the finisher every bowling side fears. Klaasen hits spin and pace alike into the stands, and the last five overs of a Durban innings belong to him.

Nicholas Pooran is the wicketkeeper-batter who can clear any boundary in the ground. When he is set, MI Cape Town's total grows in a hurry, and bowlers have nowhere safe to bowl to him.

Trent Boult is the left-arm swing bowler who moves the new ball both ways. An early wicket from Boult changes the shape of an innings, and his death bowling is built on yorkers and changes of pace.

Kingsmead

Kingsmead staged its first Test in January 1923, a draw between South Africa and England. It is still famous for the timeless Test of 1939, which ended after ten days because England had to catch their boat home, and Yuvraj Singh hit six sixes in an over here at the 2007 T20 World Cup. The ground sits close to Durban's Indian Ocean coastline.

In Season 5, Kingsmead hosts five league matches in Season 5. The published capacity is 25,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
