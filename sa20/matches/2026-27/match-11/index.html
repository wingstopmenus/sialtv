Durban's Super Giants vs Paarl Royals, Wednesday, 27 January 2027 — Durban

The 11th match of the SA20 Season 5 season is Durban's Super Giants against Paarl Royals on Wednesday, 27 January 2027, at Kingsmead in Durban. It is the 3rd home league game of five for Durban's Super Giants, and the 2nd away league game of five for Paarl Royals. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 11 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides meet again in Match 26 on 11 February 2027 at Boland Park, Paarl. That return game has the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. It is the only SA20 game on Wednesday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). That is 9:00 PM IST and 3:30 PM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Aiden Markram — Durban’s Super Giants’ captain, a top-order batter who also bowls handy off-spin. He leads a side packed with match-winners, and his own calm at the top of the order sets the tempo for the innings.

Heinrich Klaasen — Durban’s Super Giants’ middle-order destroyer, famous for the way he takes on spin. When the spinners come on, he is the batter the opposition plan for most — and the one most likely to clear the ropes.

Joe Root — Paarl Royals’ top-order anchor, whose class and timing hold an innings together. He scores all around the ground, rotates the strike, and lets the power hitters around him take the risks.

Sikandar Raza — Paarl Royals’ all-rounder, a middle-order batter and off-spinner who does a bit of everything. He bowls tight overs, fields brilliantly, and has a habit of producing a match-turning moment when his side needs one.
