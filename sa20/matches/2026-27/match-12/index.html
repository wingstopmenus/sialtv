Sunrisers Eastern Cape vs Joburg Super Kings, Thursday, 28 January 2027 — Gqeberha

The 12th match of the SA20 Season 5 Season 5 season is Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Joburg Super Kings on Thursday, 28 January 2027, at St George's Park in Gqeberha. It is the 2nd home league game of five for Sunrisers Eastern Cape, and the 3rd away league game of five for Joburg Super Kings. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 12 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides already met in Match 8 on 23 January 2027 at Wanderers, Johannesburg. This game is the return meeting, with the home side switched. It is the only SA20 game on Thursday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). That is 9:00 PM IST and 3:30 PM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Tristan Stubbs — Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s captain and middle-order power hitter. He takes on spin and pace alike in the second half of an innings, and as captain he sets the tone for a side chasing a fourth straight title.

Marco Jansen — Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s tall left-arm fast bowler, who takes the new ball and hits the bat hard. He also strikes the ball cleanly down the order, so he changes games at both ends of an innings.

Faf du Plessis — Joburg Super Kings’ most experienced batter, a top-order player with a long record in T20 leagues around the world. His calm at the top of the order and his fielding set the standard for the side around him.

James Vince — Joburg Super Kings’ elegant top-order batter, who scores quickly when he finds his timing. On his day he makes batting look easy, and the Super Kings will want him facing plenty of balls at the Wanderers.

St George's Park, Gqeberha

St George's Park in Gqeberha, Eastern Cape, is the home ground of Sunrisers Eastern Cape, who play their five home league games here. The published capacity is 19,000, and the ground hosts 5 SA20 games in Season 5 — five league matches, including the season opener. Capacities on this site are published figures, and this one has not been reconfirmed by SA20 for 2027, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

For the visiting side, this is an away ground in every sense: a different city, a different crowd, and a surface the home side knows well. There are no Season 5 results at this ground yet to read into, so the first games here will set the pattern — and the side that adapts fastest to the conditions usually takes the points.
