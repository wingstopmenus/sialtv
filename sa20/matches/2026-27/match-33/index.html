Qualifier 2, Friday, 19 February 2027 — Wanderers

Qualifier 2 is the third playoff match of SA20 Season 5. It is played at the Wanderers in Johannesburg on Friday 19 February 2027, with the first ball at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). The two sides are the loser of Qualifier 1 and the winner of the Eliminator, and they will only be known once those matches are played on 16 and 17 February.

The winner takes the second place in the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands, against the winner of Qualifier 1. The loser goes home. The Wanderers hosts seven matches in Season 5, more than any other ground, and this is the second of its two playoff matches.

How the playoffs work

Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and the top four reach the playoffs. The seeds are decided by the final points table after Match 30, the last league game, on Sunday 14 February.

Qualifier 1, on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, is 1st against 2nd, and the winner goes straight to the final. The Eliminator, on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, is 3rd against 4th — the loser is out, and the winner goes to Qualifier 2. Qualifier 2, on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, is the loser of Qualifier 1 against the winner of the Eliminator, and the winner takes the second final place. The final is on Sunday 21 February at Newlands.

There is one rest day in the playoff week: Thursday 18 February, between the Eliminator and Qualifier 2. The league stage ends on Sunday 14 February, and Monday 15 February is a rest day before the playoffs begin.

What’s at stake

Qualifier 2 is the last chance in the tournament. One side arrives having lost Qualifier 1 on Tuesday and needing to win again to reach the final; the other arrives having won the Eliminator on Wednesday and needing a second knockout win in three days. Only one of them will be playing on Sunday.

The two sides will be known once Qualifier 1 and the Eliminator are played. Until then, the only honest statement is that the loser of Qualifier 1 meets the winner of the Eliminator here, and the winner of this match plays in the final.

The road to the final

The road from here is one match long. The winner plays the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands, against the winner of Qualifier 1 — a side that has had five days of rest since Tuesday, against a side that has played a knockout on Friday. Rest against momentum is the shape of this final, whichever way the week has gone.

Wanderers Stadium

The Wanderers was built in 1956 to replace the Old Wanderers ground, and its first Test came that December against England. South Africans call it the Bullring, for its round shape and its intimidating atmosphere, and it hosted the 2003 World Cup final.

In Season 5, Wanderers hosts seven matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 — more than any other ground. The published capacity is 34,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

Frequently asked questions

When is SA20 Qualifier 2?

Friday 19 February 2027, at the Wanderers in Johannesburg. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST, which is 8:30 PM in Pakistan, 9:00 PM in India, 3:30 PM in the UK and 3:30 PM UTC.

Who plays in Qualifier 2?

The loser of Qualifier 1 meets the winner of the Eliminator. Neither side is known yet — Qualifier 1 is played on Tuesday 16 February and the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February. This page will name the two sides once those matches are played.

What does the winner get?

A place in the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands, against the winner of Qualifier 1. The loser is eliminated, and the season ends for them on Friday night.

Where can I watch Qualifier 2?

The match is shown by SuperSport in Sub-Saharan Africa, JioStar in India, Sky Sports in the UK, Fox Sports in Australia, Apex in Pakistan and Willow by Cricbuzz in North America. Broadcasters for the UAE and the Middle East, and for the rest of the world, are still to be confirmed.
