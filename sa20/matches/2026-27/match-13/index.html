Paarl Royals vs Pretoria Capitals, Friday, 29 January 2027 — Paarl

The 13th match of the SA20 Season 5 season is Paarl Royals against Pretoria Capitals on Friday, 29 January 2027, at Boland Park in Paarl. It is the 3rd home league game of five for Paarl Royals, and the 3rd away league game of five for Pretoria Capitals. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 13 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides meet again in Match 16 on 31 January 2027 at SuperSport Park, Centurion. That return game has the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. It is the only SA20 game on Friday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). That is 9:00 PM IST and 3:30 PM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Azmatullah Omarzai — Paarl Royals’ seam-bowling all-rounder, who hits the deck hard and strikes the ball cleanly down the order. He gives the Royals balance — a fourth or fifth bowling option and a finisher in one player.

Lhuan-dre Pretorius — Paarl Royals’ young wicketkeeper-batter, one of the brightest young names in South African cricket. He bats with freedom at the top of the order and gives the Royals a fresh, fearless option.

Dewald Brevis — Pretoria Capitals’ young attacking batter, who plays with freedom and clears the boundary with ease. He is the kind of player who can take a game away in a few overs, and the Capitals will want him facing as many balls as possible.

Keshav Maharaj — Pretoria Capitals’ captain and left-arm spinner. He controls the middle overs with accuracy and drift, and as captain he has the job of turning a talented squad into a side that wins tight games.

Boland Park, Paarl

Boland Park in Paarl, Western Cape, is the home ground of Paarl Royals, who play their five home league games here. The published capacity is 10,000, and the ground hosts 6 SA20 games in Season 5 — five league matches plus Qualifier 1 on 16 February. Capacities on this site are published figures, and this one has not been reconfirmed by SA20 for 2027, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

For the visiting side, this is an away ground in every sense: a different city, a different crowd, and a surface the home side knows well. There are no Season 5 results at this ground yet to read into, so the first games here will set the pattern — and the side that adapts fastest to the conditions usually takes the points.
