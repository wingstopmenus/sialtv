Pretoria Capitals vs Joburg Super Kings, Friday, 5 February 2027 — SuperSport Park

The 20th match of SA20 Season 5 brings Pretoria Capitals and Joburg Super Kings together at SuperSport Park in Centurion on Friday, 5 February 2027. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC).

The two sides meet again in Match 23 on 7 February at the Wanderers, with the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. For Pretoria it is their fourth home league game of five, and for Joburg it is their fourth away league game of five. It is the only SA20 game on Friday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

Match 20 is the last game before the league's final stretch, and it is also the first half of a two-match story: these sides meet again two days later in Johannesburg. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs.

For Pretoria, a home win at SuperSport Park would set up the return game nicely and keep their top-four hopes in their own hands. For Joburg, an away win in Centurion would take the pressure off the Wanderers return and give them a result to defend on home turf.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Andre Russell is the all-rounder who can win a match in a single over with bat or ball. Russell's power hitting is famous, and his pace at the death gives Pretoria a wicket-taking option when it matters most.

Keshav Maharaj captains Pretoria Capitals and bowls the left-arm spin that ties batters down. Maharaj's control in the middle overs builds the pressure that brings wickets at the other end.

James Vince is the opener who times the ball as sweetly as anyone in the competition. Vince's cover drives are a feature of a Joburg innings, and a fast start from him sets the tone.

Rilee Rossouw is the left-handed batter who scores quickly against spin and pace. Rossouw's range through the leg side makes him a danger from the moment he walks in.

SuperSport Park

SuperSport Park opened in 1986 as Centurion Park, and its first international was a one-day game against India in 1992. Its first Test came in November 1995, against England, the match in which Shaun Pollock made his South Africa debut. It is the home of the Titans.

In Season 5, SuperSport Park hosts five league matches in Season 5, all of them Pretoria Capitals home games. The published capacity is 22,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
