Paarl Royals vs Durban's Super Giants, Thursday, 11 February 2027 — Boland Park

The 26th match of SA20 Season 5 brings Paarl Royals and Durban's Super Giants together at Boland Park in Paarl on Thursday, 11 February 2027. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC).

The two sides already met in Match 11 on 27 January at Kingsmead, so this is the return meeting with the home side switched. For Paarl it is their fifth home league game of five — their last home game of the league stage — and for Durban it is their fifth away league game of five, their last away game. It is the only SA20 game on Thursday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

How both sides arrive in Season 5

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 17 January 2027, so there is no SA20 Season 5 form yet. Here is where Paarl Royals and Durban's Super Giants stand before a ball is bowled.

Paarl are led by David Miller, and Durban are led by Aiden Markram — two captains who bat in the middle and top of the order respectively. There is no Season 5 form to judge yet — the season starts on 17 January — but by 11 February the top four will be almost decided, and this game could be the one that settles it.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

Match 26 is the last home game of Paarl's league season and the last away game of Durban's, so both sides are playing their final game in this setting. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs.

For Paarl, a win at Boland Park would finish their home season unbeaten in spirit if not in fact, and it could be the result that carries them into the playoffs. For Durban, winning their fifth away game on the road would show they can win anywhere — and it would settle their head-to-head with Paarl for the season.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Joe Root is the batter who holds a Paarl innings together. Root finds gaps others cannot, rotates the strike, and accelerates when the moment comes — the anchor every T20 side wants.

Azmatullah Omarzai is the Afghan all-rounder who bowls with pace and hits the ball hard down the order. Omarzai's overs at the death and his late hitting give Paarl balance on both sides of the ball.

Heinrich Klaasen is the finisher every bowling side fears. Klaasen hits spin and pace alike into the stands, and the last five overs of a Durban innings belong to him.

Sam Curran is the left-arm all-rounder who does a bit of everything. Curran bowls at the start and the end of an innings, fields brilliantly, and has finished chases from positions that looked lost.

Boland Park

Boland Park opened in 1996, and its first one-day international, between India and Zimbabwe in January 1997, ended in a tie. It hosted three games at the 2003 World Cup, where Wasim Akram took his 500th one-day wicket against the Netherlands. It is the smallest ground in Season 5.

In Season 5, Boland Park hosts six matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus Qualifier 1, the first playoff it has hosted. The published capacity is 10,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
