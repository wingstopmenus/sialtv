Durban's Super Giants vs Joburg Super Kings, Saturday, 13 February 2027 — Kingsmead

The 28th match of SA20 Season 5 brings Durban's Super Giants and Joburg Super Kings together at Kingsmead in Durban on Saturday, 13 February 2027. The first ball is at 1:00 PM SAST (4:00 PM PKT, 11:00 AM UTC).

The two sides already met in Match 15 on 30 January at the Wanderers, so this is the return meeting with the home side switched. For Durban it is their fifth home league game of five — their last home game of the league stage — and for Joburg it is their fifth away league game of five, their last away game. It is one of two games on Saturday: the other is Match 29, Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Paarl Royals at St George's Park, starting at 5:30 PM SAST in a different city, so the two games cannot both be watched in person.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

The last Saturday of the league stage brings a double-header, and this is the early game. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs — so with two league matches left after this one, the margin for error is gone.

For Durban, a win in their last home game would finish their home season on a high and could be the result that takes them into the playoffs. For Joburg, winning their last away game on the coast would settle their head-to-head with Durban for the season and keep their own top-four hopes alive.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Aiden Markram captains Durban's Super Giants and bats at the top of the order. Markram's timing and composure set the platform, and his off-spin gives the side balance.

Jos Buttler is the wicketkeeper-batter who has won T20 games on his own more times than most. Buttler reads a chase as well as anyone, and his range of shots makes every part of the ground a scoring area.

Faf du Plessis is the veteran batter who has won T20 titles around the world and reads a game as well as anyone. Du Plessis steadies the Joburg top order with experience few in the competition can match.

Rilee Rossouw is the left-handed batter who scores quickly against spin and pace. Rossouw's range through the leg side makes him a danger from the moment he walks in.

Kingsmead

Kingsmead staged its first Test in January 1923, a draw between South Africa and England. It is still famous for the timeless Test of 1939, which ended after ten days because England had to catch their boat home, and Yuvraj Singh hit six sixes in an over here at the 2007 T20 World Cup. The ground sits close to Durban's Indian Ocean coastline.

In Season 5, Kingsmead hosts five league matches in Season 5. The published capacity is 25,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
