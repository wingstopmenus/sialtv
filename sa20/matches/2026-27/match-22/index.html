Paarl Royals vs Sunrisers Eastern Cape, Saturday, 6 February 2027 — Boland Park

The 22nd match of SA20 Season 5 brings Paarl Royals and Sunrisers Eastern Cape together at Boland Park in Paarl on Saturday, 6 February 2027. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC).

The two sides meet again in Match 29 on 13 February at St George's Park, with the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. For Paarl it is their fourth home league game of five, and for the Sunrisers it is their fifth away league game of five — their last away game of the league stage. It is one of two games on Saturday: the other is Match 21, Durban's Super Giants against MI Cape Town at Kingsmead, starting at 1:00 PM SAST in a different city, so the two games cannot both be watched in person.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

Match 22 is the evening half of a Saturday double-header, and for the Sunrisers it is the last away game of their league season. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs, so an away win here would be worth its weight in the run-in.

For Paarl, a home win at Boland Park keeps their playoff push moving in front of a small, loud crowd. For the Sunrisers, winning their fifth away game on the road would show they can win anywhere — and it would leave them with only home games to finish the league stage.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

David Miller captains Paarl Royals and finishes innings better than almost anyone. Miller's power against pace and spin makes the last five overs a Royals weapon, and his fielding saves runs all day.

Sikandar Raza is the all-rounder who does everything for the Royals: middle-order runs, off-spin through the middle overs, and sharp fielding. Raza has won games from nowhere more than once.

Marco Jansen is the tall left-arm all-rounder who bowls with bounce and bats with power down the order. Jansen's overs in the powerplay and at the death are two of the Sunrisers' most valuable assets.

Anrich Nortje is one of the fastest bowlers in the game. Nortje's raw pace hurries even set batters, and his yorkers at the death are hard to get away.

Boland Park

Boland Park opened in 1996, and its first one-day international, between India and Zimbabwe in January 1997, ended in a tie. It hosted three games at the 2003 World Cup, where Wasim Akram took his 500th one-day wicket against the Netherlands. It is the smallest ground in Season 5.

In Season 5, Boland Park hosts six matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus Qualifier 1, the first playoff it has hosted. The published capacity is 10,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
