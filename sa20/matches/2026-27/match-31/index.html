Qualifier 1, Tuesday, 16 February 2027 — Boland Park

Qualifier 1 is the first playoff match of SA20 Season 5. It is played at Boland Park in Paarl on Tuesday 16 February 2027, with the first ball at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). The two sides are the top two seeds from the league stage — 1st against 2nd — and they will only be known once the league stage ends on Sunday 14 February.

The reward for winning Qualifier 1 is the biggest one the playoffs offer: a place in the final on Sunday 21 February, and five days to prepare for it. The loser is not out — they get a second chance in Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February, against the winner of the Eliminator. Boland Park hosts six matches in Season 5, and this is the first playoff it has ever hosted.

How the playoffs work

Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and the top four reach the playoffs. The seeds are decided by the final points table after Match 30, the last league game, on Sunday 14 February.

Qualifier 1, on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, is 1st against 2nd, and the winner goes straight to the final. The Eliminator, on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, is 3rd against 4th — the loser is out, and the winner goes to Qualifier 2. Qualifier 2, on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, is the loser of Qualifier 1 against the winner of the Eliminator, and the winner takes the second final place. The final is on Sunday 21 February at Newlands.

There is one rest day in the playoff week: Thursday 18 February, between the Eliminator and Qualifier 2. The league stage ends on Sunday 14 February, and Monday 15 February is a rest day before the playoffs begin.

What’s at stake

Qualifier 1 is the most valuable match of the playoff week that is not the final itself. The winner goes straight to the final and gets five days of rest before it. The loser has to win again on Friday to get there, with only two days of rest after that.

The two seeds will be decided by the final league table, after Match 30 on Sunday 14 February. Until then, the only honest statement is that the two best sides of the league stage meet here, and one of them will be a win away from the title by Tuesday night.

The road to the final

The road to the final from here is short for the winner and longer for the loser. The winner waits until Sunday 21 February at Newlands. The loser plays Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, against the winner of the Eliminator on Wednesday 17 February. Win that, and they are in the final too — but with two days less rest than the side that beat them here.

Boland Park

Boland Park opened in 1996, and its first one-day international, between India and Zimbabwe in January 1997, ended in a tie. It hosted three games at the 2003 World Cup, where Wasim Akram took his 500th one-day wicket against the Netherlands. It is the smallest ground in Season 5.

In Season 5, Boland Park hosts six matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus Qualifier 1, the first playoff it has hosted. The published capacity is 10,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
