Eliminator, Wednesday, 17 February 2027 — Wanderers

The Eliminator is the second playoff match of SA20 Season 5. It is played at the Wanderers in Johannesburg on Wednesday 17 February 2027, with the first ball at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). The two sides are the 3rd and 4th seeds from the league stage, and they will only be known once the league stage ends on Sunday 14 February.

There is no second chance in the Eliminator. The loser is out of the tournament, and the winner goes on to Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February, against the loser of Qualifier 1. The Wanderers hosts seven matches in Season 5, more than any other ground, and this is the first of its two playoff matches.

How the playoffs work

Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and the top four reach the playoffs. The seeds are decided by the final points table after Match 30, the last league game, on Sunday 14 February.

Qualifier 1, on Tuesday 16 February at Boland Park, is 1st against 2nd, and the winner goes straight to the final. The Eliminator, on Wednesday 17 February at the Wanderers, is 3rd against 4th — the loser is out, and the winner goes to Qualifier 2. Qualifier 2, on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, is the loser of Qualifier 1 against the winner of the Eliminator, and the winner takes the second final place. The final is on Sunday 21 February at Newlands.

There is one rest day in the playoff week: Thursday 18 February, between the Eliminator and Qualifier 2. The league stage ends on Sunday 14 February, and Monday 15 February is a rest day before the playoffs begin.

What’s at stake

The Eliminator is the only playoff match where a loss ends the season on the spot. The 3rd and 4th seeds both finished the league stage in the top four, but only one of them will still be alive on Thursday morning — and that side will have to win twice more to lift the title.

The two seeds will be decided by the final league table, after Match 30 on Sunday 14 February. Until then, the only honest statement is that the two sides who finished third and fourth meet here, and one of them will be going home on Wednesday night.

The road to the final

The road from here is the longest in the tournament. The winner plays Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, against the loser of Qualifier 1 — and if they win that, they play the final on Sunday 21 February at Newlands. Three knockout wins in five days would take the Eliminator winner all the way to the title.

Wanderers Stadium

The Wanderers was built in 1956 to replace the Old Wanderers ground, and its first Test came that December against England. South Africans call it the Bullring, for its round shape and its intimidating atmosphere, and it hosted the 2003 World Cup final.

In Season 5, Wanderers hosts seven matches in Season 5 — five in the league stage, plus the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 — more than any other ground. The published capacity is 34,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

Frequently asked questions

When is the SA20 Eliminator?

Wednesday 17 February 2027, at the Wanderers in Johannesburg. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST, which is 8:30 PM in Pakistan, 9:00 PM in India, 3:30 PM in the UK and 3:30 PM UTC.

Who plays in the Eliminator?

The 3rd and 4th seeds from the league stage. The seeds are decided by the final points table after Match 30, the last league game, on Sunday 14 February. This page will name the two sides once the league stage ends.

What happens to the loser?

The loser is eliminated from the tournament. The winner goes on to Qualifier 2 on Friday 19 February at the Wanderers, against the loser of Qualifier 1, for a place in the final.

Where can I watch the Eliminator?

The match is shown by SuperSport in Sub-Saharan Africa, JioStar in India, Sky Sports in the UK, Fox Sports in Australia, Apex in Pakistan and Willow by Cricbuzz in North America. Broadcasters for the UAE and the Middle East, and for the rest of the world, are still to be confirmed.
