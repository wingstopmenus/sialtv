Sunrisers Eastern Cape vs Paarl Royals, Saturday, 13 February 2027 — St George's Park

The 29th match of SA20 Season 5 is Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Paarl Royals on Saturday, 13 February 2027, at St George's Park in Gqeberha. The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC).

The two sides already met in Match 22 on 6 February at Boland Park, so this is the return meeting with the home side switched — just a week later. For the Sunrisers it is their fifth home league game of five — their last home game of the league stage — and for Paarl it is their fifth away league game of five, their last away game. It is one of two games on Saturday: the other is Match 28, Durban's Super Giants against Joburg Super Kings at Kingsmead, starting at 1:00 PM SAST in a different city, so the two games cannot both be watched in person.

The Sunrisers are led by Tristan Stubbs, and Paarl are led by David Miller. There is no Season 5 form to judge yet — the season starts on 17 January — but by 13 February the table will be nearly final, and this penultimate league game could decide which of these two sides goes into the playoffs.

What’s at stake

Two places in the top four are the prize, and the points system for 2027 is still to be confirmed.

Match 29 is the penultimate league game of the season, and it is the last home game for the Sunrisers and the last away game for Paarl. Six teams play ten league matches each, home and away, and only the top four reach the playoffs — so this is close to a knockout already.

For the Sunrisers, a win in their last home game would finish their home season on a high and could be the result that takes them into the playoffs. For Paarl, winning their last away game on the coast would settle their head-to-head with the Sunrisers for the season and keep their own top-four hopes alive into the final day.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Tristan Stubbs captains Sunrisers Eastern Cape and bats in the middle order, where his clean hitting turns good totals into winning ones. He is also the fielder opposition batters try hardest to avoid.

Quinton de Kock is the wicketkeeper-batter who opens for the Sunrisers and sets the tempo. When de Kock finds the gaps early, the powerplay belongs to his side.

David Miller captains Paarl Royals and finishes innings better than almost anyone. Miller's power against pace and spin makes the last five overs a Royals weapon, and his fielding saves runs all day.

Lhuan-dre Pretorius is the young wicketkeeper-batter who has come through the South African ranks quickly. Pretorius plays with freedom at the top of the order, and bowlers cannot settle against him.

St George's Park

St George's Park is South Africa's oldest Test ground. It staged the country's first Test in March 1889, against England, and it is known for the brass band that plays through the day's play.

In Season 5, St George's Park hosts five league matches in Season 5, including the season opener on 17 January. The published capacity is 19,000, and that figure carries a note: SA20 has not reconfirmed it as the 2027 ticketed capacity, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.
