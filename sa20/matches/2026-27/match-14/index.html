Sunrisers Eastern Cape vs MI Cape Town, Saturday, 30 January 2027 — Gqeberha

The 14th match of the SA20 Season 5 Season 5 season is Sunrisers Eastern Cape against MI Cape Town on Saturday, 30 January 2027, at St George's Park in Gqeberha. It is the 3rd home league game of five for Sunrisers Eastern Cape, and the 2nd away league game of five for MI Cape Town. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 14 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides meet again in Match 19 on 4 February 2027 at Newlands, Cape Town. That return game has the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. It is one of two games on Saturday: the other is Match 15, Joburg Super Kings against Durban's Super Giants at Wanderers, starting at 5:30 PM SAST, in a different city, so the two games cannot both be watched in person.

The first ball is at 1:00 PM SAST (4:00 PM PKT, 11:00 AM UTC). That is 4:30 PM IST and 11:00 AM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Anrich Nortje — Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s express fast bowler. Genuine pace is his weapon, and on a lively surface he can hurry even set batters — the kind of bowler who can take two wickets in an over and turn a match.

Quinton de Kock — Sunrisers Eastern Cape’s wicketkeeper-opener and one of the most destructive batters at the top of a T20 order. When he gets going in the powerplay, the Sunrisers can post — or chase — almost anything.

Rashid Khan — MI Cape Town’s captain and leg-spinner, one of the most feared bowlers in T20 cricket. His four overs through the middle of an innings are where MI Cape Town look to take control, and his hitting down the order gives the side late runs when they need them.

Kagiso Rabada — MI Cape Town’s spearhead with the new ball and at the death. His pace and bounce make him a threat on any surface, and in a tight finish he is the bowler the captain turns to first.

St George's Park, Gqeberha

St George's Park in Gqeberha, Eastern Cape, is the home ground of Sunrisers Eastern Cape, who play their five home league games here. The published capacity is 19,000, and the ground hosts 5 SA20 games in Season 5 — five league matches, including the season opener. Capacities on this site are published figures, and this one has not been reconfirmed by SA20 for 2027, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

For the visiting side, this is an away ground in every sense: a different city, a different crowd, and a surface the home side knows well. There are no Season 5 results at this ground yet to read into, so the first games here will set the pattern — and the side that adapts fastest to the conditions usually takes the points.
