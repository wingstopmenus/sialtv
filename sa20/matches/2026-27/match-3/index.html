Pretoria Capitals vs Durban's Super Giants, Tuesday, 19 January 2027 — Centurion

The 3rd match of the SA20 Season 5 season is Pretoria Capitals against Durban's Super Giants on Tuesday, 19 January 2027, at SuperSport Park in Centurion. It is the 1st home league game of five for Pretoria Capitals, and the 1st away league game of five for Durban's Super Giants. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 3 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides meet again in Match 7 on 23 January 2027 at Kingsmead, Durban. That return game has the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. It is the only SA20 game on Tuesday, so the evening belongs to this fixture alone.

The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). That is 9:00 PM IST and 3:30 PM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Phil Salt — Pretoria Capitals’ attacking opener, who takes the powerplay on from the first ball. His job is to give the Capitals a flying start, and when he does, the middle order can play without pressure.

Keshav Maharaj — Pretoria Capitals’ captain and left-arm spinner. He controls the middle overs with accuracy and drift, and as captain he has the job of turning a talented squad into a side that wins tight games.

Aiden Markram — Durban’s Super Giants’ captain, a top-order batter who also bowls handy off-spin. He leads a side packed with match-winners, and his own calm at the top of the order sets the tempo for the innings.

Jos Buttler — Durban’s Super Giants’ wicketkeeper-opener and one of the great white-ball batters. He can anchor an innings or finish it in a handful of overs, and his record in T20 leagues around the world needs no introduction.

SuperSport Park, Centurion

SuperSport Park in Centurion, Gauteng, is the home ground of Pretoria Capitals, who play their five home league games here. The published capacity is 22,000, and the ground hosts 5 SA20 games in Season 5 — five league matches. Capacities on this site are published figures, and this one has not been reconfirmed by SA20 for 2027, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

For the visiting side, this is an away ground in every sense: a different city, a different crowd, and a surface the home side knows well. There are no Season 5 results at this ground yet to read into, so the first games here will set the pattern — and the side that adapts fastest to the conditions usually takes the points.
