Joburg Super Kings vs Durban's Super Giants, Saturday, 30 January 2027 — Johannesburg

The 15th match of the SA20 Season 5 season is Joburg Super Kings against Durban's Super Giants on Saturday, 30 January 2027, at Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg. It is the 2nd home league game of five for Joburg Super Kings, and the 2nd away league game of five for Durban's Super Giants. Each side plays ten league games in total, so this game is match 15 of 30 in the league stage.

These two sides meet again in Match 28 on 13 February 2027 at Kingsmead, Durban. That return game has the home side switched, so each side gets one home game in this pairing. It is one of two games on Saturday: the other is Match 14, Sunrisers Eastern Cape against MI Cape Town at St George's Park, starting at 1:00 PM SAST, in a different city, so the two games cannot both be watched in person.

The first ball is at 5:30 PM SAST (8:30 PM PKT, 3:30 PM UTC). That is 9:00 PM IST and 3:30 PM UK on the same day — an evening game for readers in Pakistan and India, and an afternoon start for readers in the UK. The season runs from 17 January to 21 February 2027, with six teams playing 34 matches in all, and the top four on the points table reach the playoffs.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Rilee Rossouw — Joburg Super Kings’ left-handed top-order batter, who attacks spin and pace alike. When he gets set, he scores all around the ground, and his powerplay hitting can put a chase — or a total — out of reach early.

Jamie Overton — Joburg Super Kings’ fast-bowling all-rounder. His pace with the ball troubles batters, and his hitting down the order gives the side late runs — a two-in-one player who changes the shape of a team.

Kane Williamson — Durban’s Super Giants’ calm top-order anchor. He reads a chase better than most, finds gaps rather than boundaries, and gives the big hitters around him the platform to play freely.

Sam Curran — Durban’s Super Giants’ left-arm all-rounder, who bowls at the start and the death and bats anywhere in the order. His variety with the ball and his nerve with the bat make him a player for the tight moments.

Wanderers Stadium, Johannesburg

Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg, Gauteng, is the home ground of Joburg Super Kings, who play their five home league games here. The published capacity is 34,000, and the ground hosts 7 SA20 games in Season 5 — five league matches plus the Eliminator on 17 February and Qualifier 2 on 19 February. Capacities on this site are published figures, and this one has not been reconfirmed by SA20 for 2027, so it is shown as published rather than as a current official figure.

For the visiting side, this is an away ground in every sense: a different city, a different crowd, and a surface the home side knows well. There are no Season 5 results at this ground yet to read into, so the first games here will set the pattern — and the side that adapts fastest to the conditions usually takes the points.
