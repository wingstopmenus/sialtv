Live scores, kept honest

SialTV follows every SA20 Season 5 match from South Africa’s six grounds. While a match is in progress, the score on this page is refreshed from verified live sources, and the time of that last refresh is printed next to the score — so a score from ten minutes ago is shown as ten minutes old, never as ball-by-ball. Between matches the page shows the latest result and the next fixture, with the points table alongside.

There is no live feed yet because the season has not started. Once a match ends, the final result — and the points table it changes — is written into the page, so the numbers here and on the SA20 Results page always agree. For the full fixture list, the SA20 Schedule page has every match, date, time and venue.

What comes next

Match 1 is Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals on Sunday 17 January 2027, at 3:30 PM SAST at St George’s Park. Match 2 follows on Monday 18 January — Paarl Royals against Joburg Super Kings at Boland Park, at 5:30 PM SAST — and Match 3 the next evening, Pretoria Capitals against Durban’s Super Giants at SuperSport Park.

Thirty league matches run to Sunday 14 February, then the playoffs: Qualifier 1 on 16 February at Boland Park, the Eliminator on 17 February at the Wanderers, Qualifier 2 on 19 February at the Wanderers, and the final on 21 February at Newlands.
