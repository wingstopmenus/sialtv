What SialTV does

Every league gets its own hub with the full schedule, the current points table, results, teams and the official broadcasters for that season. The leagues covered today are the Nepal Premier League, the ILT20, the Afghanistan Premier League — with more leagues added as their seasons are confirmed.

Scores, fixtures, squads and broadcasters are checked against official and reliable sources before they are published. Anything unconfirmed is marked as such, never guessed.

The coverage is written by Asad Sial (Tournament Data Editor), Saleem Sial (Lead Editor), Arshad Sial (Broadcast Editor), Shakir Ali (Venue Editor) and Waseem Sial (Players and Squads Writer). Their author pages list the leagues they cover and, once SialTV’s news coverage begins, their latest articles.

How the site is run

SialTV is an independent informational website. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, club, board or federation. The site earns nothing from the leagues it covers, and it never links to pirated streams or betting content.

If you spot a mistake, or you represent a league or broadcaster and want a correction, please reach out through the Contact Us page.
