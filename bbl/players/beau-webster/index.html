Beau Webster — Hobart Hurricanes all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Beau Webster is the Australia Test all-rounder in the Hobart Hurricanes squad: a right-handed batter and right-arm medium bowler who made his Test debut against India in Sydney in January 2025, and who is contracted until BBL|17 as part of the champion side’s core.

The Test all-rounder in purple

Webster is a right-handed batter who bowls right-arm medium, and his game is built on doing everything well. He bats in the middle order, bowls his seamers in the middle overs, and fields in the slips and the deep. He is the kind of player who makes a side better in five different ways.

His Test debut is the story. Called into the Australian side for the Sydney Test against India in January 2025, Webster scored runs and took wickets on debut — the kind of performance that turns a domestic all-rounder into an international name overnight.

From Tasmania to the Test side

Webster came through Tasmania’s system, playing for the state in all formats and building a reputation as one of the most reliable all-rounders in domestic cricket. His Sheffield Shield runs and wickets made his Test selection feel like a matter of time, and when the call came, he was ready.

For the Hurricanes, he is the link between the generations. He has played with the club’s champions and its rebuilders, and he is now the senior all-rounder in a side that also includes Mitch Owen and Nikhil Chaudhary.

What Webster brings to BBL|16

Webster is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the middle-order all-rounder. He bats in the middle order, bowls his medium pace in the middle overs, and gives captain Nathan Ellis an extra option with bat and ball.

Watch his batting in the middle overs. Webster’s ability to rotate the strike and pick off the loose ball is what lets the Hurricanes’ power hitters play with freedom around him. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Webster yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first innings of the campaign comes against Brisbane Heat on 17 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Beau’s team, the Hobart Hurricanes, open their campaign in Match 4. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 4 — Hobart Hurricanes vs Brisbane Heat: Thursday 17 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Bellerive Oval, Hobart (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
