Brody Couch — Scorchers bowler, BBL 2026-27

Brody Couch is an American-Australian fast bowler who can bowl at more than 90 miles per hour — and he was part of the Perth Scorchers' BBL|15 title-winning side in his first season with the club. He was born in New York, raised in country Victoria, and moved to Western Australia for more opportunities. The Scorchers re-signed him for BBL|16.

Role: Bowler · Batting: Left-hand bat · Bowling: Right-arm fast · Born: 5 December 1999, New York, USA · Team: Perth Scorchers

The quick who bowls at 90 miles per hour

Couch's job is pace. He is a right-arm fast bowler who regularly bowls at more than 144 kilometres per hour, and his skiddy action makes him hard to play even when the ball is not swinging. He played eight matches in his first season with the Scorchers and was part of the side that beat Sydney Sixers in the BBL|15 final.

He joined the Scorchers as the 18th and final member of their BBL|15 squad, replacing Lance Morris, who was set to undergo back surgery — and he made the most of it. He complemented a pace attack featuring David Payne, Jhye Richardson and Mahli Beardman, and he played in the Scorchers' side that took part in the Global Super League in Guyana, where he took six wickets at 15.5 apiece. The club re-signed him for BBL|16, and his pace is now a permanent part of the attack.

From New York to a Shield hat-trick

Couch was born in New York on 5 December 1999 and raised in country Victoria, where he played for Northcote in Premier Cricket — 31 wickets at 22.84 across all First Grade competitions in 2023-24. He made his List A debut for Victoria in March 2021 and played one Shield match and six One-Day Cup games while contracted by the state.

The move west changed his career. He joined Western Australia ahead of the 2024-25 domestic season, and in his first season he took Western Australia's first-ever Sheffield Shield hat-trick — a moment that made the cricket world learn his name. He took 23 wickets in seven Shield matches at 25.65 that season, and he has also played in Major League Cricket with the San Francisco Unicorns. His BBL career began with the Melbourne Stars, where he played 17 games across four seasons before moving to Perth.

Where he fits in the Scorchers

The Scorchers' pace attack is the best in the league — Richardson, Morris, Paris and Beardman — and Couch is the quick who gives it depth. His pace is different from the others: he is skiddier, quicker through the air, and harder to pick up off the pitch, which makes him a useful change from the taller bowlers around him.

His role this season is likely to be as first reserve quick, coming in when a senior bowler is rested or injured, and his eight matches last season show he can step in without the side missing a beat. At Perth Stadium, where the Scorchers play five home games, his pace and bounce will be hard to play — and in Chennai, where the season starts, his skiddy action could be more effective than the taller bowlers' bounce.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Scorchers topped the table last season with 14 points, and the defence of that position starts in Chennai.
