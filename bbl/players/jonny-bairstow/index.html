BBL|16 · Melbourne Renegades

Jonny Bairstow — Renegades wicketkeeper, BBL 2026-27

Jonny Bairstow is the biggest signing of the Renegades' off-season — an England keeper-batter with 100 Test caps, a Yorkshire career behind him, and a reputation as one of the most destructive white-ball batters of his generation. He joins the Renegades for BBL|16, and the club's top order has never looked more dangerous.

Role: Wicketkeeper · Batting: Right-hand bat · Team: Melbourne Renegades

The headline arrival at the top of the order

Bairstow's job is to do what he has done for England for more than a decade: keep wicket, open or bat in the top three, and score quickly from the first over. The Renegades' top order has lacked experience — Fraser-McGurk and Brown are young hitters, Jewell is a steady opener — and Bairstow brings the one thing none of them has: more than a decade of international cricket.

He is also the keeper, which solves a selection question. With Bairstow behind the stumps, the Renegades do not need to carry a specialist keeper, and the batting gets an extra spot. His one-season deal for BBL|16 is the club's statement of intent: the Renegades missed the finals last season, and they have signed a player who has won World Cups and scored Test hundreds against the best attacks in the world to change that.

Squad note: the Renegades have announced 15 players so far, as of 31 October 2026. The full squad has not been announced — clubs can carry up to 18 players and are still adding names — so this page will be updated as the club confirms more signings.

One hundred Tests, and a 2022 for the ages

Bairstow made his England debut in 2011 and went on to play 100 Test matches, reaching the milestone against India in early 2024. He is a Yorkshireman through and through, and his white-ball record for England — including the 2019 World Cup win — made him one of the most feared openers in the world.

His best year was 2022, when he scored six Test hundreds in a single calendar year, including back-to-back centuries against New Zealand and India in the space of a few weeks — a run of form that few batters in history have matched. He has also played in the Indian Premier League with Punjab Kings, and in the ILT20 with MI Emirates. The Renegades are the latest stop on a career that has taken him to every major T20 league in the world.

Where he fits in the Renegades

The Renegades' batting plan has three destructive openers — Bairstow, Fraser-McGurk and Brown — for two spots, and the club will have to choose. Bairstow's keeping gives him the edge: he takes the gloves, bats in the top three, and lets the side play an extra batter or bowler.

His experience matters most in the big moments. The Chennai opener, against the defending champions, is the kind of game where a young side can freeze — and Bairstow has played in World Cup finals. If the Renegades are to go from missing the finals to challenging for the title, as the team page suggests they can when the top order fires, Bairstow is the player most likely to make the difference.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Renegades missed the finals last season, and the climb back starts in Chennai.

Frequently asked questions

Has Jonny Bairstow played in the BBL before?

No — BBL|16 is his first Big Bash season. He has played T20 leagues around the world, including the IPL with Punjab Kings and the ILT20 with MI Emirates.

How many Test matches has Jonny Bairstow played?

He played 100 Tests for England, reaching the milestone against India in early 2024. He was part of England's 2019 World Cup-winning side.

When does Jonny Bairstow play his first match for Melbourne Renegades?

His first match is Match 1 against Perth Scorchers on Saturday 12 December 2026, starting at 2:40 PM IST at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match ever played outside Australia.
