BBL|16 · Melbourne Renegades

Ollie Peake — Renegades batter, BBL 2026-27

Ollie Peake is one of the brightest young talents in Australian cricket — a left-handed batter from Geelong who won the 2024 Under-19 World Cup with Australia, hit a last-ball six to beat the Perth Scorchers, and then became the youngest specialist batter ever to play ODI cricket for Australia, debuting against Pakistan on 30 May 2026. He is 20, and BBL|16 is his third season as a Renegade.

Role: Batter · Batting: Left-hand bat · Bowling: Right-arm off-break · Born: 11 September 2006, Geelong, Victoria · Team: Melbourne Renegades

The young star the Renegades built around

Peake's job in BBL|16 is to keep doing what he has done since he arrived: bat with a calm head in big moments. He made his BBL debut for the Renegades on 18 January 2025 against Brisbane Heat, and by the end of that season he had already produced the innings people remember — an unbeaten 42 from 30 balls, finished with a last-ball six off Aaron Hardie, to beat the eventual champions Perth Scorchers.

He is contracted through BBL|17 after signing a two-year extension in 2026, and the club sees him as a future leader: he captained Australia at the 2026 Under-19 World Cup and has already played for Australia A and Victoria. His first BBL fifty came in December 2025, 57 from 29 balls against Brisbane Heat in only his second match of the season, and he averaged 33 across six matches in BBL|15. The Renegades do not need him to be a star yet — they need him to keep growing, and the growth so far has been fast.

A World Cup winner, then an ODI debutant

Peake announced himself at the 2024 Under-19 World Cup, joining Australia's squad as an injury replacement and making an immediate impact: 25 not out against England, 49 in the semi-final against Pakistan, and 46 not out in the final against India as Australia won the title. He went on to captain the side at the 2026 tournament, where he scored back-to-back centuries against West Indies and England.

Senior cricket came quickly. He made his Sheffield Shield debut for Victoria in March 2025, top-scoring with 52 in his first innings, and was awarded a rookie state contract in May 2025. Then, on 30 May 2026, he debuted for Australia in an ODI against Pakistan in Rawalpindi — cap number 252 — and at 19 years and 261 days he became the youngest specialist batter to debut for Australia in ODIs, beating Ricky Ponting's record. He is the son of former Victorian cricketer Clinton Peake, and he has already played eight ODIs for Australia.

Where he fits in the Renegades

The Renegades' batting is full of power-hitters, and Peake is the one who can bat through an innings. Fraser-McGurk and Brown hit the ball hard and sometimes fall early; Peake is the left-hander who can rebuild, rotate the strike, and still clear the rope when the moment comes — as that last-ball six against the Scorchers showed.

He is also part of a young Victorian core at the club alongside Harry Dixon, Callum Stow and Michael Archer, and the two left-handers — Peake and Dixon — are the future of the Renegades' batting. His right-arm off-breaks give the captain a part-time option, and his fielding is sharp. If he makes the step from promising to regular match-winner in BBL|16, the Renegades' batting looks very different.
