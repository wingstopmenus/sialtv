Zak Crawley — Scorchers batter, BBL 2026-27

Zak Crawley is an England Test opener who has signed for the Perth Scorchers for a second BBL stint — replacing Finn Allen, who left for the Melbourne Stars. He played six games for the Scorchers in BBL|13, and he is available for the whole of BBL|16, including the finals. He is 28, and he is the champions' new opener.

Role: Batter · Batting: Right-hand bat · Team: Perth Scorchers

The opener signed to replace Finn Allen

Crawley's job is to replace the best batter in last season's title-winning side. Finn Allen was the Scorchers' leading run-scorer in BBL|15, and his move to the Stars left a hole at the top of the order. Crawley, an England Test opener with a 267 to his name, is the player the club has signed to fill it.

He is available for the whole season, including the finals, which matters: the Scorchers' overseas players are only useful if they are there at the end. He is the second English player in the squad, alongside leg-spinner Calvin Harrison, and the club has now filled 15 of its 18 list spots. His first stint with the club, in BBL|13, was six games — enough to know the ground, the conditions and the dressing room, and to make the second stint a smoother one.

Squad note: the Scorchers have announced 15 players so far, as of 31 October 2026. The full squad has not been announced — clubs can carry up to 18 players and are still adding names — so this page will be updated as the club confirms more signings.

Two hundred and sixty-seven, and 189 at Old Trafford

Crawley made his name as an England Test opener, and his best innings came in August 2020, when he scored 267 against Pakistan at the Ageas Bowl — one of the highest scores by an England opener in decades. He followed it with 189 against Australia at Old Trafford in the 2023 Ashes, an innings of rare aggression that set up England's win.

He has played Test cricket for England since 2019, and he is a Kent batter who has scored runs in every format. His BBL career began with the Scorchers in BBL|13, where he played six games, and his return is a sign that the club trusts him to do the job. He is a tall, elegant batter who drives the ball beautifully, and his Test record says he can bat long — a useful skill in T20 cricket, where openers who can bat through the innings are rare.

Where he fits in the Scorchers

The Scorchers' batting in BBL|16 is built around Marsh at the top, and Crawley is the opener who bats with him. His job is to give the side a fast start — and his Test record says he can do it against the best bowlers in the world.

His availability is his biggest asset. The Scorchers' overseas players have commitments elsewhere — Donald has England T20Is before the Big Bash, Harrison has England Lions — but Crawley is available for the whole tournament, including the finals. At Perth Stadium, where the Scorchers play five home games, his driving through the covers will be a weapon, and in Chennai, where the season starts, his ability to play spin will be tested. The title defence starts with him.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Scorchers topped the table last season with 14 points, and the defence of that position starts in Chennai.
