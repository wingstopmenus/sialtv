Alex Carey — Adelaide Strikers wicketkeeper, BBL 2026-27

Alex Carey is the wicketkeeper-batter the Adelaide Strikers build around: a left-handed top-order batter, Australia’s white-ball keeper, and a 2023 World Cup winner. He is locked in with the Strikers for two more seasons, and his keeping and batting at the top of the order set the tone for everything Adelaide do in BBL|16.

The keeper at the heart of the Strikers

Carey is a left-handed batter who keeps wicket, and for Adelaide that combination matters more than any single innings. He bats in the top order, takes the gloves for every ball of the innings, and captains the fielding effort from behind the stumps — the kind of player a side is built around rather than fitted in.

His value is not only the runs. A keeper who bats at the top lets Adelaide play an extra bowler or an extra batter further down, and his reading of the game from behind the stumps shapes bowling changes and field settings. The Strikers have kept him at the centre of their plans for years, and the two-season extension confirmed before BBL|16 says they intend to keep doing so.

A World Cup winner in Strikers colours

Carey has been Australia’s first-choice white-ball wicketkeeper for years, and his career peaks are the ones every Australian cricketer chases: a World Cup winners’ medal in 2023 and a World Test Championship title the same year. He has played Test cricket for Australia too, keeping wicket in the Ashes and on tours of Asia.

At domestic level he is a South Australia and Adelaide Strikers regular, and his BBL career has been built on consistency rather than one famous night — steady runs at the top, sharp keeping, and the calm of a player who has done it on the biggest stages. That experience is exactly what a Strikers side chasing its first final since 2018 needs around its younger batters.

What Carey brings to BBL|16

Carey is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet, and the list will grow — and his job is the same as ever: bat at the top, keep for twenty overs, and hold the side together when a game turns. With Ben Stokes and Mark Chapman arriving as the new overseas names, Carey’s local leadership matters even more.

Watch the first six overs. If Carey gets Adelaide through the powerplay without losing his wicket, the middle order — Matt Short, Chapman and Stokes — can play with freedom. If he falls early, the innings has to rebuild. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Carey yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first runs of the campaign will come against Sydney Thunder on 16 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Alex’s team, the Adelaide Strikers, open their campaign in Match 3. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 3 — Adelaide Strikers vs Sydney Thunder: Wednesday 16 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Manuka Oval, Canberra (Away).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
