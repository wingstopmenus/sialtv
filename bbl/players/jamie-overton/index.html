Jamie Overton — Stars all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Jamie Overton is an England all-rounder who bowls genuinely quick and bats with real power — and the Melbourne Stars signed him from the Adelaide Strikers as one of their headline overseas signings for BBL|16. His twin brother Craig is in the same squad, and the Stars' coach Cameron White has called the two of them, and Finn Allen, some of the most sought-after players in franchise cricket.

Role: All-rounder · Batting: Right-hand bat · Bowling: Right-arm fast · Team: Melbourne Stars

The quick bowler who bats

Overton's job is to bowl fast and hit the ball hard. He is a right-arm fast bowler who regularly bowls in the high 140s, and a lower-order batter who can clear the rope — the kind of player who changes a game in two overs, one with the ball and one with the bat.

He was signed from the Adelaide Strikers, where he had several excellent BBL campaigns, and the Stars see him as the replacement for Tom Curran, who took 16 wickets in 10 games last season before moving to Brisbane Heat. White's summary of him is simple: he bowls quick and bats and fields. That is exactly what a T20 side wants from an overseas all-rounder, and it is why the Stars made him one of their three overseas signings alongside his brother and Finn Allen.

Squad note: the Stars have announced 14 players so far, as of 31 October 2026. The full squad has not been announced — clubs can carry up to 18 players and are still adding names — so this page will be updated as the club confirms more signings.

From the Strikers to the Stars

Overton has played white-ball cricket for England and county cricket with Surrey, where his pace and power-hitting made him one of the most exciting all-rounders in the English game. He has also played in the Indian Premier League with Chennai Super Kings.

His BBL career began with the Adelaide Strikers, where he became a fan favourite for his pace and his hitting — and where he learned Australian conditions, which is why the Stars wanted him rather than a player new to the league. He is the twin brother of Craig Overton, a right-arm fast-medium bowler who has played for Somerset for more than a decade. The two have played together and against each other all their lives; in BBL|16 they play together for the first time in the same BBL side.

Where he fits in the Stars

The Stars' bowling attack has experience — Siddle, Hatcher, Swepson — but it has lacked genuine pace, and Overton is the bowler who fixes that. His high-140s pace is a different challenge from the fast-medium bowling around him, and on the MCG's bouncy pitch it will trouble batters who are used to facing slower bowlers.

His batting matters too. The Stars' lower order has been a weakness, and Overton at seven or eight gives the side a finisher who can turn 160 into 185. Like his brother and Finn Allen, his availability is a question — all three have commitments in the ILT20, which runs to 20 December, and the SA20, which starts on 17 January — but while he is here, he is one of the most dangerous players in the side.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Stars finished fourth last season, their best return in years, and the table is where their title chase will be measured.
