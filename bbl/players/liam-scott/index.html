Liam Scott — Adelaide Strikers all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Liam Scott is the emerging all-rounder in the Adelaide Strikers squad: a right-handed batter and right-arm medium-fast bowler from South Australia who does a bit of everything — and who, alongside Ben Stokes and Jerrssis Wadia, gives Adelaide a genuine all-round depth that few BBL sides can match.

The local all-rounder in a star-studded pack

Scott is a right-handed batter who bowls right-arm medium-fast, and his value is flexibility. He can bat anywhere from five to eight, bowl in the powerplay or the middle overs, and field in the hot spots. That versatility is what keeps a young all-rounder in a side when the stars take the headline roles.

For Adelaide, Scott is the bridge between the local core and the overseas names. Stokes takes the attention, Matt Short takes the captaincy, and Scott takes the overs and the innings that need doing without fuss. Sides that win the BBL are usually the ones whose sixth and seventh players do their jobs quietly.

South Australia’s next all-rounder

Scott came through South Australia’s pathways and played for Australia at the Under-19 World Cup in 2020, the tournament where the next generation of Australian cricketers is usually spotted. Since then he has built his senior career with South Australia, batting in the middle order and bowling his seamers in both red-ball and white-ball cricket.

The BBL is the shop window for a player like Scott. A couple of match-winning contributions — a late cameo, a three-wicket spell — can change how a career is seen, and BBL|16 is his chance to do it in a side that will be watched more closely than any Strikers team in years.

What Scott brings to BBL|16

Scott is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the flexible one: bat where the innings needs him, bowl the overs the frontline quicks cannot, and be ready for the game that turns on a single over. With Stokes in the side, Scott will learn from one of the great all-rounders while competing with him for overs.

Watch his bowling in the middle overs. If he can tie down one end while Lloyd Pope attacks from the other, Adelaide’s attack becomes very hard to score against. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Scott yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game of the campaign comes against Sydney Thunder on 16 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Liam’s team, the Adelaide Strikers, open their campaign in Match 3. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 3 — Adelaide Strikers vs Sydney Thunder: Wednesday 16 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Manuka Oval, Canberra (Away).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
