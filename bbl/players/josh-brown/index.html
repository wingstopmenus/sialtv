Josh Brown — Renegades batter, BBL 2026-27

Josh Brown is a big-striking opening batter who produced one of the greatest innings in BBL history — 140 off 57 balls, with a record 12 sixes, for Brisbane Heat against Adelaide Strikers in the BBL|13 Challenger on 22 January 2024. He won the title with the Heat that season, then moved to the Renegades, where he is contracted through BBL|17.

Role: Batter · Batting: Right-hand bat · Born: 26 December 1993 · Team: Melbourne Renegades

The power-hitter who starts games fast

Brown's job is to make the first six overs count. He is a right-handed opener who hits the ball further than almost anyone in the league, and his role is to give the Renegades a flying start — even if it means he does not always bat long.

That sounds simple, but it changes everything for the side. When Brown gets going, the field spreads, the bowlers change their plans, and the batters behind him — Fraser-McGurk, Bairstow, Jewell — bat with freedom. He is a bat-maker by trade, and he famously uses a bat he made himself, which tells you something about how seriously he takes his craft. The Renegades gave him a contract through BBL|17 because a player who can win a game in six overs is rare, and they do not want to lose him.

The 140 that broke records

Brown made his BBL debut for Brisbane Heat on 15 December 2022 against the Renegades — the club he now plays for. He was given a full contract for 2023-24, and on 22 January 2024 he produced the innings of his life: 140 off 57 balls against Adelaide Strikers in the Challenger, reaching his hundred from 41 balls, with 10 fours and a tournament-record 12 sixes.

It was the third-highest individual score in BBL history, behind only Glenn Maxwell's 154 and Marcus Stoinis's 147 not out, and the highest score ever in a BBL finals match. The Heat won by 54 runs and went on to beat Sydney Sixers in the final, where Brown top-scored again with 53 from 38 balls. He played in two BBL finals for the Heat and won one title — then brought that winning experience to Melbourne.

Where he fits in the Renegades

The Renegades' top order is built around power, and Brown is the purest power-hitter in it. Fraser-McGurk is the young star, Bairstow is the international class, and Brown is the man who has actually done it in a BBL final — twice.

He is also the most likely Renegade to win a game on his own. On a good day at the MCG, where the Renegades play most of their home games, his hitting over the short boundaries is a genuine weapon. The question is consistency: his T20 average is in the mid-20s, which is the price of batting the way he does. The Renegades accept that trade, because on his day he is unplayable.

Frequently asked questions

What is Josh Brown's best BBL innings?

140 off 57 balls for Brisbane Heat against Adelaide Strikers in the BBL|13 Challenger on 22 January 2024 — with a record 12 sixes and a 41-ball hundred, the third-highest BBL score ever.

Did Josh Brown win the BBL with Brisbane Heat?

Yes. He top-scored with 53 in the BBL|13 final as the Heat beat Sydney Sixers to win the title. He played in two BBL finals for the Heat.
