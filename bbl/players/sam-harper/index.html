BBL|16 · Melbourne Stars

Sam Harper — Stars wicketkeeper, BBL 2026-27

Sam Harper is a Victorian wicketkeeper-batter who has been with the Melbourne Stars since BBL|13 — and he is the player who sets the tone at the top of the order. He moved to the Stars from the Renegades, and his fast starts have become a trademark: in BBL|15 he hit a 35-ball fifty against the Renegades, including a 110-metre six off Jason Behrendorff.

Role: Wicketkeeper · Batting: Right-hand bat · Team: Melbourne Stars

The keeper who takes the game on

Harper's job is to keep wicket and score quickly at the top. He is a right-handed batter who attacks from the first over, and his role is to give the Stars a fast start — the platform that lets Finn Allen, Glenn Maxwell and Marcus Stoinis bat with freedom.

His most famous recent innings shows what he does. Against the Renegades in BBL|15, he opened with the Stars' Tom Rogers and the pair put on a partnership at 11.2 runs per over, with Harper hitting a 110-metre six off Behrendorff and Rogers striking a 99-metre six of his own. Harper's fifty came from 35 balls, and the Stars ended a three-game losing streak. That is his game: fast, fearless, and willing to take on the best bowlers in the league.

From the Renegades to the Stars

Harper came through Victoria's system as a wicketkeeper-batter, and he played for the Melbourne Renegades before moving to the Stars. The Stars signed him in August 2025 on a one-year deal, then extended him through BBL|17 — a sign that the club sees him as its first-choice keeper.

He is also a leader in the Victorian system. The Stars' high performance manager Clint McKay said when he re-signed that Harper's experience and level head off the field had made him a key leader in the Victorian system, and that securing him was a credit to the programme the club is building in the state. He is a homegrown Victorian, and the Stars' squad is built around homegrown Victorians — Kellaway, Anlezark, Harper — as much as its overseas stars.

Where he fits in the Stars

The Stars' batting in BBL|16 is built around Allen at the top, and Harper is the keeper-batter who bats with him or just behind him. His keeping gives the side a safe pair of hands behind the stumps, and his batting gives it a fast start — two jobs that are hard to find in one player.

His experience matters too. He has played in the BBL for years, knows the MCG well, and has batted in derby games against the Renegades — the side he used to play for. The Melbourne derby on Saturday 16 January, at the MCG, is the game he will want most: against his old club, in front of a big crowd, with the Stars' season on the line.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Stars finished fourth last season, their best return in years, and the table is where their title chase will be measured.
