Tom Straker — Adelaide Strikers bowler, BBL 2026-27

Tom Straker is the young fast bowler in the Adelaide Strikers squad: a right-arm quick who took the winning wicket in Australia’s 2024 Under-19 World Cup final triumph and produced one of the great youth bowling spells — 6 for 24 against Pakistan in the semi-final — on the way there.

The World Cup winner learning his trade

Straker is a right-arm fast-medium bowler who bats right-handed, and his game is built on pace, bounce and a strong, repeatable action. He bowls in the 130s and 140s, hits the pitch hard, and is at his best when the ball is new and the batters are trying to settle.

He is 21, and BBL|16 is the next step in a fast-moving career. The Under-19 World Cup made his name; the move from New South Wales to Queensland, a rookie contract, then a full state contract in May 2025, built the professional base. Now the BBL is where he has to show he can do it against senior international batters.

Six wickets and a World Cup

Straker was born in Caringbah, New South Wales, on 19 March 2005, and he played for Australia’s Under-19 side from January 2023. At the 2024 Under-19 World Cup in South Africa he took 12 wickets in five games, including the 6 for 24 against Pakistan in the semi-final — the best bowling figures ever in an Under-19 World Cup semi-final — and he took the winning wicket in the final against India.

Queensland saw enough to sign him, first on a rookie deal and then a full contract, and he made his List A debut for the Bulls in September 2024 and his first-class debut the following month. His BBL chance with the Strikers is the latest step: a young quick with a big-match record, learning his trade in the toughest domestic T20 league in the country.

What Straker brings to BBL|16

Straker is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the young quick in a pace attack led by Henry Thornton and Ben Stokes. He will not be asked to carry the attack; he will be asked to bowl hard, learn fast, and take his chances when they come.

Watch his first spell of the season. Young quicks are judged quickly in the BBL, and a good first over — pace, bounce, a wicket — can earn a bowler a run of games. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Straker yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game of the campaign comes against Sydney Thunder on 16 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Tom’s team, the Adelaide Strikers, open their campaign in Match 3. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 3 — Adelaide Strikers vs Sydney Thunder: Wednesday 16 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Manuka Oval, Canberra (Away).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
