BBL|16 · Sydney Thunder

Nic Maddinson — Thunder batter, BBL|16

Nic Maddinson is the experienced left-handed batter the Sydney Thunder have kept for BBL|16 — a player who has captained in the Big Bash, played Test cricket for Australia, and scored runs for Victoria and New South Wales over more than a decade. He is back as one of the senior heads in a rebuilt side.

A decade of Big Bash cricket

Maddinson has been around the Big Bash since its early years, and that experience is his value. He is a left-handed top-order batter who has opened, batted at three, and played every role a batting line-up can ask for — and he has done it for two clubs, the Renegades and the Thunder, in very different eras.

His game is built on clean striking through the off side and a willingness to take the aerial route. Left-handers who can score quickly at the top are prized in T20 cricket because they change the angles for every bowler — and Maddinson has been doing it for longer than most of the bowlers he faces have been playing.

He has also captained the Melbourne Renegades, which tells you how his teams view him: a thinker about the game, a player others listen to, and a senior head in a dressing room full of young talent.

Victoria, New South Wales, and Australia

Maddinson's domestic career has taken him from Victoria to New South Wales, and his international career includes Test cricket for Australia. He is one of those players whose first-class record — big runs, big hundreds, over many seasons — has always been stronger than his white-ball reputation, which is exactly why the Thunder value him: he is a batter who knows how to build an innings, not just how to hit one.

His Thunder career has been a second act. After his Renegades years, he joined the Thunder and became part of the side that reached the BBL|14 final — losing to Hobart Hurricanes — and then missed the finals last season. The new season, under Andrew Flintoff, is a fresh start, and Maddinson is one of the players the new coach will lean on.

For BBL|16, he is contracted as one of the senior batters in a squad that mixes Warner's experience, Konstas's youth, and Billings's class.

A new Thunder, under Flintoff

The Thunder missed the finals last season, a year after reaching the BBL|14 final. The new season is a fresh start under Flintoff, the former England captain who has signed a two-year deal, and the squad has been rebuilt around pace: Trent Boult and Lockie Ferguson join Daniel Sams and Tanveer Sangha.

Maddinson's role in that rebuild is to give the batting its experience. The Thunder's bowling looks formidable on paper; the question is whether the batting can post totals worth defending. A left-hander who has batted in every situation the Big Bash can produce is the kind of player who answers that question.

The season opens against Adelaide Strikers at Manuka Oval on 16 December.
