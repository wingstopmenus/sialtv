Jhye Richardson — Scorchers bowler, BBL 2026-27

Jhye Richardson is an Australian fast bowler who has taken five wickets in an ODI against India — and he is the spearhead of the Perth Scorchers' pace attack. He is a right-arm fast bowler who bowls with real pace and swing, and he is contracted through BBL|16 as one of the core players the title defence is built around.

The spearhead of the champions' attack

Richardson's job is to take the new ball and take wickets. He is the fastest bowler in the Scorchers' squad, and his right-arm pace — with swing early and bounce later — makes him the bowler the opposition plans for first. He is the spearhead of an attack that also includes Lance Morris, Joel Paris and Mahli Beardman.

His international record says he can do it at the highest level. He took 5 for 42 against India in an ODI in January 2019, and he has played Test and white-ball cricket for Australia. Injuries have interrupted his career — he has had shoulder and hamstring problems that have kept him out for long spells — but when he is fit, he is one of the best fast bowlers in the country. The Scorchers have managed him carefully, and his BBL|15 season was part of the title win.

Five for 42, and a career interrupted

Richardson made his name as a young fast bowler from Western Australia, and his international breakthrough came in January 2019, when he took 5 for 42 against India in the first ODI of the series at the SCG. It was the performance that made the cricket world learn his name, and it earned him a place in Australia's white-ball sides.

Injuries have been the story since. He has had shoulder surgery and hamstring problems, and he has missed long spells of cricket — including Test series he would have played in. But he has always come back, and his BBL form for the Scorchers has been strong whenever he has been fit. He is a player who knows what it is to fight back from injury, and that resilience is part of what makes him a leader in the Scorchers' dressing room.

Where he fits in the Scorchers

The Scorchers' pace attack is the best in the league, and Richardson is its leader. Morris is the wild thing, Paris is the left-armer, Beardman is the young quick — and Richardson is the spearhead, the bowler who takes the new ball and sets the tone.

His role in BBL|16 is to stay fit and take wickets. If he plays the full season, the Scorchers' attack is the best in the league; if he is injured, the side has to rely on its depth — Couch, Beardman and the spinners. The Chennai opener, on a slower surface, will test his variations, but his pace and swing will be hard to play even on a slow pitch. The title defence depends on him.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Scorchers topped the table last season with 14 points, and the defence of that position starts in Chennai.
