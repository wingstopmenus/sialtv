Ben McDermott — Hobart Hurricanes batter, BBL 2026-27

Ben McDermott is the Hobart Hurricanes batter who scored back-to-back BBL centuries in December 2021: a right-handed top-order player and Australia T20 international who is contracted until BBL|17 as part of the champion side’s batting core.

The man with back-to-back BBL hundreds

McDermott is a right-handed batter, and his game is built on clean striking and timing. He hits the ball hard through the off side, clears the ropes straight down the ground, and is at his best when the innings needs accelerating. His back-to-back centuries in December 2021 — two hundreds in two games — remain one of the great BBL batting feats.

Those hundreds are the reason his name still carries weight. A batter who has scored two BBL centuries in a week is a batter the opposition plans for, and McDermott has lived with that attention ever since.

From the Hurricanes to Australia and back

McDermott came through Tasmania’s system and the Hurricanes, and his BBL form took him into Australia’s T20 side. He has played T20 internationals for Australia, and his domestic career has been built on his ability to score quickly at the top of the order.

He is the son of former Australian fast bowler Craig McDermott, and his career has been his own: a BBL opener who became an international, then came back to Hobart as one of the senior batters in a title-winning side.

What McDermott brings to BBL|16

McDermott is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the experienced top-order batter. With Shai Hope, Tim David and Mitch Owen around him, McDermott is the player who can bat through an innings and let the power hitters play around him.

Watch his starts. McDermott’s best innings come when he gets through the first ten balls and then opens up; the danger for him is the early wicket that stops an innings before it begins. There are no BBL|16 numbers for McDermott yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first innings of the campaign comes against Brisbane Heat on 17 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Ben’s team, the Hobart Hurricanes, open their campaign in Match 4. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 4 — Hobart Hurricanes vs Brisbane Heat: Thursday 17 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Bellerive Oval, Hobart (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
