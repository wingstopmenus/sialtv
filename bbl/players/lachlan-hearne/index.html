Lachlan Hearne — Brisbane Heat batter, BBL 2026-27

Lachlan Hearne is the left-handed top-order batter the Brisbane Heat signed after his breakthrough season with Queensland: a former New South Wales junior who moved north, scored a maiden List A century, and was named Queensland’s One-Day Player of the Year for 2024-25.

The left-hander who rebuilt his career in Queensland

Hearne is a left-handed batter who bowls occasional right-arm off-break, and his game is built on patience and timing. He was born in Camperdown, New South Wales, on 19 December 2000, and he made his first-class debut for New South Wales as a 20-year-old, scoring a fifty against a strong Queensland attack.

But his New South Wales career stalled, and he was delisted after the 2022-23 summer. The move to Queensland changed everything: strong Premier Cricket for Toombul — including a double century — earned him a Bulls contract for 2024-25, and he has not looked back since.

A century, an award and a Heat deal

Hearne’s 2024-25 season was the breakthrough. He scored his maiden List A century — 107 against New South Wales — finished second in Queensland’s 50-over runs tally, and was named the state’s One-Day Player of the Year. He also made his maiden Sheffield Shield century at Adelaide Oval.

The Heat signed him on a two-year deal, and he made his BBL debut for Brisbane against Adelaide Strikers on 31 December 2025. He has also spent time as a replacement player with the Sydney Sixers, so BBL|16 is not his first taste of the league — but it is his first full season as a contracted Heat player.

What Hearne brings to BBL|16

Hearne is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the left-handed top-order option in a batting group that leans right-handed. With Usman Khawaja and Matthew Renshaw providing experience, Hearne is the younger left-hander who can bat through an innings.

Watch his starts against the new ball. Hearne’s game is built on batting time, and if he gets through the powerplay, the Heat’s middle order can play around him. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Hearne yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first innings of the campaign comes against Sydney Sixers at the Gabba on 15 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Lachlan’s team, the Brisbane Heat, open their campaign in Match 2. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 2 — Brisbane Heat vs Sydney Sixers: Tuesday 15 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), The Gabba, Brisbane (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
