Mackenzie Harvey — Adelaide Strikers batter, BBL 2026-27

Mackenzie Harvey is the left-handed batter the Adelaide Strikers re-signed for BBL|16 as part of the deadline-day trio with Henry Thornton and Jerrssis Wadia. He is a Victorian by origin, the nephew of former Australian all-rounder Ian Harvey, and a player who has already worn Melbourne Renegades colours in the BBL.

A left-hander with a point to prove

Harvey is a left-handed batter, and his game is built on clean striking through the off side and the confidence to take on the new ball. Left-handers change the angles for bowlers and captains alike, and in a top order with right-handers around him, Harvey gives Adelaide a different look at the top.

The re-signing tells its own story. Adelaide did not have to keep Harvey — they chose to, on the same day they kept Thornton and Wadia — which says the club sees him as part of the batting group it wants to build with, not a stopgap. A one-year deal is a challenge as much as a reward: score runs, and the next one follows.

From Renegades to Strikers

Harvey began his BBL career with the Melbourne Renegades, the club where he first showed he could handle the step up to franchise cricket. He later moved to South Australia’s system and joined the Strikers, where he has had to fight for a regular place in a batting order that has rarely had a spare seat.

His family name carries weight in Australian cricket — his uncle Ian Harvey played Test and one-day cricket for Australia and was one of the great white-ball all-rounders of his era — but Mackenzie Harvey’s career has been his own: Victorian junior cricket, a BBL debut young, and a steady climb rather than a sudden arrival.

What Harvey brings to BBL|16

Harvey is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is in the top order, where his left-handed batting gives the Strikers balance against the new ball. With Mark Chapman also left-handed in the middle order, Adelaide have two left-handers to break up the right-handers around them.

Watch his starts. Harvey’s best T20 innings come when he gets through the first ten balls and then opens up; the danger for him is the early wicket that stops an innings before it begins. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Harvey yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first innings of the campaign comes against Sydney Thunder on 16 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Mackenzie’s team, the Adelaide Strikers, open their campaign in Match 3. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 3 — Adelaide Strikers vs Sydney Thunder: Wednesday 16 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Manuka Oval, Canberra (Away).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
