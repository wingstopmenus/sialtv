Glenn Maxwell — Stars all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Glenn Maxwell is one of the most famous cricketers in the world — an Australia all-rounder who scored 201 not out against Afghanistan at the 2023 World Cup with a broken body and a lost cause, and who holds the BBL's highest individual score with 154 not out against Hobart Hurricanes in January 2022. For the Stars, he is the player the whole side is built around.

Role: All-rounder · Batting: Right-hand bat · Bowling: Right-arm off-break · Team: Melbourne Stars

The Big Show, still the main act

Maxwell's job in BBL|16 is the same as it has been for years: win games from nowhere. He bats in the middle order, bowls off-spin, fields like no one else in the league, and changes a match in ten balls. He is contracted through BBL|17, and he is the reason the Stars are box office even in a season when they have never won the title.

His BBL record is extraordinary. His 154 not out against Hobart Hurricanes in January 2022 is the highest individual score in BBL history — ahead of Marcus Stoinis's 147 not out and Josh Brown's 140 — and it came from 64 balls, with a strike rate that made the rest of the league look slow. He is also the Stars' most capped player and their emotional leader, the one the crowd comes to see. When he is fit and firing, the Stars can beat anyone; when he is not, the side looks ordinary.

Two hundred and one not out

Maxwell's most famous innings did not come in the BBL. At the 2023 World Cup, against Afghanistan in Mumbai, Australia were 91 for 7 chasing 292 when he walked in — and he made 201 not out from 128 balls, with cramp so bad he could barely move, to win the game. It is widely called the greatest one-day innings ever played.

He has been an Australia white-ball regular for more than a decade, played in the Indian Premier League with Royal Challengers Bengaluru, and won the 2023 World Cup with Australia. His BBL career has been with the Stars throughout, and his 154 not out in 2022 remains the benchmark for every batter in the league. He is 37 now, and the question is not whether he can still do it — his World Cup innings says he can — but how often.

Where he fits in the Stars

The Stars' batting in BBL|16 is built around three names: Finn Allen at the top, Maxwell in the middle, and captain Marcus Stoinis alongside him. Allen's arrival takes pressure off Maxwell, who has too often had to rescue the side on his own — and with Allen and Stoinis around him, he can play with more freedom.

His off-spin is also part of the plan. The Stars' spin group is Todd Murphy and Mitch Swepson, and Maxwell's four overs of off-spin give the captain a third option — and one who can bowl in the powerplay if the match-up is right. His fielding, especially in the ring, saves runs that no other Stars player saves. The Stars have never won the title, and Maxwell has been there for most of the wait. BBL|16 is another chance to end it.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Stars finished fourth last season, their best return in years, and the table is where their title chase will be measured.
