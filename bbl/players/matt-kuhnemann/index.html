Matt Kuhnemann — Brisbane Heat bowler, BBL 2026-27

Matt Kuhnemann is the left-arm spinner the Brisbane Heat build their middle overs around: a slow left-arm orthodox bowler and Australia Test spinner who took a five-wicket haul on his first tour of India. In a pace-heavy attack, he is the bowler who changes the game’s rhythm entirely.

The spinner in a pace attack

Kuhnemann is a slow left-arm orthodox bowler who bats right-handed, and his job is control. He bowls flat and fast when the batters are attacking, flights the ball when they are defending, and builds pressure over by over until a mistake comes. In T20, that control is as valuable as any wicket.

For Brisbane, he is the balance. The Heat’s attack is built on pace — Bartlett, Johnson, Neser, Vidler — and Kuhnemann is the spinner who holds the middle overs while the quicks rest. Without him, the attack would be all pace and no change of pace; with him, captain and coach can attack from both ends.

Five wickets in Indore

Kuhnemann’s career changed on Australia’s 2023 tour of India. Called into the Test side, he took a five-wicket haul in the Indore Test — 5 for 16 — on a turning pitch, bowling Australia to a win that few had predicted. It was the performance that made him a Test spinner, not just a domestic one.

Since then he has played Test cricket for Australia and remained a Queensland and Heat regular. His BBL role has always been the same: bowl four overs, go for as few as possible, and take the wickets that come from pressure. It is unglamorous work, and it wins games.

What Kuhnemann brings to BBL|16

Kuhnemann is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the middle-overs spinner. With the Heat’s quicks bowling at both ends of the innings, Kuhnemann’s four overs in the middle are where Brisbane will try to squeeze the scoring and force mistakes.

Watch his match-up against right-handed batters. Left-arm spin turning away from the right-hander is one of T20’s classic contests, and Kuhnemann’s accuracy makes it a hard one to win. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Kuhnemann yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first spell of the campaign comes against Sydney Sixers at the Gabba on 15 December.
