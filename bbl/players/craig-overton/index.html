Craig Overton — Stars all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Craig Overton is an England all-rounder who has signed for the Melbourne Stars alongside his twin brother Jamie — the two Overtons are the Stars' English connection for BBL|16. Craig is a right-arm fast-medium bowler and a useful lower-order batter who has played Test and one-day cricket for England with Somerset.

Role: All-rounder · Batting: Right-hand bat · Bowling: Right-arm fast-medium · Team: Melbourne Stars

One half of the Overton twins

Craig Overton's job is to give the Stars a seam-bowling all-rounder who can bat. He bowls right-arm fast-medium, hits the pitch hard, and bats well enough to be a genuine lower-order option — the kind of player who lets a side play an extra bowler without weakening the batting.

He joins the Stars as an overseas signing, and his availability is one of the questions around the side: the Stars' overseas players all have commitments elsewhere, with the ILT20 running to 20 December and the SA20 starting on 17 January. The club's coach Cameron White has called the Overtons, and Finn Allen, some of the most sought-after players in franchise cricket — and Craig's job is to make sure the Stars get value from him while he is here. His twin Jamie, who bowls quicker and bats higher, was signed from the Adelaide Strikers; Craig comes from Somerset, where he has been a mainstay for years.

Squad note: the Stars have announced 14 players so far, as of 31 October 2026. The full squad has not been announced — clubs can carry up to 18 players and are still adding names — so this page will be updated as the club confirms more signings.

Somerset, England, and a twin

Overton has played his whole domestic career with Somerset, where he has been a first-choice seamer and a lower-order batter for more than a decade. He has played Test cricket for England and one-day internationals, and his record in county cricket — wickets with the new ball and runs down the order — made him a natural overseas signing.

He is the twin brother of Jamie Overton, and the two have played together and against each other throughout their careers. Jamie is the quicker bowler and the more explosive batter; Craig is the steadier one, the bowler you give the ball to when you need four tight overs and a wicket. The Stars have signed both, which gives them two seam-bowling all-rounders from the same family — and a story no other BBL side can match.

Where he fits in the Stars

The Stars' bowling attack is built around experience — Siddle, Hatcher, Swepson and Murphy — and Craig Overton adds a seam-bowling all-rounder who can bat at seven or eight. That depth matters in a side whose batting is top-heavy: if the top order fails, the lower order has to be able to rebuild, and Overton is a batter who can do that.

His bowling also suits the MCG, where the Stars play most of their home games. The big outfield rewards bowlers who hit the pitch hard and make batters hit to the long boundaries, and Overton's fast-medium pace and bounce are built for that. If he and his brother both play, the Stars have two English all-rounders in the same side — a combination that could be the difference in a tight season.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Stars finished fourth last season, their best return in years, and the table is where their title chase will be measured.
