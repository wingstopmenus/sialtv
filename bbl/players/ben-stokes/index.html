Ben Stokes — Adelaide Strikers all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Ben Stokes is the headline signing of BBL|16: England’s Test captain, a two-time World Cup winner, and the biggest overseas name the Adelaide Strikers have ever landed. Signed after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market, he returns to the competition eleven years after first playing in it for the Melbourne Renegades.

The biggest signing of the summer

Stokes is a left-handed batter and right-arm fast-medium bowler, and he changes a side in both disciplines. He bats in the middle order, bowls at the death and with the new ball, and fields like a captain — the complete all-rounder, and a player whose presence alone lifts the players around him.

His arrival is the statement of intent Adelaide needed. The Strikers missed the finals last season and have not reached a final since winning the title in 2017-18. Signing a player of Stokes’ standing says the club is not waiting for a rebuild — it is trying to win now, and it has paired him with New Zealand batter Mark Chapman as the second big arrival.

Two World Cups and a Headingley for the ages

Stokes’ career is defined by matches nobody who watched them will forget. He was player of the match in the 2019 World Cup final at Lord’s, he won the 2022 T20 World Cup final with an unbeaten fifty, and his unbeaten 135 at Headingley in the 2019 Ashes is one of the great Test innings ever played.

He has since become England’s Test captain, leading the side’s attacking transformation, and he retired from white-ball international cricket before signing for Adelaide. That makes BBL|16 a new chapter: a 35-year-old great, free of international white-ball duty, playing franchise T20 on his own terms — and doing it alongside former Ashes opponent Tim Paine, now his coach at the Strikers.

What Stokes brings to BBL|16

Stokes is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is clear: bat in the middle order, bowl his full four overs most nights, and lead the dressing room by example. Alongside captain Matt Short and the emerging Liam Scott, he gives Adelaide three genuine all-round options.

The question is not talent but availability and workload. Stokes is 35 and his body has carried a heavy load for a decade, so how Adelaide manage his bowling across ten league games will decide how much of him the crowds see. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Stokes yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his Strikers debut comes against Sydney Thunder on 16 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Ben’s team, the Adelaide Strikers, open their campaign in Match 3. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 3 — Adelaide Strikers vs Sydney Thunder: Wednesday 16 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Manuka Oval, Canberra (Away).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
