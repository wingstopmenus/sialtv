Ben Manenti — Renegades all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Italy all-rounder Ben Manenti arrives at the Melbourne Renegades on the back of the best year of his career — his country's star at their first-ever Men's T20 World Cup, then a maiden first-class century as stand-in captain of South Australia. The Renegades signed him for two years on 9 October 2026, and he is their third BBL club after the Sydney Sixers and the Adelaide Strikers.

Role: All-rounder · Batting: Right-hand bat · Bowling: Right-arm off-break · Born: 23 March 1997 · Team: Melbourne Renegades

The off-spinner chasing the No. 1 spin job

Manenti's chance at the Renegades is real. Adam Zampa left for Hobart Hurricanes in the off-season, so the club's main spin-bowling role is open — and Manenti, a right-arm off-break bowler who bats hard in the middle order, has been signed to push for it.

He joins a spin group that already includes left-arm wrist-spinner Callum Stow, leg-spinner Michael Archer and New Zealand off-spinning all-rounder Michael Bracewell. What sets Manenti apart is his batting: he hits the ball cleanly enough to bat in the top six of a T20 side, which gives Luke Ronchi the option of playing him as a genuine all-rounder rather than a bowler who bats a bit. His two-year deal, rather than a one-season stopgap, says the club sees him as part of the rebuild, not just cover.

Squad note: the Renegades have announced 15 players so far, as of 31 October 2026. The full squad has not been announced — clubs can carry up to 18 players and are still adding names — so this page will be updated as the club confirms more signings.

From Italy's World Cup to a Shield century

Manenti's breakout year started in February 2026, at Italy's maiden Men's T20 World Cup appearance. He finished as Italy's highest run-scorer with 138 runs in three innings and their second-highest wicket-taker with five scalps — the standout performer of a historic campaign.

He kept the form going at home. Stepping up as stand-in captain for South Australia in their Sheffield Shield opener against Victoria, he struck a commanding 128 from 112 balls and helped his side past 500 in the first innings — his maiden first-class century, and it came the same day the Renegades announced his signing. Before that he had only played bit-part BBL roles, mostly as spin cover for the likes of Steve O'Keefe at the Sixers and Cameron Boyce at the Strikers. This is the first time a BBL club has backed him as a first-choice player.

Where he fits in the Renegades

The Renegades' batting has genuine power at the top — Jake Fraser-McGurk, Josh Brown and Jonny Bairstow — but the middle order needed a player who could both rebuild an innings and clear the rope late. Manenti is that player, and his off-spin gives the attack a different angle on Melbourne pitches that do not always suit wrist-spin.

He also strengthens a side that has lost plenty of key bowlers this off-season. With Jason Behrendorff ruled out for the season with a shoulder injury, every bowling option in the squad carries more weight, and a spinner who can be trusted for four overs and then bat at six or seven is worth more than his two disciplines suggest separately. If he holds the No. 1 spin role, he plays every game.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Renegades missed the finals last season, and the climb back starts in Chennai.

Frequently asked questions

Has Ben Manenti played in the BBL before?

Yes, but only in bit-part roles. He had brief spells with the Sydney Sixers and the Adelaide Strikers, mostly as spin cover. The Renegades are his third BBL club and the first to give him a two-year deal as a first-choice player.

What did Ben Manenti do at the T20 World Cup?

He was Italy's star at their maiden Men's T20 World Cup in February 2026 — their highest run-scorer with 138 runs in three innings and their second-highest wicket-taker with five scalps.

When does Ben Manenti play his first match for Melbourne Renegades?

His first match is Match 1 against Perth Scorchers on Saturday 12 December 2026, starting at 2:40 PM IST at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match ever played outside Australia.
