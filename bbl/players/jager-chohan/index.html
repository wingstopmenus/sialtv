BBL|16 · Sydney Sixers

Jager Chohan — Sixers leg-spinner, BBL|16

Jager Chohan — full name Jafer Ali Chohan — is the English leg-spinner the Sydney Sixers have brought back for BBL|16. Born in Camden on 11 July 2002, he bowls right-arm leg-break, models his craft on Shahid Afridi, and earned his return with a breakout English summer that included 17 wickets in ten T20 Blast games.

Back for a third Sixers summer

Chohan was first drafted by the Sixers for BBL|14 in September 2024, made his Big Bash debut on 15 January 2025 against Adelaide Strikers, and stayed for BBL|15. The Sixers re-signed him in October 2026 for BBL|16, with veteran spinner Steve O'Keefe retired and the side needing a wrist-spinner.

His role on the team page is listed as to be confirmed, but his job is clear enough: leg-spin through the middle overs, with the googly as his main weapon. Australian pitches do not always favour spin, which is exactly why a wrist-spinner who turns it both ways matters — he is the point of difference in an attack built on pace.

The signing also keeps the Sixers' English connection alive. Chohan plays county cricket for Yorkshire, and his Big Bash deal came on the back of his Blast form rather than reputation: the Sixers saw a bowler who takes wickets in clusters and backed him twice.

The Blast summer that made him

Chohan's breakthrough came in the 2024 Vitality Blast, his first full T20 season with Yorkshire. He took 17 wickets in ten matches, including 5 for 14 against Durham — bowled on his 22nd birthday — and 4 for 30 against Worcestershire in the next game.

Those two spells told the story: a leg-spinner who is not afraid to flight the ball, who bowls a googly batters read late, and who attacks rather than contains. Yorkshire had signed him on a rookie contract in early 2023 after he impressed bowling to Joe Root in England nets; by the summer of 2024 he was one of the Blast's most talked-about bowlers.

He followed it with 13 Blast wickets in 2025, and his T20 record now stands at more than fifty wickets with a best of 5 for 14. The numbers say wicket-taker; the style says entertainer.

Where he fits in the Sixers' attack

The Sixers' spin stocks changed shape over the winter. Todd Murphy moved on, Corey Rocchiccioli arrived from Western Australia as the off-spin option, and Chohan is the wrist-spin half of the pair — the one who bowls to right-handers looking to hit through the line.

He will share the middle overs with Joel Davies' left-arm spin and the all-rounders, while Sean Abbott, Ben Dwarshuis and Fergus O'Neill handle the pace. It is a balanced attack on paper: pace at both ends, off-spin one way, leg-spin the other.

His batting is a bonus, not a plan — a hard-hitting lower-order right-hander who has cleared the ropes in county cricket. The Sixers will not need it often; they need his four overs.

Frequently asked questions

What is Jager Chohan's full name?

What kind of bowler is Jager Chohan?

A right-arm leg-break bowler who also bowls a googly, and a right-handed lower-order batter. He models his leg-spin on Shahid Afridi.
