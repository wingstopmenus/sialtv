Bevon Jacobs — Brisbane Heat batter, BBL 2026-27

Bevon Jacobs is the New Zealand batter who arrives at the Brisbane Heat as the international replacement player for Tom Curran, who misses the first three matches of BBL|16 due to family commitments. Jacobs is a big-hitting right-hander, and his job is simple: score runs while Curran is away — and make it hard for the Heat to leave him out.

The replacement with a chance to stay

Jacobs is a right-handed batter, and his game is built on power. He is described by the Heat as a big hitter, the kind of batter who can change a game in ten balls, and that is exactly what a replacement player needs to do: arrive, hit the ground running, and force a selection question.

Replacement stints in the BBL have launched careers before. A player who arrives for three games and scores runs in two of them does not go home quietly — he becomes part of the conversation for the rest of the season. Jacobs will know that, and he will bat like it.

A New Zealand hitter’s first BBL campaign

Jacobs has come through New Zealand’s domestic system, where his ball-striking has marked him out as a T20 prospect. He is part of the group of young New Zealand batters — alongside Tim Robinson, who joins the Heat on a full deal — who are making their way in franchise cricket.

Little is published about his early career beyond the domestic record, and the BBL is his biggest stage so far. That is the point of a replacement signing: the Heat get a hitter for the games Curran misses, and Jacobs gets a shop window in one of the world’s most-watched T20 leagues.

What Jacobs brings to BBL|16

Jacobs is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is clear: bat in the middle order for the first three matches and score quickly. With Curran due back after that, Jacobs’ window is short, and every innings counts double.

Watch his first innings. A big score in his first game would change the whole conversation around the Heat’s overseas spots. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Jacobs yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game for the Heat comes against Sydney Sixers at the Gabba on 15 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Bevon’s team, the Brisbane Heat, open their campaign in Match 2. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 2 — Brisbane Heat vs Sydney Sixers: Tuesday 15 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), The Gabba, Brisbane (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
