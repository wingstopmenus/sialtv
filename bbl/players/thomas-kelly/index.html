Thomas Kelly — Adelaide Strikers batter, BBL 2026-27

Thomas Kelly is the South Australian batter in the Adelaide Strikers top order: a right-handed player who has grown up in the state’s system and now holds a regular place in a batting group that includes Alex Carey, Matt Short, Mark Chapman and Ben Stokes. In BBL|16, his job is to make that group deeper, not just bigger.

The local batter in a star-studded order

Kelly is a right-handed batter, and his game is built on timing and placement through the off side. He is not the power hitter of the group — that is Stokes, or Short on his day — but he is the batter who can hold an end, rotate the strike, and let the big names play around him.

That role is easy to undervalue and hard to replace. Every successful T20 side has a batter who does the quiet work: the one who is still there at the end of the powerplay, the one who turns ones into twos, the one who does not give his wicket away. Kelly is Adelaide’s version of that player, and the club has kept him in the squad because of it.

A South Australian through and through

Kelly has come through the South Australian pathways, playing for the state in both red-ball and white-ball cricket before establishing himself with the Strikers. His is the steady path: age-group cricket, state second XI, a state contract, and then a BBL place earned over seasons rather than won in a single innings.

That grounding shows in his batting. He knows the Adelaide Oval, he knows the South Australian pitches, and he knows the bowlers he faces in the BBL because he has faced them in state cricket. For a side that has added two overseas stars, a local batter who knows the conditions is a quiet advantage.

What Kelly brings to BBL|16

Kelly is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is in the top order, where he gives the Strikers a right-handed option alongside the left-handers Carey, Chapman and Harvey. His job is to bat through the powerplay and let the middle order play with wickets in hand.

Watch his strike rotation. Kelly’s best innings are the ones where the scoreboard keeps moving without a boundary every ball — and in a side full of boundary hitters, that is exactly what the innings needs. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Kelly yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first innings of the campaign comes against Sydney Thunder on 16 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Thomas’s team, the Adelaide Strikers, open their campaign in Match 3. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 3 — Adelaide Strikers vs Sydney Thunder: Wednesday 16 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Manuka Oval, Canberra (Away).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
