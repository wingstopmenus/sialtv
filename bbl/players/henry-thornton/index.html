Henry Thornton — Adelaide Strikers bowler, BBL 2026-27

Henry Thornton is the fast bowler the Adelaide Strikers turn to when they need a wicket: a right-arm quick with genuine pace who announced himself with a five-wicket haul on his BBL debut. He earned a one-year extension for BBL|16 as part of the trio of re-signings confirmed on deadline day.

Pace, bounce and a debut to remember

Thornton is a right-arm fast bowler who bats right-handed, and his job is simple to describe and hard to do: bowl fast, hit the pitch hard, and take wickets in the powerplay and at the death. When his length is right, batters cannot settle against him — the ball hurries on and the bounce is steep.

His BBL debut is still his calling card. Thrown in as a replacement player, he took a five-wicket haul in his first game for the Strikers — the kind of entrance that buys a bowler years of trust. Since then he has been a regular part of the Adelaide attack, and the one-year extension for BBL|16 confirms the club still sees him as a central piece.

From replacement player to first-choice quick

Thornton’s path has been the modern domestic story: state cricket with South Australia, a BBL chance as a replacement, and then a contract earned the hard way. He has had to fight for his place in a competition where overseas quicks often take the new ball, and he has done it by bowling fast and taking wickets.

That is why the deadline-day re-signing matters. Alongside Jerrssis Wadia and Mackenzie Harvey, Thornton was one of three Strikers re-signed together — a signal that Adelaide are backing the local core around their two big overseas arrivals, Ben Stokes and Mark Chapman, rather than rebuilding from scratch.

What Thornton brings to BBL|16

Thornton is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and he leads the pace attack with Lloyd Pope’s leg-spin holding the middle overs. With Stokes also able to bowl at the death, Thornton’s new-ball spells set the tone for the innings.

Watch his first over. If he finds his length early, Adelaide can attack with the field up; if he is off, the powerplay can run away from the side. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Thornton yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first spell of the campaign comes against Sydney Thunder on 16 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Henry’s team, the Adelaide Strikers, open their campaign in Match 3. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 3 — Adelaide Strikers vs Sydney Thunder: Wednesday 16 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Manuka Oval, Canberra (Away).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
