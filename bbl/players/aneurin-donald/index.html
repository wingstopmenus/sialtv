Aneurin Donald — Scorchers wicketkeeper, BBL 2026-27

Aneurin Donald is a Welsh wicketkeeper-batter who has signed for the Perth Scorchers as the replacement for Josh Inglis — and he arrives in the form of his life. Playing for Derbyshire in the 2026 T20 Blast, he scored 489 runs at a strike rate of 230.66, and he made his England T20 international debut in September 2026. He is 29, and BBL|16 is his first Big Bash season.

Role: Wicketkeeper · Batting: Right-hand bat · Born: 20 December 1996, Swansea, Wales · Team: Perth Scorchers

The keeper-batter signed to replace Inglis

Donald's job is to fill the biggest hole in the champions' side. Josh Inglis, the Scorchers' keeper-batter and the man who hit the winning six in the BBL|15 final, had not re-signed when the squad lists were last updated — and Donald, a wicketkeeper-batter who bats aggressively at the top of the order, is the like-for-like replacement.

His one-year deal covers full availability, including the finals, which matters: the Scorchers' overseas players are only useful if they are there at the end. He is the third overseas player in the squad, alongside Zak Crawley and Calvin Harrison, and the club has now filled 15 of its 18 list spots. His T20 Blast form says he can do the job: 489 runs at a strike rate of 230.66 for Derbyshire, with a competition-leading 37 sixes, and only three players — James Vince, Will Smeed and D'Arcy Short — have scored more runs than his 938 across the last two Blast tournaments.

Squad note: the Scorchers have announced 15 players so far, as of 31 October 2026. The full squad has not been announced — clubs can carry up to 18 players and are still adding names — so this page will be updated as the club confirms more signings.

A 234, and a decade of waiting

Donald made his first-class debut for Glamorgan more than 12 years ago, and his name was made in 2016, when he scored 234 off 136 balls against Derbyshire — reaching his double century in 123 deliveries to equal the then-record for the fastest first-class double hundred. It was one of the most famous innings in county cricket history, and it announced a prodigy.

The career that followed was not a straight climb. He moved from Glamorgan to Hampshire, scored 554 first-class runs at 39.57 in the 2019 County Championship, and then suffered a serious ACL injury that disrupted his progress. He rebuilt at Derbyshire, and his white-ball form in 2026 — 489 Blast runs, 38 off 19 balls for Trent Rockets in the Hundred final at Lord's — finally earned him an England T20 international debut in September 2026, against Sri Lanka, at the age of 29. He has also been named in England's T20I squad for five matches in Australia in November and December, before the Big Bash starts.

Where he fits in the Scorchers

The Scorchers' batting in BBL|16 is built around Marsh, Connolly, Hardie and Turner, and Donald is the keeper-batter who bats at the top of the order and gives it a fast start. His keeping means the side does not need to carry a specialist keeper — Joel Curtis, the WA keeper, is the back-up — and his batting means the top order has a player who can score at more than two runs a ball.

His arrival also changes the side's balance. With Finn Allen gone to the Stars, the Scorchers needed a new opener who could score quickly, and Donald's Blast strike rate of 230.66 says he can. The Chennai opener, on a slower surface, will test whether that form travels — but if it does, the champions have found a replacement for Inglis who is, on current form, one of the most destructive batters in the world.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Scorchers topped the table last season with 14 points, and the defence of that position starts in Chennai.
