Rishad Hossain — Hobart Hurricanes bowler, BBL 2026-27

The Bangladesh leg-spinner

Rishad is a right-arm leg-spin bowler who bats right-handed, and his game is built on wicket-taking. He flights the ball, turns it sharply, and is not afraid to be hit — the classic wrist-spinner’s bargain, and one that has served Bangladesh well in T20 cricket.

His T20 World Cup in 2024 showed what he can do on the big stage. Bowling leg-spin for Bangladesh against the world’s best batters, Rishad took wickets and held his nerve — the kind of performance that earns a franchise contract.

A World Cup spinner’s rise

Rishad came through Bangladesh’s system and broke into the national side as a leg-spinner — a rare thing in a country that has traditionally produced finger spinners. His T20 World Cup performances in 2024, including wickets against the big teams, made him one of Bangladesh’s most exciting young bowlers.

He has since played franchise cricket around the world, and his BBL chance with Hobart is the latest step. The Hurricanes have a history of backing overseas spinners, and Rishad’s wrist-spin gives them a different option from Zampa’s.

What Rishad brings to BBL|16

Rishad is listed among the announced players for BBL|16, but his contract status is not yet settled — he is also named in the uncontracted group — so his place in the side is still to be confirmed. If he plays, his role is the second leg-spinner: four overs of wrist-spin in the middle of the innings, attacking the batters while Zampa holds the other end.

Watch the team news before the season starts. Rishad’s place in the Hurricanes’ plans will become clear when the final squad is confirmed. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Rishad yet; the season starts on 12 December, and the Hurricanes open against Brisbane Heat on 17 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Rishad’s team, the Hobart Hurricanes, open their campaign in Match 4. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 4 — Hobart Hurricanes vs Brisbane Heat: Thursday 17 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Bellerive Oval, Hobart (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
