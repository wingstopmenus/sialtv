Lachy Shaw — Sixers squad member, BBL|16

Lachy Shaw — full name Lachlan Shaw — is the young New South Wales batter in the Sydney Sixers squad for BBL|16. His listed role on the announced squad is to be confirmed, but he has already played for the Sixers when it mattered: he was in the eleven for last season's Qualifier against Perth.

He has already played a final

Shaw's name will not be new to Sixers fans who watched last season's run. He was named in the Sixers' eleven for the Qualifier against Perth on 20 January 2026 — the game the Sixers lost on their way to the final — lining up alongside Steve Smith, Josh Philippe, Moises Henriques, Jack Edwards and Sean Abbott.

That is a meaningful debut by any measure: a young batter trusted in a knockout game, in a side full of internationals. The Sixers lost the game, but the selection said plenty about how the club rates him. Players do not get picked for Qualifiers by accident.

His role on the announced squad list for BBL|16 is shown as to be confirmed, so this page does not guess at it. Squad listings describe him as a batter, and he is a right-handed one from New South Wales.

A batter in the Sixers' mould

Shaw is a product of the New South Wales system, and he bats in the style the Sixers like: right-handed, technically sound, and capable of scoring quickly when the situation asks for it. He is not a slogger; he is a batter who builds an innings and then accelerates.

That profile fits the Sixers' middle order. The side has power at the top in Philippe and Seifert, an anchor in Smith, and a finisher in Silk; what it needs below them are batters who can hold an innings together when wickets fall. Shaw is being developed for exactly that job.

He was part of the Sixers' squad through last season, and his retention for BBL|16 — contracted alongside the senior core — shows the club sees him as part of the future, not a one-game experiment.

Where he sits in the squad

The Sixers have announced 16 of a maximum 18 players for BBL|16. Shaw is one of the 16, and one of a group of young New South Wales players — Jack Edwards, Joel Davies, Hayden Kerr, Riley Kingsell, Jake Scott — who form the next generation of the side.

His Qualifier appearance gives him something the other youngsters do not have yet: a big game already played. When the Sixers need a batter in a tight spot this season, the captain will remember that Shaw has been there before.

Whether he holds a regular place in the eleven is to be confirmed — the top order is crowded, with Smith, Philippe, Seifert, Silk and Ollie Davies all competing for spots. But he is in the squad, and he has shown he can handle the occasion.
