Callum Vidler — Brisbane Heat bowler, BBL 2026-27

Callum Vidler is the teenage fast bowler in the Brisbane Heat squad: a right-arm quick who shared the record for the most wickets by an Australian at the 2024 Under-19 World Cup — 12 wickets in five games, level with Adelaide’s Tom Straker — and who has been locked in by the Heat until BBL|16 and beyond.

The teenager with a World Cup record

Vidler is a right-arm fast bowler who bats right-handed, and his game is built on pace and aggression. He bowls fast, attacks the stumps, and is not afraid to bowl short. For a young quick, that fearlessness is the asset — and the thing coaches have to manage.

His Under-19 World Cup is still his calling card. Twelve wickets in five games, at a tournament where Australia won the title, put Vidler on the map before he had played senior cricket. The Heat signed him quickly, and he is now part of the young pace group the club is building for the future.

From the Under-19 World Cup to the Gabba

Vidler came through Queensland’s junior system, where his pace marked him out early. He played for Australia at the 2024 Under-19 World Cup in South Africa, taking 12 wickets — the joint-most by an Australian at the tournament — and returned home as one of the country’s most exciting young quicks.

The Heat’s plan for him is patient. He is in a pace attack with Bartlett, Johnson and Neser — three internationals — so Vidler does not have to carry the attack. He has to learn from them, take his chances, and show the pace that made his name.

What Vidler brings to BBL|16

Vidler is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the young quick in a deep pace attack. With Tom Curran unavailable for the first three matches, there may be an early opening for him — and a good first spell could earn him a run of games.

Watch his pace. Vidler’s value is speed and aggression, and if he can bowl fast without losing his length, he is a wicket-taker. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Vidler yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game of the campaign comes against Sydney Sixers at the Gabba on 15 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Callum’s team, the Brisbane Heat, open their campaign in Match 2. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 2 — Brisbane Heat vs Sydney Sixers: Tuesday 15 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), The Gabba, Brisbane (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
