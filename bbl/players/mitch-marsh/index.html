Mitch Marsh — Scorchers all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Mitch Marsh is an Australian all-rounder who captains the national T20 side — and he was player of the match in the 2021 T20 World Cup final, scoring 77 not out as Australia won their first T20 world title. For the Perth Scorchers, he is the senior batter and the player the title defence is built around.

Role: All-rounder · Batting: Right-hand bat · Bowling: Right-arm medium · Team: Perth Scorchers

The senior batter of the champions

Marsh's job is to bat at the top of the order and set the tone. He is a right-handed batter who hits the ball a long way, and a right-arm medium-pace bowler who gives the captain a useful extra option. He made 44 in the BBL|15 final as the Scorchers beat Sydney Sixers, and he is contracted through BBL|17.

His value is his big-match record. He scored 77 not out in the 2021 T20 World Cup final against New Zealand, winning player of the match as Australia won their first T20 world title, and he was part of the side that won the 2023 ODI World Cup. He has captained Australia in T20 cricket, and he is one of the most experienced white-ball players in the country. The Scorchers' batting is built around him — Marsh, Connolly, Hardie and Agar — and his presence at the top of the order gives the side a player who has won World Cups and knows how to handle pressure.

Seventy-seven not out in a World Cup final

Marsh's most famous innings came in Dubai in November 2021, when he scored 77 not out from 50 balls in the T20 World Cup final against New Zealand, winning player of the match as Australia chased down 173 with seven balls to spare. It was the innings that made him a World Cup winner and one of the most famous Australian cricketers of his generation.

He has played Test, one-day and T20 cricket for Australia, and he has been the national T20 captain. His BBL career has been with the Scorchers throughout, and he has been part of the club's most successful period — six titles, more than any other team. He is a Western Australian, the son of former Australia coach Geoff Marsh and the brother of Shaun Marsh, and he has become one of the faces of the Scorchers' success.

Where he fits in the Scorchers

The Scorchers' batting in BBL|16 is built around Marsh at the top, and his role is to give the side a fast start. Finn Allen, last season's leading run-scorer, is now with the Stars, so Marsh carries more of the top-order load — and the new overseas opener Zak Crawley will bat with him.

His captaincy experience matters too. He captains Australia in T20 cricket, and his leadership in the Scorchers' dressing room — alongside Ashton Turner's captaincy of the club side — gives the team two leaders who have won World Cups. If the Scorchers are to defend their title, it will be because Marsh had a big season at the top of the order.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Scorchers topped the table last season with 14 points, and the defence of that position starts in Chennai.
