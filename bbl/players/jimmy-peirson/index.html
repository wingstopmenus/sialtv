Jimmy Peirson — Brisbane Heat wicketkeeper, BBL 2026-27

Jimmy Peirson is the wicketkeeper the Brisbane Heat have built around for over a decade: a right-handed batter and keeper who is about to play his thirteenth season with the club, having played 124 matches for the Heat — the fourth-most BBL games by a current single-club player.

Thirteen seasons in teal

Peirson is a right-handed batter who keeps wicket, and his value is loyalty and reliability. He has been the Heat’s keeper through title wins and rebuilds, through captains and coaches, and he is still there — the constant in a side that has changed around him.

His 124 matches for the Heat put him behind only Jordan Silk, Ashton Turner and Ben Dwarshuis among current single-club players, and that longevity says everything about how the club rates him. He is not just a keeper; he is the memory of the dressing room, the player who knows what the Heat are and what they should be.

The keeper who has seen it all

Peirson has been part of the Heat’s greatest moments, including the BBL|13 title win in 2023-24, and he has kept wicket to some of the best bowlers in the competition. He has captained the side too, standing in when the captain was away, and his keeping has been the foundation the Heat’s attack is built on.

He is also a Queensland regular, and his domestic career has been built the same way as his BBL one: steadily, reliably, and without fuss. In a league of short contracts and quick changes, Peirson is the exception — a player who has stayed, and stayed good.

What Peirson brings to BBL|16

Peirson is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the same as ever: keep wicket for twenty overs, bat in the middle order, and hold the side together. With a new coach in James Hopes and a captain still to be decided, Peirson’s experience matters more than ever.

Watch his keeping to the spinners. Peirson’s work standing up to Matt Kuhnemann and the part-time spin will decide how many chances the Heat create in the middle overs. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Peirson yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game of the campaign comes against Sydney Sixers at the Gabba on 15 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Jimmy’s team, the Brisbane Heat, open their campaign in Match 2. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 2 — Brisbane Heat vs Sydney Sixers: Tuesday 15 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), The Gabba, Brisbane (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
