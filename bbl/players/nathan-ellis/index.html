Nathan Ellis — Hobart Hurricanes bowler, BBL 2026-27

Nathan Ellis is the captain of the Hobart Hurricanes and the bowler their title defence is built around: a right-arm fast-medium seamer and Australia white-ball international who took a hat-trick on his T20 international debut. He is contracted until BBL|17, and he leads the champions into BBL|16.

The captain who bowls at the death

Ellis is a right-arm fast-medium bowler who bats right-handed, and his game is built on skill at the end of the innings. He bowls yorkers, slower balls and cutters, changes pace cleverly, and is the bowler Hobart turn to when a game is on the line in the final overs.

His captaincy is the other half of the job. Ellis led the Hurricanes to their first title in BBL|14 and to third place last season, and he has to do it again with a side that has lost Matthew Wade to retirement and gained Adam Zampa from the Renegades. The extension to BBL|17 says the club trusts him to lead the next era.

A hat-trick on debut

Ellis made his T20 international debut for Australia against Bangladesh in August 2021 — and took a hat-trick, becoming the first man to do so on T20 international debut. It was the performance that announced him to the world, and he has been a regular in Australia’s white-ball sides ever since.

His domestic career has been built the same way: steadily, with Tasmania and Hobart, until the BBL could not ignore him. He is not the fastest bowler in the competition, but he is one of the smartest — and in T20 cricket, that is often worth more.

What Ellis brings to BBL|16

Ellis is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the busiest in the side: captain the team, bowl at the death, and lead a pace attack that also includes Riley Meredith, Billy Stanlake and the young quicks. With Zampa in the side, Ellis has a world-class spinner to lean on too.

Watch his death bowling. Ellis’s yorkers and slower balls in the final overs are where Hobart’s games are won and lost, and his captaincy will be tested by a side that has changed around him. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Ellis yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game as captain this season comes against Brisbane Heat on 17 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Nathan’s team, the Hobart Hurricanes, open their campaign in Match 4. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 4 — Hobart Hurricanes vs Brisbane Heat: Thursday 17 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Bellerive Oval, Hobart (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
