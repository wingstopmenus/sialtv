BBL|16 · Sydney Thunder

Sam Konstas — Thunder batter, BBL|16

Sam Konstas is the young opener the Sydney Thunder have built their batting around — a New South Wales batter who made his Test debut for Australia on Boxing Day 2024 against India at the MCG, scoring 60, and who is contracted with the Thunder until BBL|18.

Boxing Day, against India, aged 19

Konstas's Test debut is already part of Australian cricket folklore. Picked for the Boxing Day Test against India at the MCG in December 2024, aged 19, he walked out against Jasprit Bumrah — the best bowler in the world — and scored 60, playing shots that made the whole country sit up. It was a debut of rare confidence.

He is a right-handed opener who bats with intent: he takes the game on, he scores quickly, and he does not wait for the bowlers to settle. That approach made him a sensation in Test cricket, and it is the same approach the Thunder want in the Big Bash — an opener who can win the powerplay on his own.

His domestic base is New South Wales, where he has been a regular since his teens, and the Thunder have tied him down until BBL|18 — a long contract for a young player, and a sign of how highly the club rates him.

What he brings to the Thunder

The Thunder's batting has a clear shape: David Warner's experience, Konstas's daring, and a middle order of Bancroft, Maddinson and Billings to build on it. Konstas is the player who sets the tempo — if he fires in the powerplay, the innings takes off; if he falls early, the seniors have to rebuild.

His game is built for the Big Bash. He scores in front of square, he hits the ball in the air, and he does not let bowlers settle into a rhythm. On the Showground's true pitches, that is a dangerous combination — and five of the Thunder's ten league games are at home.

He is also a player who has already handled the biggest stage in Australian cricket. A Boxing Day Test against India is a bigger occasion than any Big Bash game; if he could handle that at 19, he can handle the Sydney Smash.

A new Thunder, under Flintoff

The Thunder missed the finals last season, a year after reaching the BBL|14 final — where they lost to Hobart Hurricanes. The new season is a fresh start under Andrew Flintoff, the former England captain who has signed a two-year deal as head coach, and the squad has been rebuilt around pace: Trent Boult and Lockie Ferguson join Daniel Sams and Tanveer Sangha.

Konstas's role in that rebuild is to give the batting its spark. The bowling looks formidable on paper; the question is whether the batting can post totals worth defending. A young opener who scores quickly is the simplest answer, and Konstas is the Thunder's best version of it.

The season opens against Adelaide Strikers at Manuka Oval on 16 December — Konstas's first game of a season the whole country will be watching.
