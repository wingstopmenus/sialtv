Will Prestwidge — Hobart Hurricanes bowler, BBL 2026-27

Will Prestwidge is the young fast bowler in the Hobart Hurricanes squad: a right-arm quick from a famous Queensland cricket family — son of former Queensland bowler Scott Prestwidge, brother of Jack and Georgia — who made his BBL debut for Brisbane Heat at 19 and now plays for Tasmania and Hobart.

The Prestwidge who bowls fast

Prestwidge is a right-arm fast bowler who bats left-handed, and his game is built on pace. He was born on 15 January 2002, and he was only 19 when he made his Big Bash debut for Brisbane Heat — followed a month later by his List A debut for Queensland in February 2022.

His family is cricketing royalty in Queensland. His father Scott played for the Bulls, his brother Jack is a fast bowler who has played for the Melbourne Renegades, and his sister Georgia plays for Victoria and the Brisbane Heat women. Will Prestwidge is the next in line.

From Queensland to Tasmania

Prestwidge struggled to make an impact at his native state, and ahead of the 2024-25 summer he made the switch to Tasmania — the move that changed his career. He made his Hurricanes debut in BBL|15, taking back-to-back wickets in his first game for the club, and he has since become a Tasmania regular.

The Hurricanes kept him on a two-year extension, and he is now part of the young pace group — alongside Marcus Bean and Iain Carlisle — that the club is developing behind Nathan Ellis and Riley Meredith. BBL|16 is his chance to turn a promising debut into a regular place.

What Prestwidge brings to BBL|16

Prestwidge is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the young quick pushing for games in a stacked pace attack. He found it tough to break in last season behind Ellis and Meredith, but his debut wickets showed he belongs at this level.

Watch his first spell of the season. Young quicks are judged quickly in the BBL, and a good first over — pace, bounce, a wicket — can earn a bowler a run of games. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Prestwidge yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game of the campaign comes against Brisbane Heat on 17 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Will’s team, the Hobart Hurricanes, open their campaign in Match 4. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 4 — Hobart Hurricanes vs Brisbane Heat: Thursday 17 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Bellerive Oval, Hobart (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
