Mahli Beardman — Scorchers bowler, BBL 2026-27

Mahli Beardman is one of the most exciting young fast bowlers in Australia — a right-arm quick from Dunsborough who was player of the match in the 2024 Under-19 World Cup final, taking 3 for 15 against India, and who made his T20 international debut for Australia against Pakistan in January 2026. He is 21, and he was part of the Scorchers' BBL|15 title win.

Role: Bowler · Bowling: Right-arm fast-medium · Born: 31 August 2005, Dunsborough, Western Australia · Team: Perth Scorchers

The young quick who won a World Cup final

Beardman's job in BBL|16 is to keep doing what he did last season: bowl fast, take wickets, and show why he is talked about as a future leader of Australia's pace attack. He took 13 wickets in BBL|15, helping the Scorchers to their sixth title, and he did it bowling at speeds over 140 kilometres per hour.

His breakthrough season was followed by an international debut — 2 for 33 on T20I debut against Pakistan in January 2026 — and then by a setback: a season-ending back injury, diagnosed as lumbar bone stress, that kept him out for seven months. He returned to the field in October 2026, playing for Australia A against India A in Puducherry, and he is now fit for BBL|16. The Scorchers have managed him carefully throughout his young career, taking inspiration from Pat Cummins, who also had a disjointed start to his international career because of injury.

Squad note: the Scorchers have announced 15 players so far, as of 31 October 2026. The full squad has not been announced — clubs can carry up to 18 players and are still adding names — so this page will be updated as the club confirms more signings.

Three for 15 in a World Cup final

Beardman's name was made at the 2024 Under-19 World Cup in South Africa, where he took 10 wickets at 10.50 from six appearances and was named player of the match in the final after taking 3 for 15 from seven overs against India — including the wickets of captain Uday Saharan and Musheer Khan — as Australia won by 79 runs.

The step up to senior cricket came quickly after that. He made his List A debut for Western Australia against New South Wales in November 2023, signed a two-year BBL deal with the Scorchers, and made his BBL debut against Sydney Thunder on 13 January 2025. He was named as a reserve for the senior Australian squad in September 2024 and called up to the T20I squad for India in October 2025. He is six foot two, he swings the new ball, and he is mentored by Dennis Lillee, who also mentored Mitchell Johnson.

Where he fits in the Scorchers

The Scorchers' pace attack is the best in the league, and Beardman is its youngest member — and its future. Richardson is the spearhead, Morris is the wild thing, Paris is the left-armer, and Beardman is the quick who bowls at 140kph and swings the new ball both ways.

His role in BBL|16 is to build on his 13-wicket title season and show that his back injury is behind him. The club has managed his workload carefully, and his return for Australia A in India — where he is expected to play in the BBL|16 season opener in Chennai — says he is ready. If he stays fit, he is the bowler most likely to take the most wickets for the champions this season.

The BBL Points Table will carry the standings once the season starts — the Scorchers topped the table last season with 14 points, and the defence of that position starts in Chennai.
