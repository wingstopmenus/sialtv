Hugh Weibgen — Brisbane Heat all-rounder, BBL 2026-27

Hugh Weibgen is the young all-rounder the Brisbane Heat have built their future around: a right-handed batter and off-spinner who captained Australia to the Under-19 World Cup title in 2024, and who is contracted to the Heat until BBL|17.

The World Cup-winning captain

Weibgen is a right-handed batter who bowls right-arm off-spin, and his game is built on leadership as much as skill. He captained Australia at the 2024 Under-19 World Cup in South Africa, leading the side to the title — the kind of experience that shapes a young cricketer’s whole career.

Captaining a World Cup-winning side at 19 is a rare thing. It says the selectors trusted his judgement, his teammates trusted his leadership, and he could handle pressure that most players never face. The Heat signed him on the back of it, and they have kept him ever since.

From the World Cup to Queensland and the Heat

Weibgen came through Queensland’s junior system, where his batting and off-spin marked him out as an all-rounder in the making. After the World Cup triumph, he signed with the Heat and earned a Queensland contract, joining Lachlan Hearne and Callum Vidler in the group of young Queenslanders the club is building around.

His senior career is still young. He has played for Queensland’s second XI and made his way into the state’s senior squads, and his BBL career is in its early stages too. BBL|16 is the season where a World Cup-winning captain starts becoming a senior professional.

What Weibgen brings to BBL|16

Weibgen is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the young all-rounder who can bat in the middle order and bowl off-spin in the middle overs. With the Heat’s pace attack doing the heavy lifting, Weibgen’s overs give the captain a change of pace.

Watch his batting under pressure. Weibgen’s best quality is composure — the same composure that won a World Cup — and it is what will earn him a regular place. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Weibgen yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game of the campaign comes against Sydney Sixers at the Gabba on 15 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Hugh’s team, the Brisbane Heat, open their campaign in Match 2. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 2 — Brisbane Heat vs Sydney Sixers: Tuesday 15 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), The Gabba, Brisbane (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
