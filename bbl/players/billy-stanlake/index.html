Billy Stanlake — Hobart Hurricanes bowler, BBL 2026-27

Billy Stanlake is the tall left-arm quick in the Hobart Hurricanes attack: a fast bowler and Australia white-ball international who stands over two metres tall and generates bounce that few batters can handle. He signed a two-year deal with the Hurricanes, and BBL|16 is his chance to stay fit and lead the pace attack’s left-arm option.

The two-metre left-armer

Stanlake is a left-arm fast bowler, and his game is built on height and bounce. At over two metres tall, he hits the pitch hard and gets the ball to climb — the kind of bowler who makes even good batters play defensively, and who takes wickets with the short ball.

His career has been shaped by injury. Stanlake has had seasons lost to back and side problems, and his challenge has always been staying on the park. When he is fit, he is one of the most dangerous quicks in the country; when he is not, the Hurricanes miss him.

An Australian international who keeps coming back

Stanlake has played one-day and T20 international cricket for Australia, taking wickets with his pace and bounce, and he has played for Queensland and the Hurricanes. His international career has been interrupted by injury, but his talent has never been in doubt.

The two-year deal with Hobart is a sign of trust. The Hurricanes know Stanlake’s history, and they have backed him anyway — because a fit Billy Stanlake is a bowler who changes games, and because his left-arm angle gives the attack a variety it cannot get anywhere else.

What Stanlake brings to BBL|16

Stanlake is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the tall left-arm quick in a pace attack led by Nathan Ellis and Riley Meredith. With Marcus Bean also left-arm, the Hurricanes have two tall left-armers — a rare luxury in T20 cricket.

Watch his fitness. Stanlake’s season will be decided by whether he can stay on the park for ten league games — and if he can, the Hurricanes’ attack becomes very hard to score against. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Stanlake yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first game of the campaign comes against Brisbane Heat on 17 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Billy’s team, the Hobart Hurricanes, open their campaign in Match 4. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 4 — Hobart Hurricanes vs Brisbane Heat: Thursday 17 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Bellerive Oval, Hobart (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
