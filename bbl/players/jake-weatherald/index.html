Jake Weatherald — Hobart Hurricanes batter, BBL 2026-27

Jake Weatherald is the left-handed opener in the Hobart Hurricanes squad: a South Australian batter who has played BBL cricket for the Adelaide Strikers and Tasmania, and who signed a two-year deal with the Hurricanes as part of the champion side’s batting depth.

The left-handed opener with a BBL hundred

Weatherald is a left-handed batter, and his game is built on timing and placement through the off side. He is at his best when the ball is new and the field is up, driving through the covers and cutting behind point — the classic left-handed opener’s game.

He has scored a BBL century before, for the Adelaide Strikers, and that innings is still the one people mention when his name comes up. A batter who has made a hundred in this competition knows he can do it again — and the Hurricanes signed him because of it.

From the Strikers to the Hurricanes

Weatherald began his BBL career with the Adelaide Strikers, where he opened the batting and scored his hundred, and he has since played for Tasmania and the Hurricanes. His move to Hobart is the latest step in a career that has taken him around the Australian domestic system.

For the Hurricanes, he is the experienced left-hander in a batting group that leans right-handed. With Shai Hope, Tim David and Mitch Owen around him, Weatherald is the player who gives the top order its balance — and its depth.

What Weatherald brings to BBL|16

Weatherald is one of the announced contracted players for BBL|16 — the full squad has not been announced yet — and his role is the left-handed top-order option. He can open if the side needs him to, or bat at three and hold the innings together while the power hitters play around him.

Watch his batting against the new ball. Weatherald’s best work comes in the powerplay, when the field is up and the gaps are there to find. There are no BBL|16 numbers for Weatherald yet; the season starts on 12 December, and his first innings of the campaign comes against Brisbane Heat on 17 December.

What’s next

BBL|16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027. The opener is Match 1, Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, on Saturday 12 December at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match played outside Australia — starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). Jake’s team, the Hobart Hurricanes, open their campaign in Match 4. The full list of every BBL fixture is on the BBL Schedule.

  • Match 4 — Hobart Hurricanes vs Brisbane Heat: Thursday 17 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), Bellerive Oval, Hobart (Home).
  • Finals: The finals are the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, Australia Day. Finals venues and times are still to be confirmed.
  • Points: Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1, and the top four qualify for the finals, with net run rate deciding places when teams are level on points.
