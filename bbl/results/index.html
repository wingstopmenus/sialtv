Frequently asked questions

When is the first BBL 2026-27 result?

After Match 1, on Saturday 12 December 2026 — Melbourne Renegades against Perth Scorchers in Chennai, starting at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT).

Melbourne Renegades against Perth Scorchers, the defending champions, at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai. It is the first BBL match ever played outside Australia, and the Renegades count it as a home game.
