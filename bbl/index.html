BBL 2026-27 — Schedule, Teams & Points Table

Big Bash League Season 16 runs from 12 December 2026 to 26 January 2027, with 8 teams playing 44 matches. It opens with a first — Melbourne Renegades against defending champions Perth Scorchers at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai, the first BBL match ever played outside Australia — and ends with the final on Australia Day.

BBL 2026-27 points table

The season has not started yet, so there is no points table for BBL|16. The table will appear here after the first match on 12 December, and will be updated after every match day. A win is worth 2 points and a no result is worth 1 point. The top four teams qualify for the finals, and net run rate decides places when teams are level on points.

The eight teams

Perth Scorchers

Defending champions — six BBL titles, the most of any team. Home: Perth Stadium.

Sydney Sixers

Three titles (2011-12, 2019-20, 2020-21). Home: Sydney Cricket Ground.

Brisbane Heat

Two titles (2012-13, 2023-24). Home: The Gabba, Brisbane.

Hobart Hurricanes

Champions in 2024-25. Home: Bellerive Oval, Hobart.

Melbourne Renegades

Champions in 2018-19. Home: Melbourne Cricket Ground and Junction Oval.

Adelaide Strikers

Champions in 2017-18. Home: Adelaide Oval.

Sydney Thunder

Champions in 2015-16. Home: Sydney Showground Stadium.

Melbourne Stars

The only team yet to win a BBL title, after four final defeats. Home: Melbourne Cricket Ground.

Frequently asked questions

When does BBL 2026-27 start?

The season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026 with Melbourne Renegades against Perth Scorchers at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai, and ends with the final on Tuesday 26 January 2027, Australia Day.

Why is a BBL match in Chennai?

The 12 December opener is the first BBL match ever played outside Australia. It was announced in July 2026, and the Renegades count it as a home game.

How many teams play in the BBL?

Eight teams: Adelaide Strikers, Brisbane Heat, Hobart Hurricanes, Melbourne Renegades, Melbourne Stars, Perth Scorchers, Sydney Sixers and Sydney Thunder. Each team plays 10 league matches.

How do teams qualify for the finals?

A win is worth 2 points and a no result is worth 1 point. The top four teams qualify: the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January.

Where can I see the full BBL schedule and every scorecard?

On the official BBL site and Cricket Australia's fixture pages, which carry the complete schedule with local start times, squads and news for BBL|16.
