The season starts in Chennai, not Australia

Season opener · Saturday, 12 December 2026

Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers

BBL|16, Match 1 · MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chennai · 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT, 9:10 AM UTC)

Perth arrive as defending champions, holders of six BBL titles — more than any other club. The Renegades, meanwhile, count the Chennai game as a home match, even though it is being played in India, a long way from Melbourne. It is the first Big Bash League match ever staged outside Australia, and it sets the tone for a season that runs right through the Australian summer to the final on Australia Day.

Forty league matches in five weeks

The league stage runs from 12 December 2026 to 17 January 2027, with 40 matches across eight teams — each side plays ten. There is no match on Christmas Day, but the calendar barely pauses otherwise: six days carry two matches, including Boxing Day and New Year’s Day. Then come the finals — the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January, and the final on Tuesday 26 January 2027, Australia Day, with a reserve day held back in case weather intervenes. Finals venues depend on where teams finish, so they are confirmed once the league stage ends.

Eight teams, one clear favourite

Perth Scorchers have won six of the fifteen BBL titles played so far, and they start this season as defending champions. The other seven clubs share the remaining nine titles between them — and Melbourne Stars, beaten in four finals, are still waiting for their first.

Perth Scorchers

Defending champions, with six titles — more than any other team. Adam Voges coaches, and Ashton Turner captained last season’s title win. Home: Perth Stadium.

Sydney Sixers

Three titles (2011-12, 2019-20, 2020-21), and beaten finalists last season. Matthew Mott coaches. Home: Sydney Cricket Ground.

Brisbane Heat

Two titles (2012-13, 2023-24). James Hopes coaches. Home: The Gabba, Brisbane.

Hobart Hurricanes

Champions in 2024-25, when they lifted their first title. Nathan Ellis captains, Jeff Vaughan coaches. Home: Bellerive Oval, Hobart.

Melbourne Renegades

Champions in 2018-19, and the home side for the Chennai opener. Luke Ronchi coaches. Home: Melbourne Cricket Ground and Junction Oval.

Adelaide Strikers

Champions in 2017-18. Matt Short captains, Tim Paine coaches — and Ben Stokes is the headline signing of the season. Home: Adelaide Oval.

Sydney Thunder

Champions in 2015-16. A fresh start under new head coach Andrew Flintoff, with David Warner back after captaining last season. Home: Sydney Showground Stadium.

Melbourne Stars

Still chasing a first title after four final defeats. Marcus Stoinis captains, Cameron White coaches. Home: Melbourne Cricket Ground.

Questions people ask about the BBL

When does BBL 2026-27 start?

The season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, when Melbourne Renegades meet defending champions Perth Scorchers at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai. It ends with the final on Tuesday 26 January 2027, Australia Day.

Why is a BBL match in Chennai?

The 12 December opener is the first BBL match ever played outside Australia. It was announced in July 2026, and the Renegades count it as a home game.

How many teams play in the BBL?

Eight teams: Adelaide Strikers, Brisbane Heat, Hobart Hurricanes, Melbourne Renegades, Melbourne Stars, Perth Scorchers, Sydney Sixers and Sydney Thunder. Each team plays 10 league matches.

How do teams qualify for the finals?

A win is worth 2 points and a no result is worth 1 point. The top four teams qualify: the Qualifier on 19 January is first against second, with the winner straight into the final; the Knockout on 20 January is third against fourth; the Challenger on 23 January decides the second finalist, and the final is on 26 January.
