Top performances — the numbers start on 12 December

No BBL|16 statistics exist yet — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, and the first runs, wickets and rates land after the Chennai opener. Four lists will fill in here, updated after every match:

Top run scorers

The batters with the most runs in BBL|16, with matches, innings and average alongside. The first name on the list appears after Match 1, Melbourne Renegades against Perth Scorchers in Chennai.

Top wicket takers

The bowlers with the most wickets in BBL|16, with matches and average alongside. The first wickets of the season are taken in the same match, on 12 December.

Best batting strike rates

Strike rate is runs scored per 100 balls faced — the measure of how fast a batter scores. This list ranks the quickest scorers of the season, once enough balls have been faced to mean something.

Best bowling economy

Economy is runs conceded per over bowled — the measure of how hard a bowler is to score against. This list ranks the tightest bowlers of the season, once enough overs have been bowled to mean something.

When the numbers arrive

The first statistics appear after Match 1 on 12 December 2026, and every list on this page is updated after each match day from then on. A batter needs a completed innings to hold a run total; a bowler needs a wicket to hold a place on the wicket takers list. Early in the season the lists move fast — one big innings or one four-wicket spell can top them — and they settle as the 40 league matches play out.

Every number here comes from the verified scorecard of the match it was made in. Nothing is projected, and no record from an earlier season is carried into this one: BBL|16 starts every list at zero.

Frequently asked questions

When will BBL 2026-27 stats appear?

After the first match, on Saturday 12 December 2026 — Melbourne Renegades against Perth Scorchers in Chennai. The lists on this page update after every match day from then on.

Who leads the BBL run scorers and wicket takers?

Nobody yet — the season has not started, so every list is empty. The first leaders appear after Match 1 on 12 December 2026.

What is batting strike rate?

Runs scored per 100 balls faced. A strike rate of 150 means the batter scores 1.5 runs per ball on average.

What is bowling economy?

Runs conceded per over bowled. An economy of 6.00 means the bowler concedes 6 runs per over on average — lower is tighter.

Where do the stats on this page come from?

From the verified scorecard of each match, once it is played. Nothing is projected and no number is carried over from an earlier season.
