Title history — who has won the Big Bash

Seven of the eight teams have lifted the trophy. Perth Scorchers lead with six titles, Sydney Sixers have three, Brisbane Heat have two, and Hobart Hurricanes, Melbourne Renegades, Adelaide Strikers and Sydney Thunder have one each. Melbourne Stars are the only team still waiting, after four final defeats.

Frequently asked questions

How many teams play in the BBL?

Eight teams: Adelaide Strikers, Brisbane Heat, Hobart Hurricanes, Melbourne Renegades, Melbourne Stars, Perth Scorchers, Sydney Sixers and Sydney Thunder. Each team plays 10 league matches.

Who are the defending BBL champions?

Perth Scorchers, who won their sixth title in 2025-26. They open the new season against Melbourne Renegades in Chennai on 12 December 2026.

Which BBL team has won the most titles?

Perth Scorchers, with six. Sydney Sixers are next with three, and Brisbane Heat have two.

Which BBL team has never won the title?

Melbourne Stars — the only one of the eight without a title, after four final defeats.

Where do the BBL teams play?

Each team has a home ground — Perth Stadium for the Scorchers, the SCG for the Sixers, The Gabba for the Heat, Bellerive Oval for the Hurricanes, the MCG and Junction Oval for the Renegades, Adelaide Oval for the Strikers, Sydney Showground Stadium for the Thunder and the MCG for the Stars. The full venue list is on the BBL Schedule page.
