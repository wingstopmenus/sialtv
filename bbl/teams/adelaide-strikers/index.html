Adelaide Strikers in BBL|16

The Strikers missed the finals last season, and the new campaign starts with a statement of intent: England Test captain Ben Stokes, signed after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market, and New Zealand batter Mark Chapman, the other big arrival. Around them is a settled South Australian core — Alex Carey behind the stumps, Jason Sangha and Thomas Kelly in the batting, and Henry Thornton and Lloyd Pope with the ball.

The wait since the 2017-18 title is now eight seasons. Adelaide's problem has rarely been talent — Matt Short is one of the most destructive openers in the competition — it has been turning good league campaigns into finals wins. BBL|16 gives them ten matches to prove the mix is right, starting with two away games before the Adelaide Oval crowd gets its first look at the side on 23 December.

Tim Paine coaches the side. The Strikers play five home matches, all at Adelaide Oval, and finish the league stage there too, against Perth Scorchers on Saturday 16 January.

What qualification needs

Eight teams play ten league matches each. A win is worth 2 points and a no result is worth 1 point. The top four qualify for the finals, and net run rate decides places when teams are level on points.

Last season is the guide to the cut. Perth Scorchers topped the table with 14 points from seven wins, Sydney Sixers and Hobart Hurricanes took the next two places with 13, and Melbourne Stars took the last finals place with 12 points from six wins. Six wins, in other words, is the realistic minimum — and seven wins all but guarantees a top-two place and a Qualifier, with its second chance at the final.

The finals are: the Qualifier on Tuesday 19 January (1st vs 2nd, winner straight to the final), the Knockout on Wednesday 20 January (3rd vs 4th), the Challenger on Saturday 23 January (Qualifier loser vs Knockout winner) and the final on Tuesday 26 January 2027, Australia Day, with a reserve day available. Finals venues and start times are TBC.

What to watch

Three things decide whether this is the year Adelaide go past the league stage.

Stokes and Short at the top. Short's powerplay hitting sets the tone; Stokes gives the side a world-class all-rounder who can win a match with bat or ball. If both fire, Adelaide's ceiling is a home final.

The new-ball pair. Henry Thornton's pace and Lloyd Pope's leg-spin are the wicket-taking threats. The Strikers' attack looks thin if either is injured — the squad list still has room for another bowler.

The home stretch. Three of Adelaide's last four league matches are at Adelaide Oval, including the finale against the champions. A strong finish at home is exactly how a mid-table side becomes a top-two side.

Frequently asked questions

Who captains Adelaide Strikers in BBL|16?

Matt Short. The all-rounder leads the side and opens the batting, and he is one of the few BBL captains whose place in the team is never in doubt.

When is Adelaide Strikers' first match?

Match 3 against Sydney Thunder on Wednesday 16 December 2026, 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT), at Manuka Oval in Canberra. Their first home match is against the same opponents, on Wednesday 23 December at Adelaide Oval.

Where do Adelaide Strikers play their home matches?

Adelaide Oval. All five home matches are there, including the last league match of their season against Perth Scorchers on Saturday 16 January.

How many BBL titles have Adelaide Strikers won?

One — in 2017-18. They have not reached a BBL final since that title win.
