The finals — four matches, 19 to 26 January

The top four teams on the points table qualify for the finals. The Qualifier is on Tuesday 19 January, the Knockout on Wednesday 20 January, the Challenger on Saturday 23 January, and the final on Tuesday 26 January 2027, Australia Day, with a reserve day available. Finals venues and start times depend on the standings and are still to be confirmed.

Times, venues and the Chennai opener

Six days in the league stage carry two matches: Saturday 26 December, Friday 1 January, Monday 4 January, Sunday 10 January, Saturday 16 January and Sunday 17 January. The rest of the league stage is one match a day, except for three empty days — no matches on Sunday 13 or Monday 14 December after the opener, and none on Christmas Day, 25 December.

The Melbourne Cricket Ground hosts the most league matches, six. The Gabba, Perth Stadium and Adelaide Oval host five each, the Sydney Cricket Ground and Bellerive Oval four each, and Junction Oval, Manuka Oval and Sydney Showground Stadium three each. Aurora Stadium in Launceston hosts one match, and so does MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the opener, and the first BBL match ever played outside Australia.

That opener starts at 2:40 PM IST, which is 2:10 PM PKT and 9:10 AM UTC. Most other matches start at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT). The exceptions are the early starts — 10:30 AM IST on 1 and 16 January, 8:40 AM IST on 10 January and 8:10 AM IST on 17 January — and the later starts, 3:45 PM IST on 26 December and 4 January, and 2:45 PM IST for the last league match on 17 January.

Frequently asked questions

When does BBL 2026-27 start?

Saturday 12 December 2026, with Melbourne Renegades against Perth Scorchers at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT). It is the first BBL match ever played outside Australia.

When is the BBL 2026-27 final?

Tuesday 26 January 2027, Australia Day, with a reserve day available. The venue and start time are still to be confirmed — they depend on which teams qualify.

Where are the BBL finals played?

Finals venues are still to be confirmed. They depend on the final league standings and are announced once the league stage ends on Sunday 17 January 2027.

What time do BBL matches start in Pakistan?

Pakistan time is 30 minutes behind IST. A 1:45 PM IST start is 1:15 PM PKT, and the Chennai opener at 2:40 PM IST is 2:10 PM PKT. Every time on this page is in IST.
