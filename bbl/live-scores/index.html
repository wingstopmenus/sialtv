Live scores, kept honest

SialTV follows every BBL|16 match from the first ball on 12 December 2026. While a match is in progress the score on this page is refreshed from verified live sources, and the time of that last refresh is printed next to the score — so a score from ten minutes ago is shown as ten minutes old, never as ball-by-ball. Between matches the page shows the latest result and the next fixture, with the points table alongside. Once a match ends, the final result — and the points table it changes — is written into the page, so the numbers here and on the BBL Results page always agree.

There is no ball-by-ball commentary here. For the score and margin of a finished match, the BBL Results page lists every match of the season, and the BBL Points Table shows where the result leaves the standings.

What comes next

Match 1 is Melbourne Renegades against Perth Scorchers on Saturday 12 December 2026 at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT, 9:10 AM UTC), at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai — the first BBL match ever played outside Australia. Match 2 follows on Tuesday 15 December: Brisbane Heat against Sydney Sixers at The Gabba.

The league stage runs to Sunday 17 January 2027, ending with Brisbane Heat against Sydney Thunder at The Gabba. The playoffs start with the Qualifier on Tuesday 19 January, then the Knockout on Wednesday 20 January, the Challenger on Saturday 23 January, and the final on Tuesday 26 January, Australia Day. The full fixture list is on the BBL Schedule page.
