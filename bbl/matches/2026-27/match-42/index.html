BBL Knockout 2026-27, Wednesday, 20 January 2027 — 3rd vs 4th, venue and time TBC

The Knockout of BBL 2026-27 is played on Wednesday, 20 January 2027, between the teams that finish third and fourth in the league stage. Neither the venue nor the start time has been confirmed yet. This is sudden-death cricket: the winner stays alive, the loser goes home.

One day after the Qualifier, the other half of the finals begins. The teams in third and fourth did enough across ten league matches to make the top four, but not enough to earn the Qualifier and its second chance. For them, there is no safety net. Win the Knockout and you play the Challenger on Saturday, one win from the final. Lose, and the season ends on a Wednesday night in January.

How the BBL finals work

The top four from the league stage play four finals matches to decide the champions.

The Knockout is the second of them: 3rd against 4th, and the winner goes to the Challenger on Saturday 23 January, where they meet the loser of the Qualifier. The loser of the Knockout is out of the tournament — there is no second chance in this match, which is what makes it the most unforgiving game of the season.

The other finals frame it. The Qualifier, on Tuesday 19 January, is 1st against 2nd, and its winner goes straight to the final on Australia Day, Tuesday 26 January. The Challenger, on Saturday 23 January, is the Qualifier loser against the Knockout winner. The Challenger winner then faces the Qualifier winner for the title.

League wins are worth two points, no-results one, and net run rate separates teams level on points. The top four qualify, and where a team finishes decides its path: the top two take the Qualifier and its safety net, while third and fourth take the Knockout and its sudden death. Finishing third rather than fourth matters too — it decides who carries the better league record into a one-off game.

What the winner takes from this match

The stakes of the Knockout are survival. The winner keeps their season alive and earns a Saturday date with the loser of the Qualifier, a team that finished above them in the league. The loser’s season ends the night it ends — no second match, no second chance, no final. For teams that spent six weeks fighting to make the top four, that is a brutal way to go out.

For the team that finished third, the Knockout is the match they were always going to have to win: the reward for third place is a home in the finals, but not a free pass. For the team that finished fourth, it is a free hit — nobody expects the fourth seed to win the title, which is exactly why the fourth seed sometimes does. The winner of this match will believe they can win two more and lift the trophy.

Frequently asked questions

When is the BBL Knockout played?

The Knockout is played on Wednesday, 20 January 2027, the day after the Qualifier. The venue and start time are still to be confirmed, and this page will be updated when the league confirms them. It is the second of four finals matches, followed by the Challenger on Saturday 23 January and the final on 26 January.

Who plays in the Knockout?

The teams that finish third and fourth in the league stage. Forty league matches decide the top four, with each club playing ten. Two points for a win, one for a no-result, with net run rate breaking ties. The top four qualify for the finals.

What happens to the Knockout loser?

They are out of the tournament. There is no second chance in the Knockout, unlike the Qualifier, where the loser drops into the Challenger. The Knockout winner goes to the Challenger on Saturday 23 January, where they meet the loser of the Qualifier for the second place in the final.

What happens to the Knockout winner?

They play the Challenger on Saturday 23 January against the loser of the Qualifier. Win the Challenger and they reach the final on Tuesday 26 January, Australia Day, against the winner of the Qualifier. The Knockout winner must win two more matches to lift the title.
