Perth Scorchers vs Hobart Hurricanes, Sunday, 20 December 2026 — Perth

The 7th match of the Big Bash League 2026-27 season is Perth Scorchers against Hobart Hurricanes on Sunday, 20 December 2026, at Perth Stadium. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC; 4:15 PM local time), and Perth Stadium hosts five matches this season.

Perth Stadium is one of the hardest away grounds in the BBL, and the Scorchers have built their record on making it one. Hobart arrive three days after their own home opener, as the 2024-25 champions, and they know what it takes to win a title — but doing it in Perth, against the defending champions, is a different test. The first ball is at 4:15 PM local time, an afternoon start that suits a Sunday crowd.

Two recent champions, one early marker

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. What the Scorchers and the Hurricanes have to work with is set out below.

Perth are the defending champions, and the most decorated side in the competition with six titles. Ashton Turner captained the BBL|15 title win, though the BBL|16 captaincy is still to be confirmed, and Adam Voges coaches. Zak Crawley is a new overseas name in the squad. Their home opener is a chance to show the title defence starts at Perth Stadium.

Hobart are the 2024-25 champions, and they defend that title with Nathan Ellis as captain and Jeff Vaughan as coach. Adam Zampa is the big new signing, joining a squad that already includes Tim David and Mitch Owen. The Hurricanes’ one title is recent, and winning away in Perth would be the clearest sign yet that they intend to win another.

Two points between two champions

Two points go to the winner, one each if the match is a no-result. The top four after ten league matches each go through to the finals.

This is the first match of the season between two sides that both expect to be in the playoffs, so it is also an early marker. A Perth win at home keeps the champions’ aura intact and puts Hobart on the back foot after their opener. A Hobart win in Perth would be the result of the opening week — proof that the 2024-25 champions can beat the current ones on their own ground.

The players who can decide it in Perth

Four to watch: every name links through to the player’s profile.

Cooper Connolly is the young Scorchers all-rounder who keeps getting better, and his rise has been one of the stories of recent BBL seasons. He bats with freedom in the middle order and bowls overs that break partnerships, and at Perth Stadium he plays with the confidence of a home player. Hobart will need a plan for him.

Jhye Richardson is the Scorchers bowler whose pace and skill at the death make him one of the most feared in the competition. When he is fit and firing, Perth’s attack has a cutting edge that few sides can match. Against a Hobart batting line-up built on power, his yorkers late in the innings could be decisive.

Mitch Owen is the Hurricanes all-rounder who announced himself in a big way last season, and his power hitting at the top of the order is now one of the first things opposition sides plan for. At Perth Stadium, where the ball comes on to the bat, his ability to clear the rope early could take the game away from the champions.

Adam Zampa is the Hurricanes’ big new signing, and his leg-spin is the weapon Hobart have added for exactly this kind of match. In the middle overs, against a Perth middle order that likes to attack, Zampa’s four overs are the spell the Scorchers must survive. His first match as a Hurricane, away in Perth, is a proper test.

Frequently asked questions

When does Perth Scorchers vs Hobart Hurricanes start?

Match 7 is played on Sunday, 20 December 2026 at Perth Stadium. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 4:15 PM local time in Perth. Perth Stadium hosts five matches this season, and this is the first of them.

Who captains each side in Match 7?

Perth’s captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed — Ashton Turner captained the BBL|15 title win — and Adam Voges coaches the defending champions. Nathan Ellis captains the Hobart Hurricanes, with Jeff Vaughan coaching. Hobart have their leadership settled; Perth will confirm theirs before the season starts.

Who are the players to watch?

Cooper Connolly and Jhye Richardson for the Scorchers, and Mitch Owen and Adam Zampa for the Hurricanes. Connolly is Perth’s rising all-rounder, Richardson leads their death bowling, Owen powers Hobart’s top order, and Zampa is their big new signing, playing his first match for Hobart.

How many points is a win worth in BBL|16?

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one point. Each side plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs, with net run rate deciding ties. Both Perth and Hobart expect to be in the playoff race, so two points here is an early marker between them.
