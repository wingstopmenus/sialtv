Sydney Thunder vs Melbourne Renegades, Sunday, 27 December 2026 — 1:45 PM IST, Sydney Showground Stadium

The 14th match of the BBL 2026-27 is Sydney Thunder against Melbourne Renegades on Sunday, 27 December 2026, at Sydney Showground Stadium, starting at 1:45 PM IST. The Showground hosts three matches this season, and this is the first of them.

The first ball goes down at 7:15 PM local time in Sydney, under lights at Thunder's home ground. It is the second Sydney match in two days after the Sixers' Boxing Day game at the SCG, and the Renegades make the trip north knowing a win on the road would set their season up nicely.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

The first ball of BBL|16 is bowled on Saturday 12 December 2026, so no form exists for this season yet. Here is the Thunder and the Renegades as announced so far.

Thunder begin a fresh chapter under new head coach Andrew Flintoff, rebuilt around pace with Trent Boult leading the attack. David Warner returns after captaining the side last season, and the captaincy for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed. The club's one title came in 2015-16, a decade ago, and the new coach has been brought in to change that.

The Renegades' one title came in 2018-19, and they arrive in Sydney with Luke Ronchi coaching and the captaincy still to be confirmed. Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff are among the new names in the squad, and both have the experience to steady a side that has blown hot and cold in recent seasons.

A fresh start, and a road win on offer

A win banks two points; a no-result banks one. Each side has ten league matches to earn a place in the top four.

For Thunder, this is the first home match of the Flintoff era, and a win would give the new coach exactly the start he needs. Home form decides most BBL seasons, and Sydney Showground Stadium hosts only three matches this season, so Thunder cannot afford to waste any of them. A win here also banks two points before the schedule moves on.

For the Renegades, an away win would be worth just as much. Melbourne sides travel well when their bowling holds up, and Behrendorff's new-ball spell against Warner and Sam Konstas is the kind of contest that can settle a match early. The finals begin with the Qualifier on 19 January, and net run rate settles ties on points.

David Warner is the Thunder batter to watch. He returns after captaining the side last season, and his experience at the top of the order is exactly what a rebuilt Thunder side needs on its first home night of the season.
