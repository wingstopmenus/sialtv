Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers, Saturday, 12 December 2026 — Chennai

The 1st match of the BBL 2026-27 season is Melbourne Renegades against Perth Scorchers on Saturday, 12 December 2026, at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai. It is the only BBL|16 match at this ground, and the first ball is at 2:40 PM IST (2:10 PM PKT, 09:10 UTC).

Chennai is the story of this fixture. A BBL season has always begun on Australian soil, so an opener in India changes the feel of the first week entirely — a new crowd, a different surface, and a defending champion side that has to win away from everything familiar. The Renegades, meanwhile, get a neutral-ground start instead of a home opener, which makes the first two points of the season a genuinely open contest.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. Here is where the Renegades and the Scorchers stand before a ball is bowled.

The Renegades arrive with a new-look squad and no confirmed captain yet for BBL|16. Luke Ronchi coaches, and the new names include Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff — experience at both ends of an innings. Their one title came in 2018-19, and the opening match is a chance to measure a rebuilt side against the champions straight away.

Perth are the defending champions, and the most decorated side in the competition with six titles. Ashton Turner captained the BBL|15 title win, though the BBL|16 captaincy is still to be confirmed, and Adam Voges coaches. Zak Crawley is a new overseas name in the squad. The Scorchers open the season as the team everyone else is chasing.

Two points, and the first marker of the season

Two points for a win, one each for a no-result. Every side plays ten league matches, and only the top four progress.

An opening win matters most for the tone it sets. For Perth, beating the Renegades in Chennai would confirm that the champions travel well and that the title defence starts where the last season ended. For the Renegades, beating the champions first up would put two points on the board and give a new-look side something real to build on before the schedule moves back to Australia.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Jake Fraser-McGurk is the batter the Renegades build their powerplay around. His game is built on taking the new ball on early, and against a Perth attack that likes to strike up front, the first six overs will go a long way to deciding this match. If he gets going in Chennai, the Renegades can post — or chase — a total that keeps Perth under pressure.

Jonny Bairstow is one of the Renegades’ new names for BBL|16, and he arrives as a wicketkeeper-batter with a long record of deciding short-format games. His job in this opener is to steady the innings if the early wickets fall, or to finish it if the top order fires. Either way, Perth will plan for him.

Mitch Marsh is the Scorchers all-rounder opposition sides plan around first. He can change a game with the bat in a handful of overs and offers a bowling option that lets Perth balance their side. In a season opener on a new ground, his experience of big BBL occasions is exactly what the champions lean on.

Aaron Hardie gives Perth the middle-order muscle and the extra overs that make their side so hard to beat. He bats with intent against spin and pace alike, and his bowling gives Adam Voges room to shuffle the attack. If the Chennai surface slows up, Hardie’s adaptability becomes even more valuable.

Frequently asked questions

When does Melbourne Renegades vs Perth Scorchers start?

Match 1 is played on Saturday, 12 December 2026 at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai. The first ball is at 2:40 PM IST, which is 2:10 PM PKT and 09:10 UTC, and 2:40 PM local time in Chennai. It is the BBL|16 season opener, and the only match of the season at this ground, so the start time is the one to set your day around.

Who captains each side in Match 1?

The Renegades’ captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, with Luke Ronchi coaching the side. Perth’s captaincy is also TBC — Ashton Turner captained the BBL|15 title win, and Adam Voges coaches the defending champions. Both sides will confirm their captains before the season starts, and this page will be updated when they do.

Who are the players to watch?

Jake Fraser-McGurk and Jonny Bairstow for the Renegades, and Mitch Marsh and Aaron Hardie for the Scorchers. Fraser-McGurk sets the tone up front, Bairstow is the new wicketkeeper-batter, Marsh is Perth’s most destructive all-rounder, and Hardie gives the champions middle-order power and extra overs.

Why is a BBL match being played in Chennai?

The season opens at MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai, with Melbourne Renegades against defending champions Perth Scorchers on 12 December 2026.
