Sydney Sixers vs Melbourne Renegades, Tuesday, 22 December 2026 — Sydney

The 9th match of the BBL 2026-27 season is Sydney Sixers against Melbourne Renegades on Tuesday, 22 December 2026, at the Sydney Cricket Ground. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC; 7:15 PM local time), and the SCG hosts four matches this season.

The SCG is one of the BBL’s great stages, and the Sixers have made it a fortress over the years. They arrive a week after their opener at the Gabba, still carrying the memory of a final lost by six wickets to Perth. The Renegades arrive ten days after opening the season in Chennai, with a new-look side and a new captain still to be confirmed — and a chance to show that the opener was only the start.

How both sides arrive at the SCG

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. Here is where the Sixers and the Renegades stand before a ball is bowled.

The Sixers were runners-up in BBL|15, beaten by six wickets in the final by Perth, and they arrive with a point to prove. Their captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and Matthew Mott coaches. With three titles behind them, the Sixers are one of the competition’s most consistent sides, and the SCG is where they are hardest to beat.

The Renegades arrive with a new-look squad and no confirmed captain yet for BBL|16. Luke Ronchi coaches, and the new names include Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff — experience at both ends of an innings. Their one title came in 2018-19, and their second match of the season comes at one of the toughest away grounds in the league.

Two points, and the final still in mind

Two points for a win, one each for a no-result. Every side plays ten league matches, and only the top four progress.

For the Sixers, a home win keeps the season on track after the long wait since the Gabba opener — and losing at the SCG is the kind of result that lingers. For the Renegades, winning away at the SCG would be a statement that the rebuild is real, not just a new squad list. Both sides know that by Christmas, the table will already have a shape, and neither wants to be near the bottom of it.

The players who can decide it at the SCG

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Josh Philippe s keeping and top-order batting give the Sixers a fast start at the SCG, where the powerplay can set up the whole innings. His ability to score quickly against the new ball — and to take the chances that come his way behind the stumps — makes him one of the first names on the Sixers team sheet, and one the Renegades must contain early.

Sean Abbott s experience of the SCG is worth plenty in a match like this. His death bowling is calm when the game tightens, and his lower-order hitting has rescued the Sixers before. Against a Renegades side still finding its shape, Abbott’s all-round reliability is the kind of edge that wins home matches.

Will Sutherland is the Renegades all-rounder whose batting and bowling both matter in a match like this. He hits the ball cleanly in the middle order and bowls the hard overs that captains rely on, and at the SCG his adaptability — to a surface that can help seamers early — gives Luke Ronchi options.

Jason Behrendorff is one of the Renegades’ new names for BBL|16, and his left-arm swing with the new ball is exactly what you want at the SCG. If he finds movement early against the Sixers’ top order, the Renegades can put the home side under pressure before the innings settles. His experience in big matches shows in how he bowls at the death.

Frequently asked questions

When does Sydney Sixers vs Melbourne Renegades start?

Match 9 is played on Tuesday, 22 December 2026 at the Sydney Cricket Ground. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 7:15 PM local time in Sydney. The SCG hosts four matches this season, and this is the first of them.

Who captains each side in Match 9?

The Sixers’ captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and Matthew Mott coaches. The Renegades’ captaincy is also TBC, with Luke Ronchi coaching. Both sides will confirm their captains before the season starts, and this page will be updated when they do.

Who are the players to watch?

Josh Philippe and Sean Abbott for the Sixers, and Will Sutherland and Jason Behrendorff for the Renegades. Philippe opens for the Sixers, Abbott is their most reliable all-rounder, Sutherland balances the Renegades side, and Behrendorff is their new left-arm swing bowler.

How do teams reach the BBL|16 final?

The top four after ten league matches each reach the playoffs. The Qualifier is first against second, with the winner going straight to the final. The Knockout is third against fourth, and the Challenger is the Qualifier loser against the Knockout winner. The final is on Australia Day, with a reserve day, and venues and times are still to be confirmed.
