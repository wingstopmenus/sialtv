Adelaide Strikers vs Sydney Thunder, Wednesday, 23 December 2026 — Adelaide

The 10th match of the BBL 2026-27 season is Adelaide Strikers against Sydney Thunder on Wednesday, 23 December 2026, at Adelaide Oval. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC; 6:45 PM local time), and Adelaide Oval hosts five matches this season.

Adelaide Oval under lights is one of Australian cricket’s great sights, and the Strikers’ first home match of the season is a proper occasion. They arrive a week into the season, while the Thunder play their third match in eight days — in Canberra, then Sydney, now Adelaide. The crowd will be there for Stokes, but the match will be decided by which side handles a demanding December schedule better.

Two big stories meet at Adelaide Oval

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. The squads below are what the Strikers and the Thunder have to work with.

Adelaide’s headline is Ben Stokes, signed after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market. Matt Short captains the side and Tim Paine coaches, and their one title came in 2017-18. Their first home match is a chance to show the Adelaide crowd what the new-look side can do — and to bank two points before Christmas.

The Thunder begin again under new head coach Andrew Flintoff, rebuilt around pace with Trent Boult leading the attack. David Warner returns after captaining the side last season, though the BBL|16 captaincy is still to be confirmed. Their one title came in 2015-16, and this is their third match in eight days, all in different cities.

Two points before Christmas

A win banks two points; a no-result banks one. The finals race runs through ten league matches per side.

For the Strikers, a home win before Christmas would send their supporters into the break believing this is a season worth following — and two points at Adelaide Oval are the kind that title challenges are built on. For the Thunder, winning a third match in eight days, away from home, would be proof that the Flintoff rebuild travels. The loser goes into Christmas with work to do.

Four players to watch at Adelaide Oval

Watch these four: a profile sits behind every name below.

Ben Stokes first home match as a Striker comes at Adelaide Oval, and the crowd will be there to see him. His all-round game — power with the bat, wickets with the ball, and a presence that lifts the whole side — is what Adelaide signed him for. Against a Thunder attack led by Trent Boult, his battle in the middle overs is the contest to watch.

Alex Carey s keeping and middle-order batting are central to how the Strikers play at Adelaide Oval. He reads the pace of the pitch early, rebuilds when wickets fall, and finishes when the platform is set. Against a Thunder side that has travelled hard all week, Carey’s calm could be the difference in a tight finish.

David Warner s third match of the season takes him to Adelaide, and his record at the top of the order speaks for itself. The Thunder lean on him to set the tone, whether they bat first or chase, and his experience of big crowds and big occasions is exactly what a young side needs on a ground like Adelaide Oval.

Trent Boult s swing with the new ball is the Thunder’s best weapon, and at Adelaide Oval, where the ball can move under lights, his opening spell is the first contest of the match. If he removes a Striker opener early — or keeps Ben Stokes quiet — the Thunder can control a match that the home crowd expects Adelaide to win.
