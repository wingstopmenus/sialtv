Perth Scorchers vs Melbourne Stars, Saturday, 26 December 2026 — 3:45 PM IST, Perth Stadium

The 13th match of the BBL is Perth Scorchers against Melbourne Stars on Saturday, 26 December 2026, at Perth Stadium, starting at 3:45 PM IST. It is the second match of Boxing Day, following the Sixers against the Heat at the SCG, and Perth Stadium hosts five matches this season.

Perth Stadium is the Scorchers' home ground, and the defending champions have made it one of the hardest places to visit in the league. The first ball goes down at 6:15 PM local time in Perth, under lights, with the Stars facing a side that won the BBL|15 title and has lifted the trophy six times.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

BBL|16 begins on Saturday 12 December 2026, so neither side has played a match in this season yet. The Scorchers and the Stars arrive with the squads set out below.

Perth are the defending champions, with six titles from 2013-14, 2014-15, 2016-17, 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2025-26 — more than any other team. Ashton Turner captained the BBL|15 title win, and the captaincy for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed. Adam Voges coaches the side, and Zak Crawley is a new overseas name in the squad.

The Stars are still chasing a first title after four final defeats, and this season they have added Finn Allen to a core built around Glenn Maxwell and captain Marcus Stoinis. Cameron White coaches the side. A win in Perth would be the loudest possible way to announce that this is the year the wait ends.

Champions against the team still waiting

Two points for a win, one each for a no-result. Ten league matches each decide which four sides reach the finals.

For Perth, the stakes are about authority. Champions who win at home on Boxing Day look like the team to beat all over again, and the Scorchers have spent a decade proving that defending a title suits them. A win here banks two points and reminds every other side that the road to the trophy still runs through Perth Stadium.

For the Stars, the stakes are about belief. Four final defeats hang over this club, and beating the champions in their own ground would change the story of their season before it has properly begun. The finals start with the Qualifier on 19 January and end with the final on Australia Day, 26 January, and net run rate settles ties on points.

The players who can decide it

Four players to keep an eye on, with a profile behind every name.

Mitch Marsh is the Scorchers all-rounder to watch. He can take a game away in a handful of overs with the bat and offers overs with the ball, and at Perth Stadium he is the kind of player who feeds off a big home crowd.

Jhye Richardson leads the Perth attack as a bowler with genuine pace. Against a Stars top order that likes to attack early, his new-ball spell could decide whether Melbourne settle or spend the innings chasing the game.

Glenn Maxwell is the Stars all-rounder who can win a match on his own. Few batters in the league change a scoreboard as quickly as he does, and Perth will have a plan for him from the moment he walks to the crease.

Finn Allen is the new Stars batter and one of the signings of the season. He joins Glenn Maxwell and captain Marcus Stoinis at the top of the order, and a fast start from him would take the pressure off the rest of the batting.
