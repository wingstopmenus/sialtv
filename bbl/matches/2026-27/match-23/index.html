Melbourne Stars vs Melbourne Renegades, Monday, 4 January 2027 — 12:35 PM IST, Melbourne Cricket Ground

Match 23 of the Big Bash League 2026-27 season is Melbourne Stars against Melbourne Renegades on Monday, 4 January 2027, at the Melbourne Cricket Ground. The first ball is at 12:35 PM IST, which is 12:05 PM PKT and 07:05 UTC, and 6:05 PM local time in Melbourne. It is the sixth BBL|16 match at this ground, the most of any venue this season.

A shared city makes this more than a league fixture. Both clubs call Melbourne home — the Stars at the MCG, the Renegades at the MCG and Junction Oval — so neither side travels, and the crowd will be split down the middle. Derbies in this league tend to be decided by which side handles the occasion rather than which side has the better record, and with the Stars still hunting a first title, the Renegades know a win here would sting twice.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

No BBL|16 form exists yet — the season begins on Saturday 12 December 2026, so both sides arrive with plans rather than results. What the Stars and the Renegades have to work with is set out below.

Marcus Stoinis captains the Stars, with Cameron White coaching. Finn Allen joins Glenn Maxwell and Stoinis in a side still chasing a first title after four final defeats, and that history hangs over every Stars season until it is answered. The talent is not in question; the question is whether this group can finally turn finals appearances into a trophy. At the MCG, in front of a split Melbourne crowd, that wait for a first title will feel heavier than usual.

The Renegades go into BBL|16 with their captaincy still to be confirmed, and Luke Ronchi coaching. Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff are among the new names, and the club's one title, from 2018-19, is the reminder that this side knows how to finish a season. A settled captain would help, but the squad has enough experience to start well without one. A derby win at the MCG would be the fastest way for the new names to feel like Renegades.

Two points, and a place in the finals race

Two points go to the winner, one each if the match is a no-result. The top four after ten league matches each go through to the finals.

By 4 January the table has a shape, and a derby win is worth more than the two points it banks. For the Stars, beating their city rivals at the MCG would be proof that this season is different from the four that ended in final defeats. For the Renegades, winning away from home in all but name would be a quiet statement that their rebuild under Ronchi is ahead of schedule.

Net run rate decides places when teams finish level on points, so the margin matters as well as the result. Neither side can afford to treat a mid-season derby as just another game: with ten league matches each and four finals places, every win in January is a step toward the Qualifier on 19 January, and every loss leaves less room to recover.

The players who can decide it

Four to watch: every name links through to the player’s profile.

Glenn Maxwell is the first name on the Stars' watch list, and a Melbourne derby at the MCG is the kind of occasion that suits him. If he finds his range early, the Renegades' plans for the middle overs change in a hurry, and the Stars' batting takes on a different shape entirely.

Marcus Stoinis leads the Stars as captain, so his own performance carries extra weight in a derby. A strong showing from the skipper steadies the side around him, and in a game where the crowd is split, the captain's calm is worth as much as his runs or wickets.

Jake Fraser-McGurk is the first Renegades name to watch, and a fast start from him would put the Stars under pressure before the middle overs. The Renegades' new names need time to settle; a quick contribution from Fraser-McGurk would buy them exactly that.

Jonny Bairstow is one of the Renegades' new names for BBL|16, and his first Melbourne derby is a useful early test of how quickly he settles into the side. If he finds his feet at the MCG, the Renegades' batting gains a proven presence at the top of the order.
