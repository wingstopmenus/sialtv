Hobart Hurricanes vs Adelaide Strikers, Saturday, 9 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST, Aurora Stadium, Launceston

Match 29 of the BBL 2026-27 season is Hobart Hurricanes against Adelaide Strikers on Saturday, 9 January 2027, at Aurora Stadium in Launceston. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 7:15 PM local time in Launceston. It is the only BBL|16 match at this ground.

Launceston gives the Hurricanes a second home, and a crowd that sees the champions only once a season tends to make noise. The Strikers travel to a ground they rarely visit, which levels the contest in a way Bellerive Oval might not. For the Hurricanes, a win here keeps their title defence on track; for the Strikers, spoiling the Launceston party would be a result worth remembering.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

There is no BBL|16 form yet — the season opens on Saturday 12 December 2026. The Hurricanes and the Strikers arrive with the squads set out below.

Nathan Ellis captains the Hurricanes, with Jeff Vaughan coaching, and Adam Zampa is the big new signing. The Hurricanes won the title in 2024-25, and that changes how opponents treat them: every side now prepares for Hobart as a game against the champions, home or away. Launceston gets one match a season, and the champions will want to give the crowd a win.

Matt Short captains the Strikers, with Tim Paine coaching, and Ben Stokes signed after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market. The Strikers' only title came in 2017-18, and Stokes gives them a player who can turn a tight game on his own. Adelaide's season will be judged on whether that signing delivers. A win at a one-off venue would be a scalp worth remembering for the Strikers.

Two points, and a place in the finals race

Two points for a win, one each for a no-result. Ten league matches each decide which four sides reach the finals.

For the Hurricanes, this is a home game in all but name, and champions do not drop games like this. A win in Launceston keeps them in the top-four race and rewards a crowd that gets one match a season. For the Strikers, winning at a one-off venue would be a scalp — and with Stokes in the side, they have the player to take it.

The league stage ends on 17 January, and the finals begin on 19 January, so the second week of January is where the table takes its final shape. Sides that win their home games and steal one on the road are the ones that reach the Qualifier, and this match is both of those things at once for the Hurricanes.

The players who can decide it

Four players to keep an eye on, with a profile behind every name.

Nathan Ellis captains the Hurricanes in Launceston, and the champions' skipper will want to give the one-off crowd a win. Ellis leads with the ball as well as the armband, and a strong night from him sets the tone for the whole side.

Tim David is the Hurricanes batter to watch, and his hitting can lift a Launceston crowd in a handful of overs. If David gets going at Aurora Stadium, the Strikers' attack faces a problem that only gets bigger as the innings goes on.

Matt Short captains the Strikers, and his job in Launceston is to keep his side calm in a ground the champions have claimed for the night. A strong performance from the skipper would show Adelaide can win anywhere, not just at home.

Ben Stokes is the Strikers' headline signing, and a big night in Launceston would be the kind of performance Adelaide signed him for. The whole league will be watching how Stokes handles a game the champions expect to win.
