Sixers vs Strikers — Match 22 Preview

Sydney Sixers vs Adelaide Strikers, Sunday, 3 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST, Manuka Oval, Canberra

The 22nd match of the BBL 2026-27 is Sydney Sixers against Adelaide Strikers on Sunday, 3 January 2027, at Manuka Oval, Canberra, starting at 1:45 PM IST. Manuka Oval hosts three matches this season, and this is a neutral ground for both sides.

The first ball goes down at 7:15 PM local time in Canberra. Manuka Oval has hosted BBL cricket before, and a neutral ground changes the shape of a match: no home crowd, no familiar dressing room, just two sides and a pitch neither knows as well as its own.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

BBL|16 starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no form from this season to read yet. Here is the Sixers and the Strikers as announced so far.

The Sixers come into BBL|16 as the runners-up of BBL|15, beaten by six wickets in the final by Perth. Three titles already sit in the cabinet from 2011-12, 2019-20 and 2020-21. Matthew Mott coaches the team, and the captaincy for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed.

Adelaide's one title came in 2017-18, and this season they have signed Ben Stokes after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market. Matt Short captains the side and Tim Paine coaches. The Strikers' home ground is Adelaide Oval, so a neutral ground in Canberra is a different kind of test for them.

A neutral ground, and no hiding place

A win banks two points; a no-result banks one. Each side has ten league matches to earn a place in the top four.

For the Sixers, a neutral ground takes away the SCG advantage and leaves only the cricket. A side that reached last season's final should be able to win anywhere, and a win in Canberra would show the Sixers are not dependent on home comforts to stay in the top four.

For the Strikers, Canberra is a chance to win away from Adelaide Oval and prove the Stokes signing travels. The league stage is past its halfway point by now, and every win matters more as the finals approach: the Qualifier is on 19 January, and the final is on Australia Day, 26 January.

The players who can decide it

The names below each link to that player’s SialTV profile.

Steve Smith is the Sixers batter to watch first. He bats at the heart of a side that reached last season's final, and on a neutral ground in Canberra he is the player the Strikers will plan their bowling around from the first over.

Ben Dwarshuis is the Sixers bowler who leads the attack with the ball. His left-arm pace suits Manuka Oval, and an early wicket or two from him would put the Strikers under pressure before they have settled.

Ben Stokes is the Strikers all-rounder and the biggest signing of the BBL|16 open market. He signed after the league scrapped its overseas draft, and a match in Canberra is another stage for him to show what he brings to Adelaide.

Matt Short is the Strikers all-rounder who captains the side and opens the batting. He holds the team together at the top of the order, and a fast start from him would take the pressure off Stokes and the middle order.
