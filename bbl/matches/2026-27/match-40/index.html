Brisbane Heat vs Sydney Thunder, Sunday, 17 January 2027 — 2:45 PM IST (2:15 PM PKT, 09:15 UTC, 7:15 PM AEST local), The Gabba

Match 40 of the Big Bash League 2026-27 season is Brisbane Heat against Sydney Thunder on Sunday, 17 January 2027, at The Gabba, Brisbane. The first ball is at 2:45 PM IST (2:15 PM PKT, 09:15 UTC, 7:15 PM AEST local time), and it is the fifth match of the season at The Gabba.

The last league match of BBL|16, and the last chance to change the table. When this match ends, the top four will be set and the finals will be known: the Qualifier on Tuesday, the Knockout on Wednesday, the Challenger on Saturday, and the final on Australia Day. The Heat, two-time champions, play at home; Thunder, rebuilt around pace under Andrew Flintoff, play for a place in the four. There is no better way to end a league stage than with everything still to play for.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

No BBL|16 form exists yet — the first ball of the season is bowled on Saturday 12 December 2026. That is what the Heat and the Thunder bring into BBL|16.

Brisbane start BBL|16 with their captaincy still to be confirmed, and James Hopes coaching the side. The squad announced so far is still being filled, and the Heat’s two titles — 2012-13 and 2023-24 — are the reminder that this club wins things when it gets to the finals. The Gabba is their fortress, and the last league match of the season is the night to defend it.

Thunder arrive in Brisbane as a side remade for this season. Andrew Flintoff is the new head coach, the attack has been rebuilt around Trent Boult’s pace, and David Warner is back after leading the side last season. The BBL|16 captaincy is still to be confirmed, and their one title — from 2015-16 — is the standard the rebuild is measured against.

Two points, and the finals race

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Ten matches each, four finals places — that is the shape of the race.

This is the fortieth and final league match, so the stakes are as plain as they get: win, and your finals place — and your finals seeding — is decided by your own result, not by anyone else’s. Lose, and you spend the night watching the table and hoping.

For the Heat, the stakes are about finishing at home. A win at The Gabba would send them into the finals with a crowd behind them and a week of momentum. For Thunder, the stakes are about the rebuild. A finals place in Flintoff’s first season, earned in the last match of the league stage, would be the proof that the new Thunder are a finals team again.

The players who can decide it

Each of the four below has a SialTV profile — the names link through.

Usman Khawaja is the Heat batter whose experience holds their top order together. As a batter who has played the biggest matches Australian cricket offers, his innings at The Gabba — calm, measured, and hard to dislodge — is the platform the Heat build their totals on.

Xavier Bartlett leads the Heat’s pace attack as a bowler, and The Gabba is a ground that rewards fast bowlers who bend their backs. His new-ball spell against Warner and the Thunder top order will be the first contest of the night, and the one that sets the tone for the last league match.

Trent Boult is the spearhead of Thunder’s rebuild under Andrew Flintoff, and as a bowler his left-arm swing with the new ball is among the best in the game. At The Gabba, under lights, his opening overs against Khawaja will be the match inside the match.

Daniel Sams is the Thunder all-rounder who can win a match in either discipline: wickets with the ball, and clean hitting down the order. In a last league match where every run and every wicket matters, Sams is the player Thunder will turn to when the game is on the line.

Frequently asked questions

When does Brisbane Heat vs Sydney Thunder start?

Match 40 is played on Sunday, 17 January 2027 at The Gabba, Brisbane. The first ball is at 2:45 PM IST, which is 2:15 PM PKT, 09:15 UTC and 7:15 PM AEST local time. It is the fifth match of the season at The Gabba, and the fortieth and final league match of BBL|16.

What happens after Match 40?

The finals begin. The Qualifier, 1st vs 2nd, is on Tuesday 19 January, and its winner goes straight to the final. The Knockout, 3rd vs 4th, is on Wednesday 20 January. On Saturday 23 January the Challenger pairs the Qualifier loser with the Knockout winner. The final is on Tuesday 26 January, Australia Day.

Who captains each side in Match 40?

Neither club has confirmed its BBL|16 captain yet. James Hopes coaches the Heat and Andrew Flintoff coaches Thunder. David Warner is back for Thunder after leading the side last season, and captains will be named before the first ball on 12 December.

Who are the players to watch in Match 40?

Usman Khawaja and Xavier Bartlett for the Heat, and Trent Boult and Daniel Sams for the Thunder. Khawaja anchors the Heat’s batting and Bartlett leads their pace attack, while Boult is the spearhead of Thunder’s rebuild and Sams is their all-rounder for the tight moments.
