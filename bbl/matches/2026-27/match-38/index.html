Melbourne Renegades vs Melbourne Stars, Saturday, 16 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC, 7:15 PM AEDT local), Melbourne Cricket Ground

Match 38 of the BBL 2026-27 season is Melbourne Renegades against Melbourne Stars on Saturday, 16 January 2027, at the Melbourne Cricket Ground. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC, 7:15 PM AEDT local time), at a ground hosting its sixth match of the season.

The Melbourne derby, under lights at the MCG, on the last Saturday of the league stage. The Renegades have one title, from 2018-19; the Stars have none, after four final defeats. The two clubs share a city, a ground and a rivalry that needs no extra reason — but this one has one anyway, because the finals race will still be live when these two walk out, and a derby win is worth two points and a year of bragging rights.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

The season has not started, so there is no form to read: the first match is on Saturday 12 December 2026. Here is the Renegades and the Stars as announced so far.

The Renegades start BBL|16 with their captaincy still to be confirmed and Luke Ronchi coaching. Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff are among the new names in the squad, and the club’s one title, from 2018-19, is the proof that they can win this league. The derby is the fixture where a new-look side can announce itself — and the Renegades know it.

The Stars are still chasing a first title after four final defeats, and they have added Finn Allen to a batting group built around Glenn Maxwell and captain Marcus Stoinis. Cameron White coaches the side. The MCG is their home, and a derby win here, in front of their own crowd, is the result that would make the rest of the league take their title challenge seriously.

Two points, and the finals race

A win banks two points; a no-result banks one. Each side has ten league matches to earn a place in the top four.

This is the second match of the final Saturday, and the table will be at its tightest: a derby win could lift either side into the top four, while a defeat could end a finals challenge in front of a full house.

For the Renegades, the stakes are about a season. Beat the Stars at the MCG and the new squad has its signature win; lose, and the questions about another wasted season start before the finals are even played. For the Stars, the stakes are about history: four finals lost, no title, and a derby that could be the night the story finally turns.

The players who can decide it

The names below each link to that player’s SialTV profile.

Will Sutherland is the Renegades all-rounder whose game suits the MCG: tall, strong, and able to clear the big boundaries. In a derby, his overs with the ball and his hitting late in the innings are the two ways he can swing a tight match the Renegades’ way.

Jason Behrendorff is one of the Renegades’ new names, and as a bowler he brings the left-arm swing that troubles right-handers under lights. Against a Stars top order built around power, his new-ball spell could decide the match before the middle overs begin.

Finn Allen is the Stars’ new overseas batter, joining Glenn Maxwell and captain Marcus Stoinis. As a batter he attacks from the first ball, and in a derby at his new home ground, a fast start from Allen would put the Renegades on the back foot before they have settled.

Sam Harper keeps wicket for the Stars and bats in the top order, so he sees the derby from both sides of the game. His keeping against the Renegades’ pace, and his batting when the Stars chase or set a total, make him one of the quiet keys to this match.

Frequently asked questions

When does Melbourne Renegades vs Melbourne Stars start?

Match 38 is played on Saturday, 16 January 2027 at the Melbourne Cricket Ground. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST — 1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC, 7:15 PM AEDT local time. Which Melbourne club has won the BBL?

The Renegades have, once, in 2018-19. The Stars have never won the BBL, despite reaching four finals. That is the heart of the Melbourne derby: one club with a title and a long wait since, and one club still chasing its first. The Stars have added Finn Allen to their batting group this season.

Who captains each side in Match 38?

The Stars are led by captain Marcus Stoinis, coached by Cameron White. The Renegades’ BBL|16 captaincy is still to be confirmed, with Luke Ronchi coaching. Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff are among the new names in the Renegades squad for this season.

Who are the players to watch in Match 38?

Will Sutherland and Jason Behrendorff for the Renegades, and Finn Allen and Sam Harper for the Stars. Sutherland and Behrendorff give the Renegades balance with bat and ball, while Allen is the Stars’ new attacking batter and Harper keeps wicket and bats in the top order.
