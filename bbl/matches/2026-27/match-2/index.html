Brisbane Heat vs Sydney Sixers, Tuesday, 15 December 2026 — Brisbane

The 2nd match of the BBL 2026-27 season is Brisbane Heat against Sydney Sixers on Tuesday, 15 December 2026, at the Gabba in Brisbane. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC; 6:15 PM local time), and the Gabba hosts five matches this season.

The Gabba under lights is one of the BBL’s familiar stages, and this fixture gives both sides an early read on where they stand. Brisbane open their home campaign here, while the Sixers arrive as last season’s runners-up, beaten by six wickets in the final by Perth. Neither side has played a BBL|16 match yet, so the points table is still blank and the winner takes the first two points of the tournament.

What each side brings into the new season

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. The squads below are what the Heat and the Sixers have to work with.

Brisbane’s captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and James Hopes coaches the side. The Heat have two titles, from 2012-13 and 2023-24, and their squad for this season has been announced so far and is still filling. A home opener at the Gabba is a good place to start putting that squad together.

The Sixers were runners-up in BBL|15, beaten by six wickets in the final by Perth, and they arrive with a point to prove. Their captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and Matthew Mott coaches. With three titles behind them, the Sixers are one of the competition’s most consistent sides, and they will expect to be in the playoff race again.

An early win, and what it buys

A win banks two points; a no-result banks one. The finals race runs through ten league matches per side.

Early points are cheap now and expensive later, which is why a win in Match 2 matters more than it looks. The Sixers know that a second straight season ending in a final defeat starts with dropped points in December; the Heat know that a home win in front of a Gabba crowd sets the tone for a season in which five matches come to Brisbane. The loser leaves with nothing and a short wait until their next match.

Four players to watch at the Gabba

Watch these four: a profile sits behind every name below.

Usman Khawaja is the Heat batter opposition attacks are built to contain. His timing and placement make him dangerous through the middle overs, and at the Gabba he knows the pace and bounce better than any visiting bowler. If he bats deep into the innings, Brisbane’s total climbs quickly.

Xavier Bartlett leads the Heat attack with the new ball, and his opening spell will set the shape of this match. Against a Sixers top order that includes Steve Smith, an early breakthrough changes the whole innings. Bartlett’s control at the death is just as important on a ground where totals can grow fast.

Steve Smith is the Sixers batter every plan starts with. His ability to find gaps and rotate the strike keeps the scoreboard moving even when boundaries dry up, and his experience in big matches shows in how he paces a chase. Brisbane will need a plan for him from the first ball he faces.

Josh Philippe keeps wicket for the Sixers and bats at the top of the order, where his fast starts can take a game away in the powerplay. If he gets going at the Gabba, the Sixers can build a total — or chase one — without ever letting the required rate climb. His glovework behind the stumps is just as sharp.
