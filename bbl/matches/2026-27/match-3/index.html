Sydney Thunder vs Adelaide Strikers, Wednesday, 16 December 2026 — Canberra

The 3rd match of the Big Bash League 2026-27 season is Sydney Thunder against Adelaide Strikers on Wednesday, 16 December 2026, at Manuka Oval in Canberra. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC; 7:15 PM local time), and Manuka Oval hosts three matches this season.

Manuka Oval is a neutral stage for both sides — neither calls it home — so this one is decided by cricket, not by a home crowd. The Thunder arrive under a new head coach in Andrew Flintoff, rebuilt around pace with Trent Boult, while the Strikers carry the season’s biggest signing in Ben Stokes. It is the kind of early fixture that tells you quickly whether a rebuild is working.

A fresh start against a headline signing

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. Below is the shape of the Thunder and the Strikers on paper.

The Thunder begin again under new head coach Andrew Flintoff, rebuilt around pace with Trent Boult leading the attack. David Warner returns after captaining the side last season, though the BBL|16 captaincy is still to be confirmed. Their one title came in 2015-16, and this season is framed as a fresh start rather than a continuation.

Adelaide’s headline is Ben Stokes, signed after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market. Matt Short captains the side and Tim Paine coaches, and their one title came in 2017-18. Stokes gives the Strikers a match-winner in both disciplines, and the question is how quickly the rest of the side settles around him.

Two points, and proof the rebuild works

Two points go to the winner, one each if the match is a no-result. Four finals places are on offer across ten league matches per side.

For the Thunder, a win would be proof that the Flintoff rebuild has substance on day one — new coach, new pace attack, same expectation to compete. For the Strikers, it would show that the Stokes signing is more than a headline, and that Matt Short’s side can win away from Adelaide Oval. Both teams play again within days, so the winner carries momentum straight into the next match.

The players who can decide it in Canberra

Four names to follow, each one linked to that player’s SialTV profile.

David Warner returns to the Thunder after captaining the side last season, and he remains the batter opposition bowlers fear most at the top of the order. His experience of setting up — or chasing down — big totals is exactly what a rebuilt side needs in its first match, and a fast start from him puts Adelaide on the back foot early.

Sam Konstas is the young Thunder batter to watch in this one. His rise has been rapid, and a BBL stage is where young batters either announce themselves or learn quickly. Batting alongside Warner gives him freedom, and if he settles early at Manuka Oval, the Thunder’s total gets a serious lift.

Matt Short captains the Strikers and is the all-rounder their balance depends on. He bats at the top, bowls useful overs, and leads a side that now includes Ben Stokes. Against a Thunder attack rebuilt around pace, Short’s ability to score quickly against the new ball sets the tone for Adelaide’s innings.

Ben Stokes is the signing of the BBL|16 season so far, joining Adelaide after the overseas draft was scrapped for an open market. He changes games with bat and ball and lifts every side he plays in. His first match in Strikers colours, against a Thunder side under a new coach, is one of the early fixtures of the season.

Frequently asked questions

When does Sydney Thunder vs Adelaide Strikers start?

Match 3 is played on Wednesday, 16 December 2026 at Manuka Oval in Canberra. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 7:15 PM local time in Canberra. Manuka Oval hosts three matches this season, and this is the first of them.

Who captains each side in Match 3?

The Thunder’s captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed — David Warner captained the side last season — and Andrew Flintoff is the new head coach. Matt Short captains the Adelaide Strikers, with Tim Paine coaching. Both captains’ situations will be confirmed before the season starts.

Who are the players to watch?

David Warner and Sam Konstas for the Thunder, and Matt Short and Ben Stokes for the Strikers. Warner returns after captaining last season, Konstas is the young batter to watch, Short leads Adelaide, and Stokes is the season’s biggest signing.

How many points is a win worth in BBL|16?

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one point. Each side plays ten league matches, and the top four reach the playoffs, with net run rate deciding ties. An opening win banks two points before the schedule tightens, which is why Match 3 matters for both sides.
