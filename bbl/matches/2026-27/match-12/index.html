Sixers vs Heat — Match 12 Preview

Boxing Day brings the Big Bash to the Sydney Cricket Ground, where the Sydney Sixers host the Brisbane Heat in Match 12 of BBL|16. The Sixers arrive as last season's beaten finalists, the Heat as the 2023-24 champions, and both sides will want their Christmas break to end with two points.

Sydney Sixers vs Brisbane Heat, Saturday, 26 December 2026 — 12:35 PM IST, Sydney Cricket Ground

The 12th match of the Big Bash League 2026-27 is Sydney Sixers against Brisbane Heat on Saturday, 26 December 2026, at the Sydney Cricket Ground, starting at 12:35 PM IST. It is the first of two matches on Boxing Day, and the SCG hosts four matches this season.

A midday start in India means an evening game in Sydney, with the first ball at 6:05 PM local time. The Sixers play at their home ground, the Sydney Cricket Ground, where they have lifted the title before, and a Boxing Day crowd gives the home side a familiar stage to open their account at the SCG this season.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

The season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form to read yet. This is what the Sixers and the Heat carry into the season.

The Sixers come into BBL|16 as the runners-up of BBL|15, beaten by six wickets in the final by Perth. That defeat still shapes this side: three titles already sit in the cabinet from 2011-12, 2019-20 and 2020-21, and the squad knows what a title run looks like. Matthew Mott coaches the team, and the captaincy for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed.

Brisbane Heat hold two titles, from 2012-13 and 2023-24, and they travel to Sydney with a settled coaching setup under James Hopes. Their captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and the squad announced so far is still being filled. The Heat have won a title more recently than most sides in the league, and they will not be short of belief at the SCG.

Two points, and a Boxing Day statement

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Ten league games each stand between the sides and the finals.

An opening win at a home ground is worth more than the two points on the table. For the Sixers, beating the Heat on Boxing Day would turn the memory of last season's final defeat into something useful: proof, in front of a home crowd, that this squad is ready to go one better. For the Heat, a win away from the Gabba would show their title-winning core still travels well.

The league stage runs to 17 January, with the Qualifier on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on Australia Day, 26 January. Net run rate decides places when teams finish level on points, so an early win with a healthy margin can matter twice over before the finals race tightens.

The players who can decide it

These are the four names that can decide it — each links through to a profile.

Steve Smith is the Sixers batter to watch first. He bats at the heart of a side that reached last season's final, and on a Boxing Day evening at his home ground he is the player the Heat will plan their bowling around from the first over.

Sean Abbott gives the Sixers balance as an all-rounder who can change a game with bat or ball. Against a Heat batting order built to attack, his overs in the middle of the innings and his hitting late on are both likely to matter.

Usman Khawaja is the Heat batter who sets the tone at the top of the order. If he sees off the new ball at the SCG, the Heat have the batting around him to post or chase a big total on Boxing Day.

Xavier Bartlett leads the Heat attack as a bowler who takes wickets with the new ball. An early breakthrough against the Sixers' top order would put the home side under pressure before the crowd has settled.

Frequently asked questions

When does Sydney Sixers vs Brisbane Heat start?

Match 12 is played on Saturday, 26 December 2026 at the Sydney Cricket Ground. It starts at 12:35 PM IST, which is 12:05 PM PKT, 07:05 UTC and 6:05 PM local time in Sydney. It is the first of two BBL matches on Boxing Day, with Perth hosting the second game later in the day.

Who captains each side in Match 12?

The captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed for both sides. The Sixers are coached by Matthew Mott and the Heat by James Hopes. The Sixers finished as runners-up in BBL|15, while the Heat won their second title in 2023-24, so both dugouts arrive with recent experience of the business end of the season.
