Melbourne Renegades vs Sydney Sixers, Friday, 1 January 2027 — 10:30 AM IST, Melbourne Cricket Ground

The 19th match of the Big Bash League is Melbourne Renegades against Sydney Sixers on Friday, 1 January 2027, at the Melbourne Cricket Ground, starting at 10:30 AM IST. The MCG hosts six matches this season, more than any other ground.

The first ball goes down at 4:00 PM local time in Melbourne, an afternoon start that suits a holiday crowd. Both Melbourne clubs call the MCG home in part, and the Renegades count this as a home game, so the Sixers will need to win in front of a crowd that is not theirs.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

The season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form to read yet. Below is the shape of the Renegades and the Sixers on paper.

Melbourne RenegadesTBCLuke Ronchi2018-19

The Renegades' one title came in 2018-19, and they arrive in the new year with Luke Ronchi coaching and the captaincy still to be confirmed. Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff are among the new names in the squad, and both bring the kind of experience that steadies a side in a big holiday match.

The Sixers come into BBL|16 as the runners-up of BBL|15, beaten by six wickets in the final by Perth. Three titles already sit in the cabinet from 2011-12, 2019-20 and 2020-21. Matthew Mott coaches the team, and the captaincy for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed.

A new year, and a home game for the Renegades

Two points go to the winner, one each if the match is a no-result. Four finals places are on offer across ten league matches per side.

For the Renegades, a win on New Year's Day at the MCG would give their season a lift it has often lacked. Melbourne's second club has blown hot and cold in recent seasons, and beating the beaten finalists in front of a holiday crowd would show the new signings are already making a difference.

For the Sixers, the stakes are about staying on track. A side that reached last season's final does not need to prove it can win; it needs to prove it can win again, away from home, in the games that decide the top four. The Qualifier is on 19 January and the final on Australia Day, 26 January, with net run rate deciding tied places.

The players who can decide it

Four names to follow, each one linked to that player’s SialTV profile.

Jake Fraser-McGurk is the Renegades batter to watch at the MCG. He attacks from the first ball he faces, and a fast start from him would give the Renegades a total the Sixers have to chase hard.

Will Sutherland is the Renegades all-rounder who offers runs and wickets in the same match. Against a Sixers side that bats deep, his overs in the middle of the innings could be the ones that break the game open.

Steve Smith is the Sixers batter to watch first. He bats at the heart of a side that reached last season's final, and on a New Year's Day afternoon at the MCG he is the player the Renegades will plan their bowling around.

Josh Philippe is the Sixers wicketkeeper who keeps wicket and opens the batting. A fast start from him would put the Renegades under pressure early, and his keeping against the Renegades' bowlers will matter just as much.
