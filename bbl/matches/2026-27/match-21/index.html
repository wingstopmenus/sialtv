Sydney Thunder vs Perth Scorchers, Saturday, 2 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST, Sydney Showground Stadium

The 21st match of the BBL is Sydney Thunder against Perth Scorchers on Saturday, 2 January 2027, at Sydney Showground Stadium, starting at 1:45 PM IST. The Showground hosts three matches this season, and this is Thunder's second home match of BBL|16.

The first ball goes down at 7:15 PM local time in Sydney, under lights at Thunder's home ground. The Scorchers travel across the country for this one, and Thunder will be glad to be home: the Showground is a small ground where the crowd sits close and the home side feeds off the noise.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

The first ball of BBL|16 is bowled on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no form from this season to judge either side on. The Thunder and the Scorchers arrive with the squads set out below.

Thunder begin a fresh chapter under new head coach Andrew Flintoff, rebuilt around pace with Trent Boult leading the attack. David Warner returns after captaining the side last season, and the captaincy for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed. The club's one title came in 2015-16, and the new coach has been brought in to change that.

Perth are the defending champions, with six titles from 2013-14, 2014-15, 2016-17, 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2025-26 — more than any other team. Ashton Turner captained the BBL|15 title win, and the captaincy for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed. Adam Voges coaches the side, and Zak Crawley is a new overseas name in the squad.

The new Thunder against the old champions

Two points for a win, one each for a no-result. Ten league matches each decide which four sides reach the finals.

For Thunder, beating the champions at home would be the win that makes the Flintoff era feel real. A new coach, a rebuilt attack and a home crowd is a good combination, but it needs a result against a side like Perth to mean anything. Two points here would put Thunder right in the top-four race.

For Perth, this is a routine away trip with a familiar purpose: win, bank two points, and keep the title defence moving. The Scorchers have won in Sydney before and they travel well, but the Showground is not a ground to take lightly. The finals begin with the Qualifier on 19 January and end with the final on Australia Day, 26 January.

The players who can decide it

Four players to keep an eye on, with a profile behind every name.

David Warner is the Thunder batter to watch. He returns after captaining the side last season, and his experience at the top of the order is exactly what a rebuilt Thunder side needs against the champions.

Daniel Sams is the Thunder all-rounder who offers runs and wickets in the same match. Against a Perth side that bats deep, his overs in the middle of the innings and his hitting late on are both likely to matter.

Mitch Marsh is the Scorchers all-rounder to watch in Sydney. He can take a game away in a handful of overs with the bat and offers overs with the ball, and on a small ground like the Showground his power is a serious threat.

Aaron Hardie is the Scorchers all-rounder who has become central to Perth's plans. He offers runs and wickets in the same match, and against a Thunder side still finding its shape, his versatility could tilt the game Perth's way.

Frequently asked questions

When does Sydney Thunder vs Perth Scorchers start?

Who captains each side in Match 21?
