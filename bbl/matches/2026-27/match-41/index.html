BBL Qualifier 2026-27, Tuesday, 19 January 2027 — 1st vs 2nd, venue and time TBC

The Qualifier of BBL 2026-27 is played on Tuesday, 19 January 2027, between the teams that finish first and second in the league stage. The venue and start time are still to be confirmed. This is the first match of the finals, and it is the most valuable match of the four: the winner goes straight to the final.

Two days after the league stage ends, the two best teams of the season meet with a place in the final on the line. The side that finished first earned the right to this match by winning more, and more consistently, than anyone else across ten league games. The side that finished second earned it the same way, by a smaller margin. There is no second chance for the winner — they will not need one — and there is a second chance for the loser, in the Challenger on Saturday.

How the BBL finals work

Four teams qualify, and the finals are played in four matches: the Qualifier, the Knockout, the Challenger and the final.

The Qualifier is the first of them: 1st against 2nd, and the winner goes straight to the final on Australia Day, Tuesday 26 January. The loser is not out — they drop into the Challenger on Saturday 23 January, where they get one more chance to reach the final.

The other three finals complete the picture. The Knockout, on Wednesday 20 January, is 3rd against 4th, and the loser is out — there is no second chance in the Knockout. The winner of the Knockout meets the loser of the Qualifier in the Challenger on Saturday 23 January. The winner of the Challenger meets the winner of the Qualifier in the final.

A win in the league stage is worth two points and a no-result is worth one, and net run rate decides places when teams are level on points. The top four qualify, and the order they finish in decides everything: first and second get the Qualifier and its second chance, third and fourth get the Knockout and its sudden death.

What the winner takes from this match

The stakes of the Qualifier are the best in the tournament. Win it, and your season is eight days long from here: rest, prepare, and walk out for the final on Australia Day. Lose it, and you must win the Challenger on Saturday just to get back to the same match. The winner of the Qualifier has the easiest road to the title; the loser has the hardest.

For the team that finished first, the Qualifier is the reward for ten league matches of consistency: a direct route to the final, and a safety net if it goes wrong. For the team that finished second, it is the chance to take the top seed’s place in the final and make the league table count for nothing. Either way, the winner of this match will be the favourite for the title — and the loser will know they have one more mountain to climb.

Frequently asked questions

When is the BBL Qualifier played?

The Qualifier is played on Tuesday, 19 January 2027, two days after the league stage ends. The venue and start time are still to be confirmed, and this page will be updated when the league confirms them. It is the first of four finals matches, followed by the Knockout, the Challenger and the final.

Who plays in the Qualifier?

The teams that finish first and second in the league stage. The league stage is forty matches, with each of the eight teams playing ten. A win is worth two points and a no-result is worth one, and net run rate decides places when teams are level. The top four qualify for the finals.

What does the Qualifier winner get?

A place in the final, on Tuesday 26 January 2027, Australia Day. The winner of the Qualifier goes straight to the final and does not play again until then. The loser is not out: they drop into the Challenger on Saturday 23 January, where they meet the winner of the Knockout for the second place in the final.

Is there a reserve day for the Qualifier?

A reserve day is available for the final. No reserve day has been confirmed for the Qualifier, the Knockout or the Challenger. If weather affects a finals match, the league will confirm the arrangements, and this page will be updated with what is confirmed.
