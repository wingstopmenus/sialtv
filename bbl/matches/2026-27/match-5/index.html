Melbourne Stars vs Adelaide Strikers, Friday, 18 December 2026 — Melbourne

The 5th match of the BBL 2026-27 season is Melbourne Stars against Adelaide Strikers on Friday, 18 December 2026, at the Melbourne Cricket Ground. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC; 7:15 PM local time), and the MCG hosts six matches this season — more than any other ground.

The MCG is the biggest stage in the competition, and it hosts more BBL|16 matches than any other ground. The Stars open their home campaign here still chasing a first title after four final defeats, while the Strikers arrive two days after their opener in Canberra. A Friday night at the MCG, under lights, is the kind of fixture the BBL is built for — and the Stars will want their home crowd to see a win, not another near-miss.

Two sides with very different histories

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. The Stars and the Strikers arrive with the squads set out below.

The Stars are still chasing a first title after four final defeats, and they begin again with Marcus Stoinis as captain and Cameron White as coach. Finn Allen joins Glenn Maxwell in a batting line-up built to score quickly, and the MCG is their home. The question, as ever for the Stars, is whether this is the season the wait finally ends.

Adelaide’s headline is Ben Stokes, signed after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market. Matt Short captains the side and Tim Paine coaches, and their one title came in 2017-18. The Strikers play their second match in three days here, so their depth — not just their stars — gets an early test.

Two points at the MCG, and a season’s shape

Two points for a win, one each for a no-result. Ten league matches each decide which four sides reach the finals.

For the Stars, every season starts with the same question — is this the year? — and a home win over a strong Strikers side would be a better answer than any preview can give. For Adelaide, winning a second match in three days, away from home, would put them at the top of the early table and show that the Stokes signing has substance behind it. The loser, meanwhile, is already chasing the pack in week one.

The players who can decide it at the MCG

Four players to keep an eye on, with a profile behind every name.

Glenn Maxwell is the Stars player who can win a match on his own, with the bat, in the field, and with the ball. At the MCG he has produced some of his best BBL innings, and against a Strikers attack that now has to plan for him and Finn Allen, his ability to find boundaries from nowhere is the Stars’ biggest weapon.

Marcus Stoinis captains the Stars and bats at the heart of their order, where his power and experience hold the innings together. He is also a bowling option who can break partnerships, and as captain his decisions at the MCG — when to attack, when to hold back — will shape how this match runs.

Alex Carey keeps wicket for the Strikers and bats in their middle order, where his calm under pressure is exactly what a side with big hitters around him needs. If Adelaide lose early wickets at the MCG, Carey’s job is to rebuild; if they start well, he is the finisher who turns a good total into a winning one.

Matt Short s second match as Strikers captain in three days comes at the MCG, and his all-round game is what keeps Adelaide balanced. He opens the batting, bowls overs that break partnerships, and leads a side still settling around Ben Stokes. Against the Stars’ big hitters, Short’s own scoring rate at the top could set the whole match up.

Frequently asked questions

When does Melbourne Stars vs Adelaide Strikers start?

Match 5 is played on Friday, 18 December 2026 at the Melbourne Cricket Ground. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 7:15 PM local time in Melbourne. The MCG hosts six matches this season, more than any other ground, and this is the first of them.

Who captains each side in Match 5?

Marcus Stoinis captains the Melbourne Stars, with Cameron White coaching. Matt Short captains the Adelaide Strikers, with Tim Paine coaching. Both sides have their leadership settled for BBL|16, which is more than several of their rivals can say ahead of the MCG match.

Who are the players to watch?

Glenn Maxwell and Marcus Stoinis for the Stars, and Alex Carey and Matt Short for the Strikers. Maxwell is the Stars’ match-winner, Stoinis leads them, Carey keeps wicket and steadies Adelaide’s middle order, and Short captains the side as an all-rounder.

How do teams reach the BBL|16 final?

The top four after ten league matches each reach the playoffs. The Qualifier is first against second, with the winner going straight to the final. The Knockout is third against fourth, and the Challenger is the Qualifier loser against the Knockout winner. The final is on Australia Day, with a reserve day, and venues and times are still to be confirmed.
