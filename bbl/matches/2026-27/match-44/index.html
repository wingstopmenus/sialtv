BBL Final 2026-27, Tuesday, 26 January 2027 — Winner of Qualifier vs Winner of Challenger, venue and time TBC

The final of Big Bash League 2026-27 is played on Tuesday, 26 January 2027, Australia Day, between the winner of the Qualifier and the winner of the Challenger. The venue and the start time are still to be confirmed. A reserve day is available for the final. This is the forty-fourth and last match of BBL|16, and the match that decides the champions.

Forty-three matches lead to this one. The Qualifier winner has been waiting since Tuesday 19 January, rested and ready, with the easiest road to the title. The Challenger winner has had the harder road: a Knockout win, or a Qualifier defeat and a Challenger win, to earn the second place in the final. On Australia Day, none of that matters. One match, one winner, one trophy — and the team that lifts it will be the champions of BBL|16.

How the BBL finals work

The top four qualify from the league stage, and the title is decided across four finals matches.

The final is the last of them: the winner of the Qualifier against the winner of the Challenger, on Australia Day, Tuesday 26 January. There is no second chance in a final. The winner takes the title; the loser finishes as runners-up.

The road to the final runs through the first three finals. The Qualifier on Tuesday 19 January pairs 1st with 2nd, and the winner goes directly to the final. The Knockout on Wednesday 20 January pairs 3rd with 4th, and the loser is eliminated. The Challenger, on Saturday 23 January, is the Qualifier loser against the Knockout winner, and its winner takes the second place in the final.

In the league stage a win earns two points and a no-result one, with net run rate deciding tied places. The top four qualify, and the league order sets the finals path: the top two get the Qualifier and a safety net, the next two get the Knockout and no second chance. The final is the only match of the season where the league table counts for nothing — the two teams start level, and the trophy goes to the team that wins on the day.

What the winner takes from this match

The stakes of the final are the title itself. The winner is the champion of BBL|16, the sixteenth champions in the history of the Big Bash League. The loser is the runner-up, a place no team wants to finish after six weeks of cricket. Perth Scorchers hold the record with six titles and are the defending champions; Melbourne Stars are still chasing a first title after four final defeats. Whoever wins on Australia Day will have earned it across forty-four matches.

For the Qualifier winner, the final is the match they have been building towards since Tuesday: a week to prepare, a week to rest, and one match to win. For the Challenger winner, it is the end of the hardest road in the tournament — two sudden-death wins, or a Qualifier defeat survived, to reach the same match. A reserve day is available for the final, so weather will not decide the champions if it can be avoided.

Frequently asked questions

When is the BBL final played?

The final is played on Tuesday, 26 January 2027, Australia Day. The venue and start time are still to be confirmed, and this page will be updated when the league confirms them. A reserve day is available for the final, so the champions will be decided on the field if the weather allows.

Who plays in the BBL final?

The winner of the Qualifier and the winner of the Challenger. The Qualifier, on Tuesday 19 January, is 1st against 2nd, and its winner goes straight to the final. The Challenger, on Saturday 23 January, is the Qualifier loser against the Knockout winner, and its winner takes the second place in the final.

Is there a reserve day for the final?

Yes. A reserve day is available for the final, the only finals match with one confirmed. The venue and start time are still to be confirmed. If weather affects the final, the league will confirm the arrangements, and this page will be updated with what is confirmed.

Who are the defending champions?

Perth Scorchers, with six BBL titles — the most of any team. They won BBL|15, and their title years are 2013-14, 2014-15, 2016-17, 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2025-26. Melbourne Stars are the only team yet to win a title, after four final defeats, and Hobart Hurricanes are the 2024-25 champions.
