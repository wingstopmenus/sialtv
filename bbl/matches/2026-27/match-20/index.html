Brisbane Heat vs Hobart Hurricanes, Friday, 1 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST, The Gabba, Brisbane

The 20th match of the Big Bash League 2026-27 is Brisbane Heat against Hobart Hurricanes on Friday, 1 January 2027, at The Gabba, Brisbane, starting at 1:45 PM IST. The Gabba hosts five matches this season, and this is the Heat's second home match of BBL|16.

The first ball goes down at 6:15 PM local time in Brisbane. It is the halfway point of the league stage, and both sides will know by now whether their season is on track. The Hurricanes travel north as champions; the Heat will be glad to be home, where the Gabba crowd expects a win.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

BBL|16 opens on Saturday 12 December 2026, so no form exists for this season yet. This is what the Heat and the Hurricanes carry into the season.

The Heat hold two titles, from 2012-13 and 2023-24, and they start the new year at home with James Hopes coaching. The captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and the squad announced so far is still being filled. A win against the champions would be the perfect way to open 2027.

Hobart are the champions of 2024-25, with Nathan Ellis captaining and Jeff Vaughan coaching. Adam Zampa is the big new signing, joining a squad that already knows how to win a title. The Hurricanes' title is the freshest in the league, and they travel to Brisbane expecting to defend it properly.

Two champions, one New Year's Day night

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Ten league games each stand between the sides and the finals.

For the Heat, beating the champions at the Gabba would be a marker. Two titles each, a home crowd behind them, and a new year to start well — a win here would put Brisbane in the top four and remind the league that the 2023-24 champions have not gone anywhere.

For Hobart, this is what a title defence looks like on the road. Champions are judged by how they handle away games against other champions, and a win in Brisbane would show the 2024-25 title was the start of something. The finals run from the Qualifier on 19 January to the final on Australia Day, 26 January.

The players who can decide it

These are the four names that can decide it — each links through to a profile.

Usman Khawaja is the Heat batter who sets the tone at the top of the order. If he sees off the new ball at the Gabba, the Heat have the batting around him to post or chase a big total on New Year's Day.

Xavier Bartlett is the Heat bowler who takes wickets with the new ball. An early breakthrough against the Hurricanes' top order would put the champions under pressure before the Gabba crowd has settled.

Adam Zampa is the Hurricanes bowler and their big new signing. His leg-spin in the middle overs is the spell the Heat must survive, and on a Gabba surface his four overs could decide the match.

Mitch Owen is the Hurricanes all-rounder who offers runs and wickets in the same match. He was part of the side that won the 2024-25 title, and his versatility gives Hobart options with bat and ball in Brisbane.
