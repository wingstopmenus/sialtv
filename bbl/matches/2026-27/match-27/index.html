Melbourne Stars vs Sydney Sixers, Thursday, 7 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST, Melbourne Cricket Ground

Match 27 of the Big Bash League 2026-27 season is Melbourne Stars against Sydney Sixers on Thursday, 7 January 2027, at the Melbourne Cricket Ground. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 7:15 PM local time in Melbourne. It is the sixth BBL|16 match at this ground.

The Sixers are the standard the Stars keep falling short of. Sydney's three titles came in 2011-12, 2019-20 and 2020-21, and they reached the BBL|15 final, beaten by six wickets by Perth. The Stars' four final defeats are the other side of that record: a club that keeps reaching the end and keeps falling short. Games like this one decide whether the story changes.

Marcus Stoinis captains the Stars, with Cameron White coaching. Finn Allen joins Glenn Maxwell and Stoinis, and the side is still chasing a first title after four final defeats. The Stars do not lack talent or big names; they lack the one result that would change how their history reads. The MCG is where the Stars' title hunt will be judged most harshly.

The Sixers were runners-up in BBL|15, beaten by six wickets in the final by Perth, and their captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, with Matthew Mott coaching. Three titles and a recent final appearance make them one of the league's benchmark sides, and they will expect to be in the top four again. A win in Melbourne would confirm the Sixers are still the side the Stars have to get past.

Two points, and a place in the finals race

Two points go to the winner, one each if the match is a no-result. Four finals places are on offer across ten league matches per side.

For the Stars, this is the fixture that measures them. Beating the Sixers at the MCG would be proof that the four final defeats are history, not a pattern. For the Sixers, a win in Melbourne would confirm that last season's final run was no one-off, and that they remain the side the Stars have to get past.

January is when the table starts to punish dropped home games. The Stars play at the MCG more than anywhere else this season, with six matches at the ground, so their home record will decide their season. Losing this one would leave them chasing the top four; winning it would put them firmly in the race.

The players who can decide it

Four names to follow, each one linked to that player’s SialTV profile.

Glenn Maxwell is the Stars batter the Sixers will plan around, and the MCG is his home ground. If Maxwell fires against a side with three titles, the Stars' title hunt gains its loudest statement of the season so far.

Marcus Stoinis captains the Stars against one of the league's most successful clubs, and the skipper's own performance matters most in games like this. A strong night from Stoinis would steady the Stars and show the side believes its wait for a first title is ending.

Steve Smith is the first Sixers name to watch, and his presence is a reminder of why Sydney keep reaching finals. If Smith bats deep at the MCG, the Stars face a long evening and a total that is hard to chase.

Josh Philippe is the second Sixers name to watch, and his contribution at the top of the order can set the game up for Sydney. A fast start from Philippe would put the Stars on the back foot in front of their own crowd.
