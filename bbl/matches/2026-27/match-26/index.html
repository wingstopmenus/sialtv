Adelaide Strikers vs Melbourne Renegades, Wednesday, 6 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST, Adelaide Oval

Match 26 of the BBL 2026-27 season is Adelaide Strikers against Melbourne Renegades on Wednesday, 6 January 2027, at Adelaide Oval. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 6:45 PM local time in Adelaide. It is the fifth BBL|16 match at this ground.

Adelaide Oval is one of the great grounds in the league, and the Strikers have made it a hard place to visit. The Renegades travel with their captaincy still undecided and new names to bed in, so this is a test of how quickly they can settle on the road. For the Strikers, a home game against a side still finding its shape is a chance they should take.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

The season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. The squads below are what the Strikers and the Renegades have to work with.

Matt Short captains the Strikers, with Tim Paine coaching, and Ben Stokes is the signing that changes how people talk about this side. Stokes joined after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market, and his arrival gives Adelaide a proven match-winner in a league where one player can swing a season. The Strikers won their only title in 2017-18. Adelaide Oval, with Stokes in the side, is where the Strikers' season should begin in earnest.

The Renegades' captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and Luke Ronchi coaches. Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff are among the new names, and the club's 2018-19 title is the proof that this side can finish a season strongly. The question is whether the new names settle quickly enough to make this season one of those years. An away game at Adelaide Oval asks the Renegades' new names to settle quickly.

Two points, and a place in the finals race

A win banks two points; a no-result banks one. The finals race runs through ten league matches per side.

For the Strikers, this is a home game to win and a stage for their big signing. A win with Stokes in the side would tell the rest of the league that Adelaide are serious this season, not just interesting. For the Renegades, winning away at Adelaide Oval with an undecided captaincy would be a result that steadies the whole campaign.

With ten league matches each and the finals starting on 19 January, the middle of the season is where places are won and lost. A home win here keeps the Strikers in the top-four race; a Renegades win on the road would be worth more than two points, because it would show they can win anywhere while they are still settling.

The players who can decide it

Watch these four: a profile sits behind every name below.

Matt Short captains the Strikers, and his leadership matters even more in a side with a new superstar. Short sets the tone at Adelaide Oval, and a strong performance from the skipper would show the Strikers are a team first and a headline second.

Ben Stokes is the Strikers' headline signing, joining after the overseas draft was scrapped for an open market. His first matches in Adelaide colours will be watched across the league, and a strong start would confirm that the Strikers' gamble was the signing of the season.

Jake Fraser-McGurk is the Renegades batter to watch in Adelaide, and the visitors need a fast start from him to quieten the home crowd. If he gets going early, the Renegades can put the Strikers under pressure before their attack settles.

Jonny Bairstow is one of the Renegades' new names for BBL|16, and Adelaide Oval is a proper stage for him to show what he adds. A good innings here would settle him into the side and give the Renegades a reliable presence in their batting.
