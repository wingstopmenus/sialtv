Melbourne Renegades vs Hobart Hurricanes, Thursday, 24 December 2026 — Melbourne

The 11th match of the Big Bash League 2026-27 season is Melbourne Renegades against Hobart Hurricanes on Thursday, 24 December 2026, at Junction Oval in Melbourne. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC; 7:15 PM local time), and Junction Oval hosts three matches this season.

A Christmas Eve fixture is a rare thing in the BBL, and this one closes the first stretch of the season before the break. The Renegades play their second home match in four days, while the Hurricanes arrive a week after their own home opener in Hobart — and four days after their trip to Perth. Both sides will want to go into Christmas with two points, not with a defeat to think about over the break.

How both sides arrive on Christmas Eve

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. Below is the shape of the Renegades and the Hurricanes on paper.

The Renegades arrive with a new-look squad and no confirmed captain yet for BBL|16. Luke Ronchi coaches, and the new names include Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff — experience at both ends of an innings. Their one title came in 2018-19, and this is their third match of the season, all of them against sides that expect to be in the playoff race.

Hobart are the 2024-25 champions, and they defend that title with Nathan Ellis as captain and Jeff Vaughan as coach. Adam Zampa is the big new signing, joining a squad that already includes Tim David and Mitch Owen. The Hurricanes’ one title is recent, and their Christmas Eve trip to Melbourne is a test of whether they can win away from Bellerive Oval.

Two points before the Christmas break

Two points go to the winner, one each if the match is a no-result. Four finals places are on offer across ten league matches per side.

Two points before Christmas are worth more than two points in January, because they set the shape of the table when play resumes on Boxing Day. For the Renegades, a home win would cap a first fortnight that began with the champions in Chennai. For the Hurricanes, an away win would show that the title defence travels — and that Bellerive Oval is not the only place they can win.

The players who can decide it on Christmas Eve

Four names to follow, each one linked to that player’s SialTV profile.

Jake Fraser-McGurk s second match of the season comes at home, and his powerplay hitting is the Renegades’ fastest route to a big total. At Junction Oval, where the boundaries are reachable, his ability to clear the rope early puts the Hurricanes under pressure from the first over. If he fires on Christmas Eve, the crowd will have plenty to cheer.

Will Sutherland s all-round game gives the Renegades balance at Junction Oval, where the smaller ground rewards batters who hit cleanly and bowlers who vary their pace. He bats in the middle order, bowls the hard overs, and fields sharply — and in a match played the night before Christmas, his energy could set the tone for the home side.

Tim David s power hitting is built for a ground like Junction Oval, where the ball travels and the boundaries are within reach. His job for Hobart is to finish innings with a burst that turns good totals into winning ones, and against a Renegades attack still finding its shape, his overs at the crease could decide the match late.

Mitch Owen s power at the top of the order is the Hurricanes’ early weapon, and at Junction Oval his ability to hit the ball long and straight is a serious threat. If he gets going in the powerplay, Hobart can build a total that puts the Renegades under pressure from the start of the chase — or set one that is hard to reach.

Frequently asked questions

When does Melbourne Renegades vs Hobart Hurricanes start?

Match 11 is played on Thursday, 24 December 2026 at Junction Oval in Melbourne. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 7:15 PM local time in Melbourne. Junction Oval hosts three matches this season, and this is the second of them.

Who captains each side in Match 11?

The Renegades’ captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, with Luke Ronchi coaching the side. Nathan Ellis captains the Hobart Hurricanes, with Jeff Vaughan coaching. Hobart have their leadership settled; the Renegades will confirm theirs before the season starts.

Who are the players to watch?

Jake Fraser-McGurk and Will Sutherland for the Renegades, and Tim David and Mitch Owen for the Hurricanes. Fraser-McGurk sets the tone up front, Sutherland balances the side, David finishes innings with power, and Owen powers Hobart’s top order on Christmas Eve.

What comes after BBL Match 11?

The season resumes on Boxing Day, Saturday 26 December, with two matches: Sydney Sixers against Brisbane Heat at the SCG at 12:35 PM IST, and Perth Scorchers against Melbourne Stars at Perth Stadium at 3:45 PM IST. Match 11 is the last game before Christmas, and the Boxing Day double-header opens the next stretch of the season.
