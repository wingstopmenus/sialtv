Melbourne Renegades vs Brisbane Heat, Sunday, 10 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST, Junction Oval, Melbourne

Match 31 of the Big Bash League 2026-27 season is Melbourne Renegades against Brisbane Heat on Sunday, 10 January 2027, at Junction Oval in Melbourne. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 7:15 PM local time in Melbourne. It is the third BBL|16 match at this ground.

Junction Oval is the Renegades' second home alongside the MCG, and it gives them a smaller, tighter ground to defend. The Heat travel after two games against the champions in the same week, so they arrive battle-tested but tired, and the Renegades will want to take advantage. A Sunday evening game in Melbourne is a good place to do it.

The Renegades' captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and Luke Ronchi coaches. Jonny Bairstow and Jason Behrendorff are among the new names, and the club's 2018-19 title is the reminder that this side knows how to win a season. An undecided captaincy is a question, not a verdict, and the squad has time to answer it. Junction Oval is the Renegades' smaller home, and a good place to bank two points.

Brisbane Heat go into BBL|16 with their captaincy still to be confirmed, and James Hopes coaching. The squad announced so far is still filling, and their two titles, from 2012-13 and 2023-24, show the club can build a winning side. The Heat's week has been tough — two games against the champions — and this is where that schedule bites. After two games against the champions in a week, the Heat's trip to Melbourne is a test of resilience.

Two points, and a place in the finals race

Two points go to the winner, one each if the match is a no-result. The top four after ten league matches each go through to the finals.

For the Renegades, this is a home game to win. Junction Oval should be a place visiting sides struggle, and with the finals starting on 19 January, dropped home games are the ones a side regrets in the final week. For the Heat, a win on the road after a hard week would be proof of real resilience — and two points that keep them in the race.

The league stage ends on 17 January, so by 10 January the table has little room left in it. Net run rate decides places when teams finish level on points, which means the margin matters as well as the result. Both sides know a win here is worth more than it looks on paper, because there are so few games left to make it up.

The players who can decide it

Four to watch: every name links through to the player’s profile.

Jake Fraser-McGurk is the first Renegades name to watch at Junction Oval, and the home crowd will expect a fast start from him. If he gets going early, the Heat's bowlers face a long evening at a ground where boundaries come quickly.

Jonny Bairstow is one of the Renegades' new names for BBL|16, and a home game at Junction Oval is a good place for him to settle further into the side. A strong innings from Bairstow would give the Renegades the platform their middle order needs.

Usman Khawaja is the first Heat name to watch, and the visitors need him to anchor the innings after a demanding week. If Khawaja bats deep at Junction Oval, the Heat can set or chase a total with calm rather than hurry.

Xavier Bartlett is the second Heat name to watch, and his bowling will be tested at a smaller ground. Wickets from Bartlett early would slow the Renegades down and give the Heat the control they need on the road.
