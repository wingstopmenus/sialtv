Melbourne Stars vs Brisbane Heat, Monday, 21 December 2026 — Melbourne

The 8th match of the Big Bash League 2026-27 season is Melbourne Stars against Brisbane Heat on Monday, 21 December 2026, at Junction Oval in Melbourne. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST (1:15 PM PKT, 08:15 UTC; 7:15 PM local time), and Junction Oval hosts three matches this season.

Junction Oval is the Stars’ second home in Melbourne, a smaller ground where the ball travels and totals climb. The Stars arrive three days after their MCG opener against Adelaide, while the Heat play their third match in six days — at the Gabba, then in Hobart, now in Melbourne. That schedule is a test of Brisbane’s depth, and a chance for the Stars to catch a travelling side at the right time.

What each side brings to Junction Oval

Pre-season — the season starts on Saturday 12 December 2026, so there is no BBL|16 form yet. That is what the Stars and the Heat bring into BBL|16.

The Stars are still chasing a first title after four final defeats, and they begin again with Marcus Stoinis as captain and Cameron White as coach. Finn Allen joins Glenn Maxwell in a batting line-up built to score quickly, and the MCG is their main home, with Junction Oval hosting three matches. The question, as ever for the Stars, is whether this is the season the wait finally ends.

Brisbane’s captain for BBL|16 is still to be confirmed, and James Hopes coaches the side. The Heat have two titles, from 2012-13 and 2023-24, and their squad for this season has been announced so far and is still filling. Three matches in six days, all in different cities, is a demanding start — and a quick way to find out how deep the squad really is.

Two points, and a schedule that bites

A win is worth two points, and a no-result is worth one. Ten matches each, four finals places — that is the shape of the race.

For the Stars, this is a home match they will expect to win — and sides chasing a first title cannot afford to drop home matches in week one. For the Heat, a win on the road, in their third match in six days, would be a sign of a squad with real depth, while a defeat would leave them with plenty to fix before their next home match. The table is still young, but the gap between two points and none already matters.

Four players to watch at Junction Oval

Each of the four below has a SialTV profile — the names link through.

Finn Allen joins the Stars for BBL|16, and his arrival gives Glenn Maxwell a partner who scores just as quickly at the top of the order. Allen’s powerplay hitting can take a match away in the first six overs, and at Junction Oval, where the boundaries are reachable, his range is a serious threat. Brisbane’s new-ball bowlers will have planned for him.

Sam Harper keeps wicket for the Stars and bats in their middle order, where his clean striking and quick running keep the scoreboard moving. He is the kind of player whose value shows in the margins — a stumping here, a quick single there — and in a tight match at Junction Oval, those margins decide it.

Usman Khawaja s third match in a week takes him to Melbourne, and his class at the top of the order is what Brisbane lean on when the schedule bites. His timing is undiminished by travel, and against a Stars attack built around pace, his ability to find gaps early could give the Heat the platform their middle order needs.

Spencer Johnson s pace is a weapon the Heat will lean on at Junction Oval, where the ball comes on and batters like to hit through the line. His short balls test even the best pullers, and against a Stars batting line-up that includes Glenn Maxwell and Finn Allen, his overs could be the difference between a total the Heat can chase and one they cannot.
