Adelaide Strikers vs Hobart Hurricanes, Tuesday, 12 January 2027 — 1:45 PM IST, Adelaide Oval

Match 33 of the BBL 2026-27 season is Adelaide Strikers against Hobart Hurricanes on Tuesday, 12 January 2027, at Adelaide Oval. The first ball is at 1:45 PM IST, which is 1:15 PM PKT and 08:15 UTC, and 6:45 PM local time in Adelaide. It is the fifth BBL|16 match at this ground.

Adelaide Oval in January is a fine place to watch cricket, and the Strikers will want to make it count. The Hurricanes arrive as the 2024-25 champions, a side that knows how to win a season, and the Strikers arrive with the signing the whole league is watching. One title each, five days of the league stage left after this game, and a finals place on the line — this is the kind of fixture January is made for.

How both sides arrive in BBL|16

No BBL|16 form exists yet — the season begins on Saturday 12 December 2026. Here is where the Strikers and the Hurricanes stand before a ball is bowled.

Matt Short captains the Strikers, with Tim Paine coaching, and Ben Stokes signed after the BBL scrapped its overseas draft for an open market. The Strikers' only title came in 2017-18, and Stokes is the player they signed to change that record. Adelaide's season will be judged on nights like this one, at home, against the champions. Adelaide Oval, against the champions, is the game the Strikers' season will be judged on.

Nathan Ellis captains the Hurricanes, with Jeff Vaughan coaching, and Adam Zampa is the big new signing. The Hurricanes are the champions from 2024-25, and defending a title means winning games like this one, away from home, in the final week of the league stage. Ellis leads a side that knows what January cricket demands. Winning away in Adelaide this late in the season would confirm the title defence is serious.

Two points, and a place in the finals race

Two points for a win, one each for a no-result. Every side plays ten league matches, and only the top four progress.

For the Strikers, this is a home game with finals implications. Beating the champions at Adelaide Oval would put them in the top-four race with five days left, and it would show that the Stokes signing has changed the side. For the Hurricanes, winning away from home this late in the season would confirm that their title defence is serious, not just a memory of 2024-25.

The Qualifier is on 19 January, the Knockout on 20 January, the Challenger on 23 January and the final on 26 January, so the team that wins here is one step closer to those dates. With ten league matches each and net run rate deciding tied places, the margin matters too: a big win could be the difference between a finals place and a season that ends on 17 January.

The players who can decide it

The four names that matter most, each with a profile to open.

Matt Short captains the Strikers at home against the champions, and his leadership is the foundation Adelaide build on. A strong performance from the skipper would show the Strikers are a team first, even with a superstar in their ranks.

Ben Stokes is the Strikers' headline signing, and a big night against the champions at Adelaide Oval would be the performance Adelaide signed him for. The whole league will be watching whether Stokes delivers when a finals place is on the line.

Nathan Ellis captains the Hurricanes, and his job in Adelaide is to lead a title defence on the road. A strong night from Ellis with the ball would set the standard for the champions and show they can win anywhere in January.

Tim David is the Hurricanes batter Adelaide will watch closest, because his hitting can change a game in a handful of overs. If David gets going at Adelaide Oval, the Strikers' attack faces exactly the kind of problem that decides finals places.
