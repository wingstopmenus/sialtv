The new eighth franchise

Rawalpindiz are new in every sense that matters: a new name, a new city, and a first season in PSL 12. Rawalpindi has waited a long time for its own PSL team, and in 2027 it gets one — the eighth franchise, taking the league from six teams to eight.

The story of how they came about is a swap, not a simple launch. The Sialkot franchise, picked as the eighth team in January 2026 when OZ Developers bought it for Rs 1.85bn, was sold to CD Ventures, which revived the Multan Sultans name for it. The old Multan Sultans franchise went the other way: Walee Technologies bought it, moved it to Rawalpindi and renamed it Rawalpindiz. Walee Technologies also holds the PSL media rights until 2029.

Rizwan is the captain’s name listed for the franchise. Whether he leads the side into its first match is for the franchise to confirm — the 2027 captain, like the rest of the squad, is settled by the retentions and the auction on 23 January 2027.

Fixtures — the full list is not published yet

The PCB has not published the full PSL 12 fixture list, so Rawalpindiz’s ten league opponents and dates are not known yet. What is known is the shape of the season: 18 March to 2 May 2027, 44 matches in all, at five to six venues across Pakistan.

Each team plays ten league matches — every opponent twice — and the top four reach the playoffs. The final is on Sunday 2 May 2027 at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore. Once the PCB publishes the fixtures, this page will list Rawalpindiz’s matches in order, including their first-ever PSL match.

A first season, and a city behind them

Rawalpindiz’s strength is the one thing money cannot buy at an auction: a home. Rawalpindi is a cricket city with its own ground and its own crowds, and a new team that plays as the city’s team starts with support the other franchises took years to build. The Kingsmen showed in 2026 that a new franchise can reach a final in its first season — Rawalpindiz will have watched that closely.

What to watch is the auction. A new franchise builds its whole squad in one go, and the choices made on 23 January 2027 decide not just the first season but the identity of the team. The captain is the first of those choices: Rizwan’s name is listed for the franchise, and confirming him early would give the new side a leader, a wicketkeeper and a local hero in one.

The other watch point is the name itself. Rawalpindiz is a new brand in a league of established ones, and the first season is when a nickname either sticks or it does not. Wins will do more for that than any launch event.

The table and the top four

The points table is empty — PSL 12 starts on Thursday 18 March 2027, and the first standings appear after Match 1. Eight teams play ten league matches each, and the top four go through to the playoffs. With eight teams instead of six, the middle of the table is tighter: a side can win half its games and still miss out, so dropped points against the bottom teams hurt more than they used to.

For Rawalpindiz, the maths is the same as for everyone else, but the expectation is different. A new franchise that reaches the playoffs in its first season has done something real — Hyderabad Kingsmen showed the way in 2026 by going one better and reaching the final. Fourth place is the target; anything above it is a statement. SialTV will update this page after every match day, through the final on 2 May.
