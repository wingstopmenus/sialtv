How the eighth team came about

The eighth franchise began as a Sialkot team: OZ Developers picked it up for Rs 1.85bn when it was chosen as the eighth team in January 2026. That franchise was then sold to CD Ventures, which revived the Multan Sultans name for it.

The old Multan Sultans franchise went the other way. Walee Technologies bought it, moved it to Rawalpindi and renamed it Rawalpindiz — so Rawalpindi has its own team for PSL 12, and the Multan Sultans name carries on under new owners. Walee Technologies also holds the PSL media rights until 2029.

Defending champions, and what comes next

Peshawar Zalmi are the defending champions, after beating Hyderabad Kingsmen in the PSL 2026 final — so the Kingsmen start PSL 12 chasing the team that beat them. Every team plays 10 league matches, each opponent twice, and the top four reach the playoffs. The full fixture list is on the PSL Schedule page, and the standings, once play starts, are on the PSL Points Table page.

Squads take shape at the player auction on Saturday 23 January 2027, after the retentions window ends on 13 January. SialTV will list the 2027 captains and squads here once the PCB and the franchises announce them.

Frequently asked questions

How many teams play in the PSL in 2027?

Eight teams: Islamabad United, Karachi Kings, Lahore Qalandars, Peshawar Zalmi, Quetta Gladiators, Multan Sultans, Hyderabad Kingsmen and Rawalpindiz. Each team plays 10 league matches.

Who are the defending PSL champions?

Peshawar Zalmi won PSL 2026, beating Hyderabad Kingsmen in the final, so they defend the title in PSL 12.

Who captains each team in PSL 2027?

The 2027 captains have not been announced by the PCB. The last verified captains are from 2026: Babar Azam for Peshawar Zalmi, Marnus Labuschagne for Hyderabad Kingsmen, Shadab Khan for Islamabad United, Ashton Turner for Multan Sultans, Shaheen Shah Afridi for Lahore Qalandars and Rizwan for Rawalpindiz.
