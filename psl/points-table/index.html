How the points table works

A win is worth 2 points, a no result is worth 1 point, and a loss is worth nothing. Each of the eight teams plays 10 league matches — for 40 league matches in all. The top four teams at the end of the league stage qualify for the playoffs.

When teams finish level on points, net run rate decides the order. Net run rate is the average runs per over a team scores, minus the average runs per over it concedes, across its matches. A team that wins big and loses narrowly carries a better rate than a team with the same points from close games.

The playoffs are four matches, ending with the final at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore on Sunday 2 May 2027. The exact playoff dates and venues are part of the fixture list the PCB has not published yet.

When the first table appears

The first points go on the board after Match 1, on Thursday 18 March 2027. The winner takes 2 points and the top of the table; if rain washes the match out, both teams take 1 point each. The two teams in the opener are not known yet — the full fixture list has not been published by the PCB.

After that the table moves every match day, and SialTV updates it after every result. The full fixture list is on the PSL Schedule page, and every result is on the PSL Results page.

Frequently asked questions

When will the PSL 2027 points table appear?

After the first match, on Thursday 18 March 2027. Until then every team is on zero points and the table is empty.

How many points is a win worth in the PSL?

A win is worth 2 points and a no result is worth 1 point. A loss is worth nothing.

How many teams qualify for the PSL playoffs?

Four. The top four teams on the points table after the 40 league matches qualify for the four playoff matches, ending with the final on 2 May 2027 in Lahore.

What is net run rate?

Net run rate is a team’s average runs per over scored, minus its average runs per over conceded. It decides the order when teams finish level on points.

When does the PSL league stage end?

The exact date depends on the fixture list, which the PCB has not published yet. The tournament runs for 46 days, from 18 March to the final on 2 May 2027.
