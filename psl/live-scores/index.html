Live scores, kept honest

SialTV follows every PSL 12 match from the five to six venues across Pakistan. While a match is in progress, the score on this page is refreshed from verified live sources, and the time of that last refresh is printed next to the score — so a score from ten minutes ago is shown as ten minutes old, never as ball-by-ball. Between matches the page shows the latest result and the next fixture, with the points table alongside.

There is no live feed yet because the season has not started. Once a match ends, the final result — and the points table it changes — is written into the page, so the numbers here and on the PSL Results page always agree. For the key dates, the PSL Schedule page has what is confirmed so far.

What comes next

The player auction is on Saturday 23 January 2027, then the season opens on Thursday 18 March 2027. Forty-four matches are scheduled — 40 in the league stage and four playoffs — ending with the final at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore on Sunday 2 May 2027.

The two teams in the opener are not known yet, because the full fixture list has not been published by the PCB. Once it is out, this page will show the first match in full: the teams, the venue and the start time.
