PSL 2027 key dates — the season in brief

HBL PSL 12 runs for 46 days, from Thursday 18 March to Sunday 2 May 2027. Eight teams play 44 matches — 40 in the league stage and four playoffs — at five to six venues across Pakistan. The final is at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore.

The shape of the season

Forty league matches decide the standings: each of the eight teams plays 10 league matches, and the top four qualify for the playoffs. Four playoff matches follow, ending with the final at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore on Sunday 2 May 2027.

The exact playoff dates and venues are part of the fixture list the PCB has not published yet — only the final, on 2 May in Lahore, is confirmed. The standings, once play starts, are on the PSL Points Table page, and every result is on the PSL Results page.
