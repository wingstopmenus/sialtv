Frequently asked questions

When does PSL 2027 start?

HBL PSL 12 starts on Thursday 18 March 2027 and ends with the final on Sunday 2 May 2027 at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore.

How many teams play in PSL 2027?

Eight teams — the six founding franchises, Hyderabad Kingsmen and Rawalpindiz, the new franchise for PSL 12.

When is the PSL 2027 auction?

The player auction is on 23 January 2027. Direct signings run from 23 October to 23 November 2026, and retentions close on 13 January 2027.

Why does PSL 2027 clash with the IPL again?

The March–May window was finalised by the PSL Governing Council in London on 1 September 2026. It overlaps with IPL 2027 for the third straight year, after Ramadan ends in early March.
