What comes first

The player auction is on Saturday 23 January 2027, the season opens on Thursday 18 March 2027, and the final is at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore on Sunday 2 May 2027. Forty-four matches are scheduled — 40 in the league stage and four playoffs — at five to six venues across Pakistan. The full fixture list has not been published yet by the PCB.
