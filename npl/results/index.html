Sudurpaschim Royals vs Janakpur Bolts

Nepal Premier League, Match 21 · TU International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur · 4:30 PM NST (3:45 PM PKT, 10:45 AM UTC)

Sudurpaschim Royals 120/9 (20 ov) beat Janakpur Bolts 93 all out (19.5 ov) by 27 runs.

Scott Kuggeleijn top-scored with 36 off 29 for the Royals, and Harmeet Singh took 4 for 21 for Janakpur. Ben McDermott made 33 for the Bolts, while Lalit Rajbanshi took 3 for 13 and Kuggeleijn 3 for 17. Janakpur won the toss and chose to bowl.
