Season 3 summary

Asalanka is a Sri Lankan international batter and former white-ball captain who joined Biratnagar Kings as a direct overseas signing for NPL Season 3. His rise was rapid: ODI debut against England in June 2021, T20I debut against India a month later, then player of the series in the ODI rubber against South Africa that September.

He scored 231 runs at 46.20 at the 2021 T20 World Cup and made a maiden ODI hundred — 110 against Australia in June 2022. He captained Sri Lanka’s white-ball sides from 2024, then lost the ODI captaincy in May 2026 and was left out of October’s tri-series squad against Pakistan, so Season 3 doubles as a personal reset for a proven pressure-chase batter.

His calling card is the controlled chase: an unbeaten 80 from 49 balls against Bangladesh remains the template — a player who absorbs pressure and accelerates on his own terms, strong through the leg side and adept at manipulating spin in the middle overs rather than relying on pure power. Two games left is still enough time for one innings to change the story.

Since then he has been out of the XI. The Kings’ middle order has been carried by Bam, Russell and Imran Sheikh, and the overseas slots have gone to Russell and the gap players who have actually played — Angelo Mathews, Shehan Jayasuriya and Shubham Ranjane.
