Three seasons, same price

A Category D developmental pick in 2024, Gautam has become the cheapest structural piece Chitwan owns, and the structure keeps needing him. Three seasons at the same price makes him the franchise’s control experiment: the low-cost bowler whose currency is the dot ball — the tight over that pushes the required rate up and forces batters to take risks against someone else.

The valuation tells the story in numbers: 2 lakh, then retention, then 2 lakh again. In a squad coming off a two-win Season 2, every retained name is expected to justify the faith — and a third identical deal says the franchise values exactly what he does, at exactly the price he costs.

The control brief

Gautam’s listed role is bowler and his brief is control — the least spectacular and most repeatable skill in T20. Where strike bowlers hunt wickets, the control bowler’s currency is the dot ball: the tight over that pushes the required rate up, forces the batter to take risks against someone else, and holds the innings’ shape. Three seasons of 2-lakh deals say the franchise values exactly that: not mystery, not pace, but the discipline to bowl the same hard-to-hit ball six times an over.

The usage pattern writes itself around the attack’s senior names. Rijan Dhakal and Kamal Singh Airee take the wickets; the overseas spin controls the middle; Gautam is the bowler you turn to when the innings needs holding rather than breaking open.

No overs yet in Season 3

There is no batting or bowling entry for him in the Season 3 statistics through Match 21 — he has not batted or bowled in a league game yet this season. That is the honest state of the record, not a gap in the page: the numbers will appear here as soon as he gets his first game.

The attack’s senior names have taken the overs so far — Rijan and Kamal have 19 wickets between them, and the overseas spin has filled the middle overs. Chitwan’s results so far are on the NPL Results page, and none of the five games has yet called for the control bowler from the bench.

Rotation in the last two games could open a door: two games in three days, against Janakpur and Karnali, is the sort of schedule where a fresh control bowler earns his overs. The brief would be the same as ever — six balls, hard to hit, nothing given away.

The last two league games
