Season 3 summary

Tripathi is a Nepal right-handed batter who came through the country’s Under-19 system and played at the 2024 ICC Under-19 World Cup. Lumbini bought him for NPR 5 lakh in Category B at the July 2026 auction — his third NPL franchise in three seasons, after the Janakpur Bolts and the Kathmandu Gorkhas.

His senior domestic T20 record stands at 309 runs in 12 games with three fifties, and his youth one-day numbers are stronger still: 361 runs in 19 matches, with a best of 89. Those are the figures of a batter who scores in bursts and has not yet had a long run to turn promise into a season. The Lions signed him as the squad’s designated development batter — the young player in a champion side who learns the dressing room first and waits for the game that needs him.

Debut wait

Tripathi’s name does not appear in any of the 21 published scorecards of NPL Season 3 so far. He has not played a game for the Lions this season — not because of form, but because the side he joined has barely changed its eleven.

The top order has been settled all season: Aasif Sheikh and Niroshan Dickwella open, D’Arcy Short and Rohit Paudel follow, and the middle order has run on Sundeep Jora and the all-rounders. A development batter’s chance in a side like that comes from one of three things — an injury, a tactical change, or a game where the result matters less than the future. Lumbini have two league games left, against Karnali on Thursday and Biratnagar on Saturday, and the second of those may be the moment: if the Lions have already secured their playoff place by then, the captain has every reason to give a 21-year-old his first game in the shirt.

Batting unit

The batting Tripathi is waiting to join has been the steady, unspectacular engine of the Lions’ season. Rohit Paudel leads it with 86 runs in four innings, ahead of Dickwella’s 75 and Aasif Sheikh’s 61, and the side is third in the NPL Points Table with seven points from five games and the league’s best net run rate at +1.501. Every result is on the NPL Results page.

What the unit has not yet produced is a fifty — no Lumbini batter has reached one this season — and that is the gap a young batter with three domestic fifties is signed to fill. The 2025 title side was built on its core staying together; the 2026 side is built the same way, with one new face at the top and the rest retained. Tripathi is the next face in line. The champions won the title last year with a squad that trusted its young players when the moment came; this season, the moment has not come yet.
