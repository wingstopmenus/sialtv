Bowling all-rounder. Auction — NPR 5 lakh. Captain: Harmeet Singh. Head coach: Shivnarine Chanderpaul.

Bishal Kumar Patel was signed by Janakpur Bolts for NPR 5 lakh at the Season 3 mini-auction in Kathmandu on 6 July 2026, listed as a bowling allrounder: the same role label and the same price as Amar Singh Rautela. He was one of 52 players picked from 155 registered. A left-hand batter and left-arm medium bowler from Sarlahi, born on 30 October 2003, he came through Nepal’s Under-16s.

He announced himself in March 2026 with a five-for — 5 for 24 — that sealed Madhesh’s 25-run win over Lumbini. His recent T20 bowling backs it up: seven wickets including 4 for 11 at an economy of 6.27.

Patel owns something no other Janakpur bowler has: the left-arm seamer’s angle. In a squad built around three left-arm spinners, the ball that leaves the right-hander gives Harmeet Singh a genuinely different new-ball option. The paired buys with Rautela give the captain interchangeable domestic bowling-allround options — and neither has played yet. One league match remains, against Chitwan Rhinos on Friday, and a left-armer against Chitwan’s right-hand-heavy top order is the match-up the whole season has been waiting for.

Squad depth

Seven wickets at an economy of 6.27 in recent T20 cricket, including 4 for 11, is the best recent bowling return of any domestic bowler in this squad — and Janakpur’s attack has been the side’s weaker half all season. The overseas seam option, James Neesham, has one wicket in six overs at above 10 an over. The spinners have carried the attack, and they have done it well — Harmeet Singh leads the league — but a side that has conceded 149, 174 and 132 in three of its six matches needs another way to take wickets.

The argument for Patel is not just the numbers, it is the angle. Left-arm seam against right-hand batters is the one thing this attack does not have, and Chitwan Rhinos, Friday’s opponents, bat right-handed through the top order. The argument against is the same as for every unused domestic buy: a debut in a shootout is a risk, and the captain has trusted the same XI for six matches. But the XI has lost four of them. If the Bolts want a different result on Friday, the most direct way to get one is a different bowler.
