Hemant Dhami has bowled in all six of Sudurpaschim Royals’ matches in NPL Season 3, taking 4 wickets at an economy of 6.67. He is a retained bowler in a side second in the table on 8 points, and no other Royals bowler has played every match with the ball except Scott Kuggeleijn.

Season 3 analysis

His six spells are a picture of steady, unspectacular work. He went wicketless for 5 in one over against Lumbini Lions, took 1 for 14 in three overs against Pokhara Avengers, went wicketless for 25 in one over in the five-over match against Biratnagar Kings, and then took a wicket in each of the last three matches: 1 for 26 against Chitwan Rhinos, 1 for 14 against Karnali Yaks and 1 for 16 against Janakpur Bolts in Match 21. Four wickets at an average of 25.00 is honest rather than striking — but he is there, every match, bowling his overs.

With the bat he has two innings and two ducks: 0 off 1 ball against Lumbini Lions and 0 off 1 ball against Janakpur Bolts. Batting is not his job, and the Royals do not ask it of him. His value is availability and control: in a tournament where the Royals have defended 120 in Match 21 and 176 in Match 16, a bowler the captain can turn to in any match, for any spell, is worth more than his figures suggest. The run-in — Kathmandu Gorkhas, then the playoffs — is where that reliability counts most.
