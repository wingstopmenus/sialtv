Season 3 summary

Rit Gautam has not played in NPL Season 3 yet. Janakpur Bolts paid NPR 4.25 lakh for him at the Season 3 mini-auction in Kathmandu on 6 July 2026 — one of only two pure batters the club bought — and six matches in, the middle-order place he was signed for has never opened up.

Batter. Auction — NPR 4.25 lakh. Captain: Harmeet Singh. Head coach: Shivnarine Chanderpaul.

Rit Gautam was signed by Janakpur Bolts for NPR 4.25 lakh at the Season 3 mini-auction in Kathmandu on 6 July 2026, listed as a specialist batter. He was one of 52 players picked from 155 registered, and one of only two pure batters among the club’s auction buys. A right-hand batter from Kathmandu, born on 17 September 2001, he came through Nepal’s Under-19s as an age-group centurion.

He keeps producing: in June 2026 he fired Kathmandu to the PL Singh Memorial Cup title, and his recent T20 record reads 250 runs at a strike rate of 155.28 with a highest score of 97. For a specialist there is nowhere to hide — the price says the Bolts believe his runs will come.

His Season 3 role was clear from the start: the low-cost attacking option in the middle order, likely at No. 4 or No. 5, attacking spin from ball one. Six matches have passed without that chance. The side’s batting has been the problem all season — bowled out for 93 in Match 21, past 150 only twice — and the one match left, against Chitwan Rhinos on Friday, is the last door. If it opens for Gautam, it opens in a shootout.

Squad depth

Two hundred and fifty runs at a strike rate of 155.28 is the best recent T20 return of any domestic batter in this squad, and Janakpur have spent the season short of exactly that: quick runs in the middle overs. Trit Raj Das, the side’s big domestic buy at NPR 15 lakh, has 112 runs at a strike rate of 94.12. Saif Hassan, who is not in the verified squad list, leads the side with 140 runs. Gautam’s record says he hits at a rate nobody else in the squad has shown.

The reason he has not played is the same reason the side is fifth: the XI was picked for experience — three overseas all-rounders, a retained core — and it has not been changed while losing. But a settled XI that loses four of six has run out of reasons to stay settled. Friday against Chitwan is a must-win game, and the one change that directly answers the side’s biggest weakness is the attacking batter who has been waiting all season.
