What the wait means

There is not much to analyse, and that is the honest position. Pandey has no batting or bowling entry in the season stats file through Match 21, no listed role, and no public style — the profile of a player signed as depth and used as depth. What can be said is what the auction says: 52 players picked from 155 registered is a real filter, and at NPR 2 lakh he cost the club almost nothing. The value of a signing like this is not the games he plays; it is the cover he provides and the season he spends learning.

The question for Friday is whether a must-win game is the place to find out what he can do. Janakpur’s season has been defined by a settled XI that keeps losing — two wins, four defeats, bowled out for 93 in the last match. A debutant in a shootout is a risk, but so is losing with the same eleven that have lost four times already. If Pandey plays against Chitwan, it will be because the captain decided the risk of something new was smaller than the risk of more of the same.

Where the Bolts stand

Janakpur Bolts sit fifth in the NPL points table on 4 points from six matches — two wins, four defeats, net run rate −0.303. One league match remains: Chitwan Rhinos on Friday 13 November at 4:30 PM NST (3:45 PM PKT, 10:45 AM UTC) at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. It is a straight shootout — the winner stays in the playoff race and the loser is out. Chitwan sit one place above Janakpur on 5 points, so a Bolts win takes them past the Rhinos, and leaves Chitwan needing to beat Karnali Yaks on Sunday to stay alive themselves.
