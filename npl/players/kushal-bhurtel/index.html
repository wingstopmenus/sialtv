Season 3 summary

Kushal Bhurtel has scored 136 runs in NPL Season 3, more than any other Pokhara Avengers batter, at a strike rate of 132. He has also taken four wickets, and he has captained the side in all five matches.

Batter and captain. Retained as the franchise’s marquee domestic player. Head coach: Ajantha Mendis.

Born in Butwal on 22 January 1997, Bhurtel is the defining batter of Nepal’s T20I era: 81 T20Is, 2,329 runs at an average of 32.35 and a strike rate of 136.28, with three centuries. His 74 ODIs and 1,701 runs underline a complete international batter rather than a T20 freelancer. Pokhara retained him as their marquee domestic player, and his brief this season was singular: lead a rebuilt squad — new head coach Ajantha Mendis, two overseas stars and a record domestic signing — to the franchise’s first playoff appearance.

Everything at Pokhara runs through Bhurtel this season. He leads the batting, he bowls most games, and he carries the captaincy of a side that has lost four of five. The 62 against Karnali Yaks was his only fifty of the season, but it came with seven sixes and no fours at all — a pure power innings that turned a season going nowhere into the loudest night of the tournament.

Season 3 analysis

His five innings climb, dip and then explode. He made 21 off 17 against Sudurpaschim Royals in the opener, 35 off 24 against Janakpur Bolts, 14 off 20 against Lumbini Lions and 4 off 3 against Chitwan Rhinos — then 62 off 39 against Karnali Yaks, with seven sixes. The pattern is the story of Pokhara’s season: one man scoring, the rest of the order quiet around him. His average of 27.20 is the best among Pokhara’s batters, and his 136 runs are 70 clear of the next-best in the side.

The bowling is a genuine second skill, not a captain’s filler. Four wickets in five matches, with 2 for 26 against Chitwan Rhinos and 2 for 39 against Karnali Yaks, at an economy of 8.02. He is the only Pokhara player with 100 runs and four wickets this season. The cost is the economy: 8.02 an over is the third-most expensive among the side’s regular bowlers, and it comes from bowling in the toughest phases — the powerplay and the death.

Frequently asked questions

How many runs has Kushal Bhurtel scored in NPL 2026?

136 runs in five matches, at a strike rate of 132, with a highest score of 62 against Karnali Yaks. It is the most runs by any Pokhara Avengers batter this season, 70 clear of Chamika Karunaratne’s 66.

Is Kushal Bhurtel Pokhara Avengers’ captain?

Yes. Bhurtel has captained Pokhara in all five matches of Season 3, and he also leads the side’s batting with 136 runs and has taken four wickets. Ajantha Mendis is the head coach.

Has Kushal Bhurtel taken wickets in NPL 2026?

Yes, four wickets in five matches, at an economy of 8.02. His best figures are 2 for 26 against Chitwan Rhinos, and he also took 2 for 39 in the win over Karnali Yaks.
