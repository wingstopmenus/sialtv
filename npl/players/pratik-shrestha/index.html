Pratik Shrestha — the all-rounder who has not played yet

Pratik Shrestha has not played yet in NPL Season 3. The 24-year-old all-rounder from Pokhara is one of Biratnagar Kings’ deep stocks of lower-order hitters and medium-pacers — and in a squad that already carries four all-rounders, the competition for a game is fierce.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Season 3 in numbers

No matches, no runs, no wickets. Shrestha has not played in NPL Season 3 so far — and with Rupesh Singh, Sujan Thapaliya, Basir Ahamad and Narayan Joshi all competing for the same lower-order slots, his chance may only come if the Kings rotate.

His league record is thin but not empty: one match in 2024, against Pokhara Avengers, in which he made 9 runs from 11 balls and bowled one over for 9 — and four matches in 2025, in which he made 12 runs and took 1 wicket, 1 for 26 against Chitwan Rhinos. The 2025 season also brought a 0 for 37 against Pokhara Avengers, the kind of spell that loses a place.

The stronger evidence is domestic: in the 2024/25 Prime Minister Cup he played one match for Gandaki and took 3 for 15 against Madhesh Province. A three-wicket haul in a domestic one-day game is the kind of performance that keeps a player on a franchise’s radar even when the league numbers are thin.

Match by match

Pratik Shrestha has not appeared in any of Biratnagar Kings’ five matches so far.

The Kings’ all-rounder department is the deepest in the squad, and the pecking order has been clear: Russell and Basir Ahamad play, Rupesh Singh and Sujan Thapaliya get games, Narayan Joshi got one. Shrestha is the next name in line — and the line has not moved yet.

The last two league games are the only realistic window. If the Kings win on Thursday and qualify before Saturday, the final game against Lumbini Lions could be a dead rubber — the one match in which the captain might rotate the all-rounders.

The Pokhara all-rounder

Shrestha is a right-handed batter and right-arm medium-fast bowler from Pokhara, bought by Biratnagar Kings for NPR 2 lakh at the July auction, the Category C base price. He has played for the Kings in both previous NPL seasons.

His two skills are complementary rather than spectacular: a lower-order batter who can hit a few boundaries, and a medium-pacer who bowls an honest length and lets the batters make mistakes. In a T20 squad that is exactly the profile that fills the seventh or eighth bowling slot and the seventh or eighth batting slot.

The problem is the competition. The Kings carry four other players who do the same two jobs, and three of them — Russell, Basir Ahamad and Rupesh Singh — have already played this season. Shrestha’s path to a game runs through an injury, a rest, or a dead rubber.

Where the Kings stand

Shrestha is the next all-rounder in line in a side chasing two wins: Biratnagar Kings are sixth after Match 21 — four points from five games, with a net run rate of −0.820. The full table is on the NPL Points Table, and every result is on the NPL Results page.

Two wins from the last two games would take the Kings to 8 points and almost certainly into the playoffs. Shrestha’s own season depends on whether the Kings qualify early enough to rotate — and on whether the captain reaches past three all-rounders who have already played.
