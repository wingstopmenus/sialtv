Youth career

Poudel is a Nepal Under-19 international, named in the ACC U19 Asia Cup squad in late 2024. His youth numbers are striking: 14 wickets at 13.43 in seven one-day matches with a best of 3 for 26, plus nine wickets at 15.11 in seven T20s.

His rise ran through the 2024 youth cycle: selection in Nepal’s fifteen-member squad for the ACC Under-19 Asia Cup in late 2024, then a place in Gandaki Province’s squad for the 2026 Prime Minister Cup — the domestic step that put him on the Kings’ auction radar.

He bowls slow left-arm orthodox, the classic T20 left-arm spin package: drifting the ball into right-handers and turning it away, varying pace rather than relying on extravagant turn. The open question is the step up from youth to senior franchise cricket — Under-19 batters chase; NPL middle orders pick length early. Two games in, the answer is still being written.
