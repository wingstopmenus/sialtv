Season 3 summary

Ishan Pandey has scored 95 runs in five matches for Sudurpaschim Royals in NPL Season 3, the second-most by any Royals batter — and he has scored in every match except the last one. He is a retained batter in a side second in the table on 8 points, and he has added a wicket with the ball as well.

Batter. Retained for Season 3. Head coach: Misbah-ul-Haq. Captain: Dipendra Singh Airee.

Season 3 analysis

His innings have been the steady hand at the top of the order. He made 7 against Lumbini Lions, 37 off 31 against Pokhara Avengers — his best, in the six-wicket win — 25 off 29 against Chitwan Rhinos, 23 off 13 against Karnali Yaks, and then 3 off 8 against Janakpur Bolts in Match 21, his only single-figure score after the opener. A strike rate of 105.6 and an average of 19.00 say the same thing: he gets starts, and he gets them at a decent pace, but he has not yet turned one into a fifty.

The 23 off 13 against Karnali is the innings that shows what he can do when he opens up — five sixes in the season, second only to Brandon McMullen’s six, from only 90 balls faced. And in Match 21 he added something new: 1 for 19 in three overs, his first wicket of the season, in the 27-run win over Janakpur. If the Royals are to win the last league match against Kathmandu Gorkhas and push for the top two, they need his starts to become one big score.

Frequently asked questions

How many runs has Ishan Pandey scored in NPL 2026?

95 runs in five matches, at a strike rate of 105.6, with a highest score of 37 against Pokhara Avengers. It is the second-highest total by a Sudurpaschim Royals batter this season, behind Brandon McMullen’s 124.

Has Ishan Pandey taken a wicket in NPL 2026?

Yes, one: 1 for 19 in three overs against Janakpur Bolts in Match 21. He has bowled in three matches this season — against Pokhara Avengers, Karnali Yaks and Janakpur Bolts — and that was his first wicket.

What is Ishan Pandey’s best innings in NPL 2026?

37 off 31 balls against Pokhara Avengers in Match 4, when the Royals chased 146 and won by six wickets. He also made 25 against Chitwan Rhinos and 23 off 13 against Karnali Yaks.
