Squad role

Bhandari is a right-arm leg-break bowler, the only wrist-spinner in the champions’ squad. He was signed in the 2024 auction and retained twice, and he announced himself with four for 22 against Gandaki in June 2023 to win Player of the Match.

His three for 15 came in the 2025 title run, alongside a List A best of four for 17, and the point of him has not changed: leg-spin that turns away from right-handers gives the champions a match-up weapon none of their finger spinners offer. Sher Malla and Abhishesh Gautam bowl the steady middle overs; Bhandari is the variation Rohit Paudel reaches for to break a stand. He is unlikely to start every game as a first-choice bowler, but in a playoff race decided by small margins, a wrist-spinner who has taken five wickets at 5.58 is the kind of option that wins a knockout game.
