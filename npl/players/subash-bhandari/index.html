Subash Bhandari — the international spinner who has not played yet

Subash Bhandari has not played yet in NPL Season 3. The right-arm offbreak bowler from Baglung is a Nepal international with 9 ODI caps and 7 T20I caps — and the most surprising omission in a Biratnagar Kings side that has lost three of its five games.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Season 3 in numbers

No matches, no runs, no wickets. Bhandari has not played in NPL Season 3 so far — and in a spin department led by Sandeep Lamichhane, with Aprajit Poudel getting the support-spinner games, the international off-spinner has been left out entirely.

His league record is not thin: 7 wickets in 7 matches at 31.00 with an economy of 7.33 in 2023, including a best of 3 for 28 against Chitwan Rhinos; 2 wickets in 6 matches at 33.00 with an economy of 6.33 in 2024, including a best of 1 for 11 against Pokhara Avengers. In 2025 he played 2 matches without a wicket.

The pattern is a containing spinner rather than a strike bowler: an economy of 5.67 in 2023, and a role that holds one end while the wrist-spinner attacks from the other. In a season when the Kings’ attack has leaked runs, that containing role is exactly what the side has been missing.

Match by match

Subash Bhandari has not appeared in any of Biratnagar Kings’ five matches so far.

The spin department has been Sandeep Lamichhane’s four overs, plus Basir Ahamad’s left-arm spin and Aprajit Poudel’s two games. Bhandari’s off-break — the one spin type the attack does not have — has not been used at all.

The last two league games are the only realistic window. If the Kings win on Thursday and qualify before Saturday, the final game against Lumbini Lions could be a dead rubber — the one match in which the captain might finally give the international off-spinner a game.

The international spinner waiting

Bhandari is a right-arm offbreak bowler from Baglung, retained by Biratnagar Kings on 16 June 2026. A Nepal international, he made his ODI debut in March 2023 against Papua New Guinea and his T20I debut against the same opposition, and has 9 ODI caps and 7 T20I caps.

His international numbers are solid for a containing spinner: 4 ODI wickets at 28.75 and 3 T20I wickets at 39.00. He is not a strike bowler at international level either — he is a bowler who holds an end, builds pressure, and lets the other end take the wickets.

That is the role the Kings have been missing. Sandeep Lamichhane has taken 1 wicket in four matches, and the attack’s economy has been the problem as much as its wicket-taking. An international off-spinner who bowls at 5.67 an over is the kind of player who fixes exactly that problem.

Where the Kings stand

Bhandari is the unused international in a side chasing two wins: Biratnagar Kings are sixth after Match 21 — four points from five games, with a net run rate of −0.820. The full table is on the NPL Points Table, and every result is on the NPL Results page.

Two wins from the last two games would take the Kings to 8 points and almost certainly into the playoffs — and an international off-spinner who has never played a game this season is the one selection the captain has not tried yet. Saturday against Lumbini Lions, the defending champions, is the game in which that selection would matter most.
