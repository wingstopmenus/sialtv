The 56 against Janakpur was the innings that won the game: opening with Bam, Sheikh made the second-highest score of the match as Biratnagar posted 174 for 3 and won by two runs. His 39 in the opener against Chitwan Rhinos was the highest score of that innings for the Kings, and his 5 against Karnali was the only quiet night.

The Kathmandu game was a duck — 0 from 3 balls on the night the whole batting order failed and Biratnagar were bowled out for 83. One famous innings for Karnali Yaks is the public record behind the signing, so his technique at NPL level is proven in temperament more than volume — but 100 runs in four games is already a return on a 2-lakh buy.

Auction signing

Sheikh made his name in NPL 2025 playing for Karnali Yaks at the top of the order, top-scoring with 33 off 32 balls against defending champions Janakpur Bolts in a famous three-run win as Karnali defended 129.

The context of that innings is what earned him a second franchise: Karnali defending just 129 against the defending champions, Sheikh top-scoring with a composed 33 from 32 — strike rotation under scoreboard pressure rather than slogging — in a three-run win that remains one of the NPL’s great upsets.

Bought at the Category C base price of 2 lakh on 6 July, he arrived as a reserve opener behind an international top order — low-cost top-order insurance for a squad whose first-choice combinations feature overseas and senior Nepali names. Four games in, he is batting in the top three and averaging 25.

He did not play the five-over game against Sudurpaschim Royals, when the XI was shortened to the hitters. His right-arm off-break is listed as part-time, and he has bowled just four balls all season — a partnership-breaking option rather than a planned spell.
