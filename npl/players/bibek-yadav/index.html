The numbers are thin, but the role is not. Yadav has batted in three of the four result games, which says the captain trusts him in the XI even while the runs are still coming. The question for the run-in is whether the batting catches up with the billing — 15 lakh buys expectations, and expectations need an innings.

Auction value

Category A is the auction’s top domestic bracket, and 15 lakh is over half of Chitwan’s NPR 26 lakh total spend. That number is the franchise telling the league what it thinks: this is not squad depth, it is a first-XI cornerstone bought at cornerstone money. His price was more than double the combined cost of the other five domestic buys — and he was the most expensive disclosed domestic signing in the entire squad.

The franchise framed the capture as its major auction splash, the move around which its recruitment was built. After a two-win Season 2, the Rhinos needed a domestic signing that changed the XI rather than filled it — and they paid accordingly.

Auction price

As a Category A all-rounder, Yadav was bought to do two jobs, and the price says both must be first-XI standard. The all-rounder’s T20 value is in the XI flexibility he buys: a sixth bowling option who can also bat in the top seven lets a captain carry an extra specialist somewhere else. At Chitwan, that flexibility is gold — with only three overseas signings, the domestic all-rounder is the lever that balances the side, and 15 lakh is the cost of a lever that cannot break.

The competition for that balance is real. Kushal Malla and Sikandar Raza are both all-rounders with far bigger international records, which means Yadav’s route to influence runs through doing the unglamorous jobs — the tight over, the quick single, the catch — while the marquee names take the spotlight.

Remaining fixtures

Chitwan are fourth after Match 21, with five points from five games and a net run rate of +0.009. The full table is on the NPL Points Table, and the remaining fixtures are on the NPL Schedule. Two league games are left: Janakpur Bolts on Friday 13 November and Karnali Yaks on Sunday 15 November. Win both and the Rhinos reach nine points — enough to guarantee a playoff place.
