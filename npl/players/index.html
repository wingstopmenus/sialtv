Frequently asked questions

How many players are in NPL Season 3?

129 players have a profile on SialTV — 20 for Janakpur Bolts, 17 each for Sudurpaschim Royals and Pokhara Avengers, 16 each for Kathmandu Gorkhas, Biratnagar Kings and Chitwan Rhinos, 14 for Karnali Yaks and 13 for Lumbini Lions.

Who has scored the most runs in NPL Season 3?

Lokesh Bam of Biratnagar Kings, with 194 runs in five matches — including two fifties and a highest score of 87. Ravi Bopara of Chitwan Rhinos is second with 171.

Who has taken the most wickets in NPL Season 3?

Harmeet Singh of Janakpur Bolts, with 11 wickets in six matches at an economy of 5.83 — and the season’s best figures, 4 for 17 against Sudurpaschim Royals in Match 21.

38 of the 129 are yet to bat or bowl in Season 3. They are squad members waiting for their first game — their profiles carry their auction price, role and background instead of season figures.
