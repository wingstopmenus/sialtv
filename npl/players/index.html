Players by team

Every squad member of the Nepal Premier League Season 3 is listed here — all 129 players with a profile on SialTV, grouped by team. The figures beside each name are the player’s verified Season 3 numbers through Match 25: 24 matches completed, one abandoned for rain. Ninety-four of the 129 have a batting or bowling entry in the season record so far; the other 35 are still waiting for their first game.

The leaderboard is led by two men on opposite sides of the game. Lokesh Bam of Biratnagar Kings has 227 runs in six matches, the most in the league, and Rahul Yadav of Janakpur Bolts has 12 wickets in seven, also the most, at 6.87 runs an over. Both are linked below, with every other player in their squads. The next match is Match 26 — Biratnagar Kings against Lumbini Lions, on Saturday 14 November at 5:00 PM NST.
