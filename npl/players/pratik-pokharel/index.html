Season 3 summary

Pratik Pokharel has played two matches in NPL Season 3. He made 2 not out off one ball against Janakpur Bolts and bowled three overs for 31 runs without a wicket. He is a bowler, signed at the July 6, 2026 auction for NPR 2 lakh. (The season stats file spells his surname Pokhrel.)

Bowler. Auction buy, NPR 2 lakh. Captain: Kushal Bhurtel. Head coach: Ajantha Mendis.

He arrived as part of the base-price domestic bowling group assembled after the franchise spent its headline money — NPR 15 lakh — on keeper Arjun Gharti. Listed as a bowler, he is squad depth in a fifteen-player group: the kind of July signing that can look inspired by October. His season is about earning his overs the hard way, in an attack reshaped under new head coach Ajantha Mendis.

His two games came early: one over for ten runs against Sudurpaschim Royals in the opener, then two overs for 21 runs and 2 not out off one ball against Janakpur Bolts. He has not played since Match 9, which says the coaches have settled on their attack — Shakib Al Hasan’s spin, Mahedi Hasan’s off-breaks, Bipin Khatri’s five wickets and Kushal Bhurtel’s overs — and have not yet turned back to him.

Season 3 analysis

The numbers are three overs long: 0 for 10 in one over against Sudurpaschim Royals, 0 for 21 in two overs against Janakpur Bolts, and 2 not out off one ball with the bat. An economy of 10.33 across 18 balls is expensive, but 18 balls is no sample at all — and both spells came in defeats, the second in a game Pokhara lost by nine runs. His batting average does not exist: he has not been dismissed.

The case for Pokharel is the attack around him. Pokhara’s bowlers have taken wickets — Mahedi Hasan and Bipin Khatri have five each — but the side has still lost four of five, and the bowling has not defended a total under 150 once. A fresh bowler with two games already under his belt is exactly the kind of change a side makes when its season is on the line — and with two matches left, both must-win, the line is now.
