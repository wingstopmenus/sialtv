The known quantity who has not been used

Amar Singh Rautela has not played in NPL Season 3 yet. Janakpur Bolts paid NPR 5 lakh for him at the Season 3 mini-auction in Kathmandu on 6 July 2026 — the experienced head among the domestic auction buys — and six matches in, the reliable overs he was signed for have never been called on.

Bowling all-rounder. Auction — NPR 5 lakh. Captain: Harmeet Singh. Head coach: Shivnarine Chanderpaul.

Amar Singh Rautela was signed by Janakpur Bolts for NPR 5 lakh at the Season 3 mini-auction in Kathmandu on 6 July 2026, listed as a bowling allrounder. He was one of 52 players picked from 155 registered. A right-hand batter and right-arm medium bowler from the APF Club, born on 5 September 1993, he is 33 — the experienced head among the domestic auction buys.

His recent T20 record shows both skills working: 98 runs at a strike rate of 144.12 with the bat and five wickets at an economy of 8.09 with the ball. The “bowling” qualifier in his role label matters: the club bought him primarily for a full spell of overs, with his batting as a bonus.

His case rests on reliability: the full spell of overs the overseas stars do not bowl, his batting a bonus. Across a long season, he is the known quantity Harmeet Singh reaches for — except that, six matches in, the captain has not reached for him once. The overseas all-rounders have bowled the middle overs, and the domestic spin trio has covered the rest. One league match remains, against Chitwan Rhinos on Friday, and it is a shootout: the kind of game where a 33-year-old who does not panic is worth more than a youngster with a higher ceiling.

What the wait means

A side that has lost four of six matches, with a bowling attack that has conceded 120 or more in four of them, leaving its most reliable domestic seamer unused is hard to explain by form alone. Janakpur’s seam options beyond the overseas players are thin — James Neesham has one wicket in six overs at an economy above 10 — and Rautela’s recent record says he bowls a full spell at 8.09 an over and bats at a strike rate of 144.12. Those are not star numbers, but they are the numbers of a player who does not lose games.

The counter is that the XI has been built around spin: three left-arm spinners plus Moeen Ali’s off-breaks, with Harmeet Singh’s own left-arm spin as the spearhead. In that shape there is no obvious slot for a right-arm medium bowler. But Friday against Chitwan Rhinos is not a game for shapes — it is a game Janakpur must win, against a side that has already beaten them to fourth place. If the pitch offers anything to the seamers, Rautela is the one domestic bowler in the squad who has done this job before.

What Janakpur need now

Fifth place, 4 points, one match left — that is where Janakpur Bolts stand after Match 21. The last league game is against Chitwan Rhinos on Friday 13 November at 4:30 PM NST (3:45 PM PKT, 10:45 AM UTC) at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, and only a win keeps the season alive. Chitwan have 5 points, so a Bolts win lifts Janakpur past them; then the arithmetic needs Chitwan to lose their final match to Karnali Yaks on Sunday, while Biratnagar Kings and Karnali Yaks win at most one of their two remaining games each. Lose on Friday and the season ends outright. The playoffs begin with Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 17 November and end with the final on Saturday 21 November.
