Season 3 in numbers

Two innings, 25 runs, two fours and two sixes. Nath has batted twice in five games, and the strike rate of 138.89 is the highest of any Lumbini batter to have faced more than ten balls this season — the runs have come quickly when they have come at all.

All 25 runs came in a single innings, which is why the average of 12.50 tells the story honestly: one good knock, one duck.

Squad role

Nath was retained in June 2026, and he has been in and around Nepal’s national setup since 2017 — a List A debut against the UAE in December 2017, a T20I debut against Malaysia in March 2022, and a place in the 2025 title squad, in which he scored 122 runs.

He is the third keeper in a three-keeper squad, behind Aasif Sheikh and Niroshan Dickwella, and that is his value: he covers an injury or a tactical rest, a retained title-winner ready to step in, and a left-handed option for the middle order that the other two keepers do not offer. The 28-year-old has waited most of this season for a game; the two league matches left — Karnali on Thursday, Biratnagar on Saturday — are the kind of games where a captain rotates a keeper, and the 25 off 16 against Janakpur is the reason he would be the first name called.
