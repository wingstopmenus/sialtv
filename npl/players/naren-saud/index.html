Bowling record

Saud is a right-arm fast bowler from Kailali, bought by Biratnagar Kings for NPR 5 lakh at the July auction after two seasons in Kings colours. Last season he took 7 wickets — the best return by a domestic Kings bowler — and his first-class numbers mark him as a genuine wicket-taker at every level.

His first-class debut came in February 2025, in a match that showed his class in one performance: 6 wickets at 24.17, including 4 for 41 against Tribhuvan Army Club. In T20 cricket he has 33 wickets in 20 matches at 22.72, with a best of 4 for 36 against Gandaki.

The pattern is consistent: a bowler who takes wickets in clusters and hits the pitch hard, with the natural pace to hurry batters on the Kirtipur surface. Two games into Season 3, the wicket column is still blank — but the sample is two overs, and the record behind him says the wickets will come if the captain gives him the ball.
