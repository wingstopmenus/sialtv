Sher Malla — Lumbini Lions' leading wicket-taker, NPL 2026

Eight wickets in four matches, an economy of 5.19, and the Player of the Match award on the opening night. Sher Malla is Lumbini Lions’ leading wicket-taker in NPL Season 3 — only five bowlers in the whole league have taken more. The right-arm off-break bowler has taken a wicket in every game he has bowled in.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Season 3 in numbers

Four matches, eight wickets, 83 runs conceded in 16 overs. Malla is the only Lions bowler with a wicket in every game, and his economy of 5.19 is the best of any Lumbini bowler to have played all four matches. The next-best Lions bowler, Ruben Trumpelmann, has six wickets; nobody else in the squad has more than five.

The batting is a footnote — 12 runs in two innings, including an unbeaten zero without facing a ball against Janakpur — because his job is with the ball. Eight wickets at 5.19 an over, in a season where the league’s leading wicket-taker has 11, puts him sixth on the league chart and first for his team.

The opening night was the statement: three for 11 in four overs as the Royals were bowled out for 87, and the Player of the Match award as the champions won by seven wickets. Since then he has taken at least one wicket in every game — one for 24 against Pokhara, two for 29 against Janakpur, two for 19 against Kathmandu — and he has bowled his full four overs in all four matches, which is the captain’s way of saying he is trusted with the hardest overs. His 12 with the bat against Kathmandu was the fourth-highest score in a Lumbini innings of 124, on a night when the batting failed around him.

From Emerging Player to attack leader

Malla was retained in June 2026 after a 2025 season that made him one of the league’s discoveries: joint-highest wicket-taker with 17 wickets in ten games at 13.76, and the season’s Emerging Player.

His playoff ledger in 2025 was decisive — three for 22 in the Eliminator, three for 31 in Qualifier 2, three for 18 in the final — and it is why the Lions trust him with the highest-pressure overs they have. In Season 3 he leads the spin attack, bowling in tandem with Abhishesh Gautam through the middle overs, the pairing that defined the champions’ identity last year. The 2025 formula has not changed; Malla is simply doing more of it, against sides that now plan for him.

Where the Lions stand

Lumbini are third after Match 21 — seven points from five games, with the best net run rate in the league at +1.501. The full table is on the NPL Points Table, and every result is on the NPL Results page.

Malla’s eight wickets are the backbone of a defence that has conceded fewer runs per over than any side except the team above them. The Lions have won three of their five games, and in all three wins he took at least one wicket — the attack works when he does. Two wins from the last two league games would take Lumbini to 11 points and a top-two finish; one win should still be enough for the playoffs. The run rate, already the league’s best, is the cushion if it comes down to a tie.
