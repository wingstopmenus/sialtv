Where he fits

There is no match to describe yet, so the honest picture is the squad around him.

An all-rounder’s value is that he covers two jobs, and Milan Bohara covers the two the Royals already have filled. Dipendra Singh Airee captains the side as an all-rounder — 90 runs and 5 wickets this season — and Saif Ali Zaib plays as the overseas all-rounder, batting in all six matches. With both fit and playing, there is no gap for a third all-rounder, and the Royals’ four wins from six matches have given the captain no reason to change a winning combination.

His chance, if it comes, is the same as every squad player’s: the last league match against Kathmandu Gorkhas on Friday 13 November, or the playoffs that follow. An all-rounder is the first player a captain reaches for when a bowler is injured or a batter is out of form, because he can do either job. Until then, his Season 3 story is the wait — listed, available, and still to play.

The team around him

Sudurpaschim Royals have four wins from six matches in Season 3 and sit second on 8 points, level with Kathmandu Gorkhas at the top and behind only on net run rate (+0.069). The playoff place is already secure — the only question left is whether they finish in the top two and go straight to Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 17 November, or in third or fourth and play the Eliminator on Wednesday 18 November. The last league match decides it: Kathmandu Gorkhas on Friday 13 November, 12:30 PM NST (11:45 AM PKT, 6:45 AM UTC), at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. A win takes the Royals to 10 points, and only Lumbini Lions could still finish above them.
