Season 3 summary

Kathmandu Gorkhas bought Dayanand Mandal to bowl, and five matches into NPL Season 3 the ball has still not reached his hand. He was picked up in the auction at the base price of NPR 2 lakh as a bowler, one of the domestic signings around which the Gorkhas built their squad — and through the first five matches he has no batting or bowling entry in the Season 3 stats file: no runs, no wickets, no overs bowled.

That does not mean he has not been part of the season. In a 16-man squad, several players spend the league stage training, travelling and waiting — and the bowlers waiting behind Kathmandu's first-choice attack have had a long wait, because the attack has barely changed. Karan KC has bowled in all five matches. Shahab Alam has bowled in four, Santosh Yadav in four, Sahan Arachchige in three. When an attack is taking wickets and winning four out of five, the captain has little reason to change it.

Mandal's route into the side is the same as any squad bowler's: the nets at Kirtipur, where conditions reward bowlers who attack the stumps and vary their pace on a surface that can grip. Kathmandu have two league matches left — Sudurpaschim Royals on 13 November and Pokhara Avengers on 15 November — and a playoff campaign after that. A bowler's chance in this format often comes late, through an injury, a match-up, or a dead rubber.
