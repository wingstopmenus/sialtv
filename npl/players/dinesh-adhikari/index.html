Match-winning innings

Adhikari is a Nepal all-rounder from Achham who was retained in June 2026 without going near the auction. The reason is 13 balls long. Opening the chase in the 2025 final against Sudurpaschim Royals, he smashed 42 off 13 deliveries with five sixes and a four to kill a target of 86 — and the Lions were champions.

It is the innings that defines him, and the reason the franchise kept him before Season 3. He is also a bowler of record: his best bowling is five for 27 against Koshi, the kind of figure that says the all-round label is earned rather than given. A player who can win a final in 13 balls and take five wickets in a game is not a squad filler; he is the player a captain sends in when a game needs breaking open.

Debut wait

Adhikari’s name does not appear in any of the 21 published scorecards of NPL Season 3 so far. He has not played a game for the Lions this season — which is the strangest line in the squad list, given what he did in the final last year.

The reason is the shape of the side. The middle order has run on Rohit Paudel, Sundeep Jora and the overseas batters, and the all-round slots have belonged to the captain and Sher Malla — and the Lions have won three of their five games without needing to change a winning side. A floater’s chance comes when a game drifts, when a chase needs breaking open, or when the captain wants a fresh hitter against a specific attack. Lumbini have two league games left, against Karnali on Thursday and Biratnagar on Saturday, and the second of those may be the moment: if the playoff place is already secured, there is no better game to give a final hero his first game of the defence.

Squad role

Adhikari’s natural home in this side is as a floater — a batter who can go in anywhere from one to seven, wherever the game needs him, and a bowler who can take five wickets when the ball is in his hand. That is a rare combination in a 13-player squad, and it is why he was retained without an auction.

The Lions are third in the NPL Points Table with seven points from five games and the league’s best net run rate at +1.501, defending the title they won in 2025. Every result is on the NPL Results page. The title defence has been built on the same core that won it — Paudel, Malla, Jora, Nath, Gautam, Bhandari, all retained — and Adhikari is part of that core, even if he has not played yet. The playoffs are won by players who have done it before, and nobody in the squad has done it in fewer balls than he has.
