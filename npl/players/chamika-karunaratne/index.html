Season 3 summary

Chamika Karunaratne has scored 66 runs in NPL Season 3, at a strike rate of 146.7, and taken four wickets in four matches. He is a bowling all-rounder from Sri Lanka, and one of Pokhara’s overseas signings.

Bowling all-rounder. Overseas signing. Captain: Kushal Bhurtel. Head coach: Ajantha Mendis.

Edirimuni Chamika Dinushan Perera Karunaratne was born on 29 May 1996 in Colombo, Sri Lanka: a right-handed batter and right-arm fast-medium bowling all-rounder. He made his international debut for Sri Lanka in the Canberra Test against Australia on 1 February 2019, followed by ODI and T20I debuts in 2021. He has represented Sri Lanka in 1 Test, 26 ODIs and 45 T20Is, and was in Sri Lanka’s squads for the 2021 and 2022 T20 World Cups. Kolkata Knight Riders bought him at the 2022 IPL auction.

His season has been four games of two disciplines. The batting: 4 off 11 against Janakpur Bolts, 25 off 17 against Lumbini Lions, 2 off 6 against Chitwan Rhinos and 35 not out off 11 against Karnali Yaks. The bowling: 2 for 23, 0 for 6, 2 for 34 and 0 for 21. The 35 not out is the innings that defines him — a finisher’s knock, three fours and three sixes, that turned a good total into a record one.

Season 3 analysis

The batting numbers are the story of a finisher: 66 runs at an average of 22.00 and a strike rate of 146.7, the second-fastest in the Pokhara side among batters who have faced ten balls or more. The 35 not out off 11 balls in Match 19 is the fastest innings of consequence in Pokhara’s season — six boundaries in eleven balls, in a game his side won by 63 runs. The 25 off 17 against Lumbini Lions, with two sixes, is the other innings that mattered, in a game Pokhara lost by eight wickets.

The bowling is honest rather than spectacular: four wickets in four matches at an average of 21.00 and an economy of 8.40. His best figures, 2 for 23 against Janakpur Bolts, came in a nine-run defeat; his 2 for 34 against Chitwan Rhinos came in a 30-run defeat. He has played four matches, all of them since his arrival in Match 9, and the run-in needs both his disciplines again.
