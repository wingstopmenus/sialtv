Anil Kumar Sah is the retained senior of Janakpur Bolts, the captain who led the franchise to its inaugural 2024 NPL title. Janakpur paid Rs 1.5 million for him at the inaugural auction — the most expensive player sold there — securing him through a lucky draw after all eight franchises showed interest. He moves into Season 3 under new captain Harmeet Singh after a Season 2 of shared captaincy.

His standing in Nepali cricket keeps rising: in August 2026 he was named to lead Nepal A in the Top End T20 Series. He was born in Kalaiya, Bara, and his connection to this franchise is the deepest in the squad — no other Bolt has captained the side to a title.

Squad depth

A side that has lost four of six matches, with nobody in the side past 150 runs for the season, leaving its title-winning captain on the bench is a genuine puzzle. The top order has been the problem all season: Janakpur were bowled out for 93 in Match 21, with Trit Raj Das’s 21 the top score, and the side has crossed 150 only twice in six matches. Sah’s experience — a title as captain, a Nepal A captaincy — is exactly the kind of thing a side in a shootout usually reaches for.

The counter-argument is selection, not sentiment. Santosh Karki, the only keeper among the auction buys, is also waiting for a game, and the XI that has played six matches has kept the same shape. But Friday is not a game for shape. It is a game Janakpur must win to stay alive, and the man who has already won this tournament as captain is sitting in the squad. If the Bolts lose without him, the question will follow them into the off-season.
