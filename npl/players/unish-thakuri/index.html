The Match 3 spell is the whole story so far: 3 for 22 in four overs against Janakpur Bolts, as Karnali defended 132 and won by 19 runs. Hussain Talat took 3 for 26 in the same game, but Thakuri’s were the best figures of the match — and they came on the opening night, in the game that set the season up.

Since then the returns have been tidy rather than wicket-taking: 0 for 26 in three overs against Biratnagar, 0 for 13 in a single over against the Royals. Three wickets at an average of 20.33 and an economy of 7.63 is a solid line for a 20-year-old off-spinner, and the economy is the second-best among Karnali’s regular bowlers. With the bat he has faced a single ball, unbeaten on 0 — the innings have not needed him yet.

Opening-night spell

Karnali posted 132 for 9 against Janakpur Bolts in Match 3, a total that needed defending, and Thakuri’s 3 for 22 in four overs did the defending. Janakpur were bowled out for 113, and the Yaks won by 19 runs — the first win of their season, built on the off-spinner’s spell.

It was the best bowling return of the match, better than Hussain Talat’s 3 for 26 in the same innings. Opening nights set tones, and Thakuri’s set this one: a young bowler trusted with four overs in a low total, and trusted again in the next two games he played.

Bowling style

Thakuri is the classic young off-spinning all-rounder: right-hand bat, off-break bowler, useful in both disciplines without yet owning either. His off-break turns the ball away from left-handers, which gives him a natural matchup role in a squad whose frontline spin leans left-arm — variety matters, and his is the variety the attack otherwise lacks alongside Pawan Sarraf and Dipendra Rawat.

Karnali signed him for NPR 2 lakh in the inaugural 2024 auction and retained him for Season 3, naming him sixth in the seven-player retention list announced on 14 June 2026. He has spent two full seasons inside the Yaks’ systems under head coach Gyanendra Malla. Born on 28 February 2006, he is still only 20, and his best cricket is the part the franchise is betting on.

Remaining fixtures

Karnali sit seventh on four points from five matches, with a net run rate of minus 0.846 — the worst in the league. The full table is on the NPL Points Table, and the remaining fixtures are on the NPL Schedule. Two league games are left: Lumbini Lions on Thursday 12 November and Chitwan Rhinos on Sunday 15 November. For Thakuri, the run-in is a chance to add to the three wickets — the off-break against Lumbini’s left-handers is exactly the matchup he was kept for.
