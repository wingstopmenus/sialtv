Season 3 in numbers

Two matches, three wickets, 30 runs conceded in eight overs. Gautam has bowled the fewest overs of any Lumbini bowler this season — and conceded the fewest runs per over of any of them. His economy of 3.75 is more than a run and a half better than the next-best Lions bowler.

With the bat he has one innings — 16 off 17 balls against Kathmandu, the second-highest score in a Lumbini total of 124 — but his value is the ball: three wickets, one maiden, and an economy no other Lion can match.

Bowling style

Gautam is a slow left-arm orthodox bowler, retained in June 2026 as one of seven players kept from the title-winning squad, and he has represented the franchise in every NPL season. His two for 15 in the 2025 final kept Sudurpaschim Royals from any recovery, closing a 12-wicket campaign in which he shared the playoff wicket tally with Ruben Trumpelmann.

The 2025 formula paired him with Sher Malla through the middle overs, and it is the champions’ identity: two finger spinners, bowling in tandem, squeezing the scoring until the batters make a mistake. In Season 3 that pairing has been used only twice — Gautam has played two of the five games — and the question for the last two league matches is whether the captain goes back to it. Ajay Chauhan, a slow left-arm spinner bought at the July auction, waits behind him as the direct cover. But nobody in the squad bowls left-arm spin at 3.75 an over, and in a playoff race that will be decided by small margins, that is the number that matters.

Two spells, 30 runs in eight overs, three wickets — and the same figure, 15 runs, in both games. Against Pokhara he took one for 15 in four overs as the Avengers were held to 97 for 9 and the Lions won by eight wickets. Against Kathmandu he took two for 15 in four overs, the joint-best figures in a game the Lions lost by 36 runs, and then made 16 with the bat when the chase fell apart. The maiden came in one of those two spells — the only maiden bowled by a Lion this season — and it is the number that explains his role: he does not just take wickets, he stops the scoring.

How many wickets has Abhishesh Gautam taken in NPL 2026?

Three wickets in two matches, at an economy of 3.75 runs per over — the best economy of any Lumbini Lions bowler this season — with best figures of 2 for 15 against Kathmandu Gorkhas. Figures cover matches 1–21.

A slow left-arm orthodox spinner who bowls the middle overs in tandem with Sher Malla. He was retained from the 2025 title-winning squad and has played for the franchise in every NPL season.
