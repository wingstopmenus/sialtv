Season 3 summary

Arjun Gharti has scored 65 runs in NPL Season 3, keeping wicket for Pokhara Avengers. He was bought at the July 6, 2026 auction for NPR 15 lakh — more than half of the franchise’s NPR 26.75 lakh auction spend.

Wicketkeeper-batter. Auction buy, NPR 15 lakh. Captain: Kushal Bhurtel. Head coach: Ajantha Mendis.

The price was a statement. Pokhara made the wicketkeeping slot the top priority of their rebuild, and Gharti was the answer: the only specialist wicketkeeper in the squad, which makes his place in every XI close to automatic. A keeper who bats settles two positions at once, and for a side rebuilding under a new head coach, that certainty mattered more than any other signing.

His season has been steady rather than spectacular. The 40 not out off 25 balls against Sudurpaschim Royals in the opener was the innings that justified the price — the highest score of his four games — and since then he has made 7, 6 and 12. No fifties, no ducks, and a strike rate of 92.9 that says he has been batting in difficult phases rather than feasting on them.

Season 3 analysis

The four innings tell a clear story. He made 40 not out off 25 balls against Sudurpaschim Royals in Match 4, with four fours and a six — the highest score in Pokhara’s 145 that day. Then 7 off 15 against Janakpur Bolts, 6 off 20 against Lumbini Lions and 12 off 10 against Chitwan Rhinos. The 40 not out is the outlier; the rest is a keeper-batter doing the hard middle-order work while the top order around him fails.

His value is not only in the runs. As the only specialist keeper in the squad, Gharti has kept wicket in every match Pokhara have played, and his glovework is the one settled part of a side that has changed its shape around him. The runs need to grow — 65 in four games at an average of 21.67 is honest, not match-winning — but the role is his, and the two remaining matches are his chance to turn a solid season into a decisive one.
