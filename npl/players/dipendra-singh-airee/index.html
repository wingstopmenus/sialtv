Season 3 summary

Dipendra Singh Airee has scored 90 runs and taken 5 wickets in NPL Season 3 as captain of Sudurpaschim Royals, and his 51 not out against Pokhara Avengers is the only fifty by any Royals batter this season. He has played all six matches, batted in all six, and taken a wicket in every match he has bowled.

All-rounder. Retained for Season 3. Head coach: Misbah-ul-Haq. Captain: Dipendra Singh Airee.

He was named player of the match in Match 4 against Pokhara Avengers (51 not out off 36 and 1 for 21).

Season 3 analysis

The fifty is the headline. Chasing 146 against Pokhara Avengers in Match 4, he made 51 not out off 36 balls and took the Royals home by six wickets — and he was named player of the match, having also taken 1 for 21. His other innings have been small: 27 against Lumbini Lions, 1 against Biratnagar Kings, 0 against Chitwan Rhinos, 10 against Karnali Yaks and 1 against Janakpur Bolts. One big knock, five quiet ones, and a batting average of 18.00 that hides how much the one big knock mattered.

With the ball he is the most reliable bowler in the side. Five matches bowled, five wickets: 1 for 31, 1 for 21, 1 for 16, 1 for 16 and 1 for 15, at an economy of 5.26 — the best economy among the Royals’ regular bowlers. No other Royals bowler has taken a wicket in every match he has bowled. As captain he has taken the side to second in the table on 8 points, level with Kathmandu Gorkhas, and already certain of a playoff place. The question for the run-in is whether the bat can produce a second fifty to go with the first.

How many runs has Dipendra Singh Airee scored in NPL 2026?
