Squad role

There is no match to describe yet, so the honest picture is the squad around him.

Three keepers in one squad is a luxury, and Tek Rawat is the third of them. Binod Bhandari has kept in the Royals’ matches all season and batted in five of them, and Dipak Bohara — the second keeper, listed at Rs 10 lakh — is himself still waiting for a first match. For the third keeper to play, two keepers would have to be unavailable, or the side would have to pick him as a batter alone, and neither has happened in six matches.

That does not make his place meaningless. Squads carry three keepers precisely because keeping is a specialist job that cannot be improvised, and a long tournament with a playoff run can change things quickly. His chances are the last league match against Kathmandu Gorkhas on Friday 13 November, or the playoffs that follow — and if his chance comes, it will be his first NPL appearance of the season.
