Senior role

Yadav is a Nepal batting all-rounder who was bought for NPR 2 lakh at the July 2026 auction after a decade of domestic cricket with Madhesh Province. At 33, he is the oldest player in the Lumbini Lions squad — and the most experienced domestic cricketer in it.

His March 2026 PM Cup displays won games with bat and ball, which is why a champion side spent a squad place on a 33-year-old: he knows the league, too, from five games for the Janakpur Bolts in 2024. His T20 numbers are modest but complete — a best score of 40 off 28 balls, a fastest innings of 36 not out off 14, and best bowling of two for 10, with two for 16 as the other marker. Nothing in that line is spectacular; everything in it says a cricketer who can do both jobs when asked.

Debut wait

Yadav’s name does not appear in any of the 21 published scorecards of NPL Season 3 so far. He has not played a game for the Lions this season, and the reason is the simplest one in team sport: the two roles he covers are already filled by players the captain trusts more.

The all-round slots in the eleven belong to Rohit Paudel, the captain, and Sher Malla, the leading wicket-taker in the squad — and the middle order has run on Sundeep Jora and the overseas batters around them. A 33-year-old signed as cover does not displace a captain or a leading wicket-taker; he waits for the game where one of them needs a rest, or where the match-up suits a second batting all-rounder. Lumbini have two league games left, against Karnali on Thursday and Biratnagar on Saturday, and the second of those may be the moment: if the playoff place is already secured, the captain has every reason to rest a senior player and give the veteran his game.

Squad role

Yadav is the squad’s veteran depth, signed to cover two roles at once — a middle-order bat and a bowling option — in a single squad place. That is the value of a batting all-rounder in a 13-player squad: he is the cover for two different players, and the insurance against two different problems.

The Lions are third in the NPL Points Table with seven points from five games and the league’s best net run rate at +1.501, defending the title they won in 2025. Every result is on the NPL Results page. A title defence is won by the eleven, but it is kept alive by the squad — and the squad’s oldest player, with a decade of domestic cricket and a PM Cup behind him this year, is the part of it that has not been used yet. The playoffs, if Lumbini get there, are exactly the kind of games where a veteran who has won matches with bat and ball in the same season gets his chance.
