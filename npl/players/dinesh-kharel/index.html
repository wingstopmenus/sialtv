Franchise history

Dinesh Kharel has scored seven runs in NPL Season 3, in two matches. He is an all-rounder, retained through the June 2026 retention window, and he is heading into a third campaign in Pokhara colours.

All-rounder. Retained through the June 2026 retention window. Captain: Kushal Bhurtel. Head coach: Ajantha Mendis.

Three seasons with one franchise is its own statement. Through two playoff misses, a coaching change and a full rebuild, Pokhara kept coming back to Kharel — and the verified record does not specify his playing role, so the longevity is the evidence. He is the squad’s institutional memory at Kirtipur’s TU Ground: for new head coach Ajantha Mendis and six auction buys who have never worn the franchise’s colours, Kharel is the guide to the ground’s moods and the dressing room’s routines.

His season on the field has been thin: 4 off 7 balls against Sudurpaschim Royals in the opener, 3 off 9 against Janakpur Bolts, and no game since. He has not bowled in the tournament. The value Pokhara kept him for is not in those seven runs — it is in three seasons of knowing how this franchise works, and in a dressing room full of new faces.

Season 3 analysis

Seven runs in two innings, at a strike rate of 43.8, is not a batting record to dwell on. The 4 against Sudurpaschim Royals and the 3 against Janakpur Bolts both came in defeats, and both came down the order in innings that never got going. His average of 3.50 is the lowest among Pokhara’s batters who have played twice — but averages over two innings say almost nothing, and Kharel’s selection was never about two innings.

The question for the run-in is whether experience earns him a recall. Pokhara have two must-win matches left, and a side under that pressure sometimes turns to the player who has been here before. Kharel has played in two Pokhara campaigns that ended in playoff misses; the third will be decided in the next two games, and his knowledge of this ground and this dressing room is the one thing no signing can buy.
