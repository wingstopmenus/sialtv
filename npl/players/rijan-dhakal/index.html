Season 3 in numbers

Batting — Season 3

Bowling — Season 3

Ten wickets at 8.10 apiece, at an economy of 6.75 — the second-best wicket haul in the league, and the most wickets any Chitwan bowler has ever taken in a single NPL season so far. The spells: 2 for 25 against Biratnagar in Match 2, 1 for 18 against the Royals in Match 11, 4 for 24 against Pokhara in Match 17, and 3 for 14 against Kathmandu in Match 20. A wicket in every game, and a strike rate of a wicket every 7.2 balls. Every Chitwan result is on the NPL Results page.

With the bat, a single entry: 1 not out off 1 ball against Kathmandu, the last man in as Chitwan fell 10 runs short. It is the only time he has been needed with the bat — the attack is his job, and the attack is where the season is being won.

Spell by spell

The 4 for 24 against Pokhara was the season’s best so far: four wickets in four overs, in a 30-run win, the spell that broke the middle order and turned a contest into a comfortable victory. The 3 for 14 against Kathmandu came in a losing cause — 180 for 8 proved ten runs too many — but it was the tightest bowling of the game, and it kept Chitwan in a chase that could have drifted away.

The quieter spells matter too. The 1 for 18 against the Royals came in a single over — 18 runs for one wicket in the game Chitwan lost by five wickets — and the 2 for 25 against Biratnagar opened the season with wickets in the win. Consistency is the story: four games, four spells, ten wickets, no blank.

Price bracket

Rijan Dhakal was one of Chitwan’s premium domestic picks at the inaugural 2024 auction, bought for NPR 10 lakh in Category B alongside Kamal Singh Airee. The franchise has retained him twice since — for 2025 and for Season 3 — a run of three consecutive squad-building exercises reaching the same conclusion. The spending is the scouting report: a first-XI contributor the Rhinos consider difficult to replace.

Ten lakh was premium-domestic money at the inaugural auction — the same bracket as Airee’s capture — and the franchise has now reached the same conclusion three times: bought in 2024, retained for 2025, retained again for Season 3. You do not retain a 10-lakh domestic twice on sentiment. This season, with 10 wickets in four games, the money looks like the bargain of the squad.

Remaining fixtures

Chitwan are fourth after Match 21, with five points from five games and a net run rate of +0.009. The full table is on the NPL Points Table, and the remaining fixtures are on the NPL Schedule. Two league games are left: Janakpur Bolts on Friday 13 November and Karnali Yaks on Sunday 15 November. Win both and the Rhinos reach nine points — enough to guarantee a playoff place.

Between them, Dhakal and Kamal Airee have taken 19 of the 33 wickets Chitwan have claimed in the four matches that produced a result. One more wicket takes Dhakal past Harmeet Singh and to the top of the league chart — and two wins take Chitwan to the playoffs.
