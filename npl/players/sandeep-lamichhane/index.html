Season 3 in numbers

Four matches, 1 wicket at an economy of 7.59, and 3 runs with the bat. The captain has bowled 13.5 overs in Season 3 — the most of any Biratnagar bowler — and taken one wicket, against Karnali Yaks.

The single wicket came in the 187-run chase against Karnali, his 1 for 35 in three and a half overs. The other three games brought nothing: 0 for 23 against Chitwan Rhinos, 0 for 27 against Janakpur Bolts, and 0 for 20 against Kathmandu Gorkhas. The economy rate of 7.59 is respectable — he is not being hit around the ground — but a wrist-spinner’s job is wickets, and the wickets are missing.

With the bat he has barely been needed: 0 from 2 balls against Chitwan, and 3 not out from 5 balls against Kathmandu. He bats at ten or eleven, so the bat is not the measure of his season. The measure is the one wicket, against a 2025 return of 17.

Captaincy

Lamichhane is Nepal’s all-time leading wicket-taker in both ODIs and T20Is, and the captain and marquee player of Biratnagar Kings. Retained on 16 June 2026, he was the joint-leading wicket-taker of NPL 2025 with 17 wickets — level with Sher Malla and Abinash Bohara — while captaining the side to the playoffs.

His arc is without parallel in Nepali cricket: sold for INR 20 lakh at the 2018 IPL auction at eighteen, a CAN A+ central contract at Rs 120,000 a month, and the national bowling records in both white-ball formats. He sat at the Kings’ auction table on 6 July, when the franchise spent NPR 37.25 lakh on eight domestic players.

Season 3 is his third campaign in Kings colours, and the attack is built around his four overs. He bowls right-arm leg-break with a big-spinning stock ball and the googly as his main variation — flight to invite the drive, drift to beat the edge. The method is wicket-taking rather than containing, which is why one wicket in four games reads as a drought rather than a role change.

The Karnali game was his best night and his worst result at once: 1 for 35 as Sompal Kami hit 62 not out and took 4 for 33 to win the game for Karnali. Against Chitwan he bowled four overs for 23 without a wicket in the eight-run defeat; against Janakpur he bowled four for 27 in the two-run win; against Kathmandu he bowled two for 20 as the Kings were bowled out for 83.

He did not play the five-over game against Sudurpaschim Royals — the one Biratnagar win in which the captain’s four overs were not needed. In a five-over game there is no room for a wrist-spinner to work through a spell, and the Kings won without him.

How many wickets has Sandeep Lamichhane taken in NPL 2026?

Yes. Lamichhane captains Biratnagar Kings in NPL Season 3 and is also Nepal’s all-time leading white-ball wicket-taker. He led the Kings to the playoffs in 2025.
