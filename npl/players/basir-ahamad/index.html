Player profile

Basir Ahamad is a left-handed middle-order batter and slow left-arm orthodox bowler from Kapilvastu, retained by Biratnagar Kings on 16 June 2026 for a third NPL campaign. A Nepal international with 24 ODI caps and 11 T20I caps, he was the Kings’ leading run-scorer in NPL 2024 and made 117 runs in seven matches in 2025.

His international timeline is brisk: ODI debut against Namibia on 11 July 2022, T20I debut against Kenya six weeks later, and a breakout 2025 Quadrangular T20I Series — 110 runs in three innings at a strike rate of 144.7 with a high score of 80. His T20I strike rate of 135.64 makes him one of the squad’s most reliable middle-order options on paper.

As a left-hander in a squad whose other senior batters bat right-handed, he gives the Kings natural left-right combinations through the middle, complicating bowlers’ lines and field settings. His slow left-arm orthodox is the second string to his batting: a containing option the captain can turn to for an over or two when the match-up suits.
