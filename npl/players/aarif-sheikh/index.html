Season 3 analysis

The pattern of his season is clear from the match list. He made 0 against Lumbini Lions in the opener, then 17 not out off 8 balls in the rain-shortened five-over match against Biratnagar Kings, 20 not out off 26 in the five-wicket win over Chitwan Rhinos, and 11 in each of the last two matches, against Karnali Yaks and Janakpur Bolts. Three of his five innings have reached double figures; none has passed 20.

That is both his value and his limit so far. A strike rate of 84.3 is the slowest among the Royals’ regular batters, but two not-outs in five innings show he is hard to shift once set — and in a side that has defended 120 in Match 21 and chased 130 in Match 11, a batter who stays in is worth having. The next step is a score past 20: the Royals’ only fifty this season belongs to captain Dipendra Singh Airee, and the middle order has not yet produced a second one.
