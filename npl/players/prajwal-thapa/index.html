Season 3 in numbers

No matches, no runs, no wickets. Thapa has not played in NPL Season 3 so far — and with Bam keeping in all five games and Dilsad Ali also in the squad, his chance may only come in the final league game.

That is not a judgement on the player. A third-choice keeper in a seven-match league season is insurance: the role exists so the side is covered if the first-choice keeper is injured or rested, and most seasons the insurance is never used.

His record below the NPL level is modest in volume — 2 matches and 12 runs at Under-19 ODI level, 57 runs at 11.40 with seven dismissals in seven Under-19 T20s, and one Prime Minister Cup match for Bagmati in 2022–23 in which he made 9 runs. The numbers say squad depth, not a first-team claim.

Match by match

Prajwal Thapa has not appeared in any of Biratnagar Kings’ five matches so far.

Lokesh Bam has kept wicket in all five games — and Bam is also the league’s top run-scorer, so there is no scenario in which the captain rests him for a keeper swap. Dilsad Ali, the second-choice keeper, has not played either, which tells the story: the Kings have had no reason to rotate the gloves.

The last two league games are the only realistic window. If the Kings win on Thursday and qualify before Saturday, the final game against Lumbini Lions could be a dead rubber — the one match in which a third-choice keeper might get a game.

Franchise move

Thapa is a right-handed wicketkeeper-batter from Kathmandu, bought by Biratnagar Kings for NPR 2 lakh at the July auction, the Category C base price. His route to the squad ran through Nepal’s age-group system and a series of national squad call-ups.

The early promise was real: selection in Nepal’s squad for the 2018 Under-19 World Cup, then a place in the squad for the 2023 ACC Emerging Teams Asia Cup and the 2024 Asia Cup qualification. Each call-up was a vote of confidence from the selectors — and each time, the step to the playing XI did not follow.

His youth numbers explain why: 2 matches and 12 runs at Under-19 ODI level with one dismissal, and 57 runs at 11.40 with seven dismissals in seven Under-19 T20s. Solid behind the stumps, light with the bat — the profile of a keeper who keeps the squad covered, and waits.
