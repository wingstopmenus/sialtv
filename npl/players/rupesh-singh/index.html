Auction signing

Singh is a right-handed batting all-rounder from Butwal, Rupandehi, who bowls right-arm medium-fast. Born on 22 August 2003, he was a mainstay of Nepal Under-19 and has four T20I caps for Nepal — and his 2023 NPL season is the reason the Kings paid the Category A base price.

That season he took 10 wickets in 5 matches at 13.70 with an economy of 7.27, including a best of 4 for 21 against Pokhara Avengers — and in the 2023 NPL final he took 3 for 16 against Lumbini Lions. A 10-wicket season and a final performance is the kind of record that makes a franchise bid 15 lakh.

The seasons since have been quieter: 3 wickets and 5 catches in 5 matches in 2024, and 0 wickets with 2 catches in 2 matches in 2025. The Kings bought the 2023 bowler; Season 3 has so far shown the 2024 version. Two games left is enough time for one performance to change the story.
