Rupesh Singh — the costliest Kings buy, still finding his game

Rupesh Singh was the costliest buy in Biratnagar Kings’ history — NPR 15 lakh, the Category A base price, outbidding Chitwan Rhinos — and in NPL Season 3 he has 26 runs and 2 wickets in four matches for the Biratnagar Kings. The all-rounder from Butwal has been picked in every game, and the return is still to come.

The 26 against Kathmandu Gorkhas was his only innings of substance — 26 from 27 balls in a game the Kings lost by nine wickets after being bowled out for 83. The other three games brought a duck against Chitwan Rhinos, and two games in which he did not bat at all.

The bowling has been steadier: 1 for 18 in one over against Sudurpaschim Royals, 1 for 21 in two overs against Janakpur Bolts, and 0 for 18 in two overs against Chitwan. Two wickets in five overs is a strike rate the captain can use — but 11.40 runs an over is the second-most expensive of the Kings’ regular bowlers.

The fifteen-lakh buy

Singh is a right-handed batting all-rounder from Butwal, Rupandehi, who bowls right-arm medium-fast. Born on 22 August 2003, he was a mainstay of Nepal Under-19 and has four T20I caps for Nepal — and his 2023 NPL season is the reason the Kings paid the Category A base price.

That season he took 10 wickets in 5 matches at 13.70 with an economy of 7.27, including a best of 4 for 21 against Pokhara Avengers — and in the 2023 NPL final he took 3 for 16 against Lumbini Lions. A 10-wicket season and a final performance is the kind of record that makes a franchise bid 15 lakh.

The seasons since have been quieter: 3 wickets and 5 catches in 5 matches in 2024, and 0 wickets with 2 catches in 2 matches in 2025. The Kings bought the 2023 bowler; Season 3 has so far shown the 2024 version. Two games left is enough time for one performance to change the story.

Where the Kings stand

Singh is the costliest buy in a side chasing two wins: Biratnagar Kings are sixth after Match 21 — four points from five games, with a net run rate of −0.820. The full table is on the NPL Points Table, and every result is on the NPL Results page.

Two wins from the last two games would take the Kings to 8 points and almost certainly into the playoffs — and a 15-lakh all-rounder who took 4 for 21 in this league in 2023 is exactly the profile that wins the second of those, against Lumbini Lions on Saturday, when the game is most likely to come down to the last few overs.
