Sikandar Raza — Chitwan Rhinos all-rounder, NPL 2026

Sikandar Raza is the most decorated overseas signing in Chitwan Rhinos’ history: Zimbabwe’s T20 captain, with 9,382 international runs and 252 wickets since his 2013 debut, and league titles with Lahore Qalandars, Trinbago Knight Riders and Dubai Capitals. The 40-year-old all-rounder was confirmed as a Rhinos overseas signing on August 27, 2026.

The innings: 15 against the Royals in Match 11, 37 off 16 against Pokhara in Match 17, and 2 against Kathmandu in Match 20. The bowling: 0 for 26, 0 for 19, then 2 for 19 in the Kathmandu game. The batting average of 18.00 undersells the innings that mattered. Raza’s value in this side is not accumulation — it is the over that changes a total, the spell that breaks a partnership. Three games in, the 37 against Pokhara is the proof of concept.

The 37 off 16

Against Pokhara Avengers in Match 17, Raza made 37 from 16 balls — four sixes, two fours — in an innings that turned a par total into a winning one. Chitwan made 151 for 6 and won by 30 runs; without the late acceleration, the total would have been chaseable, and the game would have been a contest instead of a comfortable win.

Nine thousand international runs do not come from one method — the technique adapts, rebuilding when early wickets fall, accelerating when the innings needs lifting. The 37 was the finisher’s version of that adaptability: in late, out early, and the total 20 runs better for it.

The Nepal connection

He has been to Nepal before: 129 runs and four wickets in five matches for Biratnagar Warriors in the 2021 Everest Premier League. The Nepal connection predates the contract — he has played in these conditions, before these crowds, before the NPL existed.

The 2022 T20 World Cup made him a global name: decisive in the wins over Ireland and Pakistan, the tournament’s standout all-round story. At 40, the arc is the victory lap that keeps winning — a T20 captain, a World Cup giant-killer, a league-title winner on three continents, arriving as the most decorated overseas name the Rhinos have ever signed.

The run-in, through the overseas star

Chitwan are fourth after Match 21, with five points from five games and a net run rate of +0.009. The full table is on the NPL Points Table, and the remaining fixtures are on the NPL Schedule. Two league games are left: Janakpur Bolts on Friday 13 November and Karnali Yaks on Sunday 15 November. Win both and the Rhinos reach nine points — enough to guarantee a playoff place.

For Raza, the run-in is where a player of his record earns his signing: the middle-order power and the off-spin in games where a playoff place is on the line.
