Known details

What is certain is the filter he survived: the Rhinos’ scouts watched domestic cricket for months and chose to spend one of six domestic buys on him at NPR 2 lakh, in a NPR 26 lakh auction where every lakh was argued over. He is in the verified fifteen-player squad for Season 3, and that is a real verdict from people who watched him play.

He was bought at the July 6, 2026 mega auction, one of six domestic purchases Chitwan made that day — alongside Ashok Dhami, Bibek Kumar Yadav, Bipin Rawal, Gautam KC and Mausam Dhakal. The auction record is the whole of the verified public record so far.

Context helps explain why the record is so thin. The six domestic buys were the last pieces of a squad built mostly on retention: seven locals kept from last season, three overseas signings, and six auction purchases to fill the gaps. In that kind of rebuild, the auction’s smallest signings get the least coverage — the headlines went to the 15-lakh capture of Bibek Kumar Yadav, and a 2-lakh domestic buy barely made the reports. Khan’s story, for now, is the story of the auction’s quiet end.
