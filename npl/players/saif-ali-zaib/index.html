Saif Ali Zaib has scored 92 runs in all six of Sudurpaschim Royals’ matches in NPL Season 3, the third-most by any Royals batter, with a top score of 35. He is the overseas all-rounder in the side, and while he has bowled just two overs all season, he has batted in every match.

Season 3 analysis

His six innings show a batter who contributes without dominating. He made 13 against Lumbini Lions, 15 against Pokhara Avengers, 17 off 10 against Biratnagar Kings, 2 against Chitwan Rhinos, 35 off 24 against Karnali Yaks — his best, in the Royals’ 176 for 6 — and 10 against Janakpur Bolts in Match 21. A strike rate of 104.5 and an average of 15.33: he gets in, he gets a start, and he gets out before the score becomes a big one.

The bowling is the puzzle. An all-rounder who has bowled two overs in six matches — 0 for 13 against Pokhara Avengers, and nothing since — is being picked as a batter, and the captain clearly prefers Kuggeleijn, Anil Kharel, Hemant Dhami and his own bowling for the overs. That is a fair call on the evidence: the attack has taken the Royals to second in the table. But it leaves a question for the run-in, against Kathmandu Gorkhas and then in the playoffs, whether a sixth bowling option is worth more than the runs he scores.

Frequently asked questions

How many runs has Saif Ali Zaib scored in NPL 2026?

92 runs in six matches, at a strike rate of 104.5, with a top score of 35 against Karnali Yaks. It is the third-highest total by a Sudurpaschim Royals batter this season, behind Brandon McMullen’s 124 and Ishan Pandey’s 95.

Has Saif Ali Zaib bowled in NPL 2026?

Barely. He has bowled just two overs all season — 0 for 13 against Pokhara Avengers in Match 4 — and has not bowled since. He is picked as a batter, and the Royals’ five main bowlers have done the work.

Is Saif Ali Zaib an overseas player for Sudurpaschim Royals?

Yes. He is the overseas name in the Royals’ verified 14-player squad list, alongside confirmed overseas signings Scott Kuggeleijn and Brandon McMullen, who are not in that list.
