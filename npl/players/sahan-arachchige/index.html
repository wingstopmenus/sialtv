Season 3 summary

Sahan Arachchige's Season 3 started later than his teammates'. He did not play in Kathmandu's first two matches — the defeat to Janakpur and the win over Karnali — and his first appearance came against Biratnagar Kings on 5 November, with the ball: 2 for 7 in two overs, as Biratnagar were bowled out for 83 and Kathmandu won by nine wickets. It was the cheapest spell of the season by any Kathmandu bowler.

The bat followed. Against Lumbini Lions on 7 November, he made 34 not out off 15 balls — three fours, a six — and took 1 for 0 in 0.3 overs, a spell so short it barely counts as one: one ball, no runs, one wicket. Against Chitwan Rhinos on 9 November, he made 33 off 20 balls and took 2 for 37. Three matches, and he has contributed in every one of them.

The background is a title-winning one. Arachchige's LPL campaign is the scouting report: 240 runs at a strike rate above 147, scored across all 11 matches, which means he produces against both pace and spin and does not need conditions to suit him. As a left-hander, his scoring zones matter tactically — he takes the attack to off-spin, and hits with the spin rather than against it. Kathmandu bought that, and in three matches they have had it.

Season 3 in numbers

Batting: 67 runs in two innings, strike rate 191.43, one not-out. Bowling: 5 wickets in three matches, average 8.80, best 2 for 7. The tables below are current through Match 20.

The strike rate of 191.43 is the fastest in the Kathmandu side by a distance — Karan KC's 147.27 is next — and the bowling average of 8.80 is the best in the side by a distance too. Five wickets in 39 balls is a wicket every 7.8 balls, the fastest strike rate of any Kathmandu bowler. Two matches is a small batting sample and three is a small bowling one, but both samples point the same way: Arachchige is the most efficient player in the squad, in both disciplines.

What the numbers do not show is the timing. His 34 not out against Lumbini came at the end of Kathmandu's 160 for 8, when the innings needed a finish. His 33 against Chitwan came in the middle of 180 for 8, when the innings needed a push. His wickets have come in the middle overs, where the game is won and lost in T20 cricket. Efficiency is one thing; efficiency at the right moment is another.

Match by match

Match 15, 5 November, vs Biratnagar Kings — 2 for 7 in 2 overs. His first appearance, and the cheapest spell of Kathmandu's season. Biratnagar were bowled out for 83 in 18.3 overs, and Arachchige's two overs cost seven runs with two wickets. He did not bat — Kathmandu needed only three batters to chase 84, and won by nine wickets in 9.4 overs. Match 18, 7 November, vs Lumbini Lions — 34 not out off 15 balls, 1 for 0 in 0.3 overs. The batting arrival. Kathmandu made 160 for 8, and Arachchige's unbeaten 34 — three fours, a six, at more than two runs a ball — was the finish the innings needed. With the ball, he bowled a single ball, conceded no runs, and took a wicket. Lumbini were bowled out for 124, and Kathmandu won by 36 runs. Match 20, 9 November, vs Chitwan Rhinos — 33 off 20 balls, 2 for 37 in 4 overs. His fullest game: 33 with the bat, in a 180 for 8 built on Rashid Khan's 52, and two wickets with the ball in a match Chitwan lost by 10 runs. The bowling was expensive — 37 runs in four overs — but the wickets were the ones that mattered, and the batting had already done its job.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Sahan Arachchige?

A Sri Lankan all-rounder, born 13 May 1996 in Ragama — a left-hand batter and right-arm off-break bowler. He is one of Kathmandu Gorkhas' three overseas signings for NPL Season 3, and his LPL title campaign brought 240 runs at a strike rate above 147 across 11 matches.

How many runs and wickets does Sahan Arachchige have in NPL 2026?

67 runs in two innings at a strike rate of 191.43, and 5 wickets in three matches at an average of 8.80. His best bowling is 2 for 7 against Biratnagar Kings, and his best batting is 34 not out against Lumbini Lions.

When did Sahan Arachchige first play for Kathmandu?

Against Biratnagar Kings on 5 November, in Kathmandu's third match of the season. He took 2 for 7 in two overs as Biratnagar were bowled out for 83, and Kathmandu won by nine wickets. He did not play in the first two matches, against Janakpur and Karnali.
