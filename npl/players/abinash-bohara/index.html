Season 3 analysis

His four spells tell the story. He went wicketless for 15 in three overs against Pokhara Avengers, took 2 for 28 in two overs in the rain-shortened five-over match against Biratnagar Kings, went wicketless for 31 in four overs against Chitwan Rhinos, and took 1 for 13 in a single over against Karnali Yaks. The wickets come in bursts; the runs come steadily, at 8.70 an over, the most expensive economy among the Royals’ regular bowlers.

The 2 for 28 against Biratnagar is his best work of the season — two wickets in a five-over game is a genuine contribution, even in a 35-run defeat. But the Chitwan spell, 0 for 31, shows the other side: when he misses, he goes for runs. With the bat he has scored 5 runs in two innings, including 2 not out in Match 21. His place for the run-in depends on whether the Royals want a wicket-taker or a container in the middle overs.
