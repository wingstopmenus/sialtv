Season 3 summary

Prashiddha Jaishi was bought to do two jobs for Kathmandu Gorkhas. Five matches into NPL Season 3, he has done neither on the field — no runs, no wickets, no overs — and his season is still a promise rather than a record. He was picked up in the auction at the base price of NPR 2 lakh as an all-rounder, one of the domestic signings around which the squad was built.

That is not a judgement on the player. It is a description of where he sits in a 16-man squad that has won four out of five matches with an unchanged core. Kathmandu's all-round slots have been filled by the captain Karan KC, the retained Mohammad Aadil Alam, and the overseas Sahan Arachchige — three players who have all contributed with bat and ball. When the men ahead of you are doing the job, the wait gets longer.

Jaishi's route into the side is the same as any squad all-rounder's: the nets at Kirtipur, where he trains daily alongside Aadil Alam — the retained all-rounder ahead of him — and watches how a 22-year-old with an ODI debut behind him prepares for a match. Kathmandu have two league matches left, against Sudurpaschim Royals on 13 November and Pokhara Avengers on 15 November, and a playoff campaign after that. An all-rounder's chance in this format often comes late, through an injury, a match-up, or a match that no longer matters for the table.
