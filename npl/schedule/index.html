Nepal Premier League 2026 Schedule & Match Results

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Fixtures & Results

Every league match is at the TU International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur. Times are in Nepal Standard Time (NST, UTC+5:45) — 12:30 PM NST is 11:45 AM PKT and 6:45 AM UTC, and 5:00 PM NST is 4:15 PM PKT and 11:15 AM UTC. The league stage is complete: 27 of the 28 matches produced a result and one was abandoned without a ball bowled. The playoffs run from Tuesday 17 November to Saturday 21 November, starting with Qualifier 1: Lumbini Lions vs Sudur Paschim Royals.
