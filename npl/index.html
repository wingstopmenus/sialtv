Playoffs

The top four teams on the points table qualify for the playoffs, all of them at the TU International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur. The winner of Qualifier 1 goes straight to the final; the loser of the Eliminator goes home, and the winner of Qualifier 2 takes the second place in the final.

  • Qualifier 1: Tuesday 17 November 2026.
  • Eliminator: Wednesday 18 November 2026.
  • Qualifier 2: Thursday 19 November 2026.
  • Final: Saturday 21 November 2026.

The playoff dates are set — which teams play each match depends on the final league standings. Every playoff starts at 4:30 PM NST.

Venue

All 32 matches of NPL Season 3 are played at one ground — the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Nepal. The league stage runs from 26 October to 15 November 2026, and the playoffs from 17 to 21 November.

One venue for the whole season means every team plays on the same pitch, in the same conditions — and the points table is a straight read of who handled Kirtipur best.

Tickets for every match at Kirtipur are on the NPL Tickets page.

Frequently asked questions

When does NPL Season 3 finish?

The league stage ends on Sunday 15 November 2026. The playoffs run from 17 to 21 November, and the final is on Saturday 21 November at the TU International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur.

How many teams play in the NPL?

Eight teams play in NPL Season 3: Kathmandu Gorkhas, Lumbini Lions, Sudurpaschim Royals, Chitwan Rhinos, Janakpur Bolts, Biratnagar Kings, Karnali Yaks and Pokhara Avengers.

How do teams qualify for the playoffs?

Each team plays 7 league matches. A win is worth 2 points and the top four teams on the points table qualify for the playoffs. Teams level on points are separated by net run rate.

Where are NPL matches played?

All 32 matches are played at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Nepal.

Where can I see the full NPL schedule and every scorecard?

On SialTV — the NPL Schedule page has every fixture and result, and the NPL Results page has every scorecard.

What happened in the latest match?

Sudurpaschim Royals beat Janakpur Bolts by 27 runs in Match 21 on 10 November — Royals 120/9, Bolts 93 all out. Brandon McMullen top-scored with 33 off 23, and Scott Kuggeleijn took 3 for 13 and was Player of the Match.

Who leads the runs and wickets?

Lokesh Bam leads the runs with 194 in 5 matches, and Harmeet Singh leads the wickets with 11 in 6 matches — both after Match 21.
