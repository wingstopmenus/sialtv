SialTV follows every NPL Season 3 match from the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. While a match is in progress the score on this page is refreshed from verified live sources, roughly every ten minutes, and the time of that last refresh is printed next to the score. Between matches the page shows the latest result and the next fixture, with the points table alongside. Once a match ends, the final result — and the points table it changes — is written into the page and the score feed, so the numbers here and on the NPL Results page always agree.

There is no ball-by-ball commentary here. For the full scorecard of a finished match, the NPL Results page lists every match with its scores and margins, and the NPL Points Table shows where the result leaves the standings.

Coming up

Match 25 is Janakpur Bolts against Chitwan Rhinos tonight, Friday 13 November 2026, at 5:00 PM NST (4:15 PM PKT, 11:15 AM UTC), at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. Janakpur sit sixth with 4 points and Chitwan fifth with 5 after Match 23 — a Bolts win takes them to 6 points and level with Biratnagar.

Three league matches remain after Match 25, the last of them on Sunday 15 November. The playoffs start with Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 17 November, then the Eliminator on Wednesday 18 November, Qualifier 2 on Thursday 19 November, and the final on Saturday 21 November — every playoff starts at 5:00 PM NST. The full fixture list is on the NPL Schedule page.
