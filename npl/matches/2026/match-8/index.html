01 — What happened

What happened

Rain cut Match 8 to five overs a side, and Biratnagar Kings made the most of the short game. They piled up 105 for 2, then held Sudurpaschim Royals to 70 for 3 to win by 35 runs at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur on Saturday, 31 October 2026, in the Nepal Premier League.

Shehan Jayasuriya set the pace with 39 off 11 balls, hitting five sixes, and Lokesh Bam added 25 off 10. Andre Russell finished the innings unbeaten on 29 off 10. Abinash Bohara took both Kings wickets to fall, for 28 runs in his two overs.

The Royals chase never got going. Sherfane Rutherford fell to the first ball, bowled by Russell, and Dipendra Singh Airee made only 1. Scott Kuggeleijn hit 28 not out off six balls, with four sixes, and Aarif Sheikh made 17 not out off eight, but 105 was too far away in 30 balls. Russell also took 1 for 8 in his two overs.

02 — The turning point

The turning point

Jayasuriya’s 11-ball 39 took the Kings to 68 for 1 inside three overs, and the total kept growing from there. The Royals then lost their first wicket to the first ball of the chase and were 7 for 2 early on. In a five-over game there was no time to rebuild, and Kuggeleijn’s late hitting only cut the margin.

03 — Top performers

Top performers

Andre Russell was Player of the Match for 29 not out off 10 balls and 1 for 8 in two overs. Jayasuriya’s 39 off 11 was the fastest innings of the match, and Bam’s 25 off 10 kept the scoring going between the two. For the Royals, Bohara’s 2 for 28 was the only wicket-taking spell, and Kuggeleijn’s 28 not out off six balls came too late to change the result.

04 — What it meant for the points table

What it meant for the points table

The win gave Biratnagar two points and left both sides on two points after three matches each — one win and two defeats apiece. Karnali Yaks stayed top of the table on four points.
