Kathmandu Gorkhas vs Sudurpaschim Royals, Friday, 13 November 2026 at 12:30 PM NST

The 24th match of the Nepal Premier League season is the first game of Friday 13 November 2026, starting at 12:30 PM NST — 6:45 AM UTC, 11:45 AM PKT — at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. It is the first of two matches on the day; Janakpur Bolts play Chitwan Rhinos in the evening game.

This is the first meeting of the two sides this season, and it is the biggest league match of the season so far. Kathmandu Gorkhas lead the table on 8 points from five matches with a net run rate of +1.168; Sudurpaschim Royals are second on 8 points from six matches with a net run rate of +0.082. The full scorecard will appear on this page once the match is played.

Both sides arrive on winning runs. Kathmandu have won four in a row since losing their opener; the Royals have won their last three. One of those runs ends on Friday, and the winner takes a grip on first place with it.

Kathmandu's season turned after the opening defeat. Janakpur Bolts beat them by 8 wickets in Match 5, bowling them out for 115, and since then they have not lost: a 4-wicket win over Karnali Yaks in Match 10, chasing 180/6; a 9-wicket win over Biratnagar Kings in Match 15, after bowling them out for 83; a 36-run win over Lumbini Lions in Match 18; and a 10-run win over Chitwan Rhinos in Match 20, defending 180/8. The batting has passed 160 three times in that run, and the bowling has kept every opponent at or below 180.

The Royals' path has been less smooth but just as effective. They lost the opener to Lumbini Lions by 7 wickets and lost a five-over game to Biratnagar Kings by 35 runs in Match 8, but they have beaten Pokhara Avengers by 6 wickets, Chitwan Rhinos by 5 wickets, Karnali Yaks by 39 runs and Janakpur Bolts by 27 runs in Match 21 — defending just 120/9 and bowling Janakpur out for 93. Three wins in a row, each in a different way, is the mark of a side that finds a way.

The points table and the playoff maths

The winner of this match is guaranteed a top-two finish. That is not a guess — it is the arithmetic. The winner reaches 10 points, and only Lumbini Lions, on 7 points with two games left, could reach 11; every other side can finish on 9 points at most. A top-two finish means a place in Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 17 November, with a second chance in Qualifier 2 even if that game is lost.

The loser is not out of the playoffs — far from it. Only three teams can finish above 8 points, so the loser, on 8 points, can finish no lower than fourth. But the route changes: instead of Qualifier 1, the loser would most likely go into the Eliminator on Wednesday 18 November, a knockout game with no second chance. For the Royals, this is also their last league match, so a defeat would leave them waiting on other results to confirm their final position.

Kathmandu have one more game after this, against Pokhara Avengers on Sunday. A win on Friday followed by a win on Sunday would give them 12 points and almost certainly first place. The top four qualify for the playoffs, with the final on Saturday 21 November, all playoff games at 4:30 PM NST at Kirtipur.

The players who can decide it

Season 3 numbers only, from matches 1 to 21. Every name below links to that player's profile.

David Warner leads Kathmandu's batting with 127 runs in four matches, including a highest score of 66 and a fifty, and Rashid Khan has 120 runs in four matches, including two fifties. Together they have given Kathmandu starts that the middle order has turned into totals of 160, 180 and 181.

With the ball, Karan KC has 7 wickets in five matches, and he captains the side. He is the bowler Kathmandu turn to when a partnership needs breaking, and his wickets have come at the moments that decided games.

For the Royals, Brandon McMullen has 124 runs in five matches and Dipendra Singh Airee has 90 runs with a fifty, while Scott Kuggeleijn and Anil Kharel have 8 wickets each. The Royals' strength is balance: no single star, but runs and wickets spread across the side, and a captain in Dipendra Singh Airee who has held the middle order together.

First meeting of the season

Kathmandu Gorkhas and Sudurpaschim Royals have not met before in Season 3, so this is their first meeting of the season. That makes Friday's game a genuine first look at how the top two match up — there is no earlier result to read, only two teams who have both beaten the same sides.

Both have beaten Chitwan Rhinos and Karnali Yaks, and the Royals have also beaten Pokhara Avengers. Kathmandu's wins have come by 4 wickets, 9 wickets, 36 runs and 10 runs; the Royals' by 6 wickets, 5 wickets, 39 runs and 27 runs. The margins say Kathmandu have been the more dominant side — their net run rate of +1.168 against the Royals' +0.082 agrees — but the Royals have won the tighter games, and a tight game is exactly what Friday promises to be.
