The turning point

Karnali lost their first wicket without a run on the board and were 26 for 3 inside five overs. Miller rebuilt from there, but every partner came and went — the biggest stand around him was worth 36. Royals' bowlers shared the wickets, and the asking rate kept climbing until the chase was gone.

Top performers

David Miller was Player of the Match despite finishing on the losing side, for an unbeaten 93 off 55 balls. For Royals, Saif Zaib's 35 set the tone, and Scott Kuggeleijn took 2 for 39. Dipendra Singh Airee went for only 16 runs in four overs, and Anil Kharel took 1 for 9 in two.

What it meant for the points table

The win took Sudurpaschim to six points, level with Lumbini Lions at the top of the table. Karnali stayed on four points.
