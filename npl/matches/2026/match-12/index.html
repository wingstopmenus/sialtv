The turning point

The finish decided it. Saif Hassan was still there on 71 when the Bolts needed 13 off the last over, and he fell off the final ball with the side two runs short. Russell’s four wickets included Saif, Anil Sah for a duck and Harmeet Singh for a duck, and no Bolts batter after Shusling passed 27.

Top performers

Andre Russell was Player of the Match for 52 not out off 29 balls and 4 for 45. Saif Hassan’s 71 off 48 was the top score of the match, and Imran Sheikh’s 56 and Lokesh Bam’s 50 set up the Kings total. Angelo Mathews took 2 for 25 for the Kings.

Points table

The win took Biratnagar to four points, and four teams now shared four points — Sudurpaschim Royals, Karnali Yaks, Janakpur Bolts and Biratnagar Kings. The Bolts stayed on four points after their second defeat in four matches.
