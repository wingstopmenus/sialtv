The turning point

Santosh Yadav's spell settled the match. The Kathmandu spinner took 3 for 11 in three overs, and his victims were the two men holding Biratnagar's innings together: Mathews, bowled for 22, and Rupesh, leg before for 26. Sahan Arachchige backed him up with 2 for 7 in two overs, and Biratnagar's middle order had no answer to either of them.

Top performers

Santosh Yadav was Player of the Match for 3 for 11. David Warner's unbeaten 44 off 24 balls, with three sixes, made the chase a formality. Karan KC took 2 for 17 and Arachchige 2 for 7, so six Kathmandu bowlers shared the ten wickets between them.

Points table

The win took Kathmandu to four points, level with Biratnagar. Both sides sat in the middle of the table, with the race for the top four still wide open.
