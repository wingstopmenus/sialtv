The turning point

The Avengers were 53 for 3 after Bhurtel’s dismissal in the seventh over, then lost four wickets for 34 runs across the middle overs. Kiran Thagunna’s 15 off five balls gave them a brief lift, but the asking rate kept climbing and the last pair could only add six runs in the final over.

Top performers

Trit Raj Das was Player of the Match for his 61 off 55. Moeen Ali’s 3 for 13, including both openers, and Harmeet Singh’s 2 for 20 did the damage with the ball. For Pokhara, Shakib’s 3 for 15 and Bhurtel’s 35 were the best of their day.

Points table

The win took Janakpur to four points from three matches — two wins and one defeat — level with Karnali Yaks at the top of the table. Pokhara stayed without a point after two defeats.
