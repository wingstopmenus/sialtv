The turning point

The match turned when Karan KC came in at 144 for 5 in the 17th over and hit 44 off 20 balls, with four fours and three sixes. His wicket at 160 for 6 left 21 to get, and Jahandad Khan’s unbeaten 22 off 10 closed the chase with two balls to spare.

Top performers

Karan KC was Player of the Match for 2 for 35 and 44 off 20 balls. Rashid Khan’s 50 off 33 was the top score of the chase, and Ariful Islam’s 44 off 22 set up Karnali’s total. Santosh Yadav’s 1 for 8 in two overs was the most economical spell of the match.

Points table

The win was Kathmandu’s first of the season and took them to two points. Karnali stayed on four points despite their first defeat, still level at the top with Janakpur.
