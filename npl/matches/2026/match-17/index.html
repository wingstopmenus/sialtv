The turning point

Pokhara were 103 for 4 in the 15th over and still in the game. Then the innings fell away: the last six wickets went for 18 runs, and Pokhara were all out for 121 in 19 overs. Rijan Dhakal took 4 for 24 and Kamal Airee 3 for 7 in two overs, including a maiden.

Top performers

Rijan Dhakal was Player of the Match for 4 for 24 in four overs. Sikandar Raza's 37 off 16 balls gave Chitwan the runs that made the difference, and Kamal Airee's 3 for 7 closed the door on the chase.

What it meant for the points table

The win took Chitwan to four points. Pokhara stayed without a point, still looking for their first win of the season.
