The turning point

Trumpelmann struck with the second ball of the match, removing Arjun Kumal for a duck, and Pokhara were 15 for 3 inside four overs. They never recovered — the middle order crawled to 52 for 7, and only Karunaratne’s 25 off 17 gave the total any shape.

Top performers

Ruben Trumpelmann was Player of the Match for 3 for 16 in four overs. Rohit Paudel’s unbeaten 47 off 32 balls led the chase, and Dickwella’s unbeaten 27 off 26 saw it home. For Pokhara, Karunaratne’s 25 was the only score above 14.

Points table

The win took Lumbini to five points and the top of the table — two wins and the shared point from the rained-off Match 7, still unbeaten. Pokhara stayed bottom on no points after three defeats.
