Chitwan Rhinos vs Karnali Yaks, Sunday, 15 November 2026 at 12:30 PM NST

The 27th match of the NPL season is the first game of Sunday 15 November 2026, the final day of the league stage, starting at 12:30 PM NST — 6:45 AM UTC, 11:45 AM PKT — at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. It is followed by the last league match of the season, Pokhara Avengers against Kathmandu Gorkhas, in the evening.

This is the first meeting of the two sides this season. Chitwan Rhinos sit fourth on 5 points from five matches; Karnali Yaks sit seventh on 4 points from five matches. Chitwan's result here decides their own fate; Karnali's decides whether they have anything left to play for in the evening. The full scorecard will appear on this page once the match is played.

The two sides arrive from opposite directions. Chitwan have alternated wins and defeats all season and have never lost two in a row; Karnali have lost their last three games, each one heavier than the last. Sunday morning is Karnali's chance to break that run — and Chitwan's chance to make sure the final day does not slip away from them.

Chitwan's season has been a study in narrow margins. They beat Biratnagar Kings by 8 runs in Match 2, had their game against Lumbini Lions abandoned in Match 7, lost to Sudurpaschim Royals by 5 wickets in Match 11, beat Pokhara Avengers by 30 runs in Match 17, and lost to Kathmandu Gorkhas by 10 runs in Match 20. Four of their five completed games have been decided by 10 runs or fewer, or by five wickets — and they have scored 129, 151 and 170 in their last three completed games, so the batting is in good order.

Karnali's season has gone the other way. They beat Janakpur Bolts by 19 runs in Match 3 and pushed Biratnagar Kings to the final over in Match 6, losing by 4 wickets after posting 186/7. Since then: Kathmandu Gorkhas chased 180/6 in Match 10, Sudurpaschim Royals beat them by 39 runs in Match 16, and Pokhara Avengers beat them by 63 runs in Match 19 after posting 217/3. Three straight defeats, and a bowling attack that has conceded 176 or more in each of them.

Chitwan arrive at their last league game with Friday's match against Janakpur Bolts still to play. A win on Sunday takes them to 7 points if they lost on Friday, and to 9 if they won — and with a net run rate of +0.009 they would be well placed for fourth place either way. A defeat leaves them on 5 points if they also lost on Friday, or 7 if they won that game, with no games left and the evening match to wait on.

For Karnali, a win on Sunday takes them to 6 points if they lost on Thursday, and to 8 if they won both games — and 8 points would keep a real hope alive, though they would still need other results to go their way and their net run rate of −0.846 to survive a tie. A defeat leaves them on 4 points if they also lost on Thursday, or 6 if they won that game, and ends their season either way. Karnali's problem is that even a win does not put qualification in their own hands — but a defeat ends the conversation entirely, and that is what Sunday morning is really about.

The top four qualify for the playoffs: Qualifier 1 on Tuesday 17 November, the Eliminator on Wednesday 18 November, Qualifier 2 on Thursday 19 November and the final on Saturday 21 November, all at 4:30 PM NST at Kirtipur. Kathmandu Gorkhas lead the table on 8 points, Sudurpaschim Royals are second on 8 and Lumbini Lions third on 7.

Players to watch

Season 3 numbers only, from matches 1 to 21. Every name below links to that player's profile.

Ravi Bopara has 171 runs in four matches for Chitwan, the second-most in the league, with a highest score of 55 and a fifty to his name. He has been Chitwan's most reliable batter in a season of close games, and Karnali's bowlers have conceded 176 or more in their last three matches — exactly the kind of attack a batter in Bopara's form wants to face.

With the ball, Rijan Dhakal has 10 wickets in four matches, the second-most in the league, and he has taken them at a rate that has kept Chitwan in games their batting could not finish. If he strikes early on Sunday, Karnali's fragile top order has very little room to recover.

For Karnali, Ariful Islam has 143 runs in five matches with 12 sixes, and Sompal Kami has 9 wickets in five matches plus 93 runs with the bat. They are Karnali's two match-winners, and both will need big games: Ariful with the bat against the league's second-best wicket-taker, Sompal with the ball against its second-best run-scorer.

Head to head

Chitwan Rhinos and Karnali Yaks have not met before in Season 3, so this is their first meeting of the season. It is a first meeting with everything on it: Chitwan's last game, Karnali's last chance, and a table where a single point separates them.

Both sides have lost to Kathmandu Gorkhas and Sudurpaschim Royals. Chitwan beat Pokhara Avengers by 30 runs in Match 17; Karnali lost to them by 63 runs in Match 19. The shared results say little; the recent form says Chitwan are the steadier side and Karnali are the more desperate one, and on a final day, desperation is not always a disadvantage.

Frequently asked questions

When does Chitwan Rhinos vs Karnali Yaks start?

Match 27 starts at 12:30 PM NST on Sunday 15 November 2026 at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. That is 6:45 AM UTC and 11:45 AM PKT. It is the first of two matches on the final day of the league stage.

Is this Chitwan Rhinos' last league match?
