Top performers

Player of the Match Rahul Yadav took 4 for 31 in four overs to break Kathmandu's batting. Moeen Ali's unbeaten 58 off 51 then guided the chase, with Saif Hassan's 40 not out off 39 at the other end — the pair were never separated after the second wicket fell.

Player of the Match: Rahul Yadav (Janakpur Bolts, 4/31).

Points table

Two points for Janakpur, none for Kathmandu. It was the Bolts' first win of the season, after their opening defeat to Karnali, and the Gorkhas' first defeat in their first game of the campaign. The NPL 2026 season was five matches old, and the table was still wide open.
