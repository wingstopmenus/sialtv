The turning point

Samra's hundred was the turning point, and it came quickly — the second wicket did not fall until 163 in the 16th over. By the time Karnali batted, the asking rate was over ten an over from the start, and wickets fell too often for any partnership to build.

Top performers

Yuvraaj Samra was Player of the Match for 102 off 48 balls. Kushal Bhurtel's 62 off 39 balls and Karunaratne's unbeaten 35 off 11 gave Pokhara the highest total of the season, and Mahedi Hasan took 3 for 24 with the ball.

Points table

The win gave Pokhara their first two points of the season, after starting the campaign without a win. Karnali stayed on four points.
