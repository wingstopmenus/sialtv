Top performers

Player of the Match Kamal Airee took 4 for 26 in four overs, including the wicket of Andre Russell that broke Biratnagar's chase. Dawid Malan's 70 off 50 was the innings of the match, and Imran Sheikh's 39 off 31 kept the Kings in it until the end.

Points table

Two points for Chitwan, none for Biratnagar. It was the first win of the season for the Rhinos and the first defeat for the Kings, who had started their campaign with a chase that fell eight runs short. The NPL 2026 season was only two matches old, and both teams still had six league games to shape their run.
