The turning point

The Bolts were 56 for 6 after eleven overs. Harmeet Singh’s 33 off 20 and Aaditya Mahata’s unbeaten 30 off 24 added 43 for the eighth wicket, but the chase still ended 16 runs short.

Top performers

Rohit Paudel was Player of the Match for his 34 off 30 balls. D’Arcy Short’s 3 for 23 was the best bowling return of the match, and Tilak Bhandari took 2 for 5 in two overs. For the Bolts, Harmeet Singh’s 33 off 20 was the top score of the chase.

Points table

The win took Lumbini to seven points — three wins and the shared point from Match 7 — clear at the top of the table and still unbeaten after four matches. Janakpur stayed on four points after their third defeat in five matches.
