01 — What happened

What happened

Sudurpaschim Royals beat Chitwan Rhinos by five wickets in Match 11 at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur on Monday, 2 November 2026, in the NPL.

Chitwan made 129 for 6, with Ravi Bopara top-scoring on 49 off 40 balls. Scott Kuggeleijn took 2 for 26 for the Royals, and Anil Kharel’s 1 for 18 in four overs kept the scoring down.

Brandon McMullen’s 43 off 21 balls set up the chase, and Ishan Pandey made 25 off 29. Aarif Sheikh finished unbeaten on 20 off 26 and Sherfane Rutherford on 21 off 15, and the Royals reached 131 for 5 with 20 balls left. Ranjit Kumar took 3 for 28 for the Rhinos.

02 — The turning point

The turning point

McMullen hit six fours and two sixes in his 43, and the Royals were 70 for 3 after six overs. Dipendra Singh Airee fell for a duck the next ball, but Pandey and the lower order kept the chase on track, and the target was reached with more than three overs to spare.

03 — Top performers

Top performers

Brandon McMullen was Player of the Match for 43 off 21 balls. Bopara’s 49 off 40 was the top score of the match, and Ranjit Kumar’s 3 for 28 was the best bowling return. Kuggeleijn’s 2 for 26 and Kharel’s 1 for 18 kept Chitwan to 129.

04 — What it meant for the points table

What it meant for the points table

The win took Sudurpaschim to four points from four matches, level with Karnali Yaks and Janakpur Bolts at the top. Chitwan stayed on three points — one win, one defeat and the shared point from the rained-off Match 7.
