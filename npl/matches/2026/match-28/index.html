Pokhara Avengers vs Kathmandu Gorkhas, Sunday, 15 November 2026 at 5:00 PM NST

The 28th match of the Nepal Premier League season is the evening game of Sunday 15 November 2026, the final day of the league stage, starting at 5:00 PM NST — 11:15 AM UTC, 4:15 PM PKT — at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. It follows the afternoon match between Chitwan Rhinos and Karnali Yaks, and when it ends, the league stage is over and the four playoff teams are known.

This is the first meeting of the two sides this season. Kathmandu Gorkhas sit third on 8 points from six matches with a net run rate of +0.882; Pokhara Avengers sit last on 2 points from six matches. The full scorecard will appear on this page once the match is played.

Pokhara arrive at the final league match knowing exactly what they need, because every other result is already in. Kathmandu arrive knowing a win would cap a season in which they have won four of their last five matches — and that even a defeat is unlikely to cost them a playoff place. The two sides want very different things from the same evening.
