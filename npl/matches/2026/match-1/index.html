Player of the Match Sher Malla took 3 for 11 in four overs at 2.75 an over, and Kishor Mahato's 4 for 22 was the other half of the new-ball squeeze. With the bat, D'Arcy Short's 40 off 18 balls — four fours, three sixes — ended the chase before it could tighten.

Player of the Match: Sher Malla (Lumbini Lions, 3/11).

Points table

Two points for Lumbini, none for the Royals — the first points of the season. The win also gave Lumbini the early net run rate lift, with the chase finished in under ten overs. The NPL 2026 season continues at Kirtipur, and the Royals had to wait for their next match to put a first win on the board.
