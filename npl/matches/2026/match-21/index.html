The turning point

The match was decided by the bowling. Harmeet Singh took 4 for 17 in four overs for Janakpur, which kept Royals to 120. But Scott Kuggeleijn answered with 3 for 13 in four overs for Royals, and Harry Tector went for only nine runs in his four overs. Janakpur's batters could not score freely against either of them.

Top performers

Scott Kuggeleijn was Player of the Match for 3 for 13, to go with his 6 off three balls with the bat. Brandon McMullen's 33 was the top score of the match, and Harmeet Singh's 4 for 17 took him to 11 wickets for the season — the most in the league — at an economy of 5.83.

Points table

The win took Sudurpaschim to eight points, level with Kathmandu Gorkhas and second in the table. Janakpur stayed on four points from six matches.
