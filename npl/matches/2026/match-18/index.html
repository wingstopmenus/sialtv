The turning point

Lumbini were 28 for 6 inside five overs. Jahandad Khan took four wickets for 23 runs, removing D'Arcy Short, Aasif Sheikh, Niroshan Dickwella and Dilip Nath, and Karan KC took two more. Jora held one end for a while, but the chase was gone before it had begun.

Top performers

David Warner was Player of the Match for 66 off 50 balls. Jahandad Khan's 4 for 23 was the best bowling of the match, and Sahan Arachchige's unbeaten 34 off 15 balls — plus the final wicket, taken without conceding a run in his three balls — rounded off a fine all-round game.

What it meant for the points table

The win took Kathmandu to six points, level with Lumbini Lions and Sudurpaschim Royals at the top of the table. Lumbini stayed on six points.
