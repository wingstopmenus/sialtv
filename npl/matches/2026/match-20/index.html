Kathmandu Gorkhas beat Chitwan Rhinos by 10 runs

01 — What happened

What happened

Kathmandu Gorkhas beat Chitwan Rhinos by 10 runs in the 20th match of Nepal Premier League Season 3 at Kirtipur on Monday 9 November 2026, defending 180 for 8 against a chase that finished on 170 for 7.

Kathmandu lost David Warner for 1 and Sahil Patel for 8 and were 13 for 2, but the middle order rebuilt. William Bosisto made 27, Rashid Khan 52 off 30 balls with four sixes, Sahan Arachchige 33 off 20 and Karan KC 20 off seven balls, and Kathmandu reached 180 for 8.

Chitwan's reply was led by Ravi Bopara, who made 55 off 35 balls, and Kamil Mishara, who hit 28 off 11 at the top. Dan Mousley made 29 off 32, but Chitwan finished seven short in the end on 170 for 7.

02 — The turning point

The turning point

The match turned in the final over. Bopara fell for 55 with the score on 164 in the 20th over, and Chitwan needed 16 off the last four balls. They managed six. Earlier, Rijan Dhakal's 3 for 14 in four overs had kept Kathmandu's total within reach.

03 — Top performers

Top performers

Rashid Khan was Player of the Match for 52 off 30 balls, the innings that took Kathmandu from 13 for 2 to 180. Ravi Bopara's 55 off 35 kept Chitwan in the hunt, and Sahan Arachchige backed his 33 with the bat by taking 2 for 37.

04 — What it meant for the points table

What it meant for the points table

The win took Kathmandu to eight points, clear at the top of the table. Chitwan stayed on four points.
