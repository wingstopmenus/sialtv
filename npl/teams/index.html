The eight teams

Kathmandu Gorkhas

1st — 8 points, NRR +1.168

Captain: Karan KC · Coach: Monty Desai

Table-toppers after five matches, even after David Warner flew home.

Sudurpaschim Royals

2nd — 8 points, NRR +0.069

Captain: Dipendra Singh Airee · Coach: Misbah-ul-Haq

Beaten finalists in both previous seasons — and fresh from beating Janakpur by 27 runs.

Lumbini Lions

3rd — 7 points, NRR +1.501

Captain: Rohit Paudel · Coach: Tinu Yohannan

Defending champions with the best net run rate in the league.

Chitwan Rhinos

4th — 5 points, NRR +0.009

Captain: Kushal Malla · Coach: Sohail Tanvir

Carried by two of the season's top wicket-takers.

Janakpur Bolts

5th — 4 points, NRR −0.303

Captain: Harmeet Singh · Coach: Shivnarine Chanderpaul

Inaugural champions in 2024 — now they must win their last match to stay alive.

Biratnagar Kings

6th — 4 points, NRR −0.820

Captain: Sandeep Lamichhane · Coach: Dhammika Prasad

Home to the league's top run-scorer, Lokesh Bam, but sixth in the table.

Karnali Yaks

7th — 4 points, NRR −0.846

Captain: Sompal Kami · Coach: Gyanendra Malla

Sompal Kami has nine wickets in five matches — joint-third in the season.

Pokhara Avengers

8th — 2 points, NRR −0.707

Captain: Kushal Bhurtel · Coach: Ajantha Mendis

One win from five, but they posted the season's highest total — 217 for 3.

Frequently asked questions

How many teams play in NPL Season 3?

Eight teams, each playing seven league matches, with the top four reaching the playoffs. All 32 matches are at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur.

Which team leads the NPL 2026 points table?

Kathmandu Gorkhas lead after Match 21 with 8 points and a net run rate of +1.168, ahead of Sudurpaschim Royals on the same points. Lumbini Lions are third with 7 points and the league's best net run rate, +1.501.
