Karnali Yaks NPL 2026 — Squad, Results & Points Table

Karnali Yaks won their first two matches of NPL Season 3, then lost three in a row. They sit seventh with four points from five matches, and the season now comes down to two games: beat Lumbini Lions on Thursday, beat Chitwan Rhinos on Sunday, and the playoffs are back in reach.

Where the Yaks stand

Two wins, three defeats, and a net run rate of minus 0.846 — that is Karnali's season after five matches. The Yaks opened with back-to-back wins, 19 runs over Janakpur Bolts and four wickets over Biratnagar Kings, and for a week they looked like a playoff side. Since then, Kathmandu Gorkhas beat them by four wickets, Sudurpaschim Royals by 39 runs, and Pokhara Avengers by 63 runs.

The table is tight enough that none of it is fatal yet. Chitwan Rhinos sit fourth on five points, and three teams — Janakpur Bolts, Biratnagar Kings and Karnali — sit on four. What separates them is net run rate, and Karnali's minus 0.846 is the worst in the league. Two wins would take the Yaks to eight points, which is probably enough — but only if Biratnagar, who can also reach eight, drop a point somewhere.

Who has delivered

Ariful Islam leads Karnali's batting with 143 runs from five matches, including 12 sixes. Pawan Sarraf has 140, and the two of them have carried the top order. David Miller has played only twice but made 100 runs, including 93 — the highest individual score by a Karnali batter this season.

Sompal Kami has done the most with the ball: nine wickets from five matches, joint-third in the season's wicket race, and 93 runs with the bat, including an unbeaten 62. Hussain Talat has six wickets, and Mark Watt and Nandan Yadav have five each.

Frequently asked questions

Where do Karnali Yaks sit in the NPL 2026 points table?

Seventh, with four points from five matches — two wins and three defeats — and a net run rate of minus 0.846, the worst in the league. Chitwan Rhinos hold the last playoff place on five points. Two wins from their last two matches would take them to eight.

Who is the Karnali Yaks captain?

Sompal Kami, who also leads the side with the ball — nine wickets from five matches, joint-third in the season — and has scored 93 runs, including an unbeaten 62. Kami has captained Karnali in all five matches so far, and no other Karnali bowler has taken more than six wickets.

Who are Karnali Yaks' top scorers this season?

Ariful Islam leads with 143 runs from five matches, including 12 sixes. Pawan Sarraf has 140, and David Miller made 100 runs in just two appearances, including 93 — Karnali's highest individual score of the season. Sompal Kami has 93 runs as well, including an unbeaten 62.

What do Karnali Yaks need to reach the playoffs?

Two wins from two: Lumbini Lions on Thursday 12 November and Chitwan Rhinos on Sunday 15 November. That takes them to eight points. Even then, Biratnagar Kings can also reach eight, so net run rate may decide it. A single defeat leaves them on six points at most, needing favours elsewhere.
