Season so far

The Royals’ season has been a climb. They lost the opener to Lumbini Lions by seven wickets, then lost a rain-shortened five-over match to Biratnagar Kings by 35 runs. Since then they have won three in a row — Chitwan Rhinos by five wickets, Karnali Yaks by 39 runs and Janakpur Bolts by 27 — and the latest win, in Match 21 on Tuesday 10 November, put them level on points with Kathmandu at the top.

Dipendra Singh Airee captains the side, and former Pakistan captain Misbah-ul-Haq is head coach. Seven players were retained from the side that reached last season’s final — Dipendra, Binod Bhandari, Aarif Sheikh, Ishan Pandey, Abinash Bohara, Hemant Dhami and Hikmat Mahara — and the settled core is why the Royals keep winning tight matches: three of their four wins this season have come by 27 runs or fewer, or by five wickets.

Newest first. The Match 21 win showed what this side does best: defend a modest total.

Brandon McMullen top-scored with 33 off 23 in Match 21, and Scott Kuggeleijn took 3 for 13 and was named Player of the Match as Janakpur were bowled out for 93; Harmeet Singh took 4 for 17 in the same match. Tharindu Ratnayake finished 27 not out and took 1 for 12 in the same game. The 39-run win over Karnali in Match 16 was the most comfortable — 176 for 6, the Royals’ highest total of the season so far, with Saif Zaib making 35 and Binod Bhandari 27. The defeat that still stings is the five-over match against Biratnagar, lost by 35 runs after rain cut the game to five overs a side.

History

No team has been as consistent as the Royals, and no team has been as unlucky. They reached the final in both seasons played so far — beaten by Janakpur Bolts in the Season 1 final in 2024 and by Lumbini Lions in the Season 2 final in 2025 — and their 14 wins from 18 matches across those two seasons are the best record of any team in the league.

Season 3 is a third attempt at the same prize. Four more wins have taken the tally to 18 wins from 24 matches, and the confirmed overseas signings this season are Saif Ali Zaib, Scott Kuggeleijn and Brandon McMullen. The Royals are based in Dhangadhi, in Sudurpaschim Province, and play their home matches at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, like every other team in the league.

The Royals cannot miss the playoffs either. Only three teams can still pass 8 points — Lumbini Lions (11 maximum), Kathmandu Gorkhas (10) and Chitwan Rhinos (9) — so even a defeat in their last match leaves them no lower than fourth. Next match

Match 24, Friday 13 November 2026, 12:30 PM NST (11:45 AM PKT, 6:45 AM UTC), TU International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur. First against second, both on 8 points, and the winner reaches 10 and takes the lead at the top. It is the first meeting of the two sides this season.

Frequently asked questions

Who captains Sudurpaschim Royals?

Dipendra Singh Airee. The all-rounder leads the side in Season 3, with former Pakistan captain Misbah-ul-Haq as head coach. Dipendra has 90 runs in six matches and 5 wickets, and the Royals have won four of their six matches under him.

Have Sudurpaschim Royals ever won the NPL?

No. The Royals have reached the final in both seasons played so far and lost both — to Janakpur Bolts in 2024 and to Lumbini Lions in 2025. They hold the league’s best win record, with 14 wins from 18 matches across the first two seasons, and are chasing a first title in Season 3.

Where do Sudurpaschim Royals stand in the NPL points table?

Second, with 8 points from six matches (four wins, two losses) and a net run rate of +0.082. They are level on points with leaders Kathmandu Gorkhas and behind on net run rate. They are already certain of a playoff place.

When is Sudurpaschim Royals’ next match?

Match 24 against Kathmandu Gorkhas, on Friday 13 November 2026 at 12:30 PM NST (11:45 AM PKT, 6:45 AM UTC), at the TU International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur. It is their last league match; a win takes them to 10 points and the top of the table.
