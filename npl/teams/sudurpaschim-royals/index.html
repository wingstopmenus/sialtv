History

Sudur Paschim Royals reached the final in both previous seasons — losing to Janakpur Bolts in 2024 and to Lumbini Lions in 2025 — and sit second in Season 3 with 10 points from 7 matches, behind Lumbini Lions on 11 points. Dipendra Singh Airee captains the side, with Misbah-ul-Haq as coach.
