Janakpur Bolts 2026 — Squad, Results & Next Match

The first champions of the Nepal Premier League, now fighting for their season. Janakpur Bolts won the inaugural title in 2024, finished last in 2025, and sit fifth with one match left in Season 3 — a must-win against Chitwan Rhinos on Friday.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Where the Bolts stand

Janakpur Bolts are fifth in NPL Season 3 after Match 21, on 4 points from six matches with a net run rate of −0.303. They have one league match left. Win it and they reach 6 points. Lose it and the season is over.

The table is tight at the bottom. Chitwan Rhinos sit fourth on 5 points, and Janakpur, Biratnagar Kings and Karnali Yaks are all on 4. The Bolts have the best net run rate of the three — −0.303 against Biratnagar’s −0.820 and Karnali’s −0.846 — so if the race comes down to a tie on 6 points, Janakpur hold the tiebreak.

What Janakpur need to qualify

First, they must beat Chitwan Rhinos on Friday. That takes them to 6 points and drops Chitwan to 5 with one match left. Then they need Chitwan to lose their last match as well — to Karnali Yaks on Sunday — and Biratnagar Kings and Karnali Yaks to win no more than one of their two remaining matches each, and Pokhara Avengers to win no more than one of theirs. If all of that happens, Janakpur finish fourth on 6 points, ahead of the other 6-point teams on net run rate. Any other combination leaves them short: a defeat on Friday ends their season outright, because Chitwan already sit on 5 points and Lumbini Lions on 7.

The season so far

Two wins, four defeats — and two of those defeats were decided in the final over. The Bolts have been in every match they have lost except the last one, when Sudurpaschim Royals bowled them out for 93.

The squad

Seventeen names, built around a new captain and three overseas all-rounders. Five players were retained from last season, eight came through the July auction, and three arrived on direct overseas contracts. The Bolts spent NPR 41.25 lakh at the auction — the second-highest outlay in the league — and kept NPR 25 lakh of their purse unspent.

Captain: Harmeet Singh. Coach: Shivnarine Chanderpaul. Marquee player: Lalit Narayan Rajbanshi, the left-arm spinner retained as the side’s marquee name after Aasif Sheikh’s departure.

The players carrying the season

One man has carried Janakpur’s bowling almost on his own. The batting has been spread thin — nobody in the side has passed 150 runs in the season so far.

Harmeet Singh leads the whole league in wickets. The captain’s 4 for 17 against Sudurpaschim Royals in Match 21 took him to 11 wickets in six matches, at an economy of 5.83. He also made 65 runs in five innings, so the bat has not been idle either.

Moeen Ali has been the side’s most complete cricketer: 108 runs in four matches, including a fifty, and 7 wickets at an economy under 5. Trit Raj Das has 112 runs with a fifty of his own, and Saif Hassan has 140 runs in four matches — the most of any Janakpur batter. Rahul Yadav sits second in the side’s wicket chart with 9, and Lalit Narayan Rajbanshi has 4 wickets in six matches.

Next match

Janakpur Bolts vs Chitwan Rhinos, Match 25, on Friday 13 November 2026 at 4:30 PM NST (3:45 PM PKT, 10:45 AM UTC), at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur.

It is the Bolts’ last league match and it is a straight shootout: the winner stays in the playoff race and the loser is out. Chitwan sit one place above Janakpur on 5 points, so a Bolts win takes them past the Rhinos on the table — and leaves Chitwan needing to beat Karnali Yaks on Sunday to stay alive themselves.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the Janakpur Bolts captain and coach?

Harmeet Singh captains Janakpur Bolts in Season 3. The USA international is a slow left-arm spinner who lost two straight NPL finals with Sudurpaschim Royals before switching sides — the first of those finals, in 2024, to Janakpur themselves. Shivnarine Chanderpaul, the former West Indies batter, is the head coach.

What do Janakpur Bolts need to qualify for the playoffs?

They must beat Chitwan Rhinos on Friday 13 November to reach 6 points. Then they need Chitwan to lose their last match to Karnali Yaks on Sunday, Biratnagar Kings and Karnali Yaks to win no more than one of their two remaining matches each, and Pokhara Avengers to win no more than one of theirs. Janakpur’s net run rate of −0.303 is the best of the four teams on 4 points, so a tie on 6 points would favour them.

Who are Janakpur Bolts’ key players this season?

Captain Harmeet Singh leads the league with 11 wickets in six matches. Moeen Ali has 108 runs and 7 wickets, Trit Raj Das has 112 runs with a fifty, and Saif Hassan has 140 runs — the most of any Bolts batter. Lalit Narayan Rajbanshi, the side’s marquee player, has 4 wickets in six matches.

When is Janakpur Bolts’ next match?

Friday 13 November 2026, against Chitwan Rhinos in Match 25, at 4:30 PM NST (3:45 PM PKT, 10:45 AM UTC) at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. It is their last league match of the season.
