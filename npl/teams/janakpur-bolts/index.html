History

Janakpur Bolts won the first Nepal Premier League in 2024, beating Sudur Paschim Royals by 5 wickets in the final. Harmeet Singh captains the side in Season 3, with Shivnarine Chanderpaul as coach. Their Season 3 is over — 7 matches, 2 wins — and they cannot reach the playoffs.

Pakistan Players in Doubt for Nepal Premier League
Seven Pakistani players were named for three franchises — but only Hussain Talat played. Farhan was sent home, Iftikhar and Hunain will not play for Janakpur this season, and the PCB reportedly asked for an extra US$25,000 per player for NOCs.
