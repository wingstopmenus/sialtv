History

Lumbini Lions are the defending champions — they won Season 2 in 2025, beating Sudur Paschim Royals by 6 wickets in the final after finishing bottom in Season 1. Rohit Paudel captains the side, with Tinu Yohannan as coach. They were the first team into the Season 3 playoffs.
