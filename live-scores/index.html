Live scores, kept honest

SialTV follows every NPL, ILT20 and APL match as it happens. While a match is in progress the score on this page is refreshed from verified live sources, roughly every ten minutes, and the time of that last refresh is printed next to the score. Between matches the page shows the latest result and the next fixture for each league. Once a match ends, the final result is written into the page and the score feed, so the numbers here and on the league results pages always agree.

There is no ball-by-ball commentary here. For the full scorecard of a finished match, each league's results page lists every match with its scores and margins.
