Match 26 — innings summary

Biratnagar Kings made 166 for 4 in 20 overs, then Lumbini Lions chased it down with 168 for 6 in 19.5 overs. Charith Asalanka top-scored for the Kings with 64 off 43 balls, and Shubham Ranjane made 31 not out off 24; Kishor Mahato took 2 for 24 for the Lions. In reply D'Arcy Short made 45 off 38 and Aasif Sheikh 45 off 41 for the Lions; Aprajit Poudel took 2 for 5 for the Kings.

Live scores, kept honest

SialTV follows every NPL, ILT20 and APL match as it happens. While a match is in progress the score on this page is refreshed from verified live sources, roughly every ten minutes, and the time of that last refresh is printed next to the score. Between matches the page shows the latest result and the next fixture for each league. Once a match ends, the final result is written into the page and the score feed, so the numbers here and on the league fixtures and results pages always agree.

There is no ball-by-ball commentary here. For the full scorecard of a finished match, each league's fixtures and results page lists every match with its scores and margins — NPL, ILT20 and APL.
