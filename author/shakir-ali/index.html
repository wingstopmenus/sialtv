About Shakir

Shakir Ali covers the grounds and host territories on the leagues’ calendars for SialTV. His venue work combines cricket history with practical details such as location, schedule windows and matchday planning.

He keeps travel advice tied to confirmed tournament information and directs readers to authorized ticket channels rather than unverified sellers.

His method: he checks venue identity, location and fixture windows before publication, separates permanent ground facts from event-specific arrangements, and avoids promising transport, entry or ticket details that organizers have not confirmed.
