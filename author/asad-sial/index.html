About Asad

Asad Sial is the cricket writer for SialTV and its Tournament Data Editor. He writes the site’s cricket coverage — league schedules, live scores, points tables, team and player pages, and where-to-watch guides — for the six leagues SialTV follows.

Every fixture, score, squad list and broadcaster he publishes is checked against official and reliable sources before it goes live. Anything that cannot be verified is marked as such, never guessed — and if a mistake slips through, it is corrected and the page’s last-updated date says so.

Asad’s coverage connects the match schedule, team directory, venue list and points table for each league so readers can move through a competition without conflicting details. Before a tournament starts, the standings remain at zero; once results arrive, he checks the completed match and table state before publishing an update. He keeps schedule, team and venue references aligned, updates standings only after a confirmed result, and checks that tournament pages link back to the correct team and ground records.

SialTV’s other author is Saleem Sial, who writes about cricket leagues here the same way he does on NPL Cricket League and APL T20 League.
