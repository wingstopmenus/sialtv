About Arshad

Arshad Sial manages the broadcast desk at SialTV. His work tracks confirmed television partners, streaming services and viewing availability across the leagues’ markets.

Broadcast rights can change by territory and platform. He separates confirmed coverage from pending markets and avoids presenting an unverified stream as an authorized option.

His method: he checks broadcaster-owned pages and current league announcements, labels unconfirmed territories instead of guessing a platform, and reviews viewing information when rights or service availability changes.
